# Character Relationships

Record relationships as directed, time-bounded edges: what A feels, believes, owes, fears, or is authorised to do toward B may differ from the reverse.

Every change cites an accepted event or decision. Distinguish kinship, affection, trust, duty, authority, contract, debt, reputation, access, custody, and control.

Use `../templates/character-relationship.md` inside the owning work. Cross-work relationship edges are invalid without an explicit approved cross-work contract.
