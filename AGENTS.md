# Repository Instructions

## Authority

- The Author alone accepts canon, plot, voice, and System changes.
- Treat the repository as confidential and private; do not publish, change
  visibility, or send its contents outside the authorized repository/workflow.
- Never silently promote a draft, inference, research claim, fixture, or agent invention into canon.
- `main` contains the accepted repository baseline, including clearly labelled
  non-canonical research, fixtures, templates, and tooling. Work on a scoped
  branch or proposal until the applicable review and Author gates are complete.

## Source handling

- Follow `governance/SOURCE-AUTHORITY.md` and the nearest nested `AGENTS.md`.
- Treat CAL0 v0.7.0 as a locked, content-pinned baseline. Use a governed successor change for modifications.
- Research and CAL0 reference characters, scenarios, and cohort outputs are non-canonical.
- Preserve stable IDs, provenance, exact decimal strings, and distinctions between causal truth, records, access, interpretation, presentation, and reader disclosure.

## Changes

- Record substantive alternatives as proposals; do not overwrite disagreement.
- A chapter is not accepted until its manuscript and explicit chapter delta are approved together.
- Update indexes and derived story state only from accepted source records.
- Do not duplicate canonical sources or create manual version-copy filenames.
