# LitRPG Research

The [`litrpg-system-research-dossier.md`](litrpg-system-research-dossier.md) is research context and comparative evidence. It is not the mechanics specification and cannot override CAL0.

Use it to understand genre conventions, explore alternatives, and support explicit successor proposals. Any mechanic drawn from research must be reconciled with the accepted design constitution, causal invariants, and Author decisions before it can become System canon.
