# Research

Research supplies evidence, comparison, terminology, and creative stimulus. It is never canon by itself.

Organise notes by subject and retain enough provenance to locate the original source. Distinguish direct evidence, paraphrase, inference, speculation, and creative adaptation. Record publication details and access dates for external sources where relevant.

To use a research conclusion in the setting, story, character history, or System, create a proposal and route it through the relevant Author decision. Copyrighted sources may inform analysis but must not be reproduced beyond permitted quotation or adapted so closely that the repository becomes a substitute for the original.
