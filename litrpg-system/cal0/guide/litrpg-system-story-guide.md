# LitRPG System Story Guide

**Guide version:** 0.1  
**Compatible specification:** 0.88  
**Compatible calibration annex:** 2.8  
**Purpose:** concise scene-planning and drafting reference

This guide is a projection of the canonical specification. When a simplified
rule conflicts with the specification, the specification wins. The guide tells
an author what usually matters in a scene; it does not replace the hidden causal
model.

## The six-layer scene rule

For every System-relevant moment, distinguish:

1. what is causally true;
2. what the System or another source has recorded;
3. what the viewpoint character may access;
4. what that character can interpret;
5. what their interface or culture presents;
6. what the reader needs to see now.

Most prose should show only layers five and six. Author notes retain the others.

## Ten rules that should almost never be broken

1. There is no universal Character Level or total power score.
2. Attributes measure absolute reliable capacity, not age-relative talent.
3. Natural maturation, organic adaptation, knowledge, Skill XP, Class XP,
   reinforcement, and assimilation are separate causes.
4. Skills and classes progress only after acceptance. Earlier experience may
   justify an offer or recognition floor, but never retroactive formal XP.
5. One event may satisfy several genuinely different live requirements, but a
   renamed, split, copied, reset, or replayed event cannot duplicate one reward.
6. Matter, energy, Mana, identity, evidence, authority, access, and entitlements
   require provenance.
7. Interface text reveals or projects; it does not create the fact, capability,
   permission, ownership, or resource displayed.
8. Damage is not automatic growth. Productive adaptation needs relevant load,
   technique, recovery, and remaining headroom.
9. Resurrection, reincarnation, copying, possession, and succession cannot
   produce two continuing holders of one identity.
10. Efficient training, industry, institutions, and magical infrastructure are
    legitimate when their outputs are causally funded.

## Attributes

The eleven attributes are:

- Body: Might, Finesse, Alacrity, Vitality.
- Boundary and perception: Perception.
- Mind: Cognition, Focus, Will.
- Soul: Depth, Coherence, Resonance.

An attribute is an absolute decimal projection of enduring reliable capacity.
It is not current energy, health, knowledge, virtue, personality, or a fixed
genetic ceiling.

Use attributes in prose as evidence of what a character can usually sustain.
Keep exact values in backend or author sheets unless the viewpoint has access
and the number matters now.

### Attribute change

- Maturation raises capacity as real structures develop.
- Purposeful training supplies a load.
- Organic adaptation is the body's, mind's, or Soul's contingent response.
- Level reinforcement is an earned absolute claim.
- Assimilation determines how much of that claim can safely become expressed
  capacity now; the remainder stays protected as backlog.
- Injury, illness, deprivation, fatigue, and hostile conditions can reduce
  current expression without deleting earned history.

Never write `100 press-ups = +0.01 Might`. Record the programme, loaded
structures, challenge, feedback, recovery, and resulting adaptation.

## Health, Stamina, and Mana

### Health

Health summarises integrity, function, homeostasis, and viability. Harm creates
specific injuries and active processes. Healing may need stabilisation, supply,
repair, reintegration, and rehabilitation. A full-looking projection cannot
restore a severed structure that has not been repaired.

### Stamina

Stamina is a task-conditioned estimate of operating headroom, not a universal
fuel bar. Local muscle fatigue, breathing, heat, pain, attention, equipment,
terrain, fear, injury, and recovery may each become the limiting factor.

### Mana

Mana capability is a network: access, perception, capture, compatibility,
refinement, containment, circulation, draw, shaping, release, reclamation, and
maintenance can fail separately. A large reserve does not imply fast draw,
control, regeneration, or immunity to interference.

## Skills

A Skill is a coherent developmental domain with a persistent ancestry and a
method graph. Its familiar name is a projection, not a magical label that owns
every related act.

To gain a Skill, a character needs a real developmental pipeline: input,
interpretation, attempt, feedback, calibration, demonstrated capability,
recognition, and acceptance. Grants must state what they actually provide.

After acceptance:

- Skill XP records genuine new development in that lineage;
- qualitative attestation checks whether the demonstrated profile supports the
  next threshold;
- each completed threshold creates one conserved reinforcement entitlement;
- a blocked threshold can retain bounded directional overhang, but cannot bank
  a cascade of later levels;
- techniques and specialisations may develop inside the Skill without creating
  artificial new XP lineages.

Skill suppression, dormancy, fusion, splitting, correction, loss, and
reacquisition preserve history and cannot repay completed levels.

## Classes

A class recognises a sustained role-pattern: conduct, judgement,
responsibility, integration, and contribution. Occupation, title, office,
authority, and class are related but distinct.

Acceptance creates prospective Class XP. Class XP is not copied from Skill XP,
kills, repetitive tasks, delegated outcomes, or a costume worn to manipulate an
offer.

The protagonist's pure-Mage path means magic remains his organising preference.
It does not forbid nonmagical Skills or classes. Anatomy, mathematics, craft,
leadership, medicine, and other domains retain their own progression; they can
later inform genuinely new magical research.

Completing one class form does not transfer its XP into a successor. Later forms
need new evidence, responsibility, opportunity, survival, and development.

## Training and learning

For physical, cognitive, meditative, magical, or Soul-directed practice, record:

- structures or capacities loaded;
- intensity, duration, volume, frequency, and composition;
- novelty, complexity, technique, feedback, and transfer;
- current foundation, age, health, condition, and prior adaptation;
- food, sleep, safety, tools, teachers, peers, and environment;
- interference, fatigue, injury, assimilation, and recovery;
- headroom, maintenance, diminishing returns, detraining, and maladaptation.

One activity may produce several separately attributable results. Mathematics
practice can increase knowledge, Mathematics Skill XP, reliable Focus, and
Cognition adaptation. It does not convert all four into one generic mental stat
reward.

Replaying a solved puzzle under many names, splitting one workout into
micro-Skills, or suffering pain without a productive adaptation process creates
no extra value.

## Magic

A spell is an executable magical method graph, not merely an effect name.
Preserve its source, inputs, dependencies, stages, control, operating conditions,
interruption points, completion semantics, residuals, and failure modes.

Research separates observation, model, representation, design, implementation,
proof, replication, improvement, and teaching. Teaching can transmit
information, examples, scaffolding, feedback, and attestation; it cannot hand
over earned mastery.

Rituals, wards, enchantments, constructs, and familiar bonds distribute work
across real actors, structures, inputs, permissions, and maintenance. Delegation
can increase scale without creating free Mana or copying personal progression.

## Combat

Do not decide combat from one level or attribute comparison. Consider:

- perception and information;
- readiness, surprise, and positioning;
- equipment, terrain, weather, and prepared structures;
- commitment, execution, propagation, reaction, and recovery;
- local capability, group coordination, logistics, reserves, and morale;
- wounds, conditions, countermeasures, concealment, and escape.

A powerful individual may defeat many people locally yet still lose to a
prepared institution that distributes information, wards, reserves, responders,
and recovery across time and space.

## Items, crafting, and loot

Items are provenance-bearing assemblies. Identity can persist through repair or
replacement when meaningful structural, functional, magical, and historical
continuity remains. A fragment does not clone the complete identity or unique
entitlements of its parent.

Keep quality, condition, durability, rarity, value, power, ownership,
authentication, binding, and appraisal confidence distinct.

Loot comes from remains, possessions, harvest, salvage, treasure, contracts, or
separate System entitlements. Enemies do not produce universal corpse drops.

## Dungeons, creatures, and ecology

True spawning and spawn points exist only inside causally bounded dungeons and
labyrinths. Spawning needs a mechanism, conserved inputs, capacity, recovery,
and ecological consequences. Resetting a room does not restock it for free.

Ordinary-world animals, plants, mythological species, magical organisms, and
alien lifeforms reproduce, migrate, compete, feed, age, and die through real
life cycles. A monster label creates neither a universal combat level nor a
loot table.

Dungeon industries and domestication can transform societies. Their continuing
output depends on renewal, stewardship, control, labour, knowledge, and the
costs displaced into the surrounding ecology.

## Parties and institutions

Parties coordinate real people; they do not merge personal attributes, Skills,
classes, XP, identities, or consent. Contribution follows causal involvement,
not the killing blow alone.

Institutions can possess distributed capability through people, assets,
procedures, archives, authority, infrastructure, and reliable coordination.
Rank and office may grant access or control without becoming personal magical
Skill. Turnover, corruption, record error, coercion, inequality, and recovery
support remain story-relevant.

## Death, resurrection, and reincarnation

Separate embodied viability, consciousness, Soul integrity, coupling, identity
lineage, preserved pattern, System continuity, and the practical return route.

A return succeeds only when its mechanism can support every required layer.
Completed progression remains historical. It is neither deleted nor repaid.
Copies and memory reconstructions may become derived people, but do not inherit
the original identity or entitlements merely by resemblance.

## The protagonist

Reincarnate Continuity preserves identity and memory patterns. It transfers no
Earth attributes, formal Skills, classes, XP, or adult biological capacity.

Embodied Integration is earned through prenatal intent, sensation, movement,
feedback, and calibration. Soul Consolidation may differentiate when genuine
Soul-local practice produces distinct evidence.

“Unusual Soul Strength” is not a scalar multiplier. Track its consequences
through actual Depth, Coherence, Resonance, boundary integrity, coupling,
recovery, safe assimilation, and earned Skills. This avoids a hidden universal
Soul-power statistic and lets different strengths, weaknesses, injuries, and
methods matter.

Before birth, the protagonist may gain:

- natural attributes through growth;
- knowledge and calibration through experience;
- accepted Skills where he has the required contact and consent;
- prospective Skill XP;
- absolute level-reinforcement claims;
- partial safe assimilation, with the rest retained as backlog.

He remains a fetus and then a child. Continuity supplies time and direction, not
adult muscles, language output, social authority, resources, or unrestricted
agency.

Long-term overwhelming power should arise from synthesis, preparation,
infrastructure, knowledge, experimentation, deep progression, and time. It does
not remove surprise, incomplete information, simultaneous-control limits,
recovery, hostile environments, political dependency, or threats to others.

## Notifications

Good notifications are concise projections:

> **Embodied Integration increased.**  
> Improved: movement–sensation calibration  
> Reinforcement: partially assimilated  
> Recovery demand: elevated

> **New class offer: Mage.**  
> Basis: sustained magical perception, shaping, study, and responsibility  
> Acceptance creates prospective Class progression.

> **Appraisal result — moderate confidence.**  
> Condition: recovering tendon  
> Unknown: deeper enchantment and Soul involvement

Do not expose backend equations, secret facts, other people's private sheets, or
perfect diagnoses unless a real capability and access path support them.

## Scene-planning checklist

Before drafting a progression or System scene, answer:

1. What changed causally?
2. Which ledger records it: maturation, training load, adaptation, Skill XP,
   Class XP, reinforcement, assimilation, resource, condition, identity,
   evidence, authority, or entitlement?
3. What did it cost, require, consume, risk, or leave unfinished?
4. What is the canonical event origin, and could any label accidentally count
   it twice?
5. What does the viewpoint actually know?
6. What can their interface legitimately reveal?
7. What is important enough to show the reader now?
8. What vulnerability, uncertainty, recovery demand, or future consequence
   remains?

## Common authoring failures

- Awarding formal XP for pre-acceptance history.
- Treating natural growth as a level reward.
- Letting one activity produce the same gain through several labels.
- Using class level as combat power.
- Giving a title the authority or capability it merely describes.
- Treating a Mana pool as generation, control, or spell knowledge.
- Allowing healing to skip repair or rehabilitation.
- Restocking dungeons without inputs and recovery.
- Copying item, person, Soul, quest, or reward identity through renaming or
  reconstruction.
- Presenting appraisal as omniscience.
- Making institutions powerless because individuals have levels.
- Making the protagonist's reincarnation a transfer of Earth progression.

When a scene seems to require one of these shortcuts, record a contradiction
instead of silently overriding the System.
