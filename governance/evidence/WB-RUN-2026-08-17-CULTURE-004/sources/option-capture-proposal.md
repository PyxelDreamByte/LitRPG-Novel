## Option A — CENSUS-AND-LADDER

A records-and-rank civilization. The administrative unit was the registered
household; the career unit was the service ladder — civil, military, and
works ladders with graded ranks, advancement by examination classes and
accumulated service record. Full personhood in practice meant being on the
rolls: the registered had standing before imperial law (a written-code
class), the unregistered were administratively invisible rather than hunted.
Absorbed peoples, the original culture among them, entered as registered
subjects at the ladders' lower rungs; families could climb across
generations through service, and some did. OPTION-LOCAL illustration: this
is one route, among others, by which ex-functionary descendants come to be
in the ridge-spur strands (WLD-CULTURE-001); the strand class is that
record's and needs no particular route. Metropole
registration was dense and audited; frontier registration was thin,
pragmatic, and gameable — a live internal difference, not a footnote.

Virtue set: order, measure, thoroughness, service, and exactness of record —
genuinely admirable in part, for the rolls fed relief in bad years,
standard measures protected the weak from arbitrary judgment, and the
ladders let low-born and absorbed families rise where birth-rank orders
would not. Sin set: disorder, falsified record, waste of works (letting
built things decay unrecorded), and private hoarding of what the rolls
should share. Neither list made the culture good or evil: exactness could
be mercy or machinery, and the same census that fed a district conscripted
it.

Faith orientation: a registry-cult class — an administrative state religion
whose divine order was imagined as ultimate record and audit, with local
cults tolerated under enrolment. The System's records and attestations were
culturally treated as administrative instruments: narrow authenticated
proofs and compelled or customary disclosures folded into service files.
This was interpretation and paperwork over unchanged natural behaviour —
fallible, incomplete, spoofable at the unauthenticated edges — never a
guaranteed readout, and never a System-executed office (UI1.2D, SOC1.2D).
All faith content is belief only.

Dress pattern: graded uniformity — cut and trim classes marking ladder
grade, insignia classes earned by service record, provincial dress
permitted under regulation, and a forbidden-marks class covering insignia
above one's grade and marks of unenrolled allegiance. Ecological-suppression
ideology: the wild was the unmeasured — land not surveyed, cleared, and
entered in the rolls was unfinished country, and suppression was the civic
work of bringing land into measure. The clearance and corridor works
WLD-LOCATION-004 records as remnants were, in this option's cultural frame,
that ideology's physical residue. This is value, not cause: it adds nothing
to WLD-HISTORY-001's multi-causal collapse.

Remnant expression at the outlet settlement, over however long has run on
shared claim 2's second clock since organised imperial presence ended
regionally — a span that record leaves unfixed: record-keeping habit,
literacy-respect, rank-through-service intuition, and engineering pride
persist strongly. Examination forms
hollowed into ceremony — recitations and apprenticeship formalities with no
examining authority behind them — and household rolls continue that no
ladder connects to anything. The audit-virtue inverted inward: thoroughness
once owed to a distant centre is now owed to the settlement itself, and
some families treat preserved partial service records as hereditary rank
claims that neighbours quietly dispute. The drift is contested: other
remnant families regard ladder-nostalgia as reverence for the machine that
abandoned them. No document survives complete or verified (WLD-LOCATION-004).

## Option B — PATRON-AND-STANDARD

A patronage-pyramid martial-colonial culture. Great houses held client
chains — households, garrisons, whole communities — and identity was
anchored in whose standard one served under; unit standards and house marks
were the culture's central identity objects. Advancement ran through patron
service as judged by the patron; full personhood in practice meant having a
patron or being one, and the masterless were exposed and suspect rather
than outlawed. Absorbed peoples entered as client-communities: original-
culture villages clienting to garrison houses, their lineages rising within
client chains, rarely into house standing. Metropole houses disdained
frontier houses, which intermarried, localized, and quietly absorbed local
custom — internal variation the option preserves.

Virtue set: loyalty kept downward as well as upward — a patron who
abandoned clients was disgraced — with courage, largesse, keeping the
standard, and protection of dependents; admirable in part, for the pyramid
genuinely fed, armed, and avenged its own. Sin set: standard-breaking,
meanness, abandonment of dependents, and masterlessness worn as pride.
Enforcement of the downward duties varied with distance and witnesses,
which is where the culture's cruelty lived without making it a cartoon.

Faith orientation: a standard-and-house cult class — house shrines and
standard-blessing observance harmonized under an imperial ruling-house cult
— belief only, orientation only. Dress pattern: house marks and client-mark
variants as the identity anchors; wearing a house's mark without service
was a serious offence class; the forbidden-marks class covered extinguished
and rebel houses' marks. Ecological-suppression ideology: the wild was the
enemy's country — suppression campaigns were the houses' warrant and glory,
how young members earned standing, and cleared land distributed to clients
was legitimacy made visible — the houses told themselves, and their
clients, that they ruled because they held back the wild. Value, not cause:
no legitimacy-erosion mechanism is established as a collapse contributor,
and WLD-HISTORY-001's wars, internal wars, and institutional greed and
corruption remain the only established pressures, with the trigger open.

Remnant expression at the outlet settlement: patron-client intuition
persists strongly — strong households gather dependents and owe them — as
does reverence for preserved or remembered standards as community relics
and a protective-duty ethos. House hierarchy hollowed: the great houses are
gone or unreachable, so "house" drifted to mean a leading local family
claiming continuity it cannot document. The warrant-to-clear inverted into
siege-honour: where ancestors pushed the wild back, holding the line is now
the glory. The drift is contested: some outlet families find standard-piety
an embarrassment kept alive by elders, and the masterless-suspicion sits
badly in a community where many arrived masterless.

## Option C — CHARTER-AND-GUILD

A chartered-corporate civilization. The empire governed as a lattice of
chartered bodies — guilds, colleges, companies, temple-corporations — each
holding delegated authority under written charter; membership was identity,
and law was charter interpretation argued between bodies. Full personhood
in practice was corporate: standing ran through one's body, and the
unincorporated had thin standing. Absorbed peoples were incorporated as
recognized native bodies under limited charters or joined imperial bodies
as junior members; ambitious original-culture families earned or bought
membership. The metropole held dense overlapping charters; the frontier ran
on one or two bodies holding sweeping local grants — a works company, a
garrison college. OPTION-LOCAL illustration: that company-country texture
resonates with the industrial village nucleus WLD-LOCATION-005 records, but
assigns the nucleus no imperial-era function, which stays open per
WLD-LOCATION-005.

Virtue set: fidelity to charter, craft excellence, mutual aid within the
body, honest accounting between bodies — admirable in part, for charters
restrained arbitrary power and gave the low-born collective standing no
lone petitioner had. Sin set: charter-breach, encroachment on another
body's chartered work, betrayal of craft secrets, and letting a chartered
work fail. The same fidelity that protected members froze out the
unincorporated, which is where this option's injustice lives.

Faith orientation: a charter-granting cult class — the divine imagined as
ultimate issuer of warrants, each body keeping licensed observance of its
patron-craft cult — belief only. Dress pattern: livery — body-membership
livery classes and craft-mark classes, grade shown in trim, with a
forbidden-marks class covering other bodies' livery and revoked charters'
marks. Ecological-suppression ideology: the wild was unchartered ground —
land became grantable, ownable, real only once a charter covered it, and
suppression was contracted out to clearance-companies as profitable duty.
Value, not cause; nothing is added to the collapse frame.

Remnant expression at the outlet settlement: membership-identity persists
strongly — belonging to a working body is who you are — with craft-mark
pride and the habit of writing agreements down and appealing to old grants.
Charter authority hollowed: the granting centre is gone, so documents —
partial, disputed, possibly forged — carry weight with no court able to
renew or adjudicate them. The delegation inverted: bodies that were
branches of a lattice now behave as sovereigns of their own function. The
drift is contested: younger outlet generations treat charter-piety as
elders' habit, and quiet operators simply work outside the old marks.

## Shared claim set (option-independent)

1. The imperial culture was literate, standardizing, and engineering-minded,
   consistent with the accepted remnant records; it administered the
   original culture's region for generations as a peripheral holding of a
   power centred elsewhere (WLD-HISTORY-001).
2. The outlet settlement (WLD-LOCATION-005) is the imperial culture's
   primary living local continuation — carried by imperial-descended people
   through the withdrawal and later gathered at the reused nucleus, which
   was abandoned before it was re-occupied (WLD-LOCATION-004 cl.3). Two
   clocks run separately: drift from the metropole may begin at centre
   failure, but post-authority remnant drift at the outlet begins only when
   organised imperial presence ended regionally, and neither that date nor
   the re-occupation date is fixed here — WLD-HISTORY-001 leaves the span
   unfixed. The result is improvised, internally varied, and contested;
   never frozen, uniform, or fanatical by default.
3. Internal variation existed historically, at minimum between metropole
   and frontier expressions, and persists in the remnant.
4. The imperial culture also changed across its own history: each
   recovery-and-fall cycle stripped further function layers and stranded
   frontier offices decayed in place at different rates
   (WLD-HISTORY-001 cl.2-3), so the form this region experienced in the
   arc's later phases was already stripped and improvised relative to its
   metropole ideal. The remnant inherited a late degraded form, not the
   full system. No cycle count, date, or stripped-function inventory is
   added.
5. All faith content, in every option, is belief and orientation-class
   only; no cosmology, rite content, or afterlife claim is confirmed.
6. All imperial and remnant use of System records is cultural practice —
   access, interpretation, presentation — over unchanged natural System
   behaviour; no guaranteed readout, no System-executed office, no
   institution owning any person's progression (UI1.2D, SOC1.2D).
7. No imperial work, record, or institution survives functioning as
   designed; remnant recognition is cultural recognition, not accurate
   knowledge of function (WLD-LOCATION-004).
8. Remnant beliefs about the empire, its fall, and its works are attitude
   and memory, not evidence of what actually occurred.

## Recommended option

Option A (census-and-ladder). It gives the sharpest structural contrast
with the ridge-spur compact — written rolls against sworn memory, ladder
against hearth-standing — which makes the standing rivalry and any later
remnant ambition arguable as administration rather than cartoon conquest.
It also best explains the functionary-descendant strand WLD-CULTURE-001
already establishes, and its records-as-instruments texture is the richest
belief-layer System material that needs no new mechanics.
