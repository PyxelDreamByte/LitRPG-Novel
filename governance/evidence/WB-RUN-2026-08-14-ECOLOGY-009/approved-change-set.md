## Selected foundation

The provisional record establishes only that the family-care migrant's
established caregiving migratory form:

1. has a low living body organised as a three-lobed, broadly tri-radial vault;
2. has a ring of short hydrostatic paddle-columns as part of its locomotor
   structure;
3. uses travelling waves along the body margin as a locomotor component, while
   the waves' exact mechanical relationship to the columns remains open;
4. has a mobile underside refuge; and
5. shelters mobile young beneath the vault, after which they spill outward to
   forage and regroup beneath it.

The selected option also retains a qualitative high-drag and route-feasibility
concern. It does not establish passable shallows, bottom-resting behaviour,
outlet stages, route mechanics, speed, endurance, passage success, or guaranteed
return.

`Brood-Vault Strider`, `living vault`, `tri-radial`, `hydrostatic
paddle-column`, and `mobile refuge` are author-facing functional or editorial
descriptions. They are not in-world names, taxonomic ranks, or Earth homologies.
