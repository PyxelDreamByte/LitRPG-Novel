## Selected physiology

The provisional record establishes only that the established migratory form:

1. uses dedicated ion-exchange organs for finite carrier-mediated regulation
   of body water and dissolved ions;
2. acclimates gradually and actively across freshwater and marine phases, with
   real time, energy, material, capacity, and failure limits;
3. keeps those organs distinct from its respiratory folds and passive-buoyancy
   chambers;
4. has multiple small compliant chambers containing metabolically produced
   low-density wax-like gel;
5. distributes those chambers longitudinally, with greater passive buoyant
   support behind the anterior mineralised shield;
6. gains passive buoyancy and fore–aft support without perfect neutral
   buoyancy, instant adjustment, salinity immunity, or depth immunity;
7. must coordinate whole-body water, ion, density, and trim effects; and
8. retains finite material, oxygen, energy, transport, waste, maintenance,
   recovery, infection, leakage, rupture, and declining-condition constraints.

Existing ordinary Mana support may reinforce living tissues only. It cannot
create gel mass, move ions without carriers and energy, provide instant
acclimation, or act as an activated buoyancy ability.
