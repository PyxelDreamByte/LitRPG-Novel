# Approved Change Set — WB-RUN-2026-08-15-LOCATION-001

## Author-approved selection

On 2026-08-15, the Author selected `A — Unequal-shoulder apron` and directly
added a combined water arrangement: a spring inside the fortified settlement,
wells, and cistern storage.

The binding selection establishes that:

1. the fortified core occupies the local crown of the ridge spur already
   established by `WLD-ECOLOGY-004`;
2. an unequal-shoulder managed apron surrounds that crown;
3. a deliberately maintained low-vegetation warning belt lies nearest the
   fortified perimeter;
4. small incomplete contour terraces occupy some usable steeper upper slopes;
5. broader fields and grazing ground occupy the gentler lower shoulder;
6. woodland resumes beyond the managed apron and comes closer on steep or
   otherwise difficult sides;
7. a constructed road descends in bends toward a lake beyond the immediate
   apron but within regular local travel and haulage reach; and
8. a spring emerges within the fortified perimeter, supplemented by wells and
   cistern storage.
