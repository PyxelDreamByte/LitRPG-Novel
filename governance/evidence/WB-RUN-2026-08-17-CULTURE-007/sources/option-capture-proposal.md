## Option A — HIGH-AND-HOME DIGLOSSIA

Two varieties, stacked. The imperial tongue persists as the high register:
record-keeping, formal adjudication and sworn-testimony occasions, outward and
inter-settlement dealings, and the ordinary daily speech of the outlet settlement.
The original tongue lives as home-and-field speech at the hillfort: households,
gardens, field and lakeside work, most everyday talk. The division is leaky
customary tendency — no rule, office, or institution polices it. Most hillfort
people command both unevenly, by strand, household, generation, and profession,
along WLD-CULTURE-001's strand leanings — functionary-descended households stronger
in the imperial tongue, locally rooted families in the original — with no strand
unanimity and no monolingual strand. The imperial tongue is no strand's property:
every strand class holds strong and weak commanders of both varieties, and command
tracks household, generation, and profession as much as strand, so the domain split
yields uneven advantage without giving any strand dominance.

Literacy, where it exists (uneven, per WLD-CULTURE-001), is overwhelmingly in the
imperial script tradition — a living skill descended from imperial practice, not
from any intact archive (WLD-LOCATION-004's ceiling stands). The original tongue is
customarily unwritten; when written at all it borrows the imperial script ad hoc,
with no settled orthographic custom — a pattern only, fixing no letterform or
spelling.

Prestige runs in two contested currents rather than one ladder: the imperial
tongue marks learning, record-standing, and outward competence; the original
tongue marks rootedness, intimacy, and — for many — the weight of sincerity, which
is why oaths feel truer in it. Option-local and illustrative — binding on no sibling
run: the oath-witness class customarily keeps its fallible records in the imperial
written tradition, while oaths — the founding oath as remembered included — are
customarily sworn aloud in the original tongue; this creates no oath text or
written charter (WLD-CULTURE-002: none exists as canon) and composes with every
compact reading and ambiguity class. The remembered swearing language implies
nothing about the founder's, the dynasty's, or any office-holder's strand origin,
all of which stay open. Which tongue a household leads with is read by neighbours
as strand texture — fallible social belief, never a System category.

## Option B — CONTACT VERNACULAR

Three varieties, generationally layered. In one settlement generation a mixed
daily speech has emerged at the hillfort, drawing on both parent tongues; it is
now the ordinary talk of walls, fields, and crews, and the walls-born generation
is strongest in it. The parent tongues recede toward the edges of life: the
original tongue toward elders, household memory-keeping, and some ritual speech;
the imperial tongue toward records, formal occasions, and dealings with the outlet
settlement — where it remains the ordinary daily speech. Neither parent tongue is
dead or dying as fact, and how far the vernacular will carry is an open, argued
question within the fiction.

Literacy stays anchored to the imperial script tradition, which holds the record
function; the vernacular and, in settlement practice, the original tongue are
unwritten. Prestige is generationally textured: parent-tongue command carries
memory-prestige and founding-generation standing; fluent vernacular marks
walls-born belonging; the imperial tongue keeps record-and-outward prestige.

The braid constraint holds: the vernacular is a common institution of speech and
dissolves no strand (WLD-CULTURE-001 cl.1 stands); household traditions persist,
and strand identity is still read — fallibly — in residual household speech habits
and non-speech markers. The outlet settlement's speakers tend to treat the
vernacular as informal hill-talk while dealing in it perfectly well when trade
wants doing — a prestige posture, not a comprehension barrier, one the rivalry
(WLD-EVENT-001) colours without any occurred incident.

## Option C — PARALLEL BILINGUALISM

Two full daily languages, side by side. Both parent tongues remain complete living
languages at the hillfort, keyed to strand and situation: households lead with
their strand leaning, mixed crews switch as the moment demands, and practiced
code-switching is ordinary adult competence. Recognised interpreter-capable
individuals and households exist, trusted to carry meaning across the tongues in
outward and cross-lake dealings and in mixed household life — personal competence
and reputation only, not an office or institution, and no part of adjudication:
no procedure, standing, entitlement, right to be heard, or right of assistance is
created, all of which WLD-CULTURE-002 owns and keeps open. It reintroduces no
cluster or quarter structure: the braid's mixed crewing stands.

Literacy is anchored to the imperial script tradition for the record function, as
everywhere. Distinctively here, a remembered script tradition of the original
tongue also exists as a scarce, uneven memory-keeping practice in some locally
rooted households — part of the same deliberate pre-imperial revival current as the
hill-fort pattern (WLD-HISTORY-001). It is a tradition class only: thin, contested,
possibly inaccurate to its own past, fixing no letterform and establishing no
archive, school, or record content.

Prestige is situational rather than stacked: each tongue carries weight in its own
domains, and neither is settlement-wide "high." What speech marks is range — both
tongues commanded well is widely read as a mark of settled belonging, a reading
households weigh differently; confinement to one reads as newcomer status or
deliberate household conservatism — all fallible social readings, fixing no status
scale and no coming-of-age recognition. The outlet settlement remains imperial-monolingual by default — ordinary
expectation, not established uniformity ("primarily, not exclusively" stands,
WLD-LOCATION-005) — so cross-lake dealings lean on the hillfort's bilinguals, a
quiet standing asymmetry the rivalry can colour.

## Recommended option

Option A (high-and-home diglossia). It is the natural sociolinguistic shadow of
the accepted history — a withdrawn power that owned the record function, a
surviving local tongue folded under it — and gives the three-culture frame its
cleanest expressive instrument without inventing a third variety, while leaving
the most open: a contact vernacular can still emerge later in-fiction.
