# Approved Change Set — WB-RUN-2026-08-15-LOCATION-003

## Author-approved selection

On 2026-08-15, the Author selected `B — Upper-shore staging + seasonal
spurs` for the lake-road shore endpoint.

The binding selection establishes that:

1. the established lake road ends at one recurring upper-shore staging place;
2. the staging place is on relatively firm ground set back from the ordinarily
   shifting wet margin;
3. several short seasonal spurs extend from that place toward nearby shore
   contacts;
4. seasonal and local conditions can change which contact and spur are usable;
   and
5. the staging place recurs while the final contact with the shore can change.

The staging place and each spur can flood, erode, ice over, attract wildlife,
or become unusable. Several spurs do not guarantee access at a particular
time.
