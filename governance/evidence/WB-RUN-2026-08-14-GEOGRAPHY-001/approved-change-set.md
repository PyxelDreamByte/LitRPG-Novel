## Guardrails applied

- Continuous central-lake freezing means surface ice, not a frozen water column.
- The lake remains freshwater; head of tide, freshwater tidal influence,
  salt-front position, storm-surge reach, and the precise relation between the
  outlet gap and coastal tidal marshes remain open.
- The lower basin floor remains distinct from downstream lower marsh and
  coastal country; the established crown corridor was not moved.
- Defensible terrain does not establish a fort, settlement, route, toll,
  controller, or institution.
- Broad coastal stability is regional-scale stability compatible with local
  storm reworking.
- The approved winter wording preserves strong upland spring melt, recurrent
  basin thaw and return, regular freezing of sheltered and slow waters, rare
  continuous central-lake surface ice, and sudden-thaw crossing hazards.
- Temperate rainforest establishes climate-supported structure, not Earth taxa.
- Fire opportunity is conditional and creates no annual fire or automatic recovery.

## Explicit exclusions

No exact map, measurement, named place, quantitative climate or hydrology,
species range, migration route, road, settlement, port, trade system, culture,
historical event, ancient-doorway fact, offworld connection, magical climate
mechanism, or System rule was promoted.
