## Author-approved world claims

1. Open ground, woodland, wet margins, and lake habitats provide distinct
   seasonal productivity pulses within the initial basin.
2. Organisms, flooding, runoff, decomposition, and migration transfer matter
   and nutrients among those habitat channels.
3. No habitat, producer guild, animal lineage, migration, or disturbance is the
   basin's sole ecological engine.
4. The initial basin is nested within a wider regional ecology rather than
   treated as a closed system.

## Binding development consequences

- Later nonlocal-life development distinguishes confirmed direct wild presence,
  seasonal or transient wild presence, imported remains or products, rare
  traveller-carried companion or captive live presence, and reported-only
  knowledge.
- Evidence in one class does not establish another. A direct sighting proves
  presence only at that time; a product does not prove local range or verified
  provenance; a migrant or captive does not establish residency or a
  self-sustaining population; and a tale does not establish existence or local
  presence.
- The basin receives full causal detail. Connected outer regions receive only
  the support needed for later approved migrations or transport. More distant
  life may remain source-and-confidence knowledge until greater detail is needed.

## Explicit exclusions

The transaction does not establish a particular migration, animal, pet,
traveller, product, trade route, market, institution, tale, outer region, or
offworld connection. It does not alter the exact triggers or wider endpoints of
`WLD-ECOLOGY-004`.

No System rule, spawning process, universal creature level, shared population
progression, automatic replenishment, or automatic power transfer from
biological products is created.
