# Approved Change Set — WB-RUN-2026-08-14-ECOLOGY-010

## Author-approved selection

The Author selected `A — MARGIN-RATCHET PROCESSION` on 2026-08-14 after all
three complete outlet-passage choreography cards were displayed.

The binding selection establishes that, where suitable support is available:

1. successive functional groups of the family-care migrant's established
   paddle-columns brace while a travelling body-margin wave drives a short
   advance;
2. the vault settles low between surges;
3. the qualitative visible rhythm is
   `settle → ripple → brace → surge → settle`;
4. the caregiving form normally attempts to regroup mobile young beneath its
   established refuge before advances;
5. during autumn descent, the same established structures brake and steer
   current-assisted slides; and
6. the behaviour requires, but does not establish, a compatible bed-or-margin
   contact and resting-opportunity chain.

`Margin-Ratchet Procession` is an editorial label, not an in-world name,
literal mechanism, formal category, convoy, or shared identity. The card's
warning that passage may be slow and conspicuous, and that abrasion,
paddle-column injury, fatigue, crowding, or broken resting opportunities may
stop it, is retained as a qualitative feasibility concern rather than a fixed
performance, event, or rate claim.

  depth, width, gradient, current, seasonal water level, tide, salinity front,

- Bracing establishes conditional load-bearing contact, not claws, adhesion,
  suction, anchoring, Earth-leg homology, walking, climbing, or overland travel.
- Settling low is a paused configuration, not bottom-resting anatomy, burial,
  suction, dormancy, sleep, or guaranteed bed contact.
- Successive groups establish no fixed count, order, repeated tripod, or new
  anatomy. The transaction uses only structures accepted by `WLD-ECOLOGY-009`.
- Exact actuation, force transfer, timing, amplitude, frequency, control,
  pressure, efficiency, energy budget, physiology, and failure thresholds stay
  open.
- Regrouping is attempted and fallible; it establishes no perfect response,
  attachment, retrieval, shared brood, multiple-carer structure, or guaranteed
  juvenile return.
- Respiration, osmoregulation, buoyancy, salinity tolerance, Mana, cognition,
  taxonomy, human knowledge, and protagonist knowledge remain open.
- Finite material and usable-energy inputs, transport, heat and waste handling,
  maintenance, injury, disease, fatigue, recovery, repair, reproduction,
  mortality, route capacity, recruitment, and carrying capacity remain causal.
- No activated ability, free Mana, Skill, class, pooled family identity, shared
  level or XP, reward, spawn, reset, refill, loot rule, or guaranteed recovery
  was created.
