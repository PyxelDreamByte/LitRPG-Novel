# The Sealed Step

Nera turned the bronze key in the east shutter. Five minutes remained before the storm. She inspected the seal, lifted her lamp, and opened the route she knew.

The east stair descended to a dry ledge above the flooded undercroft. Nera stayed on the marked steps, one hand against the salt wall. She could not swim, and the black water below gave her no reason to pretend otherwise.

The glass tablet rested beside the shutter's counterweight. When Nera raised it into the lamplight, a pale line moved beneath the fired surface. She tilted the tablet once, then again. The line followed the light. She did not know its history or meaning; direct examination told her only that it responded.

Water struck the lowest step. Procedure said to leave an unregistered object where it lay and report it. The coming surge would make that choice final. Nera slipped the tablet into her empty satchel and closed the shutter behind her.

She returned by the east stair with less than a minute remaining. Boots sounded in the public gallery above, quick and descending. Someone was already pursuing her.
