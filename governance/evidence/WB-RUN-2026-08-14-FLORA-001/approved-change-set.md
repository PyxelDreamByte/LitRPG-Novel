# Approved Change Set — WLD-FLORA-001

## Approved rule

Use a major but non-exclusive reserve-web vegetation guild in the initial basin:

1. The guild contains multiple original vegetation forms.
2. Members maintain living subsurface networks that store water and nutrients
   and support persistence through winter, surface fire, episodic flooding, and grazing.
3. Members produce tender spring growth, tall fibrous summer structures, and
   autumn fruiting or storage bodies.
4. Crown herds consume mature structural growth, while ribbon groups
   selectively harvest young growth and seasonal reproductive structures.
5. Browsing and trampling can create openings within stands and the wider mosaic.
6. Excessive or repeated pressure can exhaust reserves and kill networks.
7. Recovery is finite, biologically sourced, and rate-limited.
