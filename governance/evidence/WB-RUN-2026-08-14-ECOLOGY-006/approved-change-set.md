## Guardrails applied

- `Egg ribbon` describes egg arrangement only, not animal anatomy or a
  relationship to Lineage II ribbon forms.
- `Bird-like` describes an ecological resemblance only, not an Earth-derived
  body plan or taxonomy.
- Flooded vegetation is not identified as the reserve-web guild or any other
  named flora.
- Deposition onto sturdy plants and mineral surfaces establishes no adhesion,
  entanglement, draping, anchoring, or other retention mechanism.
- `Spring freshet` establishes favourable passage conditions and the seasonal
  productivity transition without selecting the exact route or mechanism.
- The selected predator-prey relationship is important but non-exclusive.
- Dispersed egg deposition reduces ordinary single-point failure but does not
  prevent exceptional or compound recruitment collapse.
- Healthy adults generally return seaward after breeding despite meaningful
  mortality across the cycle, and no single ordinary predator, flood, or
  fishery routinely destroys the entire generation.
- Human harvest activity is a mortality channel only; technology, access, ownership,
  law, trade, ritual, preservation, and sustainability remain open.
- Reproduction, migration, mortality, and recruitment are ordinary finite life
  cycles under CAL0, not System spawning, resetting, refilling, shared identity,
  pooled progression, or automatic power transfer.
