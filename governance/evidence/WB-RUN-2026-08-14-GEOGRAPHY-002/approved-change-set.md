# Approved Change Set — WB-RUN-2026-08-14-GEOGRAPHY-002

## Author-approved selection

The Author selected `C — MIXED OUTLET SEQUENCE` on 2026-08-14 after three
qualitative outlet-structure options were presented.

The binding selection establishes that:

1. a narrow bedrock-controlled throat opens downstream into one connected
   mixed outlet reach;
2. that reach contains boulder steps, side or lateral shelves, and holding
   pockets;
3. the reach is physically heterogeneous rather than one uniform channel or
   regular feature chain;
4. steps and shelves are candidate, state-dependent bed-or-margin contacts for
   later testing against the family-care migrant's accepted passage behaviour;
5. holding pockets are local recesses or pools that may be less exposed to the
   main flow under some conditions; and
6. `coherent migration corridor` means connected candidate route architecture,
   not demonstrated continuity, usability, capacity, safety, or success.
