# WLD-PRENATAL-001A — Ordinary prenatal-consciousness population-modelling guardrail

## Scope

This is a reusable provisional project-level default for population modelling
of ordinary members of a future named human-reference population. It binds a
work only when that work's manifest explicitly adopts this decision URI and
scope. It is not Enuma canon, a medical or metaphysical account of fetal
experience, or a rule for the reincarnate protagonist. An independent work may
override it only through its own Author decision; that override does not change
the default for other works.

## Author selection

Ordinary fetal sensation and automatic development are **neither denied nor
numerically quantified** by this decision.

Until later scoped evidence exists, population modelling assumes:

- no ordinary persistent self-aware continuity; and
- no ordinary directed prenatal System practice.

This excludes default intentional prenatal training and its prospective System
consequences from ordinary population cohorts. It does not exclude sensation,
automatic learning, maturation, or other non-directed development.

The protagonist's reincarnate continuity remains a separate exceptional
mechanism. His retained continuity and directed prenatal practice do not imply
that either is ordinary.

## Boundaries and consequences

- No prevalence has been assigned to fetal sensation, awareness, or rare non-protagonist exceptions.
- No culture, doctrine, institution, medical practice, or belief has been created.
- No named population or setting is bound beyond later use of this modelling prior within its declared scope.
- Setting-specific evidence may later refine or replace the guardrail through a new Author decision.
- No work inherits the guardrail silently; explicit manifest adoption is required.
- A work-specific override requires its own Author decision.

## Approval evidence

The Author explicitly selected **Prenatal A** and instructed that it be recorded
with this bounded project-level future-setting scope on 2026-08-12.

## Reopening conditions

Reconsider when a named population needs a prenatal-awareness distribution, a
defined causal mechanism supports persistent continuity or directed practice,
modelling exposes a contradiction, or the Author explicitly reopens it.
