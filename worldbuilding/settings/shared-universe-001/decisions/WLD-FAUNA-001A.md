# WLD-FAUNA-001A — Planetary Faunal Lineage Roster

## Question decided

How many major animal body-plan lineages anchor the planet's macroscopic
faunal diversity, and where do winged forms come from?

## Selected rule

- Seven major body-plan lineages organise the planet's macroscopic animal
  life, with familiar ecological roles distributed across different lineages
  rather than copied from Earth clades.
- Lineage I: six-limbed filament-and-scute animals; branches may repurpose
  limb pairs, including wing-pair branches producing four-legged winged forms.
- Lineage II: the tower-necked four-limbed landscape engineers already
  governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-001` through `WLD-ECOLOGY-004`.
- Lineage III: low-frame four-limbed generalists; branches may develop
  forelimb wings producing two-legged, two-winged forms.
- Lineage IV: fully aquatic flexible-trunk fin-fan animals, anchored by the
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` mass breeder.
- Lineage V: tri-radial hydrostatic animals, anchored by the
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` family-care migrant.
- Lineage VI: small-bodied segmented exoskeletal animals, including
  small-scale flyers.
- Lineage VII: unsegmented soft-bodied animals.
- Powered or gliding flight arose independently in more than one lineage; no
  single lineage owns the sky.
- Every lineage includes abundant ordinary forms, not only spectacular ones.

## Authority and boundaries

This is provisionally authoritative for Project Hearthway fauna at
silhouette-and-niche-theme level and for adopted downstream development. It
does not settle lineage names, in-world taxonomy, anatomy, reproduction,
physiology, Mana patterns, presence at any location, minor lineages below the
framework's grain, or the lineage of any sapient people.

## Author approval

After the A — Convergent skies, B — Sovereign wings, and C — Minimal
completion roster options were presented with A recommended, the Author
replied **"A"**, adding a follow-up question on flora coverage, on
2026-08-17. The flora question is routed to a separate floral-roster proposal.

## Implications

- Later creature design proceeds lineage-first from stable slots.
- Both provisionally accepted lake migrants gain lineage homes without any
  change to their existing records.
- The mythic four-legs-plus-wings silhouette and the familiar two-winged
  silhouette can both exist, in different lineages.
- Lineages VI and VII supply the ordinary small-organism backbone the
  ecological direction requires.
- No System mechanic, creature progression rule, or Earth transplant is created.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen if a needed body plan cannot fit any slot, if lineage-first design
proves too coarse or too restrictive for story needs, if a concrete
contradiction with an accepted record emerges, or on explicit Author request.
