# WLD-CULTURE-007A — Generational Language Landscape

## Question decided

Which qualitative sociolinguistic configuration governs the initial region —
which speech varieties exist, their social domains and generational
distribution, where literacy and the record function sit, and the outlet
settlement's speech position — without fixing any word, name, sound, grammar
feature, letterform, orthography, or sample utterance; without naming any
variety; without settling culture-frame content, rite language, inscription,
compact term, oath text, office, school, archive, or record content; without
the outlet's internal composition beyond its speech default; and without any
language fact for returning non-human peoples?

## Selected rule

Use an amended custom combination of the displayed options, as with the
WLD-HISTORY-001A combined collapse sequence. The landscape is generationally
split:

1. The older generation lives in the drafted Option C parallel bilingualism:
   both parent tongues stand side by side as full daily languages, keyed to
   strand and situation, with practiced code-switching as ordinary adult
   competence.
2. The younger generation, raised with both from childhood, is actively
   hybridising in an emerging Option-B-like development: using the two
   together and mixing words from both tongues within a single sentence.
3. That hybridising speech is an in-progress generational development, not a
   settled or named third language. How far it will carry, whether it
   stabilises, and whether the parent tongues recede are explicitly open and
   argued within the fiction.
4. Neither parent tongue is dying as fact, and no strand owns either tongue.
5. Literacy is anchored to the imperial script tradition for the record
   function — a living skill descended from imperial practice, not from any
   intact archive — with the original tongue customarily unwritten. Option
   C's remembered original-tongue script tradition is the amendment: it is
   not selected and is not established.
6. The outlet settlement is imperial-monolingual by default, so cross-lake
   dealings lean on hillfort speakers.
7. Recognised interpreter-capable individuals exist as personal competence
   and reputation only — never an office, never part of adjudication,
   creating no procedure, standing, entitlement, or right to be heard.

The proposal's option-independent shared claim set is promoted as displayed.

## Rationale

The generational split gives the settlement a sociolinguistic present that
matches its founding frame: adults who arrived or grew up with two intact
parent traditions still carry both, while the first cohort raised inside the
blend is doing what such a cohort does with two tongues at once. Keeping the
mixing explicitly in progress and unnamed preserves the argument as live
fiction rather than settling a language history, keeps both parent tongues
alive as usable instruments, and leaves the widest later drafting space —
stabilisation, recession, or nothing much — without a retcon. Anchoring
literacy solely to the imperial script tradition holds WLD-LOCATION-004's
ceiling and avoids inventing a second written tradition the story does not
yet need.

## Alternatives retained

- A — HIGH-AND-HOME DIGLOSSIA: two varieties stacked by domain, with the
  imperial tongue as the high register of record, adjudication, and outward
  dealings and the original tongue as home-and-field speech; not selected.
  Its literacy anchoring — imperial script tradition for the record function,
  original tongue customarily unwritten — matches the accepted amendment, and
  its oath/record texture remains option-local illustrative material binding
  nothing.
- B — CONTACT VERNACULAR: three varieties generationally layered, with a
  mixed daily speech already emerged as the ordinary talk of walls, fields,
  and crews and the parent tongues receding toward the edges of life; not
  selected as displayed. Its generational-mixing element contributes the
  younger-generation development of the accepted combination, but only as an
  in-progress development — the settled, established vernacular, its status
  as the ordinary talk of the settlement, and the parent tongues' recession
  are not selected.
- C — PARALLEL BILINGUALISM: two full daily languages side by side, keyed to
  strand and situation, with practiced code-switching as ordinary adult
  competence, interpreter-capable individuals as personal reputation, and
  situational rather than stacked prestige; not selected as displayed. Its
  parallel-bilingualism element contributes the older generation of the
  accepted combination; its remembered original-tongue script tradition is
  expressly amended out and is not established.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Card-only texture — prestige glosses, cross-option comparatives, and
intensifiers — remains nonbinding evidence unless the accepted claim set
promotes it deliberately.

## Binding consequences

- Two distinct speech varieties are the region's principal ones: the tongue
  of the withdrawn imperial power and the original tongue, author-facing
  labels only, principal rather than exhaustive.
- The hillfort's landscape is generationally split, with older-generation
  parallel bilingualism in both parent tongues keyed to strand and situation
  and practiced code-switching as ordinary adult competence.
- The younger generation, raised with both tongues from childhood, is
  actively hybridising, mixing words from both tongues within a single
  sentence.
- The hybridising speech is an in-progress generational development, not a
  settled or named third language, and its trajectory — carry, stabilisation,
  parent-tongue recession — is explicitly open and argued within the fiction.
- Neither parent tongue is dying as fact, and no strand owns either tongue.
- Literacy, where it exists, is anchored to the imperial script tradition,
  which holds the record function as a living skill descended from imperial
  practice, not from any intact archive; the original tongue is customarily
  unwritten.
- The outlet settlement is imperial-monolingual by default, so cross-lake
  dealings lean on hillfort speakers — a quiet standing asymmetry.
- Recognised interpreter-capable individuals exist as personal competence and
  reputation only, never an office, never part of adjudication, creating no
  procedure, standing, entitlement, or right to be heard.
- The shared claim set is also established: the imperial tongue owns the
  surviving written-record tradition as a living practice; comprehension
  between the settlements is ordinary with accommodation direction open; and
  uneven literacy is restated from WLD-CULTURE-001, not re-litigated.
- Declared qualitative closure of the region's sociolinguistic structure —
  which varieties exist and how they are used — and that only; names,
  vocabularies, phonologies, grammars, and letterforms remain protected
  unknowns.
- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-LOCATION-005's open language item: the
  outlet settlement's default everyday speech, and that only; internal
  composition, minority presence, and every other listed item stay open, and
  primarily still does not mean exclusively.
- Declared bounded closure of WLD-HISTORY-001's open language item: a tongue
  of the withdrawn power survives in the region as a living speech variety,
  and its regional position, and that only.
- No further closure: no original-tongue script tradition or revival current
  is established, and the hybridising speech is not established as a common
  institution of speech.
- WLD-CULTURE-001's braid stands untouched: no strand dissolves and no strand
  dominates, and speech-reading is fallible social belief, never a System
  category or status scale.
- WLD-CULTURE-002 keeps every compact term, office, and ambiguity class; any
  concrete pairing of charter, oath, testimony, or record language with a
  variety is option-local illustrative material binding nothing.
- The generational split is consistent with the settlement founding blend's
  early-stage hybridisation, which remains the sole authority for the
  founding frame; no founding-era fact and no founder, dynasty, or
  office-holder origin, strand, or speech is established here.
- Tongue, variety, hybridising speech, register, and script tradition are
  author-facing qualitative labels fixing no in-world name, word, sound,
  grammar feature, letterform, sample utterance, institution, office, law,
  measurement, proportion, or performance guarantee.
- The landscape is author-side sociolinguistic truth as a pattern, not
  automatic character knowledge; standing pressures at story start are not
  occurred events.
