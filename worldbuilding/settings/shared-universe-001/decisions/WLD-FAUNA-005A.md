# WLD-FAUNA-005A — Lineage IV Foundational Body Plan

## Question decided

What is Lineage IV's lineage-level body plan, and how much of the mass
breeder's anatomy is ancestral rather than its own specialisation?

## Selected rule

- Lineage IV is fully aquatic throughout; no branch is established as
  terrestrial or amphibious.
- Its members occupy the full aquatic size and niche range, from small forms
  to the largest animals in their waters.
- The lineage-wide inheritance is a locomotor and support core: a laterally
  flexible trunk carried on springy cartilage-analogue support, paired
  fin-fans as primary control surfaces, a muscular tail providing propulsion,
  and aquatic respiration through fold surfaces.
- The mass breeder's exact three-pair fin-fan count, wedge-shaped mineralised
  head shield, dedicated ion-exchange organs, and wax-like-gel chambers are
  derived and branch-specific, not lineage-wide.
- The mass breeder governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-008` is
  therefore one specialised migratory member, and those records continue to
  govern it unchanged.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for Lineage IV at lineage body-plan grain. It
does not settle species, names, taxonomy, sizes, branch inventory, fin-fan
counts outside the mass breeder's case, which branches carry shields, salt
organs, or gel chambers, reproduction across the lineage, distribution,
senses, feeding, Mana patterns, or presence anywhere. It alters no claim of
`WLD-ECOLOGY-007` or `WLD-ECOLOGY-008`.

## Author approval

After the A — Trunk-and-fan core, B — Full package ancestral, and C —
Minimal core cards were presented with A recommended, the Author selected
**"A — Trunk-and-fan core"** on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- The lineage has a recognisable body plan without conscripting future
  members into the mass breeder's specialised equipment.
- Shields, salt organs, and gel chambers become costed adaptations appearing
  where earned, rather than universal inheritance.
- A fully aquatic lineage is bounded by water: connected waters permit
  radiation, isolated waters strand populations, and no branch escapes
  overland, which makes established route geography ecologically
  load-bearing for this lineage specifically.
- No Earth homology, particular species, or System mechanic is created, and
  no accepted mass-breeder claim is altered.
