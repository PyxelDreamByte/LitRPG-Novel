# WLD-FAUNA-006A — Lineage V Foundational Body Plan

## Question decided

What is Lineage V's lineage-level body plan, and how much of the family-care
migrant's anatomy is ancestral rather than its own specialisation?

## Selected rule

- Lineage V bodies are organised on tri-radial symmetry rather than the
  bilateral symmetry of Lineages I, III, and IV.
- Support and movement are hydrostatic: pressurised internal fluid working
  against flexible tissue, with no rigid internal skeleton and no
  exoskeleton.
- Travelling waves passing along body margins are a basic lineage-wide
  movement mode.
- The lineage is predominantly aquatic; whether any branch is fully
  terrestrial remains open.
- The family-care migrant's low three-lobed vault silhouette, ring of short
  paddle-columns, underside refuge, and shelter–forage–regroup care system
  are derived and branch-specific rather than lineage-wide.
- The family-care migrant is therefore one specialised caregiving member, and
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` continue to govern it unchanged.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for Lineage V at lineage body-plan grain. It does
not settle species, names, taxonomy, sizes, branch inventory, whether any
branch is terrestrial, sessile, or colonial, reproduction and care across the
lineage, feeding, senses, respiration, internal organisation, distribution,
Mana patterns, or presence anywhere. It alters no claim of `WLD-ECOLOGY-009`
or `WLD-ECOLOGY-010`.

## Author approval

After the A — Radial-hydrostatic core, B — Full vault package ancestral, and
C — Symmetry only cards were presented with A recommended, the Author
selected **"A — Radial-hydrostatic core"** on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- The lineage stays mechanically defined — radial, hydrostatic, margin-wave
  driven — without conscripting members into vault anatomy.
- Ordinary Lineage V animals may be radial hydrostatic swimmers, crawlers,
  drifters, or sit-and-wait forms with no vault, paddle-columns, or parental
  care.
- The Brood-Vault Strider is repositioned as an elaborate caregiving
  specialist without any change to its own records.
- Hydrostatic support makes body-wall breaches mechanically serious and
  desiccation and freezing correspondingly dangerous.
- The treatment mirrors `WLD-FAUNA-005`, keeping the two migrant-anchored
  lineages structurally consistent.
- No Earth homology, particular species, or System mechanic is created.
