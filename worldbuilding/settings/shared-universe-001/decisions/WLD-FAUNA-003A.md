# WLD-FAUNA-003A — Lineage III Foundational Body Plan

## Question decided

What is Lineage III's bounded foundational body plan, and what life-history
signature explains its breadth as the planet's generalist radiation?

## Selected rule

- Lineage III descends from a four-limbed ancestor with a low-slung limb
  posture; upright and specialised postures are derived branch conditions.
- An internal supporting frame carries the body; the lineage has no
  exoskeleton and no scute armour comparable to Lineage I's.
- The outer covering is a flexible skin bearing branch-specific structures
  and does not combine Lineage I's filaments and scutes.
- Winged branches derive wings from the forelimbs, leaving two walking legs.
- The lineage is the planet's broadest generalist radiation, occupying prey,
  mesopredator, burrowing, climbing, and small-flyer roles.
- Its life-history signature is reproductive throughput: several to many
  young per breeding, fast juvenile growth, early dispersal, high early
  mortality, and quick generational turnover.
- Large-bodied branches may moderate that pattern; their handling remains
  open.
- Parental care occurs in some branches but is not the lineage's defining
  investment.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for Lineage III at foundational body-plan grain.
It does not settle species, names, taxonomy, sizes, branch inventory, whether
reproduction is egg-laying or live-bearing, skin structures, wing structure
or performance, diet, senses, cognition, sociality, domestication potential,
Mana patterns, or presence anywhere.

## Author approval

After the A — Litter-and-disperse, B — Two-form metamorphosis, and C —
Teaching generalists life-history cards were presented with A recommended,
the Author selected **"A — Litter-and-disperse"** on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- The lineage's breadth now has a causal mechanism rather than being asserted.
- Lineage III becomes the food web's renewable ordinary base, giving other
  lineages' predators something to eat.
- Local abundance tracks seasonal productivity closely: visible surges in
  good years, quick crashes and quick recoveries in bad ones.
- Three distinct reproductive identities now separate the developed
  lineages: guarded brood eggs (I), few slow live young (II), and
  high-throughput litters (III).
- No Earth homology, domesticate, System mechanic, or creature progression
  rule is created.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen if high-throughput reproduction cannot support required large-bodied
or long-lived branches, if a concrete contradiction with an accepted record
emerges, or on explicit Author request.
