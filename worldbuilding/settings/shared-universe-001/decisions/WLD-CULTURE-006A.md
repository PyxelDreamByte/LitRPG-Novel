# WLD-CULTURE-006A — Lifecycle Threshold-Witness Rites

## Question decided

Which cross-cutting rite architecture governs what the four lifecycle rite
classes — birth, coming of age, marriage and relationships, death —
fundamentally do in this region's three cultures at pattern level, without
choosing rite scripts, liturgy, deity names, calendar dates, music or
costume specifics, funerary-goods inventories, any in-world name or naming
language, any marriage-form norm, any afterlife fact or Soul destination,
anything owned by the culture-frame, language, or coinage sibling runs, or
any new System mechanic, readout guarantee, or CAL0 change?

## Selected rule

Use A — THRESHOLD-WITNESS, plus the proposal's shared claim set. Every
lifecycle rite is a witnessed crossing: the community formally witnesses a
passage and thereby socially ratifies the new state. Birth rites witness
acceptance into a household — the crossing is into the hearth, not into
existence. Coming-of-age rites witness the culturally interpreted signs of
ordinary awakening: they reveal, celebrate, authenticate, teach, and
support, and never create, suppress, gate, delay, or own natural access.
Marriage rites witness a bond of reciprocal obligation between persons and
households. Death rites witness the community's recognition of a death and
the reallocation of the dead person's social obligations among the living.

No rite creates, transfers, or extinguishes assembly standing, property, or
tenure, and every reallocation is social obligation only. Rite failure or
omission carries social consequence only. Whether a culture stresses who
witnesses or what is recorded is illustration only and is not part of the
accepted claim; per-culture rite expression beyond pattern level remains
open.

## Author approval

accepting Option A exactly as drafted together with the proposal's shared

## Rationale

The selected architecture deepens what canon already runs on: the compact's
grammar of oath, witness, and sworn testimony extends naturally to household
scale, and witnessing is the rite element that adapts most readily to
whatever societal structure each culture-frame run gives its culture. It
also keeps belief-space maximally open — passage cosmologies and
ancestor-name practices can still appear later as household or strand
beliefs — whereas the alternatives each spend more of the open belief and
naming space at architecture level.

## Alternatives retained

- A — THRESHOLD-WITNESS: every rite is a witnessed crossing that socially
  ratifies a new state — birth into a household, coming of age at the
  culturally interpreted signs of ordinary awakening, marriage as witnessed
  reciprocal obligation, death as witnessed recognition and reallocation of
  social obligations; selected.
- B — PASSAGE-AND-RETURN: every rite frames life as a passage from and
  toward an unseen continuity, licensing reincarnation-adjacent folk belief
  strictly as contested belief confirmed by nothing; not selected.
- C — NAME-AND-RECORD: every rite turns on names and standing as social
  practice only — provisional name and standing at birth, full name at
  recognised awakening, standings merged or linked by marriage, the name
  invoked by the living after death; not selected.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Options B and C remain nonbinding evidence.

## Binding consequences

- In all three cultures — original, imperial-remnant, and hybrid settlement
  — every lifecycle rite is a witnessed crossing that socially ratifies the
  new state; each culture times and staffs its rites by its own custom, none
  fixed.
- All three cultures possess all four rite classes, each internally varied
  in keeping them.
- Birth rites witness acceptance into a household: the crossing is into the
  hearth, not into existence.
- Coming-of-age rites witness the culturally interpreted signs of ordinary
  awakening; ceremony wraps natural awakening and never modifies it, and
  awakening timing follows natural law identically for everyone regardless
  of ceremony, culture, or omission of ceremony.
- Marriage rites witness a bond of reciprocal obligation between persons and
  households.
- Death rites witness the community's recognition of a death and the
  reallocation of the dead person's social obligations among the living, and
  establish nothing about what actually happens to Souls.
- Rites are socially binding only: none triggers, gates, delays, guarantees,
  or owns any System event, and none creates, transfers, or extinguishes
  assembly standing, property, or tenure — every reallocation, marriage's
  inherited-duty obligation class included, is social obligation only.
- Rite failure or omission carries social consequence only.
- Birth customs aimed at the unborn are belief with no mechanical effect,
  and no ordinary person has prenatal System access, practice, or
  continuity; the series contract's WLD-PRENATAL-001A guardrail is respected
  as a read-only constraint and adopted into no setting record here.
- Appraisal-adjacent customs, marriage-negotiation disclosure included,
  route only through consent, voluntary disclosure, and narrow authenticated
  proof — custom, never guarantee — with uneven and often unavailable
  access.
- Marriage and relationship forms remain an open qualitative field in every
  culture: household-formation logics and obligation classes only, with
  plural versus monogamous variety explicitly varied and open.
- Whatever rite practice the hybrid settlement keeps, it echoes no office
  and claims no governance power.
- Declared qualitative closure of the awakening, coming-of-age, and
  guardianship-custom space WLD-CULTURE-001 left open: the four rite classes
  exist in all three cultures under the witnessed-crossing architecture; all
  observance content remains open.
- Whether a culture stresses who witnesses or what is recorded is
  illustration only and is not established; WLD-CULTURE-003, WLD-CULTURE-004,
  and WLD-CULTURE-005 each remain free to give their own culture a different
  rite logic, and WLD-CULTURE-005 alone owns the settlement's founding
  frame.
- WLD-CULTURE-007 and WLD-CULTURE-008 stay untouched: attestation fixes no
  script, literacy, speech variety, naming language, or record medium, and
  rite obligations fix and exclude no exchange medium, valuation, or
  monetary custom.
- Witness, crossing, acceptance, attestation, record, and standing are
  author-facing qualitative descriptions fixing no in-world name, language,
  rite script, liturgy, calendar, office, law, institution, measurement,
  proportion, or performance guarantee.

## Story and character impact

- Later scenes may use witnessed births, comings of age, marriages, and
  funerals as social ratification and as sources of contested standing, but
  every particular rite, officiant, occasion, dispute, belief, and outcome
  requires its own evidence.
- Contested standing from disputed or omitted rites, witness-availability
  politics, and record disputes are standing pressures at story start, never
  occurred events.
- A coming-of-age scene can carry full social weight while changing nothing
  about natural awakening, which arrives on its own schedule for the
  witnessed and the unwitnessed alike.
- Funeral scenes may carry a culture's or household's belief about the dead
  without the narrative confirming or denying any of it.
- Characters know another culture's, strand's, or household's rite customs
  only through an available, fallible source.
