# WLD-CULTURE-003A — Original Kin-Hall and Season-Court Culture

## Question decided

Which qualitative organizing principle governs the region's original culture
— the locally rooted pre-imperial tradition that survived the imperial period
folded and now contributes, non-exclusively, to the settlement's braid —
across its societal structure, virtues and shames, faith orientation, dress
pattern, System folk tradition, and survival mode, without choosing any
in-world name, deity, speech content, or script; any rite, linguistic,
exchange-media, imperial-culture, or hybrid-culture content; any strand
exclusivity or protagonist fact; or any CAL0 change, new mechanic, or
confirmed metaphysics?

## Selected rule

Use A — KIN-HALL AND SEASON-COURT, plus the proposal's shared claim set. The
extended kin-hall — linked households of one recognised descent web — is the
social unit, and a person without a hall is a person in danger. Halls
federate through season-courts: recurring assemblies at fixed customary
places, keyed to the seasonal rhythms, where law is spoken, disputes are
heard, marriages and fosterings are arranged, and goods are exchanged. Law is
customary precedent recited by a memory-trained speaker class — authoritative
in standing, fallible in fact, openly arguable at court. Leadership classes
are hall-elders, court-ground hosting lineages, and the speaker class; status
runs from full hall-members through dependents and fostered outsiders to the
hall-less, whose protection is a court obligation and a live moral test. The
fortified height is this culture's communal work — refuge in danger, highest
court ground in peace.

Its virtues are hall-hospitality, true recall, season-duty, and refuge-duty —
the height denied to none who flee; its shames are hall-betrayal, false
recall, refusing refuge, and waste of common stores, and the virtues
genuinely grind against one another, hospitality against stores-prudence and
true recall against hall loyalty. Its faith orientation reveres, as contested
belief only, ancestors as the source of precedent, land-and-season powers
propitiated at year-turns and courts, and the System read as the impartial
order that never misremembers — folk interpretation, never confirmed
metaphysics, with no System channel bearing on testimony, recollection, or
court evidence. Its dress is layered material classes with woven
edge-patterning marking hall and descent web and worked hard-material
fasteners as the status axis. Under the empire the season-courts were the
part displaced and partly used, the halls folded inward and survived, and the
heights lapsed mainly through disuse.

## Rationale

The selected principle gives the revived hill-fort pattern its most natural
depth — height as refuge and highest court ground — and supplies
assembly-habit and spoken-precedent memory that harmonises with the
charter-oath compact as resonance without claiming descent. Its speaker class
and court-and-witness folk tradition also create the richest
fallible-knowledge texture while leaving every sibling option open.

## Alternatives retained

- A — KIN-HALL AND SEASON-COURT: extended kin-halls federated through
  recurring season-courts, customary precedent recited by a memory-trained
  speaker class, virtues of hall-hospitality, true recall, season-duty and
  refuge-duty, ancestor and land-and-season faith with the System read as the
  impartial never-misremembering order, edge-pattern and fastener marker
  classes, and season-court displacement with halls folded inward under the
  empire; selected.
- B — OATH-WEB: sworn personal bonds outranking blood, composition law before
  oath-arbiters, warden lineages holding the heights, oath-token and
  token-quality marker classes, and the wardens broken under the empire; not
  selected.
- C — HEARTH-AND-BOUND-LAND: the household hearth as legal, ritual, and moral
  unit under hearth-bond obligation chains, elder-arbiter law, federation
  works on the heights, hearth-mark and mantle marker classes, and tenure
  overwritten under the empire; not selected.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Options B and C remain nonbinding evidence, as do Option A's option-local
illustrative imperial instruments, which this decision does not establish.

## Binding consequences

- The extended kin-hall — linked households of one recognised descent web —
  is the original culture's social unit, and a person without a hall is a
  person in danger.
- Halls federate through season-courts: recurring assemblies at fixed
  customary places keyed to the seasonal rhythms, for law, dispute, marriage
  and fostering arrangement, and exchange; no place, calendar, procedure, or
  exchange medium is established.
- Law is customary precedent recited by a memory-trained speaker class —
  authoritative in standing, fallible in fact, openly arguable; leadership
  classes are hall-elders, court-ground hosting lineages, and the speaker
  class; status runs from full hall-members through dependents and fostered
  outsiders to the hall-less, whose protection is a court obligation.
- Virtues are hall-hospitality, true recall, season-duty, and refuge-duty —
  the height denied to none who flee; shames are hall-betrayal, false recall,
  refusing refuge, and waste of common stores; and the grind between
  hospitality and stores-prudence and between true recall and hall loyalty is
  genuine and internally argued. Their cost is real and unevenly borne —
  hospitality and refuge-duty are paid out of common stores by people who did
  not choose the guest, and hall standing leaves dependents and the hall-less
  genuinely exposed — so neither list renders the culture simply good or bad.
- The fortified height is this culture's communal work — refuge in danger,
  highest court ground in peace. A pre-imperial fortified-height tradition is
  originated by this record as this culture's own background and as a
  tradition class only; whether any later settlement's fortification revives
  it is available resonance for
  canon://world/shared-universe-001/culture/settlement-founding-fusion-blend
  and is not claimed here.
- Declared origination, narrowly bounded: WLD-HISTORY-001 carries no
  fortified-height claim and gains none from this one; heights are a
  tradition class, not identified places, sites, works, or any settlement's
  fortification; and no date, phase, count, construction, siting, or
  infrastructure origin follows.
- Faith orientation, as contested belief only: ancestors as the source of
  precedent, land-and-season powers propitiated at year-turns and courts, and
  the System read as the impartial order that never misremembers. No System
  channel bears on testimony or recollection, attests truth, verifies
  precedent, or is available as court evidence; the reading is folk
  interpretation, often wrong, and sceptics within the culture say so.
- Dress is layered material classes with a woven edge-pattern
  belonging-marker class marking hall and descent web and a worked
  hard-material fastener status-marker class; no organism, fibre, technique,
  or garment form is established.
- System folk tradition follows WLD-CULTURE-001's practice layers — everyday
  court-and-witness use, hall-hoarded advantage, thin speaker-class
  specialist study, abundant often-wrong folk belief — all cultural layers
  over unchanged natural System behaviour.
- Under the empire, at function level only: season-court judgement was
  displaced, continuing only so far as a recognition extended from outside the
  culture covered it, and convenient gatherings were turned to imperial use;
  some hall lines kept a recognised public standing on the imperial frame's
  own terms, sought and paid for by the more ambitious lines and judged
  variously by their neighbours, while others folded inward and survived as
  household memory-keeping, family weave, and quiet refuge custom; and the
  heights lapsed mainly through disuse under imperial presence and its
  organised ecological suppression, with any active discouragement remembered
  differently and left open.
- The concrete imperial instrument is not established here; it belongs to
  canon://world/shared-universe-001/culture/imperial-charter-and-guild-culture
  alone, and this decision asserts no imperial registration, enumeration,
  count, roll, ladder, office, grant, body, or term — including the form in
  which any recognition or public standing was held, sought, or paid for.
- The shared claim set is also established: the culture predates the empire
  and survived folded, not erased; it is internally varied and never uniform;
  its speech variety and its lifecycle rites exist with no content fixed
  here; it contributes to the braid most visibly within the locally-rooted
  strand class but equals no strand exclusively; and all faith content is
  belief, interpretation, and presentation only.
- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-CULTURE-001's open faith-content space,
  for this culture only: a faith orientation exists at the level of which
  classes of thing are revered and how the System is read; all observance
  content, shrine and practitioner classes, prayer, offering, doctrine, and
  their accuracy remain open, and no faith claim is confirmed metaphysics.
- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-CULTURE-001's open clothing space, for
  this culture only: layered functional material classes carrying one
  belonging-marker class and one status-marker class; every organism, fibre,
  technique, garment form, colour, decoration, and manufacture, and all
  settlement dress, remain open.
- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-CULTURE-001's open festival-observance
  space, for this culture only: this culture's own assemblies are keyed to
  seasonal rhythms and carry year-turn and court propitiation; calendar,
  dates, season names, cadence, all observance content, and the settlement's
  festivals in every respect remain open.
- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-CULTURE-001's open store-accounting and
