# WLD-FLORA-005A — Tower-Flora Foundational Growth Architecture

## Question decided

What foundational growth architecture defines the K2 tower-flora kingdom
established by `WLD-FLORA-004`?

## Selected rule

- Tower flora are individual, non-networked organisms; root contact between
  towers is ordinary competition or incidental, not kingdom-defining resource
  sharing.
- A tower grows from a single persistent living core column.
- Each growth season the core lays down an external structural casing layer
  that then dies; a mature tower is a living column sheathed in successive
  dead, self-supporting casings.
- Because living tissue lies deep, surface damage, scorching, and
  girdling-equivalent injuries are survivable in principle; damage or decay
  reaching the living core is the characteristic mortal wound.
- Towers are long-lived and slow to replace; losing mature towers is a
  lasting landscape change.
- Seasonal growth is strongly pulsed, and reproduction concentrates in
  episodic heavy seeding years rather than uniform annual output.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for the K2 kingdom at qualitative strategy grain.
It does not settle species, dimensions, lifespan, chemistry, casing or core
anatomy, energy capture, leaf-analogues, roots, branch structure, seeding
mechanisms or periods, kingdom assignments of the rainforest or basin
woodland, human uses, Mana patterns, or presence anywhere.

## Author approval

After the A — Core-and-casing, B — Stack towers, and C — Braid towers
architecture cards were presented with A recommended, the Author selected
**"A — Core-and-casing"** on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- Woodland and rainforest-candidate development gains an alien-but-legible
  foundation inverted from Earth trees.
- Fire, wound, disease, and harvest stories gain grounded hooks: survivable
  scorching, mortal core breaches, and possible kill-free casing removal, none
  yet established as practice.
- Masting-analogue years give later food-web records a pulse mechanism to
  develop.
- No Earth-tree homology, human practice, or System mechanic is created.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen if the core-and-casing architecture cannot support required woodland
ecology or material culture, if a concrete contradiction with an accepted
record emerges, or on explicit Author request.
