# WLD-LOCATION-005A — Outlet Industrial-Remnant Settlement

## Question decided

What second settlement, if any, should exist in the initial region, and with
what bounded qualitative siting, capability, cultural composition, and
story-start relationship to the initial ridge-spur settlement?

## Selected rule

Promote the Author's direct specification as bounded qualitative claims. A
second settlement stands in the initial region, built around a reused imperial
industrial village nucleus — an instance of WLD-LOCATION-004's reused
imperial works node class. Running water gives the site a qualitative
water-supply and water-power advantage, and qualitatively workable metal
resources occur in the nearby hills. The settlement has grown on the combined
strength of its defensible location, its water, and its industrial capability.
It lies on the other side of the basin lake from the initial ridge-spur
settlement, toward the seaward end of the outlet, near the outlet's mouth
toward the ocean. It is culturally a primarily imperial-remnant community, in
contrast to the ridge-spur settlement's blend of original and imperial
cultural strands. Standing tensions and a trade rivalry exist between the two
settlements at story start; WLD-EVENT-001 governs that standing pressure.

Every relation is qualitative. Grown means only qualitatively larger or more
established than a minor hamlet, and near the outlet's mouth is a qualitative
position, not a mapped location.

## Author approval

During the 2026-08-17 multiple-choice acceptance session the Author directly
specified this settlement rather than choosing from displayed options: a
second settlement built around an imperial industrial village with running
water and metals in the hills, grown on its defensible location, water, and
industrial capability, on the other side of the basin lake from the
ridge-spur settlement near the mouth of the outlet toward the ocean,
primarily an imperial-remnant community in contrast to the ridge-spur
settlement's blended original-imperial culture, with standing tensions and
trade rivalry between the two settlements at story start.

The directly specified second settlement gives the initial region a bounded
counterpart community with an industrial, imperial-remnant character, a
contrasting cultural composition, and a governed story-start rivalry, without
selecting names, maps, measurements, works detail, resources detail,
population, institutions, shore works, events, or any future conflict.
