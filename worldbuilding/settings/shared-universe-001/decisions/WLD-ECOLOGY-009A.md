# WLD-ECOLOGY-009A — Basin-Lake Family-Care Migrant Foundational Body Plan

## Question decided

What foundational body plan and directly visible caregiving interface should
govern `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`'s family-care migrant?

## Author decision metadata

- **Status:** `PROVISIONAL`
- **Accepted by:** Author
- **Accepted on:** 2026-08-14
- **Approval evidence:** after all three complete visual option cards were
  displayed and chat confirmation was required, the Author replied `A`,
  selecting `A — Brood-Vault Strider`

## Selected operational rule

Use `A — BROOD-VAULT STRIDER`.

The family-care migrant's established caregiving migratory form is a low,
three-lobed or broadly tri-radial living vault. A ring of short hydrostatic
paddle-columns forms part of its locomotor structure. Travelling waves move
along the body margin and contribute to locomotion, without yet fixing their
mechanical relationship to the columns.

The underside forms a mobile refuge. Mobile young shelter beneath it, spill
outward to forage, and regroup beneath the refuge.

The selected option retains a qualitative high-drag and route-feasibility
concern. This does not establish outlet passability, a shallow route,
bottom-resting behaviour, an outlet stage, or any successful route solution.

`Brood-Vault Strider`, `living vault`, `tri-radial`, `hydrostatic
paddle-column`, and `mobile refuge` are author-facing functional or editorial
descriptions, not in-world names, formal taxonomy, or Earth homologies.

## Alternatives considered

1. `A — BROOD-VAULT STRIDER` — low three-lobed living vault with a ring
   of short hydrostatic paddle-columns, travelling margin waves, and a mobile
   underside refuge; **selected**.
2. `B — HELIX-TOW ESCORT` — stiff spindle with three helical wave-bands,
   a patrolled foraging court, and temporary breakaway tow-filaments; not
   selected.
3. `C — FOLD-CAGE ROSETTE` — central barrel with seven muscular vanes that
   propel, fold for migration, and flare as a perforated refuge; not selected.

## Rationale and evidence

The selected package makes family care physically visible, creates a strong
alien contrast with the mass breeder, and supports mobile young that forage
outward and regroup without requiring attachment or permanent carriage.
Its high-drag concern remains an explicit feasibility gate instead of being
hidden by a cost-free movement or Mana solution.

  caregiving migratory form only; juvenile morphology remains open.
- Three-lobed or tri-radial qualifies the vault shape only and establishes no
