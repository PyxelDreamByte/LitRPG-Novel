# WLD-ECOLOGY-003A — Lineage II Two-Stage Symbiont Inheritance

## Question decided

How do young Lineage II animals acquire and maintain the symbiont community
required by their distributed fermentation lattice?

## Selected rule

- Before birth, each developing young receives a small compatible founder culture.
- The founder culture is a viable beginning, not the complete mature community.
- After birth, repeated contact and shared feeding with parents and relatives
  build the diverse adult community.
- This allows local dietary adaptation and makes compatible kin or foster
  groups biologically important.
- Orphans can survive if fostered by compatible adults, while prolonged
  isolation is dangerous.
- Social contact can help restore disrupted symbionts, but the same contact and
  seasonal routes can spread digestive disease.

## Authority and boundaries

This is provisionally authoritative for Project Hearthway Lineage II fauna
development. It does not settle reproductive containment, exact transfer anatomy, post-birth inoculation
behaviour, microbial taxa, maturation timing, compatibility limits, specific
diseases or treatments, species-level differences, or complete social organisation.

## Author approval

The Author reviewed the recommended two-stage model and the strict-vertical and
environmental alternatives, then replied **“yes”** on 2026-08-14.

## Implications

- Family groups can carry locally adapted fermentation communities.
- Compatible fostering can preserve orphan survival.
- Isolation creates a genuine developmental and medical risk.
- Group contact provides both recovery capacity and a disease-transmission route.
- No new System rule, progression source, or specialised Mana ability is created.
