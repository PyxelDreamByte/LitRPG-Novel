# WLD-FLORA-003A — Basin Reserve-Web Fragment Viability

## Question decided

Under what general condition can a severed reserve-web section survive and
become an independent colony patch?

## Selected rule

Severed fragments survive only when they retain enough viable modules,
reserves, and functioning connections to sustain themselves. Viable fragments
can slowly stabilize as independent patches; inadequate fragments decline and
die. Fragmentation therefore does not make every break an effortless
propagation event.

## Author approval

The Author selected this model by replying **“yes”** on 2026-08-14.

## Rationale

This rule preserves local resilience while making survival conditional, slow,
materially constrained, and capable of failure rather than automatic propagation.

## Binding consequences

- Severance creates a candidate fragment, not a guaranteed new patch.
- Independent survival requires enough viable modules, reserves, and functioning connections.
- A viable fragment can slowly stabilize as an independent patch.
- An inadequate fragment cannot persist independently and declines and dies.
- Fragment-founded patches remain ordinary causal ecology rather than spawn,
  refill, duplicated identity, or personal progression.

- Severed patches can contain both slowly stabilizing and failing fragments.
- Fragmentation leaves persistent consequences rather than instant death,
  multiplication, or reset.
- No character knowledge, terminology, propagation practice, or cultural interpretation is established.
