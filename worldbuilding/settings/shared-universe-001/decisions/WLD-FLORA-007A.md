# WLD-FLORA-007A — Free-Seeder-Flora Foundational Growth Architecture

## Question decided

What foundational strategy defines the K3 free-seeder kingdom established by
`WLD-FLORA-004`?

## Selected rule

- Free-seeders are individual, non-networked, short-lived, fast-growing
  plants specialising in openings: disturbed ground, fire scars, flood
  scours, trample gaps, and abandoned worked land.
- They are poor long-term competitors against established stands and persist
  by exploiting change rather than holding ground.
- The kingdom's signature is the seed vault: a durable dormant capsule that
  can wait in soil for years or decades until disturbance cues wake it.
- The population's persistent form is the buried vault bank; growing plants
  are cheap, fast, and brief.
- Disturbed ground erupts primarily from banks already present, and old
  worked or cleared land can carry buried seed legacies.
- Dispersal exists but is secondary at kingdom grain and remains open.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for the K3 kingdom at qualitative strategy grain.
It does not settle species, forms, chemistry, vault anatomy, dormancy limits,
cue specifics, viability decay, bank densities, dispersal mechanisms,
crop-ancestor candidates, human uses, Mana patterns, or presence anywhere.

## Author approval

After the A — Vault-seed, B — Wanderer, and C — Tempo strategy cards were
presented with A recommended, the Author selected **"A — Vault-seed"** on
2026-08-17.

## Implications

- Rewilding scenes gain a concrete mechanism: suppressed land waking from
  buried banks under seventy-year-old clearances.
- Farming gains grounded weed pressure: fields fight their own buried banks.
- Each producer kingdom now has a distinct persistence strategy: K1 living
  networks, K2 standing structure, K4 anchors, K3 dormant vaults.
- No germination guarantee, crop, human practice, or System mechanic is
  created.
