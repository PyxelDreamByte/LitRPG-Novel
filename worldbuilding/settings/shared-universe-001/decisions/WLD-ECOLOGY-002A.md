# WLD-ECOLOGY-002A — Lineage II Distributed Fermentation Lattice

## Question decided

What digestive architecture makes the selected crown and ribbon
vegetation-architect diets plausible?

## Selected rule

- Lineage II possesses several connected digestive chambers that route food by
  toughness and nutrient density.
- Symbiotic microorganisms perform much of the chemical breakdown.
- Crown forms have larger, slower-processing chambers for bulk structural vegetation.
- Ribbon forms have smaller, faster-processing chambers for selective seasonal foods.
- Basal Mana circulation reinforces membranes, circulation, and thermal
  stability; it does not create calories or replace food.
- Abrupt diet changes can destabilise the symbionts and cause illness.

## Authority and boundaries

This is provisionally authoritative for Project Hearthway fauna development.
It does not settle precise chamber anatomy, symbiont species or transmission,
metabolic values, gestational development, disease, treatment, or specialised
Mana organs.

## Author approval

The Author reviewed the recommended model and alternatives, then replied
**“yes”** on 2026-08-13.

## Implications

- Food remains the source of matter and chemical energy.
- Crown and ribbon bodies inherit a common digestive architecture with
  different capacity and throughput.
- Diet disruption can change health, movement, reproduction, and migration.
- Later work must explain how young acquire a compatible symbiont community.
- No new System rule or progression source is created.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen for an infeasible energy budget, incompatible body plan, annual flora
shortfall, concrete medical or story contradiction, or explicit Author request.
