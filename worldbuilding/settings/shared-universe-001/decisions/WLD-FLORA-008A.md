# WLD-FLORA-008A — Thread-Decomposer Foundational Body Architecture

## Question decided

What foundational body-and-feeding architecture defines the K6
thread-decomposer kingdom established by `WLD-FLORA-004`?

## Selected rule

Selected as an Author custom combination of displayed options A and B
(weaver-and-bloom):

- Thread decomposers are macroscopic, non-photosynthesising heterotrophs
  feeding through thread-form bodies; the Lineage II digestive symbionts
  remain separate microorganisms.
- They are the planet's primary macroscopic decomposer-recycler kingdom.
- Their ordinary state is diffuse feeding threads living hidden in soil,
  litter, and dead material.
- On locating concentrated dead material, threads consolidate it into
  wrapped, felted masses that decompose from within, leaving spent husks.
- Reproduction erupts episodically in fruiting structures keyed to wet and
  thaw windows.
- Where thread decomposers are absent or fail, dead material can persist
  abnormally long.
- Mutualist partnerships with producer kingdoms remain a bounded branch
  possibility, not the kingdom default.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for the K6 kingdom at qualitative strategy grain.
It does not settle species, thread or mass anatomy, chemistry, digestion
pacing, wrapping thresholds, fruiting forms, toxicity, edibility, felts or
husks as usable materials, partnerships, human uses, Mana patterns, or
presence anywhere.

## Author approval

After the A — Weaver, B — Bloom, and C — Partner-first cards were presented
with A recommended, the Author proposed combining A and B ("A+B?"); the
combined weaver-and-bloom model was presented and the Author confirmed
**"Yes — record A+B"** on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- Decomposition is invisible in its ordinary state, visible at working
  wrapped masses, and conspicuous in seasonal fruiting pulses.
- Wrapped masses, felt sheets, husk shells, and fruiting eruptions give K6
  observable signatures; missing weavers leave tell-tale preserved deadfall.
- Spent felts and husks stand as candidate materials for later culture
  records without any established use.
- No Earth fungus homology, human practice, or System mechanic is created.
