# WLD-HISTORY-001A — Imperial Collapse Arc and Neighbor Frame

## Question decided

What qualitative phase structure, contributing-pressure frame, collapse-era
ecological and pest-like-race development, chronology anchoring, and bounded
neighbor frame should govern the previous regional power's roughly
seventy-year cascading retreat without settling its trigger, its name or map,
any named neighbor, the parked pack species' development, or any plot event?

## Selected rule

Use a custom combination of the displayed options in sequence, as with the
WLD-LOCATION-001A combined water arrangement:

1. Centre-failure first: central institutions failed first, stranding
   frontier garrisons and offices that decayed in place at different rates.
2. Oscillating partial recoveries: central power was temporarily restored
   under capable leadership more than once before falling again, each cycle
   stripping further function layers.
3. Final frontier-first drawdown: the last phase was a staged withdrawal of
   remaining frontier holdings, this region among them, ending organised
   imperial presence in the region.

Contributing pressures across the empire during the arc were wars with other
races, internal wars, and greed and corruption within imperial institutions —
multi-causal, none singly sufficient, exact weighting open. The collapse's
trigger remains explicitly open.

During the arc, dangerous wildlife gradually increased as organised
ecological suppression lapsed, and pest-like races spread and caused chaos.
The pest-like races are the parked sapient pack species (violent, aggressive,
territorial, pack-like, low average intelligence, with somewhat more
intelligent, more aggressive pack leaders); "goblin" is author-facing
shorthand only. Only the species' collapse-era spread becomes bounded
history; everything else about it remains parked.

The proposal's shared option-independent neighbor frame is promoted as
displayed: five presence classes reusing WLD-ECOLOGY-005's provenance
discipline, non-transfer rules, and no named polities. The settlement
founding (25-35 years before story start) falls after the final drawdown
phase had begun regionally, and the wildlife return spans the arc per the
combined sequence.

## Rationale

The combined sequence keeps the collapse cascading and multi-phase as the
foundation capture requires while giving the region both a stranded-decay
early history and a decisive organised ending: centre failure strands
frontier functions early, the diminishing recoveries strip layers without
restoring the whole, and the final frontier-first drawdown closes organised
imperial presence before the settlement founding window. The named pressures
keep the history multi-causal without settling a root cause, the bounded
pest-like-race spread gives the parked species a historical footprint without
developing it, and the shared neighbor frame channels all future neighbor
work through provenance-disciplined presence classes.

## Alternatives retained

- A — Frontier-first staged withdrawal, late central snap: early deliberate
  frontier drawdown with a late central administrative snap; not selected as
  displayed. Its frontier-first staged-withdrawal element contributes the
  final phase of the accepted combined sequence; its late central snap is not
  selected.
- B — Centre-failure first, stranded decay in place: central institutions
  fail first, stranding frontier garrisons and offices that decay in place at
  different rates; not selected as displayed. Its centre-failure-first
  element contributes the first phase of the accepted combined sequence.
- C — Oscillating partial recoveries before final lapse: diminishing cycles
  of contraction, partial reassertion, and re-abandonment, each stripping a
  layer; not selected as displayed. Its oscillation element contributes the
  middle phase of the accepted combined sequence.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Card-only texture — failure-order inventories, fragment-class descriptions,
cross-option comparatives, and intensifiers — remains nonbinding evidence
unless the accepted claim set promotes it deliberately.

## Binding consequences

- The previous regional power's retreat was a cascading arc spanning roughly
  seventy years before story start, in the combined sequence: centre failure
  first, then oscillating partial recoveries, then a final frontier-first
  drawdown.
- Central institutions failed first, stranding frontier garrisons and offices
  that decayed in place at different rates; no particular garrison, office,
  location, or fate is established.
- Central power was temporarily restored under capable leadership more than
  once before falling again; each cycle stripped further function layers; no
  cycle count, date, duration, function inventory, or leader identity is
  established.
- The final phase was a staged withdrawal of remaining frontier holdings,
  this region among them, ending organised imperial presence in the region;
  no withdrawal order, route, evacuation event, last garrison, or exact
  regional end date is established, and fragment survivals remain open.
- Contributing pressures were wars with other races, internal wars, and greed
  and corruption within imperial institutions — multi-causal, none singly
  sufficient, weighting open; no particular war, opponent, campaign, battle,
  faction, or corruption episode is established.
- The collapse's trigger or root cause remains explicitly open; cascading
  retreat is the shape of the event, not its explanation.
- During the arc, dangerous wildlife gradually increased as organised
  ecological suppression lapsed; the return spans the arc; no species,
  population, encounter, or corridor detail is established, and
  WLD-ECOLOGY-004 remains the story-start migration authority.
- During the arc, pest-like races spread and caused chaos; the spread is
  bounded qualitative history with no origin, range, population, raid,
  victim, territory, or present-day distribution established.
- The pest-like races are the parked sapient pack species; "goblin" is
  author-facing shorthand only, never in-world taxonomy; the species gains no
  biology, culture, language, ecology, relations, or ability development, and
  its ethical-storytelling questions remain parked.
- The former power was centred elsewhere with this region among its
  peripheral holdings; its name, capital, extent, rulers, and map remain
  open, and doorway relations stay under the constitution's protected
  unknowns.
- Chronology anchors: arc begins roughly seventy years before story start;
  settlement founding falls 25-35 years before story start and after the
  final drawdown phase had begun regionally; the first succession approaches
  at story start.
- The five-class neighbor frame binds with its non-transfer rules; no named
  neighbor polity, map position, army, alliance, border, current war,
  particular neighbor, good, resident outsider, or tale is instantiated.
- The collapse is institutional and demographic, not environmental; no System
  event caused, accompanied, or ended it; basin beliefs about the collapse
  and neighbors may be wrong without touching natural law; author-side
  history is not automatic character knowledge.
- No protagonist, character, plot event, or future-timeline claim is
  established; no project default is adopted; no CAL0 entry changes.

## Story and character impact

- Later history, culture, location, and neighbor development can attach to
  any phase of the combined arc, but every particular war, cycle, garrison,
  fragment, neighbor, good, tale, and event requires its own evidence.
- Founding-era memory gains a hostile-country texture from the wildlife
  increase and pest-like-race spread without any instantiated encounter,
  raid, or loss.
- Every future neighbor, returning people, trade good, resident outsider, and
  rumour enters through a bounded presence class with observer-relative,
  incomplete knowledge.
- Characters may hold wrong, partial, or rival accounts of the collapse and
  of neighbors; no character automatically knows the author-side arc.

System cause, mechanic, spawning, reset, safe zone, or System-granted
