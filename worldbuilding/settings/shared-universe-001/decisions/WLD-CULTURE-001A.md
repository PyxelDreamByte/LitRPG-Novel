# WLD-CULTURE-001A — Settlement Strand-Braid Cultural Fabric

## Question decided

Which qualitative integration pattern governs the mixed refuge community's
cultural fabric — how origin strands persist, braid, fade, or cluster, and
how System understanding and use varies across them — without choosing
names, languages, faith content, the protagonist's origin culture,
demographic percentages, specific rituals, governance or compact terms, any
new System mechanic or CAL0 change, or any settlement-zone placement,
harvest technique, or shore institution left open by the LOCATION and
ECOLOGY records?

## Selected rule

Use A — STRAND-BRAID, plus the proposal's shared claim set. Several
persistent origin strands keep distinct household-level traditions while
daily work, defence, and seasonal festivals braid them together under
negotiated common institutions. No strand dissolves and no strand
dominates. The strand classes — locally rooted pre-collapse families,
displaced arrivals from failed districts, ex-imperial functionary or
garrison descendants, and itinerant-craft or trade lineages — are
open-ended qualitative classes, and households may straddle them.

Views of the imperial inheritance run as a lived spectrum —
salvage-pragmatism, nostalgia, resentment, taboo — across and within
strands. System practice varies by class of use — everyday pragmatic use,
hoarded craft advantage, specialist study, folk belief and
misinterpretation — cross-cutting the strands. Every such claim is
cultural: belief, access, interpretation, or presentation. Natural System
behaviour is unchanged, no readout is guaranteed, and no strand's account
is privileged.

## Author approval

On 2026-08-17 the Author selected, by explicit multiple choice among the
displayed options:

> A — STRAND-BRAID

accepting Option A exactly as drafted together with the proposal's shared
claim set.

## Rationale

The selected pattern preserves the capture's mixed-refuge character as a
continuing lived fact rather than a fading origin story, gives the
approaching first succession its richest legitimate constituency texture
without pre-writing the compact, and keeps maximum protagonist-origin
flexibility because every strand stays distinct and every strand is
braided into common life.

## Alternatives retained

- A — STRAND-BRAID: persistent origin strands with distinct household
  traditions, braided by negotiated common institutions, deliberately mixed
  crews, and common festivals; attitude and System-practice spectra
  distributed across and within strands; selected.
- B — CRUCIBLE: one emergent settlement identity dominating public life,
  origin traditions persisting privately and generationally fading, with
  the founding-generation gap a live tension; not selected.
- C — QUARTER-MOSAIC: origin communities clustered spatially with
  recognised brokers mediating between them, thin common institutions, and
  visibly sorted attitudes and lores; not selected.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Options B and C remain nonbinding evidence.

## Binding consequences

- Several persistent origin strands coexist, keeping distinct
  household-level traditions while sharing negotiated common institutions
  for work, defence, and festivals; no strand dissolves and no strand
  dominates.
- The strand classes include, non-exhaustively: locally rooted pre-collapse
  families; displaced arrivals from failed districts; ex-imperial
  functionary or garrison descendants; and itinerant-craft or trade
  lineages. Households may straddle classes.
- Routine cross-strand crewing exists for lake-road haulage within
  WLD-LOCATION-002's fair-weather bands and for corridor-season watches
  and closures under WLD-ECOLOGY-004; in surge seasons mixed crews are the
  negotiated compact-era custom, not an arithmetic necessity.
- Settlement festivals keyed to the seasonal rhythms are common
  institutions with distinguishable strand contributions; no ritual,
  calendar date, or observance content is established.
- Views of the imperial inheritance form a lived spectrum —
  salvage-pragmatism, nostalgia, resentment, taboo — with strand-level
  leanings but no single settlement attitude and no strand unanimity.
- System practice varies by class of use — everyday pragmatic use, hoarded
  craft advantage, specialist study, folk belief and misinterpretation —
  cross-cutting the strands; all such practice is cultural and none
  changes, reveals, or guarantees any natural System behaviour or readout.
- The shared claim set is also established: the mixed refuge community of
  1,200–2,000 under the founder's dynasty and council compact with first
  succession approaching (restated, not re-litigated); author-facing
  non-exhaustive strand classes; no exclusive or complete System truth
  anywhere; uneven literacy and historical memory; and every strand class
  open as a possible protagonist origin.
- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-ECOLOGY-004's open practice space:
  corridor-season watch duty exists as a routine practice; exact practices
  remain open, and no field or road is identified as a secondary-path
  overlap.
- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-LOCATION-002's open
  customary-practice space: a routine cross-strand haulage crewing custom
  exists; its exact form remains open, and the accepted travel bands are
  not divided, extended, shortened, or recalculated.
- WLD-CULTURE-002 owns the compact's structure, offices, decision classes,
  representation, and succession provisions; this decision establishes only
  that watch duty exists and is crewed across strands.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-006's mortality-channel-only boundary stands untouched; the
  spring pulse appears only as prose-level labour pressure, and harvest
  institutions of every kind remain open.
- Strand, braid, class, crew, watch, festival, lore, and every other label
  are author-facing qualitative descriptions fixing no in-world name,
  language, classification, ritual content, institution, office, law,
  measurement, proportion, or performance guarantee.
- The fabric is author-side cultural truth as a pattern, not automatic
  character knowledge; standing pressures at story start are not occurred
  events.
