# WLD-LOCATION-002A — Initial Ridge-Spur Lake-Road Practical Travel Scale

## Question decided

What broad practical travel scale should govern the already-established road
between the initial ridge-spur settlement and the lake without fixing exact
distance, route engineering, transport, seasonal performance, or guaranteed
journey time?

## Selected rule

Use B — Half-day working route. Under the displayed fair-weather comparison,
an ordinary unladen downhill journey has a broad 1–1½-hour reference band and
a routine laden uphill return has a broad 2–3-hour reference band. A useful
shore visit followed by a loaded return can consume most of a working day, but
the lake remains part of regular settlement life.

The bands are author-facing planning anchors, not exact, measured, minimum,
maximum, scheduled, universal, or guaranteed journey times.

## Author approval

On 2026-08-15 the Author replied:

> I choose B — Half-day working route for the practical lake-road travel scale.

## Rationale

The selected scale best preserves the Author's “fairly far but not too far”
direction. It makes same-day lake activity credible while allowing burden,
remaining daylight, shore work, delay, and the transition through alien
environments to matter. It does not require any particular vehicle, pack
animal, road work, shore facility, institution, or encounter.

## Binding consequences

- The established road uses the author-facing practical scale “half-day
  working route.”
- An ordinary fair-weather unladen downhill comparison uses a broad
  1–1½-hour reference band.
- A routine fair-weather laden uphill comparison uses a broad 2–3-hour
  reference band.
- The direction and load qualifiers are inseparable from their respective
  bands; unladen ascent and laden descent remain unselected.
- A useful shore visit and loaded return can consume most of a working day.
- Actual journeys may fall outside the bands because of traveller, load,
  terrain, road, weather, season, daylight, obstruction, injury, or animal
  conditions.
- No surveyed distance, guaranteed time, standard traveller or load, road
  design, vehicle, pack animal, traffic pattern, shore infrastructure,
  institution, encounter, event, or System mechanic is selected.
