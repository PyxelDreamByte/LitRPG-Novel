# WLD-FLORA-002A — Basin Reserve-Web Modular Colonies

## Question decided

How are the reserve-web guild's living subsurface networks spatially organized?

## Selected rule

Use bounded modular colony patches. Each patch contains numerous rooted
modules connected through a spatially limited living network. The
connections permit finite local sharing of resources. Damage
can sever links, isolate portions, and kill modules or sections. Distinct
patches do not form one basin-wide organism, and local resource sharing does not
constitute shared consciousness.

## Author approval

The Author selected this model by replying **“yes”** on 2026-08-14.

## Rationale

This topology preserves the reserve-web's local resilience without creating a
superorganism. It makes resource transfer, damage, fragmentation, and recovery
spatially bounded and causally legible.

- `WLD-FLORA-001`

## Binding consequences

- Reserve-web networks are organized as bounded modular colony patches.
- Numerous modules may share resources locally through living connections.
- Severance can isolate or kill parts of a patch.
- There is no continuous basin-wide organism or shared consciousness.
- No shared personal level, statistics, reward, spawn, or refill mechanism is established.
