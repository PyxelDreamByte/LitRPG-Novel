# WLD-FLORA-009A — Crust-and-Cushion-Flora Foundational Survival Strategy

## Question decided

What foundational survival strategy defines the K5 crust-and-cushion kingdom
established by `WLD-FLORA-004`?

## Selected rule

- Crust-and-cushion flora are low, slow, surface-hugging pioneers of bare and
  hostile surfaces: rock, stone, dead casings, walls, and scoured or
  disturbed ground.
- Their two broad growth habits are thin surface crusts and dense dome
  cushions.
- The kingdom's signature is metabolic shutdown and rapid revival: members
  tolerate near-total desiccation and deep freezing in a dormant state that
  can look entirely dead, then revive quickly on wetting or thaw.
- Growth is extremely slow and confined to active windows; established cover
  is old cover.
- Undisturbed crust and cushion cover is therefore a qualitative mark of
  elapsed undisturbed time, without any calibrated dating method.
- Their growth and decay initiate soil formation, opening succession for the
  other kingdoms.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for the K5 kingdom at qualitative strategy grain.
It does not settle species, forms, colours, chemistry, energy capture,
reproduction, shutdown limits, revival speeds, growth or colonisation rates,
soil-formation pacing, any dating method or in-world knowledge, human uses,
Mana patterns, or presence anywhere.

## Author approval

After the A — Revival flora, B — Microclimate cushions, and C — Stone-veneer
crusts cards were presented with A recommended, the Author selected
**"A — Revival flora"** on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- Ruins, uplands, and stone gain readable texture: dead-grey drought states
  and rain revivals.
- Seventy-year ruins can wear seventy years of crust, giving abandonment an
  honest visual weight without a measurable clock.
- Soil initiation links K5 to every later succession and recovery story.
- Cushion microclimates and mineral binding remain available as later branch
  details rather than kingdom-wide commitments.
- No dating method, human practice, or System mechanic is created.

ecology, if a concrete contradiction with an accepted record emerges, or on
