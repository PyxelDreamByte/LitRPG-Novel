# WLD-FAUNA-004A — Lineage VI Foundational Body Plan

## Question decided

What is Lineage VI's bounded foundational body plan, how does its exoskeleton
grow, and which growth mode is ancestral?

## Selected rule

Selected as an Author-directed structural revision (all three displayed
growth modes retained as branches) plus selection of option A for ancestry:

- Lineage VI has a segmented body enclosed in a jointed external skeleton,
  with segment counts and regional specialisation varying by branch.
- The lineage is the planet's most species-rich macroscopic animal radiation,
  and most members are small-bodied.
- Some branches fly; flight is small-scale and independent of the flight
  evolved in Lineages I and III.
- The lineage supplies pollinator-analogue, litter and soil, parasitic,
  scavenging, predatory, and swarm-prey roles.
- Skeletal growth mode is a branch-level axis with three modes:
  - moult branches shed the whole skeleton periodically, pass through a soft
    vulnerable phase, may undergo life-stage transformation, and remain small;
  - seam-growth branches never shed, expanding continuously at flexible
    inter-plate seams, with no soft phase and no life-stage transformation;
  - rolling-replacement branches shed and regrow plates individually, never
    becoming wholly soft.
- Whole-body moulting is the ancestral condition; rolling replacement and
  seam growth are derived escapes from its soft phase and size ceiling.
- Freed from the moult constraint, seam-growth branches include the lineage's
  large-bodied members, which exceed the sizes Earth arthropods reach.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for Lineage VI at foundational body-plan grain.
It does not settle species, names, taxonomy, segment or limb counts, sizes,
upper size limits, branch inventory, skeleton composition, seam or moult
mechanics, the respiratory and structural support that permits large body
sizes, reproduction, development, sociality, Mana patterns, or presence
anywhere.

## Author approval

After the growth-mode cards were presented, the Author directed that all
three modes be retained as branches within the lineage and that some members
exceed Earth arthropod sizes; the revised three-branch model was presented
with an ancestry question, and the Author selected **"A — Moult ancestral"**
on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- Growth mode predicts the kind of animal, giving one lineage three
  internally coherent branch families instead of a single rule.
- Small moulting forms remain the abundant everyday default of the biosphere.
- Large-bodied Lineage VI animals are an earned innovation of seam-growth
  branches, supplying a biologically grounded route to forms Earth never
  produced.
- Each mode carries distinct costs: periodic defencelessness, seam weakness
  and feeding dependence, or permanently fresh weak plates.
- Body sizes beyond Earth arthropod scale still require respiratory,
  circulatory, and structural support that remains open and binding.
- No Earth homology, particular large creature, or System mechanic is
  created.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen if the three-branch structure proves unworkable, if large-bodied
branches cannot be supported physiologically, if a concrete contradiction
with an accepted record emerges, or on explicit Author request.
