# WLD-CULTURE-008A — Dual-Sphere Exchange Media

## Question decided

Which qualitative pattern governs how value moves in and between the
ridge-spur settlement and the outlet settlement — the money-object classes
in use, the local credit customs beside them, and how exchange settles
under the standing rivalry — without choosing any coin name, denomination,
legend, or specific metal; any price, exchange rate, quantity, or unit; any
market, assay, mint, toll, tariff, treaty, or trade-good list; any
harvest-trade practice or law; any tax, tribute, or compact-finance
institution; or any script, literacy claim, or word for money?

## Selected rule

Use Option C — DUAL-SPHERE, plus the proposal's shared claim set. Two
customary spheres of worth ride one above the other. The external or
prestige sphere runs on surviving imperial coin: inter-settlement trade,
storage of worth, prestige transfers, and — where a consented communal
burden is discharged in movable worth at all — the customary medium of that
discharge, with no levy, rate, treasury, or compact-finance institution
established. The internal or customary sphere runs on obligation: witnessed
debts, labour-exchange, tally customs, and in-kind return inside each
settlement.

Converting between spheres is socially marked — noticed, storied, sometimes
resented — with no rule, prohibition, or rate. The cross-lake rivalry's
contested-exchange-terms pressure lives in the coin sphere as argument
classes over acceptance, wear, and standing: tendencies, never occurred
incidents. Each of the three cultures holds a distinct live relationship to
the same thinning coin stock — original households keeping to stores,
stock, and obligation and using coin as a boundary tool for strangers;
imperial-remnant tradition treating coin as a bearer of record and
standing; the hybrid braid negotiating two-sphere fluency — each a tendency
with internal variation and no unanimity. Coin worth is social belief and
custom; the System recognises no currency and attests no value.

accepting Option C exactly as drafted together with the proposal's shared

## Rationale

The selected pattern lets the trade rivalry and settlement politics both
speak through money without inventing a single institution, gives each of
the three cultures a distinct, live relationship to the same coin stock
rather than a shared attitude, and keeps minting and validation open as a
future story lever for the outlet settlement's metal advantage.

## Alternatives retained

- Option A — DEAD-STANDARD COINS: old imperial coin in qualitative metal
  classes as the region's one trusted movable medium, trusted on metal and
  familiarity and on no surviving authority, with tally-and-trust credit
  carrying daily exchange inside each settlement; not selected.
- Option B — WEIGHED-METAL AND TALLY: coin faith broken with the empire,
  weighed metal and workable metal stock as the money-object classes, old
  coins persisting as bullion or keepsakes, notched-tally credit carrying
  daily life, and the outlet settlement the natural supplier and validator
  of weighed metal; not selected.
- Option C — DUAL-SPHERE: an external prestige sphere of surviving imperial
  coin above an internal customary sphere of obligation, labour-exchange,
  tallies, and in-kind return, with socially marked conversion and the
  rivalry living in the coin sphere; selected.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Options A and B remain nonbinding evidence.

## Binding consequences

- Two customary spheres of worth exist: an external or prestige sphere of
  surviving imperial coin (inter-settlement trade, storage of worth,
  prestige transfers, and the customary medium where a consented communal
  burden is discharged in movable worth at all) and an internal or
  customary sphere of obligation, witnessed debts, labour-exchange, tally
  customs, and in-kind return.
- Imperial coin circulates in qualitative worth classes sourced from
  pre-drawdown holdings, salvage finds, and cross-lake inflow; no authority
  mints or validates coin; wear, clipping, and hoarding attrition are live
  qualitative concerns and the trusted stock is thinning.
- No functioning imperial mint or treasury authority survives anywhere in
  the region, and no community currently mints coin.
- Internal-sphere credit customs are qualitative only: witnessed debts,
  obligation held in memory and in tally media, labour-exchange, and
  in-kind return, with no ledger institution, unit, record form, office, or
  enforcement.
- Conversion between spheres is socially marked — noticed, storied,
  sometimes resented — with no rule, prohibition, or rate, and the marking
  itself varies by household and situation.
- WLD-EVENT-001's contested-exchange-terms pressure expresses itself in the
  external sphere as argument classes over coin acceptance, wear, and
  standing; tendencies only, and no occurred exchange, negotiation,
  embargo, or incident is established.
- Coin-facing tendencies: original-culture households keep to the internal
  sphere and use coin as a boundary tool for strangers; imperial-remnant
  tradition treats coin as a bearer of record and standing; the hybrid
  braid negotiates two-sphere fluency, with straddling households often
  brokering conversions. Attitudes toward money objects and exchange media
  only, each with internal variation and no unanimity.
- The outlet settlement tends to treat the coin sphere as native ground;
  the hillfort tends to treat it as an instrument for dealing outward.
- Salvage coin from remnant ground is dispersed single or small finds
  within WLD-LOCATION-004's salvage activity class — finite, diminishing,
  contestable, with no right, ownership, law, market, or price; that
  record's no-cache default stands and no hoard exists by implication.
- Whether the outlet settlement ever tests, re-melts, or strikes metal or
  coin is an explicit open question; no mint, assay, or validation
  institution exists.
- Coin worth is social belief and custom; the System recognises no
  currency, attests no value, and no readout, Skill, appraisal, or office
  guarantees a coin's or metal's worth.
- Declared bounded closure of part of WLD-LOCATION-005's open
  economy-and-institution space and its open-other-communities boundary: no
  community in the region currently mints coin or holds treasury authority;
  everything else those openings hold stays open.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-006's harvest-institution boundary and WLD-CULTURE-002's open
  compact-finance space stay untouched.
- The Author-directed founding frame is established by
  canon://world/shared-universe-001/culture/settlement-founding-fusion-blend
  alone; this decision only keeps its coin-facing tendencies consistent
  with that frame.
