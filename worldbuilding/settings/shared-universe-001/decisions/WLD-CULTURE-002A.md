# WLD-CULTURE-002A — Founder Charter-Oath Compact Structure

## Question decided

What qualitative balance mechanism should govern the settlement's standing
founder-council compact — which office classes each side holds, which
decision classes need which side or both, where refugee and newcomer
representation enters, how craft, harvest, defence, and System-study
expertise gets a voice, and which ambiguity classes the compact is known to
contain — without choosing names, law-code text, succession outcomes or
candidates, faction rosters, ownership regimes, or fishing and harvest law,
and with first-succession rules staying incompletely specified by design?

## Selected rule

Use C — Charter-oath assembly, exactly as drafted, together with the
proposal's option-independent shared claim set. The compact rests on a
binding founding oath the founder swore in the founding era: the oath
defines the dynasty's duties (defence, outward dealings, upholding the
compact) and its limits (no burden without consent, no seizure of
households' subsistence, no altering the compact alone). The council is
chosen by an assembly of the settlement's households and holds two powers:
it adjudicates disputes over whether the oath has been kept, and its consent
is required for communal burdens. The dynasty's authority is real and broad
within the oath's bounds; the council's is real because it judges those
bounds and holds consent.

Expertise enters as sworn craft, harvest, defence, and System-study
testimony in adjudication and burden deliberation; System-study testimony
interprets accessible signs, never a guaranteed readout. Whether the oath
binds heirs automatically, only upon re-swearing, or lapses with the founder
is unresolved, and the oath's terms are partly disputed recollection. That
first-succession ambiguity is a designed open pressure cited by
WLD-EVENT-001 and is never resolved here.

On 2026-08-17 the Author selected "C — Charter-oath assembly" in the

accepting Option C exactly as drafted together with the proposal's shared
claim set, with PROVISIONAL authority.

## Rationale

The selected structure gives both sides genuinely non-ceremonial authority
— oath-bounded dynastic breadth against assembly-rooted adjudication and
consent — while locating every designed ambiguity exactly where the
approaching first succession will bear: on an oath sworn by a living founder
who has never needed replacing, whose binding on heirs, receiving body, and
disputed terms create contestable process without naming candidates or
outcomes.

## Alternatives retained

- A — Dual-key compact: standing authority divided into a dynastic key and
  a council key with a defined dual-key class; displayed, not selected.
- B — Presided stewardship: a presiding, executing dynasty over rotating
  council stewardships with emergency action subject to later review;
  displayed, not selected.
- C — Charter-oath assembly: a binding founding oath defining dynastic
  duties and limits, with an assembly-chosen council adjudicating the oath
  and holding burden consent; selected.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Options A and B remain nonbinding evidence.

## Binding consequences

- A standing founder-council compact rests on a binding founding oath the
  founder swore in the founding era, within the accepted 25-35-years-ago
  founding range.
- The oath defines the dynasty's duties — defence, outward dealings,
  upholding the compact — and its limits: no burden without consent, no
  seizure of households' subsistence, no altering the compact alone.
- The council is chosen by an assembly of the settlement's households and
  holds two powers: oath adjudication and consent to communal burdens.
- Both sides hold real, actually exercised authority; neither is merely
  ceremonial.
- Dynasty-side office classes: the oath-bound ruling seat and a sworn
  deputy class under the same oath. Council-assembly side: an adjudicator
  class, a burden-consent class, and an oath-witness class whose records
  are fallible, incomplete, and disputable.
- Refugee and newcomer standing follows the household hearth regardless of
  origin, with contested margins over dependents, lodgers, and the
  not-yet-householded.
- Craft, harvest, defence, and System-study expertise enters as sworn
  testimony; harvest knowledge-holders exist and hold that recognised
  testimonial standing as the single declared closure of WLD-ECOLOGY-006's
  open harvest space; System-study testimony is interpretation of
  accessible signs, never a guaranteed readout.
- The compact is a human institution — customary, fallibly recorded,
  socially enforced — with no System-granted power or enforcement in any
  office, title, or term.
- The ageing founder still rules at story start; the first succession under
  the compact is approaching; its provisions are incompletely specified or
  contested, and its known ambiguity classes (heir binding, receiving body,
  disputed recollection of terms, adjudication when the seat is party) stay
  deliberately unresolved as a designed open pressure for WLD-EVENT-001.
- Emergency authority, wherever placed, is grounded in the accepted
  ordinary crisis classes, never in System events.
- No name, title text, oath or law-code wording, office count, seat count,
  quorum, calendar, procedure, enforcement mechanism, property or tenure
  regime, succession outcome, candidate, faction roster, or fishing or
  harvest law is selected.
- No CAL0 change, party mechanic, pooled capability, guaranteed appraisal,
  category eligibility, new mechanic, constant, or notification behaviour
  is created.

## Story and character impact

- Later scenes may use the oath's duties and limits, assembly and council
  powers, sworn testimony, and the known ambiguity classes as structural
  pressure, while every actual dispute, adjudication, refusal, succession
  move, and outcome requires its own evidence.
- The designed first-succession ambiguity supplies WLD-EVENT-001's
  story-start pressures with contestable process and no candidates,
  factions, or outcomes.
- Refugee and newcomer households hold a real but imperfect channel through
  hearth standing, and its contested margins can motivate later friction
  without new institutions.
- Characters know the compact's terms, ambiguities, and disputes only
  through available, fallible sources.

## Residual unknowns

- All in-world names and title wording for the seat, offices, council,
  assembly, compact, and oath
- The exact first-succession process and its resolution — deliberately
  unresolved; the ambiguity classes feed WLD-EVENT-001 and must not be
  settled as background fact
- Assembly-standing margins, council size, seat counts, cadence, quorum,
  and selection procedures
- How sworn expert standing is recognised in practice and how contested
  that recognition is
- The compact's record form: what is written, remembered, witnessed, or
  disputed, and who keeps it
- Law content of any kind: offences, penalties, tenure, inheritance, water
  and road arrangements, and every harvest question
- The compact's history of past emergencies, adjudications, reviews, and
  precedents
- How returning non-human peoples relate to the outward-dealings duty;
  their histories and aims remain unflattened and undeveloped
- How the compact is known, misunderstood, or mythologised inside and
  outside the settlement
