# WLD-ECOLOGY-007A — Basin-Lake Mass-Breeder Foundational Body Plan

## Question decided

What foundational body plan should govern the first, mass-breeding aquatic
migrant established by `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`?

## Selected rule

Use `MASS-BREEDER-BODY-A`. The migrant belongs to a fully aquatic alien
biological lineage. Its migratory form has a streamlined, laterally flexible
trunk supported by springy cartilage, three paired fin-fans, a strong muscular
tail, a wedge-shaped mineralised head shield, and aquatic respiratory surfaces
that form folds which may be exposed or held in a more protected configuration.
Mana contributes to ordinary tissue reinforcement as part of its physiology;
this is not a specialised activated ability or earned System reinforcement.

`WLD-ECOLOGY-006` continues to govern egg-ribbon deposition, adult return and
mortality, predation, recruitment, and population variability.

## Author approval

After the complete flexible fin-fan body-plan recommendation was presented, the
Author replied **“yes”** to **“Should this become the mass-spawner’s foundational
body plan?”** on 2026-08-14. The record uses `mass-breeder` to distinguish
ordinary biological mass reproduction from literal CAL0 spawning.

## Rationale

The selected package produces an original but readable aquatic silhouette and
a plausible foundation for sustained swimming and fine control. It also creates
useful physical constraints—mineral demand, anterior mass, exposed respiratory
tissue, salinity transition, finite endurance, and Mana cost—that later ecology
can resolve without making the animal an Earth transplant or a collection of
cost-free magical abilities.

## Binding consequences

- The mass breeder is fully aquatic through its currently governed migratory
  and reproductive phases; no terrestrial locomotion, nesting, or bypass is established.
- The listed structures are the shared family-grade foundation, but no formal
  taxonomic rank, single species, invariant adult form, or Earth homology follows.
- `Springy cartilage`, `fin-fan`, `head shield`, and `respiratory fold` are
  author-facing structural descriptions, not proof of vertebrate or fish anatomy.
- The body plan is compatible with continuous freshwater-sea migration but
  does not prove route passability, speed, endurance, homing, salinity immunity,
  or guaranteed return.
- Shield mass and minerals, tissue fatigue, fin drag and injury, respiratory
  exposure, oxygen delivery, osmoregulation, buoyancy, food, recovery, disease,
  parasites, and route capacity remain causal constraints.
- Mana supports ordinary tissue function only within a later-defined causal
  source, carrier, capacity, cost, recovery, and failure pathway.
- Mana support is not CAL0 reinforcement, a buff, invulnerability, instant
  repair, free energy, a Skill, a class, a spell, an aura, a reward, or a
  universal rule for all fauna.
- The family-care migrant's body plan remains open.
