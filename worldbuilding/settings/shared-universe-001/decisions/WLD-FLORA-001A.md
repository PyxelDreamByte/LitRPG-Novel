# WLD-FLORA-001A — Basin Reserve-Web Vegetation Guild

## Question decided

What major open-ground vegetation strategy supports the initial basin's
seasonal productivity and the established Lineage II feeding roles?

## Selected rule

- A reserve-web guild is a major but non-exclusive part of the basin's
  open-ground vegetation.
- It contains multiple original vegetation forms rather than one species or
  one continuous basin-wide organism.
- Members maintain living subsurface networks that store water and nutrients
  and support persistence through winter, surface fire, episodic flooding, and grazing.
- They produce tender spring growth, tall fibrous summer structures, and autumn
  fruiting or storage bodies.
- Crown herds consume mature structural growth; ribbon groups select young
  growth and seasonal reproductive structures.
- Browsing and trampling create openings, while excessive pressure can exhaust
  stored reserves and kill the living network.

## Authority and boundaries

This is provisionally authoritative for Project Hearthway flora, regional
ecology, and adopted downstream story development. It does not settle exact
species, anatomy, reproduction, productivity, chemistry, distribution,
disturbance tolerances, Mana use, complementary flora, or human exploitation.

## Author approval

The Author reviewed the reserve-web recommendation and its annual boom-and-bust
and slow woody alternatives, then replied **“yes”** on 2026-08-14.

## Implications

- The basin has resilient but exhaustible seasonal open-ground forage.
- Different growth phases support the two Lineage II feeding strategies.
- Above-ground damage need not kill a network, but recovery consumes finite reserves.
- Moderate disturbance creates mosaics; sustained overpressure causes depletion.
- No Earth species, System refill node, spawn cycle, or progression source is created.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen for an infeasible seasonal budget, unavoidable monoculture or
superorganism behaviour, incompatible soils or hydrology, a concrete story
contradiction, or an explicit Author request.
