# WLD-CULTURE-004A — Imperial Charter-and-Guild Culture

## Question decided

Which qualitative organizing logic governs the withdrawn power's culture —
historically, and as the drifted living remnant culture of the outlet
settlement — without choosing any empire name, capital, map, or date; any
collapse cause beyond WLD-HISTORY-001's multi-causal frame; any rite
content, language content, or monetary fact; any outlet-settlement
government, law, institution, leadership, size, or founding fact; any claim
that the remnant settlement is culturally uniform or fanatical; or any new
System mechanic, readout, or CAL0 change?

## Selected rule

Use Option C — CHARTER-AND-GUILD, plus the proposal's shared claim set. The
empire governed as a lattice of chartered bodies — guilds, colleges,
companies, temple-corporations — each holding delegated authority under
written charter; membership was identity, and law was charter interpretation
argued between bodies. Full personhood in practice was corporate: standing
ran through one's body, and the unincorporated held thin standing. Absorbed
peoples were incorporated as recognized native bodies under limited charters
or joined imperial bodies as junior members. The metropole held dense
overlapping charters; the frontier ran on one or two bodies holding sweeping
local grants.

Its virtues were fidelity to charter, craft excellence, mutual aid within
the body, and honest accounting between bodies — genuinely admirable in
part, for charters restrained arbitrary power and gave the low-born
collective standing no lone petitioner had. Its sins were charter-breach,
encroachment on another body's chartered work, betrayal of craft secrets,
and letting a chartered work fail — and the same fidelity that protected
members froze out the unincorporated. Faith orientation is a
charter-granting cult class, belief only. Dress is livery, with
body-membership and craft-mark classes and a forbidden-marks class. The
ecological-suppression ideology held the wild to be unchartered ground made
real only by charter, with clearance contracted to companies as profitable
duty — a value, never an added collapse cause.

At the outlet settlement, membership-identity and craft-mark pride persist,
agreements are written down and old grants appealed to, charter authority
has hollowed because the granting centre is gone so partial, disputed,
possibly forged documents carry weight with no court able to renew them, and
former branch bodies have inverted into sovereigns of their own function.
The drift is contested, and the remnant is internally varied, never uniform
or fanatical by default.

## Author approval

On 2026-08-17 the Author selected, by explicit multiple choice among the
displayed options:

> Option C — CHARTER-AND-GUILD

accepting Option C exactly as drafted together with the proposal's shared

## Rationale

The selected logic makes the empire's inheritance argued rather than
administered: membership, written grants, and craft standing give the outlet
remnant a self-understanding that hollows into disputed paperwork without a
court, which yields contested drift and rivalry pressure with no new
mechanic, no monetary fact, and no institution named. It leaves the
original culture as a bare structural position, so the sibling records keep
every internal question.

## Alternatives retained

- A — CENSUS-AND-LADDER: registered households, graded civil, military, and
  works service ladders with examination classes, personhood following
  registration, order-and-measure virtues, a registry-cult class, graded
  uniformity in dress, the wild as the unmeasured, and a remnant of
  hollowed examinations, continuing household rolls, and inward-turned
  audit-virtue; displayed and recommended, not selected.
- B — PATRON-AND-STANDARD: great houses holding client chains, identity
  anchored in whose standard one serves, advancement by patron judgment,
  loyalty-and-largesse virtues with downward duties, a standard-and-house
  cult class, house marks and client-mark variants in dress, the wild as
  the enemy's country, and a remnant of persistent patron-client intuition
  and standard-relics with hierarchy hollowed; displayed, not selected.
- C — CHARTER-AND-GUILD: a lattice of chartered bodies with delegated
  authority under written charter, corporate personhood, charter-fidelity
  and craft virtues, a charter-granting cult class, livery dress, the wild
  as unchartered ground, and a remnant of persistent membership identity
  with charter authority hollowed and branch bodies inverted into
  sovereigns of their own function; selected.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Options A and B remain nonbinding evidence, and the proposal's Option A
recommendation is preserved as drafted history.

## Binding consequences

- The withdrawn power governed as a lattice of chartered bodies — guilds,
  colleges, companies, temple-corporations — each holding delegated
  authority under written charter; membership was identity, and law was
  charter interpretation argued between bodies.
- Full personhood in practice was corporate: standing ran through one's
  body, and the unincorporated held thin standing.
- Absorbed peoples were incorporated as recognized native bodies under
  limited charters or as junior members of imperial bodies, and ambitious
  original-culture families earned or bought membership; the region's route
  of incorporation remains open.
- Internal variation existed, at minimum between the metropole's dense
  overlapping charters and the frontier's reliance on one or two bodies
  holding sweeping local grants.
- The virtue set — charter-fidelity, craft excellence, mutual aid within the
  body, honest accounting between bodies — was genuinely admirable in part;
  the sin set — charter-breach, encroachment, betrayed craft secrets, a
  failed chartered work — condemned real harms; and the same fidelity froze
  out the unincorporated. Neither list renders the culture simply good or
  evil.
- Faith orientation is a charter-granting cult class with licensed
  patron-craft observance per body. Belief only; no cosmology, rite content,
  or afterlife claim is confirmed.
- Dress followed livery: body-membership livery classes and craft-mark
  classes, grade shown in trim, with a forbidden-marks class.
- The ecological-suppression ideology held the wild to be unchartered ground
  made grantable, ownable, and real only by charter, with suppression
  contracted to clearance-companies as profitable duty. Value, not cause.
- At the outlet settlement, membership-identity and craft-mark pride
  persist, agreements are written and old grants appealed to, charter
  authority has hollowed with the granting centre gone so partial, disputed,
  possibly forged documents carry weight with no court able to renew or
  adjudicate them, former branch bodies have inverted into sovereigns of
  their own function, and the drift is contested.
- The imperial-descended households admitted into the ridge-spur settlement
  in its early days came out of this culture; the admission and all its
  consequences belong to
  `canon://world/shared-universe-001/culture/settlement-founding-fusion-blend`
  and are not duplicated or extended here.
- The shared claim set is also established: a literate, standardizing,
  engineering-minded culture administering the original culture's region for
  generations as a peripheral holding of a power centred elsewhere; the
  outlet settlement as the primary living local continuation, carried by
  people through the withdrawal to a nucleus abandoned before re-occupation,
  on two separately running clocks with the span unfixed; historical and
  persisting internal variation; a late degraded inherited form after each
  recovery-and-fall cycle stripped further function layers; faith as belief
  and orientation class only; System-record use as cultural practice over
  unchanged natural behaviour; no imperial work, record, or institution
  surviving functional; and remnant belief as attitude and memory, not
  evidence.
