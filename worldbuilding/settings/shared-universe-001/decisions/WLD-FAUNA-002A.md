# WLD-FAUNA-002A — Lineage I Foundational Body Plan

## Question decided

What is Lineage I's bounded foundational body plan, and which of its three
limb pairs becomes the wing pair in winged branches?

## Selected rule

- Lineage I descends from a six-limbed ancestor with a tail.
- Branches repurpose limb pairs for locomotion, handling, display, defence,
  digging, climbing, swimming, or flight.
- The outer covering combines insulating or sensory filaments with flexible
  scutes, in proportions varying by branch and body region.
- They are warm-active animals.
- Reproduction uses flexible, mineral-latticed brood eggs requiring guarded
  nests or brooding; no branch is established as live-bearing.
- Large-bodied branches reproduce slowly, making breeding adults and
  successful nesting sites ecologically consequential.
- In winged branches the middle limb pair becomes the wing pair, leaving four
  well-spaced walking legs with wings rising near the body's centre of mass.
- In non-flying branches the middle pair carries the lineage's most variable
  roles, including grasping, digging, display, and reduced vestiges.
- Winged branches build flight surfaces from the lineage's existing
  filament-and-scute materials rather than novel membranes; exact structure
  remains open.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for Lineage I at foundational body-plan grain. It
does not settle species, names, taxonomy, sizes, branch inventory, wing
structure or performance, filament and scute microstructure, egg chemistry,
clutch sizes, parental roles, diet, senses, cognition, Mana patterns, or
presence anywhere.

## Author approval

After the A — Middle pair, B — Forelimbs, and C — Hindlimbs wing-derivation
cards were presented with A recommended, the Author selected **"A — Middle
pair"** on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- The staging capture's Lineage I sketch now has bounded provisional
  authority instead of being loose staging material.
- The lineage supplies both ordinary six-limbed animals and the world's
  four-legs-plus-wings mythic silhouettes.
- Guarded nesting makes defensible nest sites consequential, losable places.
- Slow large-body reproduction makes megafaunal branches vulnerable to
  sustained pressure, fitting a landscape where such animals are only now
  returning.
- No Earth homology, System mechanic, or creature progression rule is
  created.
