# WLD-SETTING-001A — Project Hearthway shared-setting bootstrap

## Scope

This decision provisionally establishes only:

- `setting://shared-universe-001` as the shared-universe container; and
- `work://series/project-hearthway-main` as its main ongoing series.

Their manifests point to one another. `Project Hearthway` and `Project
Hearthway Main Series` are temporary editorial titles and may change without
changing either stable identity.

## Explicit exclusions

This decision does **not** accept:

- any geography, history, culture, institution, species, ecology, character,
  event, premise, plot, or prose;
- the foundation capture or any part of it as setting canon;
- Ancestry Development or any other change to CAL0; or
- either optional project-level modelling default.

Those materials remain proposed and require their own reviews and Author gates.

## Author approval

On 2026-08-13, the Author selected an ongoing series. The Author was then shown
this narrow bootstrap relationship and explicitly told that all geography,
cultures, creatures, history, System ideas, and temporary names would remain
proposed. The Author replied **“yes.”**

## Consequences

- The Project Hearthway setting manifest has `ACCEPTED` workflow status and
  narrowly `PROVISIONAL` canon status.
- The new series manifest remains `PROPOSED`; its contract and story content are
  not accepted.
- The series may use Project Hearthway as its declared shared-setting
  development boundary.
- No unlisted setting record or project default becomes inherited authority.

## Residual unknowns

All substantive world, story, character, and System questions remain open. The
next worldbuilding work may turn the staging capture into bounded proposals,
but no proposal becomes canon through this bootstrap.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen if the Author changes the work form, the universe requires multiple
incompatible setting authorities, a stable identity collision emerges, the
relationship fails repository validation, or the Author explicitly requests it.

The validation-ready record is
[`WLD-SETTING-001A.decision.json`](WLD-SETTING-001A.decision.json).
