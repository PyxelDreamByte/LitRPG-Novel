# WLD-ECOLOGY-005A — Initial-Basin Coupled Pulse-Mosaic Exchange

## Question decided

What high-level ecosystem engine and wider-area boundary should organize
development of the initial basin?

## Selected rule

- Open ground, woodland, wet margins, and lake habitats provide distinct
  seasonal productivity pulses.
- Organisms, flooding, runoff, decomposition, and migration transfer matter and
  nutrients among those habitat channels.
- No habitat, producer guild, animal lineage, migration, or disturbance is the
  basin's sole ecological engine.
- The basin is nested within an otherwise undefined wider regional ecology.
- Later nonlocal-life development must distinguish confirmed direct wild
  presence, seasonal or transient wild presence, imported remains or products, rare
  traveller-carried companion or captive live presence, and reported-only
  knowledge. Evidence in one class does not establish another.

## Author approval

After the coupled pulse-mosaic recommendation was presented, the Author replied
on 2026-08-14, **“yes + consider the basin but also the wider are for migrations
that some animals might make or for rare pets that travellers have or
meats/produce that come from far away or tales of animals etc that aren't in the
local area.”** `Yes` selected the coupled model; the remainder added the
wider-region development and provenance requirement.

## Rationale

The selected model makes the lake ecologically consequential without reducing
the terrestrial mosaic to scenery. It preserves the reserve-web guild and
Lineage II as bounded parts of a larger web, supports several original flora and
fauna families, and permits the wider world to enter protagonist-facing scenes
without confusing products, captives, migrants, stories, and local populations.

## Binding consequences

- Full causal detail belongs inside the basin; connected outer regions receive
  the support needed for later approved migrations or transport; more distant
  life may remain source-and-confidence knowledge.
- A direct sighting establishes presence only at that time. Products do not
  establish local range or verified provenance. Migrants or captives do not
  establish residency or a self-sustaining population. Tales do not establish
  existence or local presence.
- The Author's examples are later development channels, not accepted instances
  of a particular animal, migration, traveller, product, route, market, or tale.
- The horizon is regional and planetary. It establishes no offworld connection
  and does not connect ordinary travellers to the ancient doorway myths.
- No new System rule, spawning process, creature level, shared progression, or
  automatic power transfer is created.

## Residual unknowns

Exact productivity, seasons, mechanisms, outer geography, migrations,
organisms, products, travellers, logistics, institutions, prices, tales,
knowledge, and offworld provenance remain open for later bounded decisions.
