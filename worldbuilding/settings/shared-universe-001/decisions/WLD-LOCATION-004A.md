# WLD-LOCATION-004A — Basin Imperial-Remnant Corridor-and-Clearance Skeleton

## Question decided

What qualitative pattern should govern the withdrawn power's surviving
physical works in and near the initial basin — the arrangement of remnant
routes, clearances, structures, water-and-ground works, and boundary or
administrative traces, their condition classes, and how salvage relates to
them as an activity class — without fixing names, maps, counts, positions,
ownership, law, institutions, story events, character knowledge,
ancient-doorway facts, or any System-facing property of any remnant?

## Selected rule

Use A — Corridor-and-clearance skeleton. The dominant surviving signature is
linear: a decayed engineered route network and the broad cleared lines of the
former power's organised ecological suppression lattice the lower basin and
its approaches, with ruinous waystation-type and watch-type shells, scattered
drainage and ground works, and occasional boundary and marker traces. Most of
the skeleton is regrown, shells are ruinous, a few stretches or nodes are
reused, and some stretches are avoided for practical reasons. The proposal's
shared claim set applies unchanged.

By Author direction, one cross-cite is added: the imperial-remnant industrial
village nucleus of the second settlement (WLD-LOCATION-005, promoted in the
same batch) is an instance of this record's reused-node class.
WLD-LOCATION-005 holds authority for everything about that settlement; this
record neither locates nor describes it beyond the class relationship.

Regrowth, ruin, reuse, and avoidance are condition classes, not selected
events, positions, or guarantees. The ridge-spur settlement road's possible
imperial derivation stays open, and the ancient doorways stay excluded.

## Author approval

On 2026-08-17 the Author selected, by explicit multiple-choice selection:

> Option A — CORRIDOR-AND-CLEARANCE SKELETON

The selection adopts Option A exactly as drafted together with the shared
claim set, and directs the WLD-LOCATION-005 reused-node cross-cite recorded
above. Nothing beyond the Author's selection is added.

## Rationale

The selected pattern is the most direct forward extension of the foundation
capture, which already lists abandoned imperial clearances and corridors
among the rewilding mosaic's elements. It distributes remnant texture across
the whole basin where every accepted ecology and location record can touch
it, rather than concentrating story gravity in one dungeon-like destination,
and keeps the strongest questions — corridor reoccupation, road derivation,
salvage friction — open at useful pressure.

## Alternatives retained

- A — Corridor-and-clearance skeleton: decayed linear route-and-clearance
  lattice across the lower basin with regrown, ruinous, reused, and avoided
  condition classes and dispersed low-yield salvage; selected.
- B — Nucleus-and-spokes: one substantial derelict administrative, garrison,
  and works nucleus at an unfixed distance with decayed spoke routes,
  expedition salvage, and a partly avoided dangerous complex; not selected.
- C — Dispersed small works: many minor installations scattered through the
  basin, including one flooded or lake-claimed installation, with broad
  shallow salvage and no single large complex; not selected.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Options B and C remain nonbinding evidence.

## Binding consequences

- Physical remnants of the withdrawn power exist in and near the initial
  basin, recognised by present inhabitants as that power's work by
  workmanship, materials, and scale — cultural recognition, not accurate
  functional knowledge.
- The dominant surviving signature is a decayed linear skeleton: engineered
  route segments and suppression clearances, with small ancillaries, drainage
  and ground works, and boundary traces.
- Present condition spans four qualitative classes: mostly regrown; shells
  ruinous; a few stretches or nodes reused; some avoided for practical
  reasons.
- Legibility is partial; no complete route or clearance line is known to
  survive.
- Up to roughly seventy years of decay, accumulating from each work's own
  local abandonment in the cascading retreat, degrade every surviving work;
  partial incidental usefulness is the ceiling, and no work is continuously
  occupied since abandonment.
- No remnant grants working machinery, intact knowledge stores, functioning
  mechanisms, or preserved archives by default; any exception is a new,
  separately argued proposal.
- Salvage is dispersed and low-yield with finite, diminishing, contestable
  value; no salvage right, ownership, law, institution, market, price, or
  named practice exists.
- Some regrown cleared ground qualitatively coincides with ground crown herds
  and travellers move through; no stretch is identified, no WLD-ECOLOGY-004
  corridor or secondary path is located, and clearances are not claimed to
  cause or anchor route fidelity.
- Whether the settlement's constructed road reuses any imperial-era alignment
  or foundation is deliberately left open.
- No remnant exists at the WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002 outlet reach, at the
  WLD-LOCATION-003 staging place or spurs, or in any WLD-LOCATION-001
  settlement zone; the constructed road corridor is excluded from this bar.
- WLD-LOCATION-002 remains the sole authority for its broad fair-weather
  whole-journey anchors; no remnant creates a travel band or route claim.
- Regrown cleared lines are ordinary WLD-ECOLOGY-005 mosaic openings, browsed
  per WLD-ECOLOGY-001; no organism or colony is placed in any remnant.
- The second settlement's imperial-remnant industrial village nucleus is an
  instance of the reused-node class; WLD-LOCATION-005 is the authority for
  everything about that settlement.
- Skeleton, route segment, suppression clearance, waystation-type,
  watch-type, marker trace, reuse, avoidance, and salvage are author-facing
  qualitative labels fixing no in-world name, measurement, count, position,
  standard, institution, entitlement, or guarantee.
- The skeleton is author-side spatial truth and does not become automatic
  character knowledge of any remnant's location, condition, origin, or
  value.
- All remnants are ordinary made things under ordinary physics and CAL0; no
  safe zone, spawn point, Mana source, System structure, doorway, readout,
  mechanic, or CAL0 change is created.

## Explicit exclusions

- The ancient archways or doorways: pre-imperial, separately parked,
  constitution-protected; no remnant is a doorway, doorway housing, or
  doorway-related work, and no claim touches their nature, builders, or
  operability.
- Maps, distances, directions, elevations, dimensions, counts, or any
  quantitative geography or engineering; named sites, works, builders, or any
  in-world name.
- Treasure inventories, caches, intact libraries, preserved archives,
  functioning mechanisms, or working imperial machinery of any kind.
- The parked sapient pack species: still parked, unconnected to any remnant,
  and gaining no biology, culture, or ability development.
- Story events, characters, plot use, or current-event claims beyond standing
  background texture; any System mechanic or CAL0 change; project-default
  adoption (WLD-SOUL-001A and WLD-PRENATAL-001A remain unadopted).

  shells, reused nodes, and avoided ground as standing background texture,
  while every actual journey, find, dispute, encounter, and outcome requires

  low-grade friction, but no dispute, party, practice, or event follows
  automatically.

  unreliable-footing texture only through their own records.

  through an available source.
