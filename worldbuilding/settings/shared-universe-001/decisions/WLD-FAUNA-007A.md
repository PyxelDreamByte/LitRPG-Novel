# WLD-FAUNA-007A — Lineage VII Foundational Body Plan

## Question decided

What is Lineage VII's bounded foundational body plan, which life modes does
it contain, and which mode is ancestral?

## Selected rule

Selected as an Author-directed structural revision (all three displayed life
modes retained as branches) plus selection of option A for ancestry:

- Lineage VII bodies are unsegmented and soft, without a rigid internal
  skeleton or a jointed exoskeleton.
- Bodies are broadly bilateral and elongate along a single axis, in contrast
  with Lineage V's tri-radial organisation.
- Movement is driven by muscular waves running along the body axis rather
  than by Lineage V's travelling margin waves.
- The lineage occurs in water, soil, and other moist substrates.
- Life mode is a branch-level axis with three modes:
  - filter branches draw suspended material from water, sessile or weakly
    mobile on submerged surfaces;
  - substrate branches live inside material — soil, mud, litter, sediment,
    decaying matter — driving through it by muscular waves and processing it
    as they go;
  - grazer branches move over exposed surfaces, feeding from them and
    relying on protective secretions.
- Aquatic filter feeding is ancestral; substrate branches derived from
  filterers by invading sediment and soil, and grazer branches derived from
  substrate branches by emerging onto exposed surfaces.
- Substrate branches own substrate turnover — the mixing, aeration, and
  breakdown that make soils work — complementing rather than duplicating the
  `WLD-FLORA-008` thread decomposers, which wrap and digest concentrated dead
  material from outside.
- Internal parasitism derives from the substrate habit, since living within a
  body is living within material.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for Lineage VII at foundational body-plan grain.
It does not settle species, names, taxonomy, sizes, branch inventory, feeding
mechanisms, digestion, senses, respiration, circulation, whether any branch
carries mineralised structures or shells, reproduction, development, which
branches are parasitic or their hosts and effects, Mana patterns, or presence
anywhere.

## Author approval

After the life-mode cards were presented, the Author directed that all three
modes be included; they were recorded as branches within the lineage rather
than as separate lineages because they share one body plan, and the revised
model was presented with an ancestry question, to which the Author selected
**"A — Filter ancestral"** on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- The faunal roster's seven lineages now all have foundational body plans.
- The lineage's universal moisture dependence and its surface forms'
  protective secretions both have causal explanations.
- Substrate branches hold the soil-turnover job no other lineage or kingdom
  claims, keeping the food web's recycling division of labour explicit.
- Internal parasites derive from the lineage's own habit rather than being
  bolted on, with medical and veterinary consequences available but not
  established.
- Most of the lineage is hidden most of the time, so its members are
  something scenes uncover rather than constantly display.
- No Earth homology, particular parasite or host, or System mechanic is
  created.
