# WLD-FLORA-004A — Planetary Floral Kingdom Roster

## Question decided

Should the planet's producer and decomposer life get a kingdom roster parallel
to the faunal lineage roster, and at what grain?

## Selected rule

- Six major kingdoms organise the planet's macroscopic producer and decomposer
  life at growth-strategy-theme level.
- K1 — networked modular flora: living subsurface networks linking rooted
  growth modules; the reserve-web guild of `WLD-FLORA-001` through
  `WLD-FLORA-003` belongs to this kingdom.
- K2 — tower flora: long-lived, tall, self-supporting woody-analogue
  structures.
- K3 — free-seeder flora: short-lived, unnetworked, fast-dispersing
  individual plants.
- K4 — water flora: submerged, floating, and wet-margin producers.
- K5 — crust-and-cushion flora: low, slow, surface-hugging pioneer growth.
- K6 — thread decomposers: non-photosynthesising thread-and-body networks in
  decomposer, recycler, and mutualist roles.
- Every kingdom includes abundant ordinary forms.

## Authority and boundaries

This is provisionally authoritative for Project Hearthway flora at
strategy-theme level and adopted downstream development. It does not settle
kingdom names, in-world taxonomy, energy capture, chemistry, anatomy,
reproduction, dispersal, Mana patterns, presence anywhere, minor kingdoms
below the roster's grain, or the kingdom of any referenced vegetation
(rainforest, woodland, wet-margin, or egg-substrate plants).

## Author approval

After the A — Six kingdoms, B — Three groups, and C — No roster options were
presented with A recommended, the Author selected **"A — Six kingdoms"** on
2026-08-17.

## Implications

- Later flora guilds slot into stable kingdoms the way fauna slot into
  lineages.
- The reserve-web guild gains a kingdom home with its records unchanged.
- The rainforest, woodland, wet-margin, and egg-substrate vegetation have
  candidate homes pending their own records.
- Decomposers and root-symbiont partners finally have a slot for the open
  food-web work.
- No System mechanic, guaranteed harvest, or Earth transplant is created.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen if a needed producer or decomposer strategy cannot fit any kingdom, if
kingdom-first design proves too coarse or restrictive, if a concrete
contradiction with an accepted record emerges, or on explicit Author request.
