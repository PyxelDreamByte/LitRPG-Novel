# WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002A — Initial Outlet Mixed-Reach Sequence

## Question decided

What dominant qualitative physical sequence should extend the narrow,
naturally defensible outlet gap established by WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001 without
pre-empting hydrology, organism fit, or passage success?

## Author decision metadata

- **Status:** PROVISIONAL
- **Accepted by:** Author
- **Accepted on:** 2026-08-14
- **Approval evidence:** after A, B, and C outlet-structure options were
  presented, the Author replied “C”, selecting C — Mixed outlet sequence

## Selected operational rule

Use C — Mixed outlet sequence.

A narrow bedrock-controlled throat opens downstream into one connected mixed
reach containing irregular boulder steps, lateral or side shelves, and local
holding pockets. The reach is heterogeneous rather than one uniform channel or
regular feature chain.

Boulder steps and side shelves are candidate, state-dependent bed-or-margin
contact surfaces against which WLD-ECOLOGY-010 passage can be tested. Holding
pockets are local channel recesses or pools that may be less exposed to the
main flow under some conditions and can be tested as potentially
lower-velocity locations.

Coherent migration corridor means only one connected physical system and
candidate route architecture. It does not establish a continuous support or
rest chain, guaranteed low velocity, safety, recovery, passability, seasonal
usability, route capacity, or organism success.

## Alternatives considered

1. A — Bedrock step-pool chain — low stone sills alternating with shallow
   basins and deeper pools; not selected.
2. B — Alternating margin shelves — a central channel bordered by staggered
   ledges, backwaters, and eddies; not selected.
3. C — Mixed outlet sequence — a narrow bedrock throat opening into a
   connected mixed reach containing irregular boulder steps, side shelves, and
   holding pockets; **selected**.

Rejected-option details remain decision evidence only. They do not establish
regular sills, basin depths, a swift central channel, eddies, debris-jam
behaviour, predictable ambush points, or crowding effects.

## Rationale and evidence

The selected option is the smallest forward extension that turns the wholly
open outlet profile into a connected qualitative feature set. Its heterogeneity
permits later state-dependent hydrology and organism-fit testing while
preserving all quantitative, seasonal, ecological, and outcome uncertainty.

## Consequences and affected sources

- The throat precedes the downstream mixed reach. No exact count, spacing, or
  order among the downstream steps, shelves, and pockets is selected.
- The reach is one connected physical system but is not a proven continuous
  support, rest, or movement chain.
- Boulder steps and side shelves are only candidate contacts whose wetting,
  exposure, stability, accessibility, and load-bearing suitability remain
  state dependent and untested.
- Holding pockets are local channel recesses or pools. They may be less exposed
  to the main flow under some conditions, but they are not guaranteed
  low-velocity water, refuges, safe zones, checkpoints, healing sites, recovery
  sites, or refill points.
- Exact flow, depth, discharge, gradient, turbulence, water level, flood, ice,
  debris, tidal, salinity, erosion, and deposition behaviour remains open.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-010 passage can be tested against the accepted feature set, but
  actual contact fit, support continuity, resting opportunity, seasonal
  viability, passability, and organism success remain unresolved.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-006 continues to govern only the qualitative migration and
  freshet relationship; WLD-ECOLOGY-009 continues to govern body plan and
  route-feasibility concerns; WLD-ECOLOGY-010 continues to govern organism-side
  choreography.
- Material, water, usable energy, route space, organism condition, injury,
  mortality, recruitment, and recovery remain finite and causal.
- An observer may see channel form and local conditions at an observed time,
  but cannot infer hidden hydraulics, physiology, annual route capacity,
  seasonal usability, or species-wide success without evidence.
- No exact geology, inhabitant, habitat occupancy, human structure or use,
  cultural practice, Mana property, System interaction, spawn, reset, refill,
  reward, or guaranteed recovery is created.
