# WLD-CULTURE-005A — Settlement Founding-Fusion Blend Logic

## Question decided

Which qualitative blend logic governs how the ridge-spur hillfort
settlement holds its two parent inheritances — the locally rooted
original culture and the withdrawn power's imperial culture — through
WLD-CULTURE-001's accepted strand-braid, and what founding frame stands
behind that blend, without fixing either parent culture's content, any
rite, speech variety, or exchange-medium fact, any in-world name, faith
content, costume detail, compact term, office, or succession provision,
any demographic proportion, or any protagonist fact?

## Selected rule

Use B — FOUNDING-FUSION, plus the proposal's shared claim set, plus the
Author-directed founding frame.

The settlement's founding-era disposition is remembered as fusion —
inherited forms worked into settlement-specific composites — a remembered
pattern, variously and disputably held, with no founding occasion,
decision, deliberation, or event fixed. The charter-oath compact is
remembered as the model case: an oath sworn in what residents call the
original manner joined to witnessed, fallibly kept records in what they
call the imperial manner. Standing accrues to households and persons seen
to embody the joined way, while purists of either inheritance keep real
but narrower prestige in their own circles, and the young composite
identity is conscious and contested. The settlement virtue is the joined
work; its shame is unravelling — each either newly minted here or
sharpened and re-anchored from a parent register, never claimed as novel
against a parent frame. Faith blends by syncretism as belief practice
over the common occasions, with traditionalists of both inheritances
keeping older forms in household privacy, and dress shows a recognisable
settlement-specific composite.

The Author-directed founding frame, which this record establishes as
bounded claims: the settlement's founder was a native of the original
culture; in the early founding days he admitted imperial refugees into
the fold to help build the settlement; under his leadership there has
been unity and mutual respect between the inheritances; cultural
hybridisation is early-stage and ongoing, not complete; and consequently
the original culture is the settlement's host culture and the
imperial-descended are guests-turned-members, the original culture being
unsuppressed at the settlement.

## Author approval

On 2026-08-17 the Author selected, by explicit multiple choice among the
displayed options:

> B — FOUNDING-FUSION

accepting Option B exactly as drafted together with the proposal's shared
claim set, and in the same review directing the founding frame stated
above.

## Rationale

The selected blend deepens the accepted strand-braid with a remembered
disposition rather than a narrated founding, gives the approaching first
succession a live cultural argument about re-performance without
resolving any designed ambiguity, and — with the Author-directed founding
frame — grounds the settlement's present host-and-member relations in a
native founder's admission of imperial refugees while keeping the
hybridisation explicitly early-stage and unfinished.

## Alternatives retained

- A — DOMAIN-SPLIT: parent inheritances persist by domain, with
  imperial-attributed forms predominating in public administration,
  record keeping, walls-and-works, and defence and original-attributed
  forms in household, seasonal, land, and hospitality life;
  register-switching, boundary friction, and domain-read dress; the
  proposal's recommended option; not selected.
- B — FOUNDING-FUSION: a remembered founding-era disposition of fusion,
  inherited forms worked into settlement-specific composites, the compact
  as model case, standing for the joined way against narrower purist
  prestige, syncretic common observance, and a composite dress; selected.
- C — PRAGMATIC PATCHWORK: no settlement-wide blend logic, each household
  and strand braiding its own mix, thin procedural common institutions, a
  procedural keeping-terms status scale, plural coexisting observance,
  and no settlement dress style; not selected.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local
capture. Options A and C remain nonbinding evidence.

## Binding consequences

- The blend logic expresses through WLD-CULTURE-001's strand-braid,
  deepening the accepted fabric and replacing nothing.
- The founding-era disposition is remembered as fusion, variously and
  disputably held, with no founding occasion, decision, deliberation, or
  event fixed.
- Declared narrow closure of the founder-origin question left open in
  WLD-CULTURE-002's founder-and-dynasty space: the settlement's founder
  was a native of the original culture. The dynasty's later composition,
  every office-holder's origin, and every designed first-succession
  ambiguity class remain untouched and explicitly still open.
- In the early founding days the founder admitted imperial refugees into
  the fold to help build the settlement; this is a standing founding-frame
  fact, not a narrated founding scene, and no occasion, count,
  proportion, or date is established. The frame consumes WLD-HISTORY-001's
  disclaimed founding-composition space and extends WLD-LOCATION-001's
  declared-open founder space, which WLD-CULTURE-002 already fills in part;
  neither record requires a boundary edit.
- Under the founder's leadership there has been unity and mutual respect
  between the inheritances — a qualitative condition, not unanimity and
  not the absence of friction.
- Cultural hybridisation is early-stage and ongoing, not complete; no
  rate, endpoint, or timetable is established.
- The original culture is the settlement's host culture and the
  imperial-descended are guests-turned-members, with the original culture
  unsuppressed at the settlement — present settlement relations only,
  altering nothing in WLD-HISTORY-001's account of the withdrawn power's
  historical regional administration.
- The compact is remembered as the model fusion case; the reading is
  resident belief and adds no compact term, office, text, or ambiguity
  resolution, and the succession ambiguities read as a re-performance
  question that sharpens the designed pressure and resolves nothing.
- Standing accrues to those seen to embody the joined way while purists
  of either inheritance keep real but narrower prestige; the composite
  identity is conscious and contested.
- The settlement virtue is the joined work and the settlement shame is
  unravelling, each newly minted here or sharpened from a parent
  register, neither claimed as novel against any parent frame.
- Faith blends by syncretism as belief practice over non-lifecycle common
  occasions, with household-private traditionalist forms beneath it;
  nothing metaphysical is confirmed.
- Dress shows a recognisable settlement-specific composite, with marked
  parent dress for household and purist occasions; no garment, textile,
  material class, or costume detail is fixed.
- Hillfort-side perception only: the settlement reads the outlet as the
  unblended sibling and believes the outlet reads it as culturally mixed;
  the standing rivalry colours the perception as claims of standing. This
  decision establishes no outlet-side fact — an abstention of its own,
  denying nothing a sibling record establishes.
- Generational texture is established as standing tension: anxious
  founders, confident walls-born, and a visible few reaching back for
  purer parent forms.
- "Original-derived" and "imperial-derived" remain fallible attributions;
  a custom believed imperial may be original, and the reverse.
- Parent-culture content, lifecycle rites, speech varieties, and exchange
  media remain wholly the batch siblings' territory.
- Every open boundary of every touched record stays open except the
  single declared founder-origin closure.

## CAL0 classification

Ordinary setting extension. Standing, virtue, observance, and dress
claims are cultural layers over unchanged natural System behaviour (CAL0
UI1.0D, UI1.2D). Syncretic observance is belief practice only: the System
and Souls are objective architecture that cultures interpret, no
observance triggers, gates, guarantees, modifies, or reveals any System
event or readout, and no afterlife, Soul, or death claim is made (CAL0
WLD1.0D, UI1.1D, RES0.10D). No institution, office, or standing gains
System-executed power (CAL0 SOC1.2D). No CAL0 change, new System
mechanic, readout guarantee, or project-default adoption is created, and
the WLD-PRENATAL-001A guardrail is respected read-only.
