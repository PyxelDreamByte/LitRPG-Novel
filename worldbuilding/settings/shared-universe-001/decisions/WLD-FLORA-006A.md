# WLD-FLORA-006A — Water-Flora Foundational Growth Architecture

## Question decided

What foundational growth architecture defines the K4 water-flora kingdom
established by `WLD-FLORA-004`?

## Selected rule

- Water flora are individual, non-networked organisms spanning submerged,
  floating-surface, and wet-margin emergent forms.
- Each organism has a durable, long-lived anchor gripping bed, stone, or bank
  that holds its stored reserves, plus expendable flexible streamer growth
  extended through the growing season.
- Streamers are shed or sacrificed to ice, flood scouring, and grazing;
  winter and flood survival is anchor-and-reserves survival, with regrowth
  following the spring freshet.
- Kingdom life rhythms are strongly coupled to the seasonal water cycle, and
  disturbance tolerance is a kingdom-defining pressure.
- Mature anchored forms with stiffened streamer bases are the natural but
  still unassigned candidate for the lake's sturdy egg-substrate plants;
  floating members remain a bounded branch possibility rather than the
  kingdom theme.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for the K4 kingdom at qualitative strategy grain.
It does not settle species, forms, dimensions, chemistry, energy capture,
reproduction, salinity tolerances, the actual egg-substrate assignment,
floating-branch existence, symbioses, Mana patterns, or presence anywhere.

## Author approval

After the A — Anchor-and-streamer, B — Buoy flora, and C — Turf flora
architecture cards were presented with A recommended, the Author selected
**"A — Anchor-and-streamer"** on 2026-08-17.

## Implications

- Lake and shore scenes gain a seasonal texture of bare post-freshet anchors,
  summer streamer beds, and storm-shed drift.
- Anchor loss, not streamer loss, is the lasting ecological damage.
- The sturdy egg-substrate connection stays a natural candidate without
  breaching `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`'s non-assignment boundary.
- No Earth-plant homology, human practice, or System mechanic is created.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen if the architecture cannot support required lake ecology or nursery
functions, if a concrete contradiction with an accepted record emerges, or on
explicit Author request.
