# WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001A — Initial-Region Physical Scaffold

## Question decided

What qualitative physical geography, drainage, coast, and seasonal climate
should anchor the initial region's ecosystem and later human geography?

## Selected rule

- Arrange the immediate region broadly from wet rocky western and northwestern
  uplands through a sheltered central lake basin. Beyond the eastern or
  southeastern outlet gap, downstream lower marsh country leads to a broken
  drowned coast.
- Use a freshwater through-flow lake fed by rivers and streams and drained to
  the sea through an outlet river crossing a narrow, naturally defensible gap.
- Treat the land as an ancient river basin later reshaped by glaciation.
- Treat the anciently inundated coast as broadly stable at regional scale but
  locally changeable under storms.
- Use a transitional maritime–continental climate, a major western windward
  temperate-rainforest biome, and scattered damp basin refugia.
- Use the approved variable winter, sudden-thaw, mild-to-warm summer, occasional
  multi-week dry-spell, uncommon severe-drought, and possible surface-fire pattern.

## Author approval

On 2026-08-14, the Author stated that the region should resemble a mix of
ancient Britain, Doggerland, and Scandinavia as inspiration. The Author then
selected a freshwater open lake “fed by rivers/streams” with “an outlet river
that proceeds to the sea,” chose a “defensible gap,” and replied **“yes”** to
the proposed transitional climate, western rainforest with basin refugia,
layered river–glacial origin, ancient broadly stable drowned coast, winter and
summer patterns, and regional west-to-east arrangement.

## Rationale

The selected scaffold combines sheltered heartland geography with river,
coast, upland, wetland, and seasonal connections. It supplies ordinary causal
support for the coupled basin ecosystem while leaving detailed maps, species,
routes, cultures, and historical consequences open for later decisions.

## Binding consequences

- Ecosystem and story development must preserve the qualitative regional
  arrangement, through-flow freshwater drainage, layered geomorphic history,
  ancient stable-at-regional-scale drowned coast, and transitional seasonal climate.
- “Defensible” establishes a terrain affordance, not a fortification, occupant,
  road, toll, settlement, or political controller.
- “Temperate rainforest” establishes climate and broad vegetation structure,
  not Earth taxa or an in-world technical term.
- “Broadly stable” permits local erosion, deposition, channel shifts, storm
  damage, flooding, and loss of small coastal landforms.
- “Lower basin floor” and “downstream lower marsh country” remain distinct; the
  existing crown corridor is not moved through the outlet gap or to the coast.
- The lake remains freshwater. Head of tide, freshwater tidal influence,
  salt-front position, storm-surge reach, and the detailed relationship between
  the outlet gap and coastal tidal marshes remain open.
- Continuous central-lake freezing means surface ice, not a frozen water column.
- Upland snow feeds a strong spring melt; basin snow can thaw and return several
  times within a winter; sudden thaws can bring flooding, moving ice, and
  dangerous river crossings.
- Exact migration, travel, trade, import, and knowledge claims still require
  their own causal evidence and Author gates.
- No Earth setting, Earth history, Earth organism, magical climate cause,
  offworld link, or System mechanic is established.
