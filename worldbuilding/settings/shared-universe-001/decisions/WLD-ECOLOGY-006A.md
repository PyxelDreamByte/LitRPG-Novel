# WLD-ECOLOGY-006A — Basin-Lake Spring Breeding Pulse

## Question decided

What qualitative lake-productivity and breeding-migration relationship should
anchor the first aquatic fauna-family development?

## Selected rule

- The freshwater through-flow lake is biologically productive but spatially
  and seasonally varied.
- The spring freshet creates favourable passage from the sea and marks the
  start of the lake's seasonal rise in productivity.
- An unnamed mass-breeding migrant arrives first and deposits dense ribbons or
  clusters of eggs onto sturdy plants and mineral surfaces across several
  nursery habitat classes. Healthy adults generally return seaward despite
  meaningful mortality across the cycle.
- A distinct family-care migrant follows, remains through one warm season while
  rearing young, and escorts surviving young seaward before autumn storms and
  first freezes.
- Its young consume the first migrant's eggs and juveniles as an important but
  non-exclusive food source.
- Both migrants compete with resident lake fauna. Humans, aquatic predators,
  terrestrial shore hunters, and bird-like fishing organisms exploit the
  seasonal abundance.
- Reproductive risk is dispersed across places and times so that no single
  ordinary predator, flood, or fishery routinely destroys the entire generation.
- Exceptional or compound recruitment failures remain possible.
- The dispersed egg-deposition pattern creates feeding grounds that shift
  across the lake over the season.

## Author approval

The Author described a lake that is productive as a whole but whose organisms
prefer different areas and seasons, proposed both mass-breeding and family-care
migrants in the same annual cycle, and added that they interact with resident
fauna, humans, land animals, and bird-like fishers. The Author then replied
`yes` to the direct but non-exclusive juvenile predation relationship, the
spring-freshet sequence, the one-warm-season rearing cycle, and the distributed
egg-ribbon nursery pattern. The last `yes` directly answered a recommendation
that the first migrant deposit dense egg ribbons or clusters onto sturdy plants
and mineral surfaces across those nursery belts so that one ordinary predator,
flood, or fishery would not destroy the entire generation.

## Rationale

The selection makes the large lake ecologically active across much of the warm
season without making one migration the basin's sole engine. Offset migrant
cycles create prey, competition, predation, and human-use relationships while
leaving room for resident aquatic families and later quantitative checks.

## Binding consequences

- River-mouth shallows, protected bays, submerged ridges, and flooded
  vegetation margins exist as qualitative nursery habitat classes; their map,
  dimensions, composition, and frequency remain open.
- Flooded vegetation is not identified as the reserve-web guild.
- Egg ribbons describe egg arrangement only and establish no animal anatomy or
  connection to Lineage II ribbon forms.
- Egg deposition onto plants and mineral surfaces establishes no adhesion,
  entanglement, draping, anchoring, or other retention mechanism.
- Bird-like identifies an ecological resemblance only, not feathers, flight,
  Earth taxonomy, or a named clade.
- Human exploitation establishes a mortality channel, not a fishing
  technology, institution, law, trade, ritual, or sustainable harvest.
- No single ordinary predator, flood, or fishery routinely destroys the entire
  generation, but this establishes no sustainable harvest rate and does not
  prevent exceptional or compound collapse.
- The migrations are ordinary variable life cycles, not a System spawning,
  reset, refill, pooled progression, or automatic reward mechanism.

## Residual unknowns

Names, taxonomy, anatomy, exact routes and timing, salinity transition,
population sizes, reproduction and care mechanisms, mortality and recruitment
rates, predators, resident competitors, human practice and knowledge, nutrient
transfer, exact egg-retention mechanics, Mana anatomy, abilities, progression,
and appraisal remain open. The
later body-plan recommendation was not selected and has no authority.
