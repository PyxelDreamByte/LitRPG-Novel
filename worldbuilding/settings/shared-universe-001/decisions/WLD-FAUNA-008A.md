# WLD-FAUNA-008A — Basin-Lake Resident Fauna Assemblage

## Question decided

What year-round animal community does the basin lake hold, which lineages
supply it, and how does it relate to the annual spring migrant surge?

## Selected rule

- The lake holds a permanent resident animal community, present year-round
  and independent of the spring migrant pulses.
- Resident aquatic animals are drawn principally from Lineage IV, with
  Lineage V, VI, and VII members also present.
- The bird-like fishing organisms referenced by `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` are
  Lineage III forelimb-winged branch members, resolving their previously open
  lineage assignment. `Bird-like` remains an ecological resemblance only.
- Terrestrial shore hunters are drawn from Lineages I and III.
- Residents must survive winter in the lake, including the regular freezing
  of sheltered bays.
- The resident community is structurally dominant: it sets the lake's
  ecological structure and carries most of its standing animal biomass
  year-round.
- The spring migrant pulse arrives into an already-occupied lake. It
  concentrates in particular places and weeks and functions as a large
  seasonal subsidy rather than as the lake's organising force, and it does
  not restructure the resident community.

## Authority and boundaries

Provisionally authoritative for the basin lake's resident assemblage at
qualitative composition-and-relationship grain. It does not settle species,
names, sizes, population figures, the resident inventory, which Lineage IV
branches occur or whether any carry derived organs, whether any resident is a
Lineage V vault-bearing form, overwintering behaviour, resident reproduction,
human fishing practice, disease, parasites, or predation rates.

Structural dominance does not downgrade the migrant pulse. `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`
continues to govern the dispersed nursery grounds, shifting feeding grounds,
concentrated predators, human harvest activity, competition between migrants
and residents, and recruitment variability exactly as established. No accepted
record is reopened or amended by this decision.

## Author approval

After the A — Two-speed community, B — Suppressed residents, and C —
Resident-dominant lake cards were presented with A recommended, the Author
selected **"C — Resident-dominant lake"** on 2026-08-17. Because the displayed
card's wording described migrants as marginal, which would have contradicted
`WLD-ECOLOGY-006`, the scoping was returned to the Author, who confirmed
**"Residents structure, pulse still major"** on the same date. That confirmed
scoping is the accepted rule.

## Implications

- The lake is populated year-round, so winter and lean-season scenes have a
  living lake rather than an empty one.
- The long-open bird-like fisher assignment is closed, giving Lineage III's
  two-winged silhouette its first established ecological role.
- Shore hunting connects the lake to the terrestrial lineages.
- Winter, not summer competition, becomes the resident community's binding
  constraint, which fits the established freeze pattern.
- The migrant pulse keeps its full established significance as a
  concentrated seasonal subsidy.
- No species, human practice, harvest rate, or System mechanic is created.

## Reopening conditions

Reopen if structural resident dominance cannot be reconciled with later
quantitative pulse or productivity modelling, if a concrete contradiction
with an accepted record emerges, or on explicit Author request.
