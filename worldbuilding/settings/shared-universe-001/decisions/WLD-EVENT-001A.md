# WLD-EVENT-001A — Story-Start Standing Pressures

## Question decided

Which qualitative configuration of standing, unresolved pressures shapes the
settlement and its region at story start — which pressure classes exist, what
public salience each carries, how they couple, and through which evidence
classes the returning peoples are known locally — without establishing any
plot event, occurred incident, named party, timeline, or resolution?

## Selected rule

Use the Author-amended custom option — RIVALRY-FORWARD. Five standing
pressure classes are simultaneously live at story start and none is resolved:

1. The approaching first succession, its rules incompletely settled per
   WLD-CULTURE-002's designed succession ambiguity, which that record alone
   defines.
2. Returning non-human peoples, known at the settlement only through
   reported-only accounts, transient signs attested at a regional distance,
   and imported objects of uncertain provenance per WLD-ECOLOGY-005's
   provenance classes; no direct presence or contact at or near the
   settlement.
3. Seasonal ecological strain grounded solely in accepted mechanisms —
   corridor disruption and crop exposure per WLD-ECOLOGY-004, pulse-mosaic
   variability per WLD-ECOLOGY-005, and lake seasonal variation within
   WLD-ECOLOGY-006's bounds — with the harvest-institution boundary intact.
4. Cross-lake tensions and trade rivalry with the imperial-remnant industrial
   settlement (WLD-LOCATION-005), which audibly organises settlement politics
   and colours the succession question. Qualitative forms may include
   contested exchange terms, intermittent embargo-like interruptions, and
   competitive claims of standing — all standing tensions, with no occurred
   incident, negotiation, or conflict event, and no future war,
   control-assertion, or escalation claim.
5. Returning dangerous wildlife as a felt pressure class: recovering
   megafauna per the accepted corridor and pulse records, plus qualitative
   predator and raider classes — large pack predators, large solitary
   predators, uncanny solitary humanoid-reading predators, and raiding bands
   of the pest-like pack species whose collapse-era spread WLD-HISTORY-001
   records. The parked sapient pack species stays parked.

Returning-peoples reports and seasonal strain build in the background under
the rivalry-forward configuration. Every claim is a standing state or
tendency, never an occurred event.

## Author approval

On 2026-08-17, through the explicit multiple-choice review of the displayed
options, the Author selected an amended custom option rather than A, B, or C:
rivalry-forward salience with five standing pressure classes, adding the
cross-lake trade rivalry and a returning-dangerous-wildlife felt pressure
class to the displayed set. The Author described the wildlife classes in
author-facing shorthand as 'hags, goblins, mega-fauna, wolves, bears (our
world equivalents)'; those Earth-creature words are shorthand only, never
in-world taxonomy, and establish no species identity, biology, or local
presence.

## Rationale

The amended configuration keeps every settled reality of the foundation
capture live at story start while giving the setting the standing cross-lake
rivalry the plot intent staged outside canon requires as background. It does
so without scheduling any event, resolving any pressure, developing the
parked species, or pre-deciding the succession, and it preserves the
background build of the remaining pressures for later story use.

## Alternatives retained

- A — BRAIDED-THREE: the three displayed pressure classes all live with no
  dominant salience and contested ranking; not selected.
- B — SUCCESSION-FORWARD: succession politics audible while the other
  displayed pressures build quietly; not selected.
- C — CONTACT-FORWARD: returning-peoples reports dominant with a flagged
  near-contact evidence class and succession publicly deferred; not selected.
- D — RIVALRY-FORWARD (Author-amended custom option): five pressure classes
  including cross-lake trade rivalry and returning dangerous wildlife, with
  the rivalry audibly organising politics; selected.

The exact displayed option language is preserved in the work-local capture.
Options A, B, and C remain nonbinding evidence.

  the settlement and its region, and none is resolved.

  non-transfer rules govern all such evidence.

  arising only through the accepted WLD-ECOLOGY-004/-005/-006 mechanisms,

  audibly organise settlement politics and colour the succession question.

  claim is created; series plot intent stays staged outside setting canon.
- Returning dangerous wildlife is a felt pressure class: recovering megafauna
  per the accepted corridor and pulse records plus the four qualitative
  predator and raider classes, with all species identity, taxonomy, biology,
  and local presence detail open and no specific creature established as
  present near the settlement.
- The pest-like pack species remains parked and gains no biology, culture,
  language, ability, organisation, or presence development beyond
  WLD-HISTORY-001's recorded collapse-era spread.
- Returning-peoples reports and seasonal strain build in the background under
  the rivalry-forward configuration.
- Every claim is a standing state or tendency, never an occurred event, and
  the configuration is author-side truth known to characters only through
  available sources.
- No pressure has a System cause; System-facing interpretations are belief,
  and no System event, mechanic, or CAL0 change is created.

## Story and character impact

- Scenes, politics, and council business can draw on five standing pressures
  from the first chapter, while every actual incident, negotiation, contact,
  sighting, shortage event, and escalation requires its own evidence.
- The rivalry-forward salience lets cross-lake politics colour succession
  argument without deciding either question.
- Background pressures can surface unevenly by household, profession, and
  occasion without any fixed ranking or schedule.
- Characters know any pressure only through available sources; reports and
  rumours can be incomplete or wrong.
