# Initial-Region Physical Scaffold

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative physical geography,
hydrology, geomorphic sequence, coastal character, climate regime, and
winter/summer pattern of Project Hearthway's initial region.

## Provisional claims

1. Wet rocky uplands lie broadly west and northwest of a sheltered central lake
   basin. Beyond an eastern or southeastern outlet gap, downstream lower marsh
   country leads toward a broken drowned coast.
2. The basin lake is freshwater and through-flowing. Multiple rivers and streams
   feed it, and an outlet river drains it toward the sea through a narrow,
   naturally defensible gap.
3. The landscape preserves successive geomorphic phases: an ancient river basin
   was later reshaped by glaciation.
4. Ancient inundation formed the drowned coast. Its broad regional outline is
   now stable, while storms can alter local channels and bars or remove small landforms.
5. Drowned valleys, tidal marshes, peat islands, sandbars, wooded islands, and
   narrow sea passages occur within the coastal mosaic.
6. The region has a transitional maritime–continental climate. Western
   windward terrain supports a major alien temperate-rainforest biome, with
   scattered damp refugia also present within the basin.
7. Upland snow accumulates reliably and feeds a strong spring melt. Basin snow
   comes most years but can thaw and return several times within the same winter.
8. Sheltered lake bays, wetlands, and slow river reaches freeze regularly.
   Continuous surface ice across the lake's deeper central waters occurs only
   in unusually severe winters.
9. Sudden thaws can produce floods, moving ice, and dangerous river crossings.
10. Summers are normally mild to warm, with long daylight and regular rain.
    Occasional hotter dry spells can last several weeks; severe drought is uncommon.
11. The western rainforest and damp ravines usually remain moist during those
    spells, while basin open ground, habitat edges, and peaty margins can become
    dry enough to support surface fire.

## Boundaries

- The Britain, Doggerland, and Scandinavia comparison is author-facing
  inspiration only. It establishes no Earth location, chronology, culture,
  species, language, or hidden connection.
- “Temperate rainforest” describes a climate-supported vegetation structure;
  its alien species, ancestry, anatomy, extent, productivity, in-world name, and
  human use remain open.
- Within this record, “defensible” describes terrain potential only.
  `WLD-LOCATION-001` later establishes a bounded initial ridge-spur settlement
  working landscape and combined spring–well–cistern water system. Exact wall
  form, ownership, road dimensions, and hydrogeology remain outside this record.
- Exact map scale, place names, distances, travel times, elevations, slopes,
  contours, river courses, lake form, quantitative outlet profile, and coast
  shape remain open. `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002` later establishes only the outlet's
  qualitative mixed-reach feature sequence.
- Exact geological dates, rock types, glacial sequence, soil map, mineral
  resources, inundation cause, former shorelines, and cultural consequences
  remain open.
- Exact lake depth, volume, mixing, chemistry, water levels, freeze duration,
  inflow count and discharge, outlet capacity, head of tide, freshwater tidal
  influence, salt-front position, and storm-surge reach remain open. The lake
  remains freshwater.
- The lower basin floor remains distinct from downstream lower marsh and
  coastal country. This record does not move the existing crown corridor through
  the outlet gap or to the coast.
- Central-lake freezing means continuous surface ice, not a frozen water column.
- Exact temperatures, rainfall, snowfall, winds, storm frequency, drought,
  fire, calendar, and day length remain open beyond the qualitative pattern above.
- `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` later establishes broad qualitative lake productivity and
  two seasonal sea–lake migrant relationships, while `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` later
  selects one organism-side outlet-passage choreography for the family-care
  migrant. `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002` later establishes a narrow bedrock throat and a
  downstream mixed reach containing irregular boulder steps, side shelves, and
  holding pockets. That qualitative sequence is only a candidate physical
  basis for passage attempts: exact feature dimensions and continuity,
  hydraulics, seasonal water states, salinity transition, route capacity,
  organism compatibility, passability, and passage success remain open. Exact
  habitat distribution, quantitative productivity, species ranges, other
  transit modes, population scale and timing, other migrations, trade,
  imports, settlements beyond `WLD-LOCATION-001`'s bounded initial-settlement
  landscape, cultures, histories, current events, and character knowledge also
  remain open.
- This record establishes no magical climate cause, ecological reset, ambient
  spawning, ancient doorway effect, or offworld connection.
