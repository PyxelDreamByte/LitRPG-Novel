# Initial Outlet Mixed-Reach Sequence

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative physical sequence of
Project Hearthway's initial basin outlet from its bedrock-controlled throat
into the connected downstream mixed reach.

## Provisional claims

1. A narrow bedrock-controlled throat opens downstream into one connected
   mixed reach.
2. The mixed reach contains irregular boulder steps, lateral or side shelves,
   and local holding pockets.
3. The reach is heterogeneous rather than one uniform channel or regular
   feature chain.
4. Boulder steps and side shelves are candidate, state-dependent bed-or-margin
   contact surfaces against which WLD-ECOLOGY-010 passage can be tested.
5. Holding pockets are local channel recesses or pools that may be less exposed
   to the main flow under some conditions and can be tested as potentially
   lower-velocity locations.
6. Coherent migration corridor means only that the throat and mixed reach
   belong to one connected physical system and supply a candidate route
   architecture for later feasibility work.

## Boundaries

- This record is a forward extension of WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001's narrow,
  naturally defensible outlet gap. It does not alter the freshwater
  through-flow lake, downstream marsh country, drowned coast, regional arrangement, or
  broader river–glacial history.
- Bedrock-controlled establishes broad substrate control at the throat, not an
  exact rock type, geological origin, age, provenance, weathering, jointing,
  mineral value, Mana property, formation process, or stability.
- Narrow and opens downstream are qualitative. Exact throat and reach length,
  width, depth, elevation, gradient, cross-section, orientation, distance, map
  position, and travel time remain open.
- Boulder steps are irregular channel-bed transitions, not a regular
  staircase, constructed weir, fish ladder, continuous waterfall, dry route,
  or guarantee of a braceable or climbable surface. Exact boulder source,
  material, number, dimensions, spacing, movement, and stability remain open.
- Side shelves are lateral channel-form surfaces. Their material, number,
  dimensions, slope, height, spacing, continuity, exposure, inundation,
  vegetation, and use remain open. They are not roads, quays, terraces,
  fortifications, or guaranteed support.
- Holding pockets are local channel recesses or pools. They may be less exposed
  to the main flow under some conditions, but no pocket is guaranteed to have
  low velocity, safe water, suitable depth, adequate turnover, usable
  substrate, respiratory suitability, food, space, or freedom from debris,
  crowding, disease, or predation.
- The presence of a holding pocket does not by itself establish a refuge,
  checkpoint, assigned waiting area, safe zone, healing or recovery site, Mana
  well, Health or Stamina restore, or refill point.
- The throat precedes the downstream mixed reach. No exact number, order,
  spacing, repetition, or continuity among the boulder steps, side shelves,
  holding pockets, or any other downstream feature is established.
- Heterogeneous means that more than one qualitative channel-form class occurs
  within the connected reach. It establishes no exact hydraulic diversity,
  habitat mosaic, distribution, abundance, or ecological use.
- Coherent migration corridor means one connected physical system and
  candidate route architecture only. It establishes no continuous support or
  rest chain, guaranteed contact, guaranteed resting opportunity, route memory,
  navigation cue, seasonal window, annual use, passability, return, survival,
  or organism success.
- Feature wetting, exposure, accessibility, load-bearing suitability, and
  local flow exposure remain state dependent. No surface is guaranteed usable
  and no pocket is guaranteed less exposed to main flow at a particular time.
- Exact discharge, velocity, depth, turbulence, eddies, water level, flood,
  freeze-thaw, ice, debris, erosion, deposition, boulder movement, sediment
  transport, water chemistry, tidal reach, salinity transition, and
  storm-surge effects remain open.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-006 continues to govern the qualitative seasonal migration and
  spring-freshet relationship. WLD-ECOLOGY-009 continues to govern the
  family-care migrant's body plan and route-feasibility concern. WLD-ECOLOGY-010
  continues to govern its conditional outlet-passage choreography.
- The selected feature set can be tested against WLD-ECOLOGY-010, but actual
  body and brood fit, contact mechanics, continuity, depth use, buoyancy,
  respiratory exchange, usable energy, fatigue, injury, recovery, timing,
  crowding, capacity, passability, mortality, recruitment, and success remain

- No organism is established as inhabiting, feeding, breeding, resting,
  sheltering, migrating through, or successfully using any particular outlet
  feature. Habitat occupancy, food webs, predation, disease, competition,
  carrying capacity, and ecological recovery remain open.
- The sequence establishes no crossing, road, bridge, quay, boat route,

- An observer may directly see channel form, wetted or exposed surfaces,
