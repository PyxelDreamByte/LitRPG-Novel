# Imperial Collapse Arc and Neighbor Frame

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative shape of the previous
regional power's roughly seventy-year cascading retreat — its combined
three-phase structure, contributing pressures, collapse-era wildlife and
pest-like-race developments, and chronology anchors — together with the
bounded five-class frame through which basin communities know their present
political neighbors.

## Provisional claims

1. The previous regional power's retreat from organised presence was a
   cascading arc spanning roughly seventy years before story start, combining
   the displayed phase patterns in sequence: centre failure first, then
   oscillating partial recoveries, then a final frontier-first drawdown.
2. In the first phase, central institutions failed first, stranding frontier
   garrisons and offices that decayed in place at different rates.
3. In the second phase, central power was temporarily restored under capable
   leadership more than once before falling again, and each recovery-and-fall
   cycle stripped further function layers rather than restoring the prior
   condition.
4. In the final phase, the power carried out a staged withdrawal of its
   remaining frontier holdings, this region among them, ending organised
   imperial presence in the region.
5. Contributing pressures across the empire during the arc included wars with
   other races, internal wars, and greed and corruption within imperial
   institutions; these were multi-causal contributions, none singly
   sufficient, and their exact weighting remains open.
6. The collapse's trigger or root cause remains explicitly open; the
   cascading retreat is the established shape of the event, not an
   explanation of it.
7. During the arc, dangerous wildlife gradually increased as organised
   ecological suppression lapsed; the return spans the arc and tracks the
   combined phase sequence rather than any single event.
8. During the arc, pest-like races spread and caused chaos.
9. The spreading pest-like races are the parked sapient pack species:
   biologically violent, aggressive, territorial, and pack-like, with low
   average intelligence and somewhat more intelligent, more aggressive pack
   leaders. "Goblin" is author-facing shorthand for this species only, never
   an in-world name or taxonomy.
10. The former power was centred elsewhere, and this region was among its
    peripheral holdings.
11. Shared chronology anchors hold: the collapse arc began roughly seventy
    years before story start; the settlement founding fell 25-35 years before
    story start and after the final frontier-first drawdown phase had begun
    regionally; and the first succession approaches at story start.
12. Present political neighbors exist and are known to basin communities only
    through five bounded presence classes reusing WLD-ECOLOGY-005's
    provenance discipline adapted to societies: direct sustained contact,
    seasonal or transient contact, transported goods, resident outsiders, and
    reported-only knowledge.
13. Both human successor communities and returning non-human peoples can
    occur in any presence class; the frame does not sort peoples by species,
    and no class implies hostility, unity, or a single category of returning
    actor.
14. Non-transfer rules bind the frame: contact proves contact only at that
    time; presence-class membership is observer-relative and may differ
    between basin communities; and no class instantiates any particular
    neighbor, good, individual, or tale — the classes are development
    channels, not existing facts.

## Boundaries

- Cascading retreat, centre failure, stranded, decay in place, partial
  recovery, capable leadership, function layer, staged withdrawal, drawdown,
  frontier holding, organised ecological suppression, pest-like, spread,
  chaos, presence class, and peripheral holding are author-facing qualitative
  labels; they fix no in-world name, institution, office, rank, date finer
  than the shared anchors, measurement, map, or classification.
- The former power's name, capital, heartland, language, extent, formal
  structure, rulers, generals, officials, and map relation beyond the
  region's peripheral status remain open; its relation to the ancient
  doorways and traveller myths stays governed by the setting constitution's
  protected unknowns.
- The three contributing pressures are contributions, not a settled root
  cause; no particular war, opposing race or people, campaign, battle, front,
  faction, rebellion, treaty, corrupt office, or corruption episode is
  established, and any later cause work must remain multi-causal or be
  explicitly bounded by its own proposal.
- Centre failure establishes no particular stranded garrison, office,
  location, practice, lifespan, or fate; decay rates differed, but no
  individual pocket's history is established.
- More than once fixes no cycle count; the oscillations establish no cycle
  dates, durations, extents, restored or stripped function inventories, or
  leader identity; capable leadership names no person, dynasty, or office and
  implies no judgement about any other period.
- The final drawdown establishes no withdrawal order, route, destination,
  evacuation event, last garrison, or exact regional end date; the end of
  organised imperial presence does not establish the presence or absence of
  any remaining individual, successor community, reused work, partial
  tradition, or expertise pocket, all of which remain open for later
  development.
- The wildlife increase is qualitative history: no species, population,
  corridor geography, seasonal dates, herd scale, route-fidelity mechanism,
  attack, loss, or encounter is established, and WLD-ECOLOGY-004 remains the
  sole authority for the story-start migration pattern with all its
  declared-open items still open.
- What organised ecological suppression consisted of — methods, scale,
  institutions, and costs — remains open; its lapse follows the combined
  phase sequence without any regional date finer than the shared anchors.
- The pest-like-race spread is bounded qualitative history: no origin,
  homeland, route, range, population, number, settlement, raid, battle,
  victim, territory, or present-day distribution is established, and chaos
  selects no particular event.
- The parked sapient pack species gains no development beyond its
  identification and collapse-era spread: names, biology, reproduction,
  social learning, language, material culture, ecological role, relations
  with other peoples, the meaning of its sapience, and the
  ethical-storytelling questions recorded at parking remain parked and
  unresolved. The Earth-creature word "goblin" appears only inside explicit
  author-facing-shorthand disclaimers and never as in-world taxonomy.
- The plural pest-like races establishes no additional species; the accepted
  identification concerns the single parked pack-species concept, and any
  further pest-like people requires its own proposal.
- The founding anchor is ordinal only: the settlement founding falls within
  its accepted 25-35-year window and after the final drawdown phase had begun
  regionally; no founding date, founder, population composition, founding
  event, institution, or motivation finer than accepted records is
  established here.
- The collapse is institutional and demographic, not environmental: no
  climate change, ecological reset, or magical cause is established, and
  WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001's physical scaffold is untouched.
- Whether any accepted local feature, including WLD-LOCATION-001's
  constructed road, is of imperial origin remains open; no settlement name,
  founder identity, institution, practice, or infrastructure origin is
  claimed, and WLD-LOCATION-002's accepted travel anchors are untouched.
- The neighbor frame creates no named neighbor polity, map position, army,
  alliance, border, or current war and instantiates no particular neighbor,
  good, resident outsider, or tale; a transported good proves neither its
  maker's location, condition, nor intent; a resident outsider proves nothing
  about their society's aims, unity, or reach; a report proves nothing about
  existence, location, or present condition.
- The arc and the frame are author-side historical truth and a
  records/access/belief-layer structure: no character automatically knows any
  phase, cause, neighbor, or presence-class fact, and basin communities may
  narrate, date, and mythologize the collapse and their neighbors wrongly,
  partially, or rivalrously without touching natural law.
- No System event caused, accompanied, or ended the collapse; no mechanic,
  spawning, reset, safe zone, or System-granted institutional power is
  implied; ordinary finite matter, energy, labour, manpower, upkeep, and
  knowledge transmission remain causal.
- No protagonist, character, plot event, or future-timeline claim is
  established; succession politics, contested attitudes to imperial
  inheritance, and incomplete neighbor knowledge are standing pressures at
  story start, not occurred events.
- This record adopts no WLD-SOUL, WLD-PRENATAL, or other project default and
  changes no CAL0 entry.
