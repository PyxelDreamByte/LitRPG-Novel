# Lifecycle Threshold-Witness Rites

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative pattern-level rite architecture for
the four lifecycle rite classes — birth, coming of age, marriage and
relationships, death — across Project Hearthway's three regional cultures:
what those rites fundamentally do, and what they conclusively do not do.

## Provisional claims

1. In all three cultures — the original culture, the imperial-remnant
   culture, and the hybrid settlement — every lifecycle rite is a witnessed
   crossing: the community formally witnesses a passage and thereby
   socially ratifies the new state. Each culture times and staffs its rites
   by its own custom, none fixed here.
2. All three cultures possess all four rite classes, and each is internally
   varied in keeping them.
3. Birth rites witness acceptance into a household: the crossing is into
   the hearth, not into existence.
4. Coming-of-age rites witness the culturally interpreted signs of ordinary
   awakening. They reveal, celebrate, authenticate, teach, and support; they
   never create, suppress, gate, delay, or own natural access, and awakening
   timing follows natural law identically for everyone regardless of
   ceremony, culture, or omission of ceremony.
5. Marriage rites witness a bond of reciprocal obligation between persons
   and households.
6. Death rites witness the community's recognition of a death and the
   reallocation of the dead person's social obligations among the living.
   They establish nothing about what actually happens to Souls; afterlife,
   reincarnation, and ancestor content is contested belief.
7. Rites are socially binding only. No rite triggers, gates, delays,
   guarantees, or owns any System event, awakening included, and no rite
   creates, transfers, or extinguishes assembly standing, property, or
   tenure — every reallocation, marriage's inherited-duty obligation class
   included, is social obligation only.
8. Rite failure or omission carries social consequence only.
9. Birth customs aimed at the unborn are beliefs and practices with no
   mechanical effect on the child's progression, and no ordinary person has
   prenatal System access, practice, or continuity.
10. Appraisal-adjacent customs, including any marriage-negotiation
    disclosure, route only through consent, voluntary disclosure, and
    narrow authenticated proof — custom, never guarantee. Access to
    authenticated proof is uneven and often unavailable, so such customs
    are aspirational, elite, or specialist-mediated in distributions left
    open.
11. Marriage and relationship forms are an open qualitative field in every
    culture: household-formation logics and obligation classes — dwelling,
    labour, care, inherited duties — vary by culture, strand, and
    household, and no marriage-form norm is established.
12. Whatever rite practice the hybrid settlement keeps, it echoes no office
    and claims no governance power.

## Boundaries

- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-CULTURE-001's open awakening,
  coming-of-age, and guardianship-custom space: the four rite classes exist
  in all three cultures under the witnessed-crossing architecture. All
  observance content remains open.
- This record is a forward extension filling that space, and apart from the
  single declared closure stated above, every open boundary of every record
  this one touches stays open.
- Witness, crossing, acceptance, attestation, record, standing, and every
  other label in this record are author-facing qualitative descriptions.
  They fix no in-world name, language, rite script, liturgy, calendar,
  office, law, institution, measurement, proportion, or performance
  guarantee.
- Per-culture rite expression beyond pattern level remains open. The
  per-culture emphases sketched in the source proposal — whether a culture
  stresses who witnesses or what is recorded — are illustration only and
  are not established: WLD-CULTURE-003, WLD-CULTURE-004, and WLD-CULTURE-005
  each remain free to give their own culture a different rite logic from its
  own societal structure and faith orientation.
- Coming-of-age ceremony wraps the natural awakening stage exactly as CAL0
  WLD1.0D licenses and never modifies it. An unwitnessed child still
  awakens; no age, band, timing rule, or eligibility by category is fixed
  (CAL0 WLD1.1D, WLD2.2D); and the staged awakening topology is referenced,
  not extended.
- Death rites establish belief only. Nothing about Soul destination,
  survival, dissolution, return, or ancestor presence is established or
  implied; the System's own death reporting stays bounded and
  uncertainty-declared (CAL0 RES0.10D); and whether any funeral practice has
  real causal effect beyond symbol is deliberately left unclaimed rather
  than denied.
- Birth blessings, maternal observances, and protective customs aimed at the
  unborn are belief with no mechanical effect. The series contract's adopted
  WLD-PRENATAL-001A guardrail is respected as a read-only constraint —
  ordinary people have no persistent prenatal continuity, System access, or
  directed prenatal practice — and this record adopts no project default
  into the setting and names no reincarnate or exceptional individual. This
  categorical closure is distinct from the deliberate openness left to
  funeral practice under CAL0 RES0.10D and does not share it.
- Appraisal-adjacent marriage customs are voluntary disclosure and narrow
  authenticated proof only (CAL0 UI1.0D, UI1.2D). No readout is guaranteed,
  no appraisal is omniscient, nobody is screened for latent capacity, and
  access is uneven under the layered System practice WLD-CULTURE-001
  establishes.
- No rite creates, transfers, or extinguishes assembly standing, property,
  or tenure. WLD-CULTURE-002's contested margins over dependents, lodgers,
  and the not-yet-householded, its untouched property and tenure space, and
  every designed succession ambiguity stay open and unanswered, and no rite
  practice acquires an office or governance power (CAL0 SOC1.2D).
- Marriage and relationship forms stay an open qualitative field: obligation
  classes only, no numeric norm, no household-size or partner-count rule,
  and plural versus monogamous variety across the three cultures stays
  explicitly varied and open.
- WLD-CULTURE-003, WLD-CULTURE-004, and WLD-CULTURE-005 own their own
  cultures' societal structure, faith orientation, and rite logic, and
  WLD-CULTURE-005 alone owns the settlement's founding frame. This record
  neither restates nor alters any of it, establishes no cultural precedence,
  suppression, or completed blending at the settlement, and adds nothing to
  WLD-HISTORY-001's account of the empire's historical regional
  administration.
- WLD-CULTURE-007 stays untouched: recorded and attested claims fix no
  script, literacy level, speech variety, naming language, or record
  medium — records may be written, tallied, or memorised in whatever forms
  that record establishes — and all in-world names, vocabularies,
  phonologies, and script content remain protected unknowns.
- WLD-CULTURE-008 stays untouched: marriage obligations and death-rite
  reallocations name obligation classes only, fixing and excluding no
  exchange medium, valuation, price, dowry value, market, or monetary
  custom.
- Rites deepen WLD-CULTURE-001's strand-braid as strand-distinct household
  customs, never merging or ranking strands. Every strand class stays an
  open protagonist origin, no protagonist fact is established, and within
  the settlement braid rite timing is household and strand custom: the
  festival calendar, dates, and all observance content stay open, this
  record ties no rite occasion to a festival season, and whether any does
  remains open.
- WLD-HISTORY-001 remains the sole authority for the chronology.
  Imperial-remnant rite fragments are believed inheritances of unestablished
  accuracy, prior custom is imported and uneven memory, and no imperial-era
  doctrine, phase, date, or event is established.
- WLD-LOCATION-004's no-intact-archives ceiling stands: record customs
  assume no intact archive or registry institution and no record accuracy,
  and whatever partial, disputed, or fallible imperial documents
  WLD-CULTURE-004 establishes stand untouched. WLD-LOCATION-005's outlet
  settlement keeps its self-described primarily imperial-remnant character,
  internally varied, with minority presence open.
- WLD-EVENT-001 continues to own story-start pressures. No wedding, funeral,
  naming, or ceremony is an occurred event; contested standing from disputed
  or omitted rites, witness-availability politics, and record disputes are
  standing pressures only; and no incident, dispute, or outcome is
  established.
- No rite ties to any harvest practice, organism use, foodway, material, or
  shore institution. WLD-ECOLOGY-004, WLD-ECOLOGY-005, and WLD-ECOLOGY-006
  stand untouched, including the spring-pulse mortality-channel-only
  boundary and the provenance discipline, and no crop, fibre, fabric, or
  species is established — material classes only.
- No quantitative claim of any kind is made: no age, count, proportion,
  demography, price, measurement, date, or calendar. WLD-RARITY-001D's
  deferral of universal proportions stands.
- The architecture is author-side cultural truth as a pattern. A character
  knows another culture's, strand's, or household's rite customs only
  through an available, fallible source, and no character surveys the whole
  pattern.
- No new System mechanic, readout, or CAL0 change is created; no
  WLD-SOUL-001A or other project default is adopted; no Soul scalar or
  prevalence figure is used; the parked sapient pack species remains parked
  and the returning non-human peoples remain undeveloped; and no
  protagonist, character, plot, chapter, or story-event claim is made.
