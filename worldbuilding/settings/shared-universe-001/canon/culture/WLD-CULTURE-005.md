# Settlement Founding-Fusion Blend Logic

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative blend logic by which
Project Hearthway's ridge-spur hillfort settlement holds its two parent
inheritances through the accepted strand-braid — the remembered
founding-era fusion disposition, its status and virtue fields, its
syncretic non-lifecycle observance and its composite dress — together
with the settlement's founding frame: the founder's origin, his early
admission of imperial refugees, and the host-and-member relations that
follow at the settlement today.

## Provisional claims

1. The hybrid settlement culture expresses through WLD-CULTURE-001's
   strand-braid. This blend logic describes how the braid holds its two
   parent inheritances; it deepens the accepted fabric and replaces
   nothing.
2. The settlement's founding-era disposition is remembered as fusion —
   inherited forms worked into settlement-specific composites. It is a
   remembered pattern, variously and disputably held, with no founding
   occasion, decision, deliberation, or event fixed.
3. The settlement's founder was a native of the original culture. This is
   a declared narrow closure of the founder-origin question left open in
   WLD-CULTURE-002's founder-and-dynasty space.
4. In the early founding days the founder admitted imperial refugees into
   the fold to help build the settlement.
5. Under the founder's leadership there has been unity and mutual respect
   between the two inheritances — a qualitative settlement-relations
   condition, not unanimity and not the absence of friction.
6. Cultural hybridisation between the inheritances is early-stage and
   ongoing, not complete.
7. Consequently the original culture is the settlement's host culture and
   the imperial-descended are guests-turned-members, and the original
   culture is not suppressed at the settlement. This is a present
   settlement-relations claim only and does not alter WLD-HISTORY-001's
   account of the withdrawn power's historical regional administration.
8. The charter-oath compact is remembered as the model case of the
   fusion: an oath sworn in what residents call the original manner,
   joined to witnessed, fallibly kept records in what they call the
   imperial manner (WLD-CULTURE-002; no oath text or written charter
   exists, and the memory of the founding pairing is itself a cultural
   reading).
9. Standing accrues to households and persons seen to embody the joined
   way, while purists of either inheritance keep real but narrower
   prestige inside their own circles. The young composite identity is
   conscious and contested, claimed by some residents and disputed by
   others.
10. Under this blend the compact's designed succession ambiguities read
    as a question of re-performance — whether a successor must re-enact
    the remembered fusion or may lean on one inheritance's form —
    sharpening the designed ambiguity and resolving nothing.
11. The virtue field organises around joining. Imperial-attributed esteem
    for the deliberate, designed act and original-attributed esteem for
    the binding spoken word reinforce each other in the fusion ideal of
    the sworn-together work. Both conflict with each parent register's
    fidelity virtue: what a purist honours as keeping the received form
    whole, the fusion register suspects as clinging, and the purists
    return the suspicion, holding the composites shallow. Neither side is
    simply right, and both positions are alive inside every strand. The
    specific parent esteems named here are option-local illustration only,
    presupposing no sibling option; the accepted parent frames
    WLD-CULTURE-003 and WLD-CULTURE-004 supply their content.
12. The settlement virtue this blend carries is the joined work: a thing
    — an agreement, a household, a craft piece — made properly of both
    inheritances and holding. Its shame is unravelling: publicly pulling
    a shared thing apart into its parent halves, or claiming one
    inheritance's precedence at a common table. Either may be newly minted
    here or sharpened and re-anchored from a parent register that already
    prizes made-and-kept work, and neither is claimed as novel against the
    accepted parent registers.
13. Faith blends by syncretism as belief practice: deliberately joined
    observance composites hold the common occasions, while devout
    traditionalists of both inheritances keep older forms in household
    privacy — coexistence beneath the composite, confirming nothing.
14. Dress shows a recognisable settlement-specific composite that
    outsiders read as the hillfort's own rather than as either parent;
    marked parent dress survives for household and purist occasions.
15. Both parent inheritances are genuinely present, each internally
    varied, and both are honoured as ancestors of the fusion; neither is
    despised by default, though purists of each resent the other's share,
    and every accepted attitude position exists somewhere in the
    settlement.
16. Hillfort-side perception only: the settlement reads the outlet
    settlement as the unblended sibling — what its own remembered
    disposition did not become — at once a foil for settlement pride and
    an object of quiet admiration for unbroken imperial continuity, and
    it believes the outlet reads it in turn as culturally mixed. The
    standing rivalry (WLD-EVENT-001) gives the perception its competitive
    edge as claims of standing, tendencies only. Neither perception
    measures actual composition, and this record establishes no
    outlet-side fact.
17. Generational texture: founders remember the fusion as choice and
    labour and hold it a little anxiously; the walls-born wear it as
    plain identity, more confidently than its makers; and a visible few
    reach back past the fusion for purer parent forms, as fashion or
    protest, unsettling founders who remember why the joining was made.
18. "Original-derived" and "imperial-derived" are attributions settlement
    people make — cultural recognition, not accurate history, mirroring
    WLD-LOCATION-004's discipline. A custom believed imperial may be
    original, and the reverse.
19. No blend position, register, attitude, or stance is the protagonist's
    by implication; every strand class and every stance remains open.
20. All System-practice implications of this record remain cultural
    layers over unchanged natural law.

## Boundaries

- This record is a forward deepening of WLD-CULTURE-001's declared-open
  cultural space. It closes only the blend pattern of that record's open
  dress, faith-pattern, and virtue space; all observance, dress, virtue,
  and festival content remains open, the strand-braid is not replaced or
  competed with, and every strand's distinct household traditions —
  imperial-derived ones included — persist untouched, unranked, and
  unflattened.
- Declared narrow closure of the founder-origin question: WLD-CULTURE-002
  establishes the founder, his binding founding oath, and his dynasty
  without fixing the founder's own origin, and this record closes that
  one question — the founder was a native of the original culture.
  Nothing else closes. The dynasty's later composition, the origins of
  every office-holder in the ruling seat, sworn deputy, adjudicator,
  burden-consent, and oath-witness classes, and every one of the
  compact's designed first-succession ambiguity classes remain untouched
  and explicitly still open, as does everything else WLD-CULTURE-002
  leaves open.
- WLD-CULTURE-002 is cited, never restated. No compact term, office,
  text, procedure, representation rule, or ambiguity resolution is added
  here; the resident reading of the compact as the model fusion case is
  belief about the compact, not a compact term; and the re-performance
  reading sharpens the designed succession pressure without establishing
  any process, candidate, contender, faction, or outcome.
- Guests-turned-members and host culture are qualitative
  settlement-relations descriptions. They fix no legal status, tenure,
  right, admission rule, membership procedure, precedence order, or
  category, and WLD-CULTURE-002's hearth-based assembly standing —
  standing follows the household hearth regardless of origin — is cited
  unchanged, neither narrowed, ranked, nor qualified by them.
  WLD-CULTURE-001's accepted claim that no strand dissolves and no strand
  dominates stands unchanged: host culture is a culture-level relations
  description, and it ranks, displaces, or subordinates no strand.
- The host-culture claim is present settlement relations only. It does
  not alter, soften, or contradict WLD-HISTORY-001's account of the
  withdrawn power's historical regional administration, adds no
  historical phase, cause, policy, date, or event, and establishes
  nothing about any other community's cultural relations.
- The founder frame is standing fact, not a narrated founding scene. No
  founding occasion, decision, deliberation, negotiation, arrival,
  admission event, refugee count or proportion, founder name, biography,
  character, household, or motive is established, and WLD-HISTORY-001
  remains the sole authority for the founding range and the wider
  chronology, with no date finer than its accepted anchors fixed here. The
  frame consumes WLD-HISTORY-001's disclaimed founding-composition space
  and extends WLD-LOCATION-001's declared-open founder space, which
  WLD-CULTURE-002 already fills in part: the founder's origin and his early
  admission of imperial refugees stand as facts, with no date, count,
  proportion, event, or chronology change, and neither record requires a
  boundary edit.
- Unity and mutual respect is a qualitative condition, not unanimity, not
  the absence of friction, and not a guarantee. Purist resentment,
  boundary suspicion, and generational tension are established as
  standing pressures inside it, and WLD-CULTURE-001's accepted
  imperial-inheritance attitude spectrum stands undisturbed and
  unnarrowed.
- Early-stage hybridisation fixes no rate, stage count, endpoint,
  timetable, generation boundary, or completion. Whether the fusion
  deepens, stalls, drifts, or reverses remains open.
- Fusion, joining, composite, purist, register, host, guest, member,
  standing, virtue, shame, hillfort, and every other label in this record
  are author-facing qualitative descriptions. They fix no in-world name,
  language, classification, ritual content, institution, office, law,
  measurement, proportion, or performance guarantee, and every in-world
  name, self-designation, and linguistic logic remains a protected
  unknown.
