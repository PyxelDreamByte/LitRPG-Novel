# Founder Charter-Oath Compact Structure

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative balance mechanism of the
standing founder-council compact governing Project Hearthway's initial
ridge-spur settlement: the charter-oath assembly structure, its office
classes, its consent and adjudication powers, its representation root, its
expert-testimony channel, and its known ambiguity classes.

## Provisional claims

1. A standing founder-council compact governs the settlement. It rests on a
   binding founding oath the founder swore in the founding era, within the
   accepted 25-35-years-ago founding range.
2. The oath defines the dynasty's duties — defence, outward dealings, and
   upholding the compact — and its limits: no burden without consent, no
   seizure of households' subsistence, and no altering the compact alone.
3. The council is chosen by an assembly of the settlement's households and
   holds two powers: it adjudicates disputes over whether the oath has been
   kept, and its consent is required for communal burdens.
4. Both sides hold real, actually exercised authority. The dynasty's
   authority is real and broad within the oath's bounds; the council's is
   real because it judges those bounds and holds consent. Neither side is
   merely ceremonial.
5. Dynasty-side office classes: the oath-bound ruling seat and a sworn
   deputy class under the same oath.
6. Council-assembly-side office classes: an adjudicator class hearing oath
   disputes; a burden-consent class conveying or refusing assembly consent;
   and an oath-witness class keeping the compact's memory and records, which
   are fallible, incomplete, and disputable.
7. Refugee and newcomer representation enters at the assembly root: standing
   follows the household hearth regardless of origin, so newcomer households
   vote once established, with contested margins over dependents, lodgers,
   and the not-yet-householded.
8. Craft, harvest, defence, and System-study expertise enters as sworn
   testimony in adjudication and burden deliberation. Harvest
   knowledge-holders exist and hold that recognised testimonial standing.
   System-study testimony interprets accessible signs, never a guaranteed
   readout.
9. The compact is a human institution: customary, fallibly recorded, and
   socially enforced. No office, title, or compact term carries any
   System-granted power or enforcement.
10. The ageing founder still rules at story start, and the settlement's
    first succession under the compact is approaching.
11. The compact's known ambiguity classes: whether the founding oath binds
    heirs automatically, only upon re-swearing, or lapses with the founder —
    it was sworn by a living founder who has never needed replacing; whether
    the assembly or the council receives a successor's oath; which disputed
    recollections of the oath's terms control when witnesses disagree; and
    how adjudication proceeds if the seat itself is the disputed party
    during a succession.
12. The first-succession provisions are incompletely specified or contested
    in some respect — known at story start, deliberately unresolved here.
    Emergency authority, wherever placed, is grounded in the accepted
    ordinary crisis classes, never in System events.
