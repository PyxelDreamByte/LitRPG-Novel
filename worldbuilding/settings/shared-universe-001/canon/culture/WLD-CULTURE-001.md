# Settlement Strand-Braid Cultural Fabric

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative cultural-fabric and
System-practice-variation pattern for Project Hearthway's mixed refuge
settlement.

## Provisional claims

1. Several persistent origin strands coexist in the settlement, keeping
   distinct household-level traditions — foodways, teaching lines,
   memory-keeping, work customs — while sharing negotiated common
   institutions for work, defence, and festivals. No strand dissolves and no
   strand dominates.
2. The strand classes include, non-exhaustively: locally rooted pre-collapse
   families; displaced arrivals from failed districts; ex-imperial
   functionary or garrison descendants; and itinerant-craft or trade
   lineages. Households may straddle classes by marriage or fostering.
3. Routine cross-strand crewing exists for lake-road haulage within
   WLD-LOCATION-002's fair-weather bands and for corridor-season watches and
   closures under WLD-ECOLOGY-004. In surge seasons mixed crews are the
   negotiated, deliberate compact-era custom, not an arithmetic necessity.
4. Settlement festivals keyed to the seasonal rhythms are common
   institutions with distinguishable strand contributions.
5. Views of the imperial inheritance form a lived spectrum —
   salvage-pragmatism, nostalgia, resentment, taboo — distributed across and
   within strands, with strand-level leanings but no single settlement
   attitude and no strand unanimity.
6. System practice varies by class of use — everyday pragmatic use, hoarded
   craft advantage, specialist study, and folk belief and misinterpretation —
   cross-cutting the strands.
7. No strand, cluster, lineage, or specialist holds exclusive or complete
   System truth. Every System-practice claim in this record is cultural —
   records, access, belief, interpretation, presentation — and none alters
   natural System behaviour.
8. The settlement is and remains a mixed refuge community of 1,200–2,000
   under the founder's dynasty and council compact, with the first
   succession approaching (capture-settled; restated, not re-litigated).
9. Literacy and historical memory exist in the settlement but are uneven —
   across strands, households, generations, and professions — consistent
   with the capture's dark-age-not-absence framing.
10. Every strand class remains open as a possible protagonist origin.

## Boundaries

- This record is a forward culture extension filling WLD-LOCATION-001's
  declared culture-and-institution opening. It does not alter the accepted
  geography, ecology, location, or travel records, and every open boundary
  of every record it touches stays open.
- Strand, braid, class, crew, watch, festival, lore, and every other label
  in this record are author-facing qualitative descriptions. They fix no
  in-world name, language, classification, ritual content, institution,
  office, law, measurement, proportion, or performance guarantee.
- The strand classes are qualitative classes, not named cultures, and the
  class list is open-ended. No demographic percentage, strand proportion,
  household count, or settlement-interior arrangement is established, and
  WLD-RARITY-001D's deferral of universal proportions stands.
- No strand is selected as the protagonist's origin. Every strand class
  remains open for the series contract to choose later, and no protagonist
  fact of any kind is established.
- WLD-CULTURE-002 owns the founder-council compact's structure, offices,
  decision classes, representation, and succession provisions. This record
  restates the compact's existence without re-litigating it and establishes
  only that watch duty exists and is crewed across strands; command, levy
  authority, and any watch-related office or obligation remain
  WLD-CULTURE-002's territory, and no governance claim, law code, or compact
  term is fixed here.
- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-ECOLOGY-004's open practice space:
  corridor-season watch duty exists as a routine practice. Exact route
  geography, dates, rosters, warning methods, barriers, compensation, and
  emergency procedures remain open, and no field or road is identified as a
  secondary-path overlap.
- Declared qualitative closure of WLD-LOCATION-002's open customary-practice
  space: a routine cross-strand haulage crewing custom exists. Its exact
  form, schedule, convoy arrangement, toll, and authority remain open, and
  WLD-LOCATION-002 remains the sole authority for its broad fair-weather
  whole-journey bands, which this record does not divide, extend, shorten,
  or recalculate.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-006 continues to establish human exploitation of the spring
  abundance pulse as a mortality channel only. In this record the pulse
  appears solely as prose-level labour pressure; no crewing, worker role,
  technique, harvest rule, fishing institution, law, ownership, trade,
  ritual, preservation method, sustainable rate, or shore institution is
  established, and harvest institutions of every kind remain open.
- No strand, cluster, or household is assigned to any particular field,
  road, spur, zone, or water source, and no structure, worker role,
  schedule, or shore institution is placed at WLD-LOCATION-003's staging
  place or spurs.
- WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001's qualitative seasonal texture motivates the seasonal
  labour rhythms; no climate value, calendar, or map is fixed.
- Festivals are keyed to the seasonal rhythms, but no festival calendar,
  date, observance content, ritual, costume, foodway detail, craft, or
  teaching institution is established.
- The imperial-inheritance spectrum is attitude only. WLD-HISTORY-001
  establishes the qualitative collapse chronology and bounded neighbor
  frame; this record adds no historical phase, cause, date, name, or event,
  no attitude here is evidence of what actually occurred, and no imperial
  work, record, or site is identified or characterised.
- Every System-practice claim sits on the records, access, belief,
  interpretation, or presentation layer. Natural System behaviour is
  unchanged, no readout is guaranteed, no appraisal is omniscient, no
  notification is universal, and no strand's, lineage's, or specialist's
  account is privileged or complete (CAL0 UI1.0D, UI1.1D, UI1.2D).
- Hoarded craft advantage is hoarded access and interpretation, not better
  natural law; which advantages are hoarded, by whom, and their real versus
  believed efficacy remain open. Specialist study yields fallible theory,
  and folk belief may be wrong — and often is.
- No institution gains System-executed power (CAL0 SOC1.2D), no
  eligibility-by-category belief is promoted to fact (CAL0 WLD1.1D), no age-
  or level-band progression is implied (CAL0 WLD2.2D), and awakening,
  coming-of-age, and guardianship customs remain open (CAL0 WLD1.0D
  topology unapplied).
- No named culture, language, faith content, or self-designation is
  established; all in-world names and linguistic logic remain protected
  unknowns.
- Uneven literacy and historical memory establish no archive, school,
  record content, or record accuracy. WLD-ECOLOGY-005 continues to govern
  provenance: all life referenced is local, reported traveller knowledge
  proves nothing about nonlocal existence or presence, and no import,
  product, or traveller-carried organism is established.
- The fabric is author-side cultural truth as a pattern. A character knows
  another strand's customs, another household's lore, or the distribution
  of attitudes only through an available source, and no character surveys
  the whole fabric.
- Cross-strand acquaintance and obligation networks, quiet inequality from
  hoarded advantage, and plausible record disputes are standing pressures
  at story start, not occurred events. No incident, dispute, festival
  occurrence, or succession event is established.
- No new System mechanic, readout, or CAL0 change is created; no WLD-SOUL,
  WLD-PRENATAL, or other project default is adopted; and the parked sapient
  pack species remains parked, with no biology, culture, or ability
  development.
