# Initial Ridge-Spur Lake-Road Shore Endpoint Arrangement

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative upper-shore staging and
seasonal-spur arrangement for the established road between Project
Hearthway's initial ridge-spur settlement and the lake.

## Provisional claims

1. The established lake road ends at one recurring upper-shore staging place.
2. The staging place is on relatively firm ground set back from the ordinarily
   shifting wet margin.
3. Several short seasonal spurs extend from the staging place toward nearby
   shore contacts.
4. Which shore contact is usable, and therefore which spur can serve as the
   final approach, can vary with seasonal and local conditions.
5. The staging place recurs while the final shore contact can change.
6. The staging place and any individual spur can flood, erode, ice over,
   attract wildlife, or become unusable; several spurs do not guarantee access
   at a particular time.
