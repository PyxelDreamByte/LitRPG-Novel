# Initial Ridge-Spur Settlement Working Landscape

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative local-crown placement,
unequal-shoulder managed apron, near-settlement land-cover sequence, lake-road
relationship, and layered water access-and-storage arrangement for Project
Hearthway's initial ridge-spur settlement.

## Provisional claims

1. The fortified core occupies the local crown of the ridge spur already
   established by WLD-ECOLOGY-004.
2. An unequal-shoulder managed apron surrounds that crown.
3. The innermost part of the apron is a deliberately maintained
   low-vegetation warning belt.
4. Beyond the warning belt, small and incomplete contour terraces occupy some
   usable steeper upper slopes.
5. Broader fields and grazing ground occupy the gentler lower shoulder.
6. Woodland resumes beyond the managed apron and comes closer to the fortified
   core on steep or otherwise difficult sides.
7. A constructed road descends in bends toward a lake that lies beyond the
   immediate managed apron but within regular local travel and haulage reach.
8. A spring emerges within the fortified perimeter and is incorporated into
   settlement water access; wells supplement that access, and cisterns provide
   stored water.

## Boundaries

- This record is a forward local implementation of WLD-ECOLOGY-004's
  fortified ridge-spur core within WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001's initial-region
  scaffold. It does not alter the principal lower-basin crown-herd corridor,
  the lake, regional drainage, climate, coast, or wider basin arrangement.
- Local crown, unequal shoulder, inner, upper, lower, steeper, gentler,
  beyond, closer, and descends are qualitative relations. Exact map position,
  area, elevation, contours, slopes, orientation, distances, precise
  journey-time distributions, viewsheds, and relationship to any named place
  remain open.
- Fortified perimeter establishes the boundary within which the selected
  spring emerges. It does not establish wall material, design,
  dimensions, gates, towers, buildings, construction, condition, capacity,
  access, or defensive performance.
- The low inner warning belt is deliberately maintained and is distinct from
  the terraces, fields, grazing ground, and woodland beyond it. Its width,
  vegetation, management method, sightline performance, fire behaviour,
  erosion, and actual safety remain open.
- The warning belt does not make the whole managed apron uniformly bare,
  short, treeless, permanently clear, predator-free, hidden-threat-free, or
  safe.
- Small incomplete contour terraces establish a qualitative landform and
  development state on some usable steeper upper slopes. Their number,
  dimensions, retaining form, soil, drainage, stability, completion sequence,
  maintenance, crops, yields, ownership, labour, and failure remain open.
- Broader fields and grazing ground establish qualitative worked land on the
  gentler lower shoulder. Their extent, boundaries, soils, fertility, crops,
  domesticates, rotations, stocking, yields, storage, tenure, labour, and
  institutions remain open.
- Woodland resumes beyond the managed apron and can approach more closely on
  steep or difficult sides. Its taxa, structure, density, extent, succession,
  forest type, relationship to WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001's western rainforest or damp
  refugia, management, use, and hazards remain open.
- No reserve-web organism, colony, patch, fragment, substrate, lineage,
  predator, prey, domesticate, crop, pathogen, or other specific organism is
  established as present in or using any settlement zone.
- Constructed road establishes deliberate route work. Descends in bends is
  qualitative and does not fix its alignment, turn count, switchbacks,
  gradient, width, surface, drainage, bridges, retaining works, maintenance,
  vehicle compatibility, capacity, condition, or all-season usability.
- The road leads toward the lake, which is beyond the immediate managed apron
  but within regular local travel and haulage reach. WLD-LOCATION-002 later
  supplies broad fair-weather planning anchors for an unladen downhill journey
  and a laden uphill return. WLD-LOCATION-003 later supplies only one recurring
  upper-shore staging place and several short seasonal spurs to changeable
  nearby shore contacts. No exact distance, guaranteed journey time, view,
  staging placement, spur count or layout, terminal shore contact, landing,
  harbour, lake-shore settlement, intermediate route, transported good, load
  standard, vehicle, institution, or guaranteed access is established here.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-004 continues to establish that secondary crown-herd paths
  overlap some outer fields, roads, and lake approaches. This record does not
  identify any particular terrace, lower-shoulder field, road segment, or lake
  approach as one of those overlaps and guarantees no passage, encounter,
  closure, damage, or crisis.
- A spring emerges within the fortified perimeter and is incorporated into
  water access. Exact geology, aquifer,
  fracture, soil, emergence point, capture structure, count, yield, flow,
  recharge, seasonality, water quality, and reliability remain open.
- Wells supplement water access. Their number, location, depth, construction,
  lifting method, yield, recharge, drawdown, water quality, and relationship to
  the spring remain open.
- The spring and wells may share groundwater, recharge, drawdown,
  contamination, freeze-thaw effects, and drought exposure. Their coexistence
  does not establish independent sources or operational redundancy.
- Cisterns provide storage only. Their number, location, material, capacity,
  collection source, catchment, inflow, lining, cover, leakage, overflow,
  treatment, allocation, cleaning, and operational reserve remain open.
- Cistern storage does not create water or guarantee replenishment. It can
  shift access through time only within finite collection, capacity, condition,
  leakage, use, and water-quality limits.
- No spring, well, cistern, or combined arrangement is guaranteed secure,
  perennial, potable, inexhaustible, adequate for an undefined population,
  uncontaminated, freeze-proof, drought-proof, siege-proof, magical, mutually
  independent, maintained, or operational.
- Exact treatment, sanitation, drainage, wastewater handling, waste placement,
  contamination pathways, monitoring, rationing, maintenance, failure,
  emergency use, ownership, authority, and recovery remain open.
- Clearing, cultivation, grazing, road upkeep, groundwater access, water
  lifting, collection, and storage require finite land, water, soil, material,
  usable energy, labour, maintenance, and recovery where later records
  establish their methods.
- This record establishes no settlement name, population, age, founder,
  dynasty, council, succession, culture, language, law, ownership, tenure,
  labour institution, occupation, water right, road authority, practice,
  belief, conflict, current event, or plot.
- An observer may directly see land cover, slopes, a road, visible waterworks,

- No System settlement designation, safe zone, defensive modifier, farming

- This record establishes no relationship between the initial settlement and
