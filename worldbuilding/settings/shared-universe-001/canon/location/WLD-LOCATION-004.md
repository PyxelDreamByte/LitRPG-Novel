# Basin Imperial-Remnant Corridor-and-Clearance Skeleton

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative pattern for the withdrawn
power's surviving physical works in and near the initial basin: a decayed
corridor-and-clearance skeleton with bounded condition classes and salvage as
an activity class.

## Provisional claims

1. Physical remnants of the withdrawn power exist in and near the initial
   basin; present inhabitants generally recognise them as that power's work
   by workmanship, materials, and scale — cultural recognition, not accurate
   knowledge of any remnant's original function.
2. Organised ecological suppression left physical traces — cleared lines and
   ground works, qualitative kinds only; their regrowth is part of the
   basin's ordinary recovering mosaic, not a magical or System process.
3. No remnant functions as designed: up to roughly seventy years of decay,
   regrowth, frost, flood, and stripping, accumulating from each work's own
   local abandonment in the cascading retreat, have degraded every surviving
   work; partial incidental usefulness is the ceiling. No imperial work in or
   near the basin is continuously occupied since its abandonment; reuse ranges
   from intermittent reclamation to sustained re-occupation of abandoned
   works, and the second settlement's industrial village nucleus
   (`WLD-LOCATION-005`) is the principal sustained instance.
4. No remnant grants working imperial machinery, intact knowledge stores,
   functioning mechanisms, or preserved archives by default; any exception is
   a new, separately argued proposal, never an implication.
5. Salvage of remnant materials is an activity class with finite,
   diminishing, contestable value; no salvage right, ownership, law,
   institution, market, price, or named practice is established.
6. All remnants are ordinary made things under ordinary physics and CAL0: no
   safe zone, spawn point, Mana source, System structure, doorway, or
   System-facing property of any kind.
7. The withdrawn power's dominant surviving signature is a decayed linear
   skeleton lattice across the lower basin and its approaches: engineered
   route segments and suppression clearances, with ruinous waystation-type
   and watch-type shells along both, scattered drainage and ground works, and
   occasional boundary and marker traces.
8. Present condition spans four qualitative classes: most of the skeleton is
   regrown, structure shells are ruinous, a few stretches or nodes are reused
   by present communities, and some stretches are avoided for practical
   reasons.
9. Legibility is partial: the pattern reads in stretches and is lost in
   others; no complete route or clearance line is known to survive.
10. Some regrown cleared ground qualitatively coincides with ground crown
    herds and travellers move through; no stretch is identified, no
    WLD-ECOLOGY-004 corridor or secondary path is located, and clearances are
    not claimed to cause or anchor route fidelity.
11. Whether the settlement's constructed road (WLD-LOCATION-001) reuses any
    imperial-era alignment or foundation is deliberately left open.
12. Salvage from the skeleton is dispersed and low-yield: worked stone and
    fittings from many small ruins — finite, diminishing, contestable; no
    right, law, market, or institution.
13. No remnant exists at the WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002 outlet reach, at the
    WLD-LOCATION-003 staging place or spurs, or in any WLD-LOCATION-001
    settlement zone — the fortified core and its managed apron of warning
    belt, terraces, and fields-and-grazing shoulder; the constructed road
    corridor is expressly excluded from this bar and governed by claim 11.
14. The imperial-remnant industrial village nucleus of the second settlement
    is an instance of this record's reused-node class; WLD-LOCATION-005 is
    the authority for everything about that settlement, and this record
    neither locates nor describes it beyond the class relationship.

## Boundaries

- This record is a forward extension of the foundation capture's accepted
  retreat, withdrawn-suppression, and abandoned-clearance facts, not a
  retcon. It does not alter the basin frame, outlet sequence, ecological
  engines, migration relationships, settlement landscape, travel anchors, or
  shore-endpoint arrangement.
- Skeleton, route segment, suppression clearance, waystation-type,
  watch-type, marker trace, node, reuse, avoidance, and salvage are
  author-facing qualitative labels fixing no in-world name, classification,
  measurement, count, position, standard, institution, entitlement, or
  guarantee. Imperial is author-facing shorthand for the withdrawn power and
  fixes no in-world name.
- No map, distance, direction, elevation, dimension, count, engineering
  quantity, or other quantitative geography is established. Where any remnant
  lies relative to the settlement, road, lake, corridor, outlet, or any
  accepted feature remains open; the stated qualitative exclusions are the
  only relational commitments.
- Regrown, ruinous, reused, and avoided are condition classes only. No
  particular stretch, shell, or node is placed, counted, assigned a class, or
  guaranteed to persist, and class membership can change through ordinary
  processes.
- Avoidance is practical — regrowth, flooding, or wildlife making a stretch
  worse than surrounding ground — not ritual, unless a later accepted culture
  record establishes otherwise.
- Reuse means intermittent reclamation of abandoned works: travelling
  surviving firm stretches that serve current needs and quarrying shells for
  worked stone and fittings, as activity classes. No particular community,
  stretch, structure, practice, schedule, occupation, or event is
  established.
- Regrown cleared lines are ordinary WLD-ECOLOGY-005 mosaic openings, browsed
  per WLD-ECOLOGY-001. No organism, reserve-web patch, or colony is placed in
  any remnant, and no remnant habitat becomes the basin's engine.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-004's exact corridor geography and route-fidelity mechanism
  stay open. Qualitative coincidence with regrown cleared ground identifies
  no stretch, locates no corridor or secondary path, and answers neither
  question.
- WLD-LOCATION-002 remains the sole authority for its broad fair-weather
  whole-journey anchors. No remnant route segment creates a travel band,
  route, shortcut, or journey claim, and neither accepted anchor is divided,
  extended, shortened, or recalculated.
- Salvage establishes no right, ownership, law, market, price, technique,
  party, season, yield, dispute, trade, or institution. Salvage material is
  finite and diminishing; nothing refills, resets, or preserves itself.
- Decay, regrowth, frost, flood, and stripping are finite ordinary processes.
  Construction detail — materials, styles, inscriptions, readability by any
  present person — and the local collapse chronology of abandonment order and
  timing remain open per WLD-HISTORY-001.
- What each work was originally for, the accuracy of present beliefs about
  any remnant, and what records, traditions, or scholarly theories exist
  remain open. Recognition of the builders is cultural belief about origin,
  not accurate knowledge of function, and no belief becomes fact by
  repetition.
- The ancient archways or doorways are pre-imperial, separately parked, and
  constitution-protected. No remnant here is a doorway, doorway housing, or
  doorway-related work, and no claim touches their nature, builders, or
  operability.
- The parked sapient pack species remains parked and unconnected to any
  remnant, gaining no biology, culture, or ability development.
- No treasure inventory, cache, intact library, preserved archive,
  functioning mechanism, or working imperial machinery exists by default; any
  exception is a new, separately argued proposal.
- No story event, character fact, plot use, or current-event claim beyond
  standing background texture is established. Wildlife denning, ambush
  structure, salvage disputes, and unreliable regrown ground are
  implications requiring their own later records, not selected facts.
- The skeleton is author-side spatial truth. A character knows a remnant, its
  condition class, its origin, or its salvage value only through an available
  source. Familiarity does not reveal hidden voids, buried works, concealed
  wildlife, past function, or a System value.
- The cross-cite to WLD-LOCATION-005 establishes only that the second
  settlement's industrial village nucleus is an instance of the reused-node
  class. Everything else about that settlement — position, layout, community,
  practice, history, and condition — is WLD-LOCATION-005's authority alone.
- This record supplies only the basin-specific reused-works texture that
  WLD-HISTORY-001 defers to it. WLD-HISTORY-001's wider fragment-class option
  space, collapse chronology, and neighbor frame are untouched.
- Ordinary finite land, matter, energy, labour, time, carriage, maintenance,
  obstruction, and recovery remain causal where later records establish
  them.
- No safe zone, spawn point, dungeon, Mana source, System structure, readout,
  mechanic, movement effect, map revelation, free resource, guaranteed
  recovery, or CAL0 change is established. This is an ordinary setting
  extension under CAL0 WLD2.0D and WLD2.1A+D, and no WLD-SOUL, WLD-PRENATAL,
  or other project default is adopted.
