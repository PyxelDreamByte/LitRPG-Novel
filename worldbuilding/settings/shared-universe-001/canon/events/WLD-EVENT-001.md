# Story-Start Standing Pressures

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative configuration of the
standing, unresolved pressures shaping Project Hearthway's initial settlement
and its region at story start. Every claim is a standing state or tendency;
no occurred event, party, timeline, escalation, or resolution is established.

## Provisional claims

1. At story start, five standing pressure classes are simultaneously live in
   the settlement and its region, and none is resolved:
   approaching-first-succession political pressure, returning-peoples external
   pressure, seasonal ecological strain, cross-lake trade rivalry with the
   imperial-remnant industrial settlement, and returning dangerous wildlife.
2. The first succession of the founder's rule is approaching, and the rules
   that would govern it remain incompletely settled, per WLD-CULTURE-002's
   designed succession ambiguity, which that record alone defines.
3. Returning non-human peoples are a real regional phenomenon consistent with
   the displacement-and-return history WLD-HISTORY-001 records; at story start
   they are known at the settlement only through reported-only accounts,
   transient signs attested at a regional distance, and imported objects of
   uncertain provenance, per WLD-ECOLOGY-005's provenance classes, with no
   direct presence or contact at or near the settlement.
4. Seasonal ecological strain is a standing condition at story start: stores
   sit lower than customary comfort and subsistence planning is under visible
   adjustment, arising only through accepted mechanisms — corridor disruption
   and crop exposure per WLD-ECOLOGY-004, pulse-mosaic variability per
   WLD-ECOLOGY-005, and lake seasonal variation within WLD-ECOLOGY-006's
   bounds.
5. Cross-lake tensions and trade rivalry stand between the settlement and the
   imperial-remnant industrial settlement WLD-LOCATION-005 records; at story
   start this rivalry audibly organises settlement politics and colours how
   the approaching succession is argued.
6. The rivalry's qualitative forms can include contested exchange terms,
   intermittent embargo-like interruptions of exchange, and competitive claims
   of standing; each is a standing tension or tendency, and none is an
   occurred incident, negotiation, or conflict event.
7. Returning dangerous wildlife is a felt standing pressure class: recovering
   megafauna per the accepted corridor and pulse records (WLD-ECOLOGY-004,
   WLD-ECOLOGY-005, WLD-ECOLOGY-006), together with qualitative predator and
   raider classes — large pack predators, large solitary predators, uncanny
   solitary humanoid-reading predators, and raiding bands of the pest-like
   pack species whose collapse-era spread WLD-HISTORY-001 records.
8. Under the selected rivalry-forward configuration, returning-peoples reports
   and seasonal strain build in the background: real and unresolved while
   receiving less public deliberation than the rivalry at story start.
