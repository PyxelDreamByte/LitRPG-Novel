# Lineage II Two-Stage Symbiont Inheritance

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative family-level model for how Project
Hearthway's developing Lineage II crown and ribbon branches acquire, mature,
and recover the symbionts used by their fermentation lattice.

## Provisional claims

1. Before birth, each developing young receives a small compatible founder culture.
2. That culture is a viable beginning, not the complete adult community.
3. Repeated contact and shared feeding with parents and relatives build the
   diverse mature community after birth.
4. Family groups can thereby maintain symbionts adapted to local diets, and
   compatible kin or foster groups have biological importance.
5. Orphans can survive with compatible foster adults; prolonged isolation is
   dangerous, especially during development or recovery.
6. Social contact can help restore damaged communities, while the same networks and
   seasonal routes can spread digestive disease.

## Boundaries

- Reproductive containment, exact pre-birth transfer anatomy, and post-birth
  inoculation behaviours remain open.
- Microbial taxa, functions, maturation timing, compatibility limits, and local
  variation remain open.
- Specific diseases, treatments, quarantine practices, species-level
  differences, and detailed social organisation remain open.
- No System progression, reward, Skill, trait payload, specialised Mana
  ability, or Ancestry Development mechanic is established.
