# Basin-Lake Mass-Breeder Foundational Body Plan

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative family-grade body plan and ordinary
Mana-use boundary for the first, mass-breeding aquatic migrant established by
`WLD-ECOLOGY-006`.

## Provisional claims

1. The mass-breeding migrant belongs to a fully aquatic alien biological lineage.
2. Its established migratory form has a streamlined, laterally flexible trunk
   supported by springy cartilage.
3. Three pairs of fin-fans (six fin-fans) and a strong muscular tail form its
   visible locomotor
   foundation.
4. A wedge-shaped mineralised head shield forms the front of the body.
5. Its aquatic respiratory surfaces form folds that may be exposed or held in
   a more protected configuration and support active aquatic respiration.
6. Mana contributes to ordinary tissue reinforcement as part of its physiology
   rather than creating a specialised activated ability.
7. `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` continues to govern its egg-ribbon deposition, adult
   return and mortality, predation, recruitment, and population variability.

## Boundaries

- `Foundational body plan` describes a shared structural template. It does not
  establish a formal taxonomic rank, one species, invariant morphology, or the
  number and range of related organisms.
- The descriptive terms establish no fish, shark, vertebrate, gill, fin-ray,
  jaw, limb, or caudal-fin homology and no direct Earth-animal transplant.
- Fully aquatic establishes no terrestrial locomotion, nesting, air breathing,
  leaping, crawling, or overland bypass and does not define stranding tolerance.
- Compatibility with migration does not prove outlet passability, route depth,
  speed, endurance, homing, salinity immunity, or guaranteed return.
- Exact size, proportions, colour, skin, sexual variation, juvenile morphology,
  and change across life stages remain open.
- Support-lattice microstructure, segmentation, stiffness, fatigue, repair, and
  propulsion gait remain open.
- Fin-fan positions, shapes, folding, actuation, specialisation, damage tolerance,
  and exact functions remain open. The three pairs establish no Earth-fin homology.
- Tail profile, terminal structures, movement, burst performance, and sustained
  performance remain open.
- Shield composition, thickness, growth, attachment, repair, openings, sensory
  role, and ecological function remain open; no ramming, digging, feeding,
  egg-placement, or armour-immunity function is established.
- Respiratory-fold position, structure, ventilation, circulation, exchange
  capacity, protection, and life-stage variation remain open.
- `WLD-ECOLOGY-008` establishes dedicated ion-exchange organs and
  longitudinally distributed wax-like-gel chambers as the established
  migratory form's foundational osmoregulatory and passive-buoyancy strategy.
  Exact organ anatomy, transport chemistry, acclimation performance, chamber
  geometry and material, quantitative density and trim, hydrodynamic
  correction, and life-stage variation remain open.
- Diet, digestion, senses, navigation, schooling, rest, daily timing,
  fertilisation, reproductive anatomy, release posture, and egg attendance remain open.
- Mana source, access, carrier, storage, circulation, tissue targets, capacity,
  metabolic coupling, cost, recovery, and failure remain open.
- `Mana-supported tissue reinforcement` is not CAL0 level reinforcement, a
  buff, invulnerability, instant repair, free energy, a spell, Skill, class,
  aura, reward, specialised activated ability, or universal rule for all fauna.
- Cognition, sapience, formal Skills, classes, levels, appraisal, and human or
  character knowledge remain open.
- Egg ribbons describe egg arrangement only and establish no ribbon-shaped
  adult or relationship to Lineage II ribbon forms.
- `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` separately governs the family-care migrant's foundational
  body plan and shelter–forage–regroup care interface. It establishes no shared
  anatomy, ancestry, physiology, or Mana pathway with the mass breeder.
- Food, oxygen, minerals, energy, recovery, heat and waste handling, disease,
  parasites, injury, predation, route capacity, reproduction, and carrying
  capacity remain binding ecological constraints.
