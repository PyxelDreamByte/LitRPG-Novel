# Lineage I Foundational Body Plan

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational body plan for Lineage I,
the six-limbed filament-and-scute lineage established by `WLD-FAUNA-001`,
including the wing-pair derivation used by its winged branches.

## Provisional claims

1. Lineage I descends from a six-limbed ancestor with a tail.
2. Branches repurpose limb pairs for locomotion, handling, display, defence,
   digging, climbing, swimming, or flight.
3. The outer covering combines insulating or sensory filaments with flexible
   scutes, in proportions varying by branch and body region.
4. They are warm-active animals.
5. Reproduction uses flexible, mineral-latticed brood eggs that require
   guarded nests or brooding.
6. Large-bodied branches reproduce slowly, making breeding adults and
   successful nesting sites ecologically consequential.
7. In winged branches the middle limb pair becomes the wing pair, leaving
   four well-spaced walking legs with wings rising near the body's centre of
   mass.
8. In non-flying branches the middle pair carries the lineage's most variable
   roles, including grasping, digging, display, and reduced vestiges.
9. Winged branches build flight surfaces from the lineage's existing
   filament-and-scute materials rather than novel membranes.

## Boundaries

- `Filament`, `scute`, `brood egg`, and `wing pair` are author-facing
  functional descriptions, not in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- The covering establishes no fur, feather, hair, reptile-scale, or
  turtle-scute homology; the six-limbed frame establishes no insect,
  vertebrate, or Earth-tetrapod homology.
- Qualitative only: species, names, sizes, proportions, colours, lifespans,
  and the branch inventory remain open.
- `Warm-active` describes sustained activity and thermal independence at
  qualitative grain. Exact thermoregulation, metabolic rates, temperature
  tolerances, torpor, hibernation, and seasonal strategies remain open.
- Limb repurposing lists possible roles, not a fixed branch map. No
  particular branch, limb configuration, or role combination is established.
- Wing structure, vane or brace arrangement, wingspan, size ceilings, flight
  performance, launch, endurance, range, gliding branches, and flightless
  descendants of flyers remain open. Mid-pair derivation establishes wing
  position and leg count only, not aerodynamic capability.
- Brood eggs are flexible and mineral-latticed at qualitative grain; exact
  lattice chemistry, shell mechanics, size, clutch size, incubation period,
  temperature needs, hatching, and viability remain open.
- Guarded nests or brooding establish a care requirement, not a guarantee of
  success, a fixed nest form, site type, parental sex or number, colony
  behaviour, or defence capability. Nests can fail, and no branch is
  established as live-bearing.
- Slow large-body reproduction is a qualitative tendency; no reproductive
  rate, age at maturity, population size, or recovery time is established.
- Lineage II's live birth from retained egg capsules belongs to a separate
  lineage and is unaffected by this record.
- No Lineage I branch is established as present, abundant, or absent at any
  particular location, including the initial basin, and no branch is
  identified with any megafaunal, mythic, returning, or settlement-adjacent
  animal.
- Diet, digestion, senses, cognition, sociality, communication, migration,
  disease, and predator-prey relationships remain open.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, universal creature level, spawn mechanism, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Earth life of any era and mythological creatures may inspire author-facing
  design; no Earth species is transplanted directly.
- Food, energy, materials, reproduction, disease, injury, mortality, and
  carrying capacity remain binding ecological constraints.
