# Thread-Decomposer Foundational Body Architecture

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational body-and-feeding
architecture for the K6 thread-decomposer kingdom established by
`WLD-FLORA-004`, selected as an Author custom combination of the displayed
weaver and bloom options.

## Provisional claims

1. Thread decomposers are macroscopic, non-photosynthesising heterotrophs
   feeding through thread-form bodies.
2. They are distinct from microbial life; the Lineage II digestive symbionts
   of `WLD-ECOLOGY-002`/`003` remain separate microorganisms.
3. They are the planet's primary macroscopic decomposer-recycler kingdom.
4. Their ordinary state is diffuse feeding threads living hidden in soil,
   litter, and dead material.
5. On locating concentrated dead material, threads consolidate it into
   wrapped, felted masses that decompose from within, leaving spent husks.
6. Reproduction erupts episodically in fruiting structures keyed to wet and
   thaw windows.
7. Where thread decomposers are absent or fail, dead material can persist
   abnormally long.
8. Mutualist partnerships with producer kingdoms remain a bounded branch
   possibility, not the kingdom default.

## Boundaries

- `Thread`, `weaver`, `wrapped mass`, `felt`, `husk`, and `fruiting
  structure` are author-facing functional descriptions, not in-world names or
  formal taxonomy.
- The architecture establishes no Earth fungus, mycelium, mould, or mushroom
  homology.
- Qualitative only: species, thread and mass anatomy, chemistry, digestion,
  wrapping thresholds, consolidation pacing, and mass sizes remain open.
- Wrapping concentrated finds is the working pattern, not a guarantee;
  finds can be missed, contested, abandoned, or too dispersed, and no
  decomposition rate is established.
- Fruiting structure forms, cues, seasonality, dispersal, toxicity, and
  edibility remain open; episodic eruption establishes no fixed calendar.
- Abnormal persistence of dead material where K6 fails is a tendency, not a
  preservation guarantee; other decay paths remain possible and open.
- Spent felts and husks are candidate materials only; no harvest, craft,
  product, or human use is established.
- Producer partnerships, parasitic branches, predatory branches, and any
  living-tissue feeding remain open possibilities requiring their own
  records.
- No thread-decomposer member is established as present, dominant, or absent
  at any particular location.
- Earth and mythological inspiration remains author-facing; no Earth species
  is transplanted.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, spawn point, refill node, guaranteed harvest, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Moisture, temperature, energy, nutrients, substrate, time, disturbance,
  and competition remain binding ecological constraints.
