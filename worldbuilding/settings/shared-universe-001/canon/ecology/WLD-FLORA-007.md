# Free-Seeder-Flora Foundational Growth Architecture

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational strategy for the K3
free-seeder kingdom established by `WLD-FLORA-004`, fixed at qualitative
strategy grain.

## Provisional claims

1. Free-seeders are individual, non-networked, short-lived, fast-growing
   plants.
2. They specialise in openings: disturbed ground, fire scars, flood scours,
   trample gaps, and abandoned worked land.
3. They are poor long-term competitors against established stands and persist
   by exploiting change rather than holding ground.
4. The kingdom's signature is the seed vault: a durable dormant capsule that
   can wait in soil for years or decades until disturbance cues wake it.
5. The population's persistent form is the buried vault bank; growing plants
   are cheap, fast, and brief.
6. Disturbed ground erupts primarily from vault banks already present, and
   old worked or cleared land can carry buried seed legacies.
7. Dispersal exists but is secondary at kingdom grain.

## Boundaries

- `Free-seeder`, `seed vault`, and `vault bank` are author-facing functional
  descriptions, not in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- The strategy establishes no Earth annual, weed, seed-bank, or other plant
  homology.
- Qualitative only: species, forms, sizes, silhouettes, chemistry, energy
  capture, and growth habits remain open.
- Vault anatomy, dormancy limits, longevity, cue specifics, viability decay,
  bank densities, and depth behaviour remain open; no vault is guaranteed
  viable, and banks can be exhausted, destroyed, or absent.
- Buried legacies are a possibility of old worked land, not a guarantee at
  any particular place; no specific clearance, field, or site is established
  as carrying one.
- Eruption from existing banks is the primary pattern, not an exclusive rule;
  arrival by dispersal remains possible and its mechanisms remain open.
- Crop-ancestor potential is niche-space description only; no crop,
  domestication, cultivation, weed status, or human practice is established.
- No free-seeder member is established as present, dominant, or absent at any
  particular location.
- Interactions with K1 openings dynamics remain governed by the reserve-web
  records where they overlap; this record adds no reserve-web claim.
- Grazing, pests, diseases, symbioses including any K6 partnership, and
  animal dispersal relationships remain open.
- Earth and mythological inspiration remains author-facing; no Earth species
  is transplanted.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, spawn point, refill node, guaranteed harvest, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Water, energy, nutrients, space, growth time, disturbance, disease, and
  decay remain binding ecological constraints.
