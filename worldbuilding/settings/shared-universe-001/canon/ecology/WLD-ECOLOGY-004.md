# Lineage II Peripheral Migration Corridor

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative broad seasonal relationship between
Lineage II movement, the lower basin, and Project Hearthway's initial ridge-spur
settlement.

## Provisional claims

1. Crown herds ordinarily follow a predictable principal corridor through the
   lower basin near the settlement.
2. They normally pass through the local area twice during each annual cycle.
3. The fortified settlement core occupies a ridge spur above and outside the
   principal corridor.
4. Secondary paths overlap some outer fields, roads, and lake approaches.
5. Ordinary passages can cause closures, travel disruption, and crop loss but
   do not normally amount to a siege.
6. Severe weather, digestive disease, or forage failure can divert a crown herd
   toward the settlement and create an exceptional crisis.
7. Ribbon groups create smaller, more frequent pressures around woodland and
   lake margins.

## Boundaries

- Exact route geography, seasonal dates and direction, passage duration, herd
  scale, and route-fidelity mechanism remain open.
- `WLD-LOCATION-001` later establishes the settlement's bounded ridge-spur
  working landscape and lakeward road. It does not fix the exact migration
  corridor or secondary-path map, or guarantee overlap at any particular field
  or road segment.
- Exact human warning, closure, harvest, barrier, compensation, and emergency
  practices remain open.
- Typical damage, diversion probability, crisis duration, recovery costs, and
  detailed ribbon-group movements remain open.
- No deliberate hostility, magical compulsion, System spawn cycle, quest event,
  shared herd level, reward, Skill, trait payload, specialised Mana ability, or
  Ancestry Development mechanic is established.
