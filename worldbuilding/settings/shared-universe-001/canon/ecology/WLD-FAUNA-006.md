# Lineage V Foundational Body Plan

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative lineage-level body plan for Lineage
V, the tri-radial hydrostatic lineage established by `WLD-FAUNA-001`,
distinguishing its ancestral core from the derived specialisations of the
family-care migrant governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-010`.

## Provisional claims

1. Lineage V bodies are organised on tri-radial symmetry rather than the
   bilateral symmetry of Lineages I, III, and IV.
2. Support and movement are hydrostatic: pressurised internal fluid working
   against flexible tissue, with no rigid internal skeleton and no
   exoskeleton.
3. Travelling waves passing along body margins are a basic lineage-wide
   movement mode.
4. The lineage is predominantly aquatic.
5. The family-care migrant's low three-lobed vault silhouette, ring of short
   paddle-columns, underside refuge, and shelter–forage–regroup care system
   are derived and branch-specific rather than lineage-wide.
6. The family-care migrant is therefore one specialised caregiving member of
   the lineage, and `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` continue to govern
   it unchanged.

## Boundaries

- `Tri-radial`, `hydrostatic`, and `margin wave` are author-facing functional
  descriptions, not in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- The plan establishes no cnidarian, echinoderm, worm, or other Earth-clade
  homology and no direct Earth-animal transplant.
- Tri-radial symmetry describes qualitative body organisation. It establishes
  no perfect symmetry, fixed angles, three of any organ, three identities, or
  invariant external form, and derived branches may be secondarily
  asymmetric.
- Hydrostatic support establishes a mechanical principle, not exact fluid,
  chambering, pressure, valves, musculature, control, or performance, and it
  does not preclude local stiffening tissues short of a rigid skeleton.
- Travelling margin waves establish local tissue motion contributing to
  movement, not environmental waves, Mana pulses, sonar, signalling, an aura,
  group control, or a specialised ability. Their actuation, speed, and
  efficiency remain open, and `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` continues to govern the
  family-care migrant's bounded outlet-passage sequence.
- `Predominantly aquatic` is qualitative; whether any branch is fully
  terrestrial, amphibious, sessile, or colonial remains open, as do
  desiccation and freezing tolerances.
- Body-wall breaches are mechanically serious for hydrostatic bodies as a
  structural tendency; no wound threshold, healing rate, or mortality outcome
  is established.
- Which branches, if any, carry vaults, paddle-columns, underside refuges, or
  parental care remains open; this record establishes none of them for any
  organism other than the family-care migrant, and adds nothing to that
  migrant's own anatomy or behaviour.
- Qualitative only: species, names, sizes, proportions, colours, lifespans,
  and the branch inventory remain open.
- Reproduction and care across the lineage remain open; the family-care
  migrant's care system is its own established behaviour and is not
  lineage-wide.
- Feeding, digestion, senses, respiration, circulation, internal
  organisation, cognition, sociality, distribution, migration, and disease
  remain open.
- No Lineage V branch other than the family-care migrant is established as
  present, abundant, or absent at any particular location, including the
  basin lake.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, universal creature level, spawn mechanism, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Earth life of any era and mythological creatures may inspire author-facing
  design; no Earth species is transplanted directly.
- Food, oxygen, energy, materials, reproduction, disease, injury, mortality,
  and carrying capacity remain binding ecological constraints.
