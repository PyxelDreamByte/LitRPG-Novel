# Water-Flora Foundational Growth Architecture

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational growth architecture for
the K4 water-flora kingdom established by `WLD-FLORA-004`, fixed at
qualitative strategy grain.

## Provisional claims

1. Water flora are individual, non-networked organisms; contact between
   organisms is ordinary competition or incidental interaction, not
   kingdom-defining resource sharing.
2. The kingdom spans submerged, floating-surface, and wet-margin emergent
   forms.
3. Each organism has a durable, long-lived anchor gripping bed, stone, or
   bank; the anchor is the organism's persistent core and holds its stored
   reserves.
4. Expendable flexible streamer growth extends from the anchor through the
   growing season and is shed or sacrificed to ice, flood scouring, and
   grazing.
5. Winter and flood survival is anchor-and-reserves survival; regrowth
   follows the spring freshet.
6. Kingdom life rhythms are strongly coupled to the seasonal water cycle,
   and disturbance tolerance is a kingdom-defining pressure met differently
   by different members.
7. Mature anchored forms with stiffened streamer bases are a natural
   candidate for the lake's sturdy egg-substrate plants, which remain
   formally unassigned.

## Boundaries

- `Anchor`, `streamer`, and `stiffened base` are author-facing functional
  descriptions, not in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- The architecture establishes no Earth kelp, holdfast, reed, waterweed, or
  other plant homology.
- Qualitative only: species, forms, dimensions, proportions, lifespan,
  growth rates, and silhouettes beyond the theme remain open.
- Anchor and streamer anatomy, chemistry, reserve materials, strength,
  flexibility, and decay remain open.
- Streamer shedding is a strategy theme, not a guarantee: severe scouring,
  ice, drought, disease, grazing, or substrate loss can still kill anchors,
  and no tolerance threshold or regrowth rate is established.
- Energy capture, reproduction, dispersal, seasonal timing mechanisms, and
  overwintering states remain open.
- The kingdom's floating-surface habit is a bounded branch possibility;
  whether dedicated floating members exist, and in what form, remains open.
- The `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` sturdy egg-substrate plants and flooded vegetation
  are not assigned to K4 or to this architecture; candidacy requires its own
  record.
- No water-flora member is established as present, dominant, or absent at any
  particular location, including the basin lake.
- Salinity tolerances, brackish and coastal range, and any marine members
  remain open.
- Grazing relationships, symbioses including any K6 partnership, pests, and
  diseases remain open.
- Earth and mythological inspiration remains author-facing; no Earth species
  is transplanted.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, spawn point, refill node, guaranteed harvest, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Water, light or other energy, nutrients, substrate, space, growth time,
  disturbance, disease, and decay remain binding ecological constraints.
