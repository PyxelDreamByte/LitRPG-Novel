# Lineage II Distributed Fermentation Lattice

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative family-level digestive architecture for
Project Hearthway's developing Lineage II crown and ribbon branches.

## Provisional claims

1. Several connected digestive chambers route foods of different toughness and
   nutrient density through a distributed fermentation lattice.
2. Symbiotic microorganisms perform much of the chemical breakdown.
3. Crown forms have larger, slower-processing chambers for bulk structural vegetation.
4. Ribbon forms have smaller, faster-processing chambers for selective seasonal foods.
5. Ordinary Mana reinforces membranes, circulation, and thermal stability; it
   does not create calories or replace food.
6. Abrupt diet changes can destabilise the symbionts and cause illness.

## Boundaries

- Two-stage symbiont inheritance is governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-003`; exact chamber
  and reproductive anatomy, transfer behaviours, symbiont identity, maturation,
  compatibility, metabolic values, specific diseases, and treatments remain open.
- This record does not establish a specialised Mana-catalytic organ for the
