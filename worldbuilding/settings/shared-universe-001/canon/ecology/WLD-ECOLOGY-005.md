# Initial-Basin Coupled Pulse-Mosaic Exchange

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative high-level ecosystem architecture for
Project Hearthway's initial lake-basin region, its coupling to a wider regional
ecology, and the provenance boundaries required when nonlocal life enters
worldbuilding or story development. The later physical implementation of the
bounded initial region is recorded separately by `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001`; geography
beyond it remains open.

## Provisional claims

1. Open ground, woodland, wet margins, and lake habitats provide distinct
   seasonal productivity pulses within the initial basin.
2. Organisms, flooding, runoff, decomposition, and migration transfer matter
   and nutrients among those habitat channels.
3. No habitat, producer guild, animal lineage, migration, or disturbance is the
   basin's sole ecological engine.
4. The initial basin is nested within a wider regional ecology rather than
   treated as a closed system.

## Wider-horizon and evidence boundaries

- The Author's examples—wider migrations, rare traveller-carried animals,
  distant meats or produce, and tales of animals absent locally—are development
  channels. No particular example is established as existing by this record.
- Later nonlocal-life development must distinguish confirmed direct wild
  presence, seasonal or transient wild presence, imported remains or products, rare
  traveller-carried companion or captive live presence, and reported-only
  knowledge.
- A direct sighting establishes presence only at that time. A product does not
  establish local range or verified provenance. A migrant or captive does not
  establish residency or a self-sustaining population. A tale does not
  establish existence or local presence.
- A traveller-carried animal does not automatically establish domestication,
  ownership, loyalty, consent, nonsapience, safe husbandry, or wild establishment.
- `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001` is the later approved qualitative physical implementation
  compatible with this ecological frame. It does not alter this record's
  ecological claims. `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` is the later bounded implementation of
  broad lake productivity and two overlapping spring migrant relationships.
  Quantitative productivity, pulse magnitude, exact timing, taxa, route and
  transit mechanics, other migrations, products, transport, trade,
  institutions, prices, tales, and knowledge remain open.
- This is a regional and planetary horizon. It establishes no offworld organism,
  traveller, product, route, doorway use, or connection.
