# Tower-Flora Foundational Growth Architecture

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational growth architecture for
the K2 tower-flora kingdom established by `WLD-FLORA-004`, fixed at
qualitative strategy grain.

## Provisional claims

1. Tower flora are individual, non-networked organisms; root contact between
   towers is ordinary competition or incidental interaction, not
   kingdom-defining resource sharing.
2. A tower grows from a single persistent living core column.
3. Each growth season the core lays down an external structural casing layer
   that then dies; a mature tower is a living column sheathed in successive
   dead, self-supporting casings.
4. Because living tissue lies deep, surface damage, scorching, and
   girdling-equivalent injuries are survivable in principle; damage or decay
   that reaches the living core is the characteristic mortal wound.
5. Towers are long-lived and slow to replace; loss of mature towers is a
   lasting landscape change.
6. Seasonal growth is strongly pulsed, compatible with the established
   climate and pulse-mosaic ecology.
7. Reproduction concentrates in episodic heavy seeding years rather than
   uniform annual output.

## Boundaries

- `Tower`, `core`, `casing`, and `seeding year` are author-facing functional
  descriptions, not in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- The architecture establishes no Earth wood, bark, growth-ring, heartwood,
  cambium, or tree homology; the core-and-casing inversion is its own
  structure.
- Qualitative only: species, branch structure, dimensions, proportions,
  lifespan, growth rates, and silhouettes beyond the theme remain open.
- Core and casing anatomy, chemistry, material properties, strength, decay,
  flammability, and insulation remain open.
- Survivable surface damage is a structural tendency, not invulnerability; no
  tolerance threshold, healing rate, or fire immunity is established, and
  crown or root damage, drought, disease, and pests can still kill without a
  core breach.
- The possibility of removing casing without killing a tower is a structural
  implication only; no harvest practice, tool, yield, or human use is
  established.
- Energy capture, leaf-analogue structures, root form, water handling, and
  winter state remain open.
- Seeding mechanisms, structures, dispersal, edibility, period, synchrony
  scale, and triggers remain open; episodic seeding establishes no fixed
  cycle or guaranteed mast.
- No kingdom assignment is made for the western temperate rainforest, basin
  woodland, or any other named vegetation; K2 remains only their candidate
  home.
- No tower-flora member is established as present, dominant, or absent at any
  particular location.
- Pests, diseases, decomposition, and symbioses, including any partnership
  with K6 thread decomposers, remain open.
- Earth and mythological inspiration remains author-facing; no Earth species
  is transplanted.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, spawn point, refill node, guaranteed harvest, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Water, energy, nutrients, space, growth time, disturbance, disease, and
  decay remain binding ecological constraints.
