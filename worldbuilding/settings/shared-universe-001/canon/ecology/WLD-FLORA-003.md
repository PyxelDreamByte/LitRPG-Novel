# Basin Reserve-Web Fragment Viability

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative fragment-survival and independent-
patch stabilization rule for reserve-web colony patches in Project Hearthway's
initial basin.

## Provisional claims

1. Severance alone does not guarantee fragment survival or a new patch.
2. A severed fragment can persist only when it retains enough viable modules,
   reserves, and functioning connections to sustain itself.
3. A viable fragment can slowly stabilize as an independent colony patch.
4. An inadequate fragment cannot sustain itself independently and declines and dies.
5. Fragmentation is a possible but non-automatic route to a separate patch.

## Boundaries

- Exact module counts, reserve quantities, connection topology, weighting among
  requirements, stabilization time, mortality curve, wound response, and
  seasonal variation remain open.
- Reconnection, fusion, competition, parasitism, disease, displaced-fragment
  survival, and other reproductive or dispersal routes remain open.
- `Independent patch` is an ecological topology. Biological individuality,
  genetic continuity, and System appraisal remain open.
- External soil, water, mineral, energy, chemistry, temperature, and Mana
  requirements remain open.
- No human knowledge, terminology, cultivation, deliberate propagation,
  management, ownership, law, belief, or cultural meaning is established.
- This record establishes no personal progression identity, spawn, refill,
  instant stabilization, or guaranteed fragment survival.
