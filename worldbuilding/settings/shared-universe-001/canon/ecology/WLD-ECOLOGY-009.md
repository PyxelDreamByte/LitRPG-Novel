# Basin-Lake Family-Care Migrant Foundational Body Plan

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational body plan, locomotor
structure, and mobile-refuge care interface for the family-care migrant governed
by `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`.

## Provisional claims

1. The family-care migrant's established caregiving migratory form has a low
   living body organised as a three-lobed, broadly tri-radial vault.
2. A ring of short hydrostatic paddle-columns forms part of its locomotor
   structure.
3. Travelling waves move along the body margin and contribute to locomotion;
   their general mechanical relationship to the paddle-columns remains open
   outside the bounded outlet-passage choreography later governed by
   `WLD-ECOLOGY-010`.
4. The underside of the living vault forms a refuge that moves with the
   caregiving form.
5. Mobile young shelter beneath the vault, spill outward to forage, and
   regroup beneath the refuge.

## Boundaries

- This record governs the family-care migrant's established caregiving
  migratory form only. Juvenile morphology and all other life-stage anatomy
  remain open.
- `Brood-Vault Strider`, `living vault`, `tri-radial`, `hydrostatic
  paddle-column`, and `mobile refuge` are author-facing functional or editorial
  descriptions, not in-world names, formal taxonomy, or Earth homologies.
- Three-lobed or broadly tri-radial describes the qualitative vault shape only.
  It establishes no perfect symmetry, fixed angles, three paddle fields,
  repeated internal organs, three identities, or juvenile morphology.
- `Ring` and `short` are qualitative. Exact paddle-column number, spacing,
  dimensions, orientation, pressure source, internal fluid, chambering, fibres,
  valves, muscles, stiffness, control, repair, and failure remain open.
- `Paddle-column` establishes a living locomotor structure, not an Earth leg or
  fin homology, substrate walking, rowing efficiency, hovering, climbing,
  terrestrial movement, or overland bypass.
- Travelling margin waves establish local tissue motion that contributes to
  locomotion, not environmental waves, Mana pulses, sonar, telepathy, an aura,
  group control, or a specialised ability. `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` later establishes
  only a bounded outlet-passage sequence linking a margin wave, successive
  paddle-column bracing, and a short advance; all other mechanical
  relationships remain open.
- The underside refuge moves with the caregiving form but is not established as
  a shell, mineral armour, built nest, womb, sealed pouch, protected water
  pocket, fortification, invulnerable enclosure, or predator-proof space.
- Shelter, outward foraging, and regrouping establish no permanent carriage,
  physical attachment, guaranteed return after every excursion, provisioning,
  guarding, retrieval, signalling, escort formation, carer number or sex,
  genetic kinship, or brood size.
- The selected option carries a qualitative high-drag and route-feasibility
  concern. `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` later governs a bounded margin-ratchet
  passage choreography, and `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002` later supplies a qualitative
  mixed outlet sequence of candidate contact surfaces and holding pockets.
  None of these records establishes measured drag, a continuous compatible
  bed-or-margin chain, passable shallows, bottom walking, bottom resting,
  quantitative outlet profile, route depth, substrate continuity, seasonal
  hydraulics, salinity profile, guaranteed low-velocity holding water, speed,
  endurance, homing, passage success, survival, or guaranteed return.
- `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` continues to govern seasonal arrival, one-warm-season care,
  the juvenile prey relationship, competition, predation and harvest, escorted
  seaward departure, mortality, recruitment, and population variability.
- The family-care form does not inherit the mass breeder's
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` anatomy, `WLD-ECOLOGY-008` osmoregulation and buoyancy
  strategy, or ordinary Mana-supported tissue-reinforcement boundary.
- Reproduction, birth or hatching, early immobile stages, carers, sexes,
  kinship, brood size, adult diet, provisioning, guarding, retrieval,
  signalling, refuge use during travel, and escort formation remain open.
- Buoyancy, osmoregulation, respiratory medium and chemistry, exchange and
  internal transport, salinity tolerance, quantitative outlet profile and passability,
  passage mechanics beyond `WLD-ECOLOGY-010`, speed, endurance, fatigue, and
  all juvenile physiology remain open.
- No reserve-web or other named-flora relationship is established.
- Mana source, carrier, storage, circulation, tissue effects, capacity, cost,
  recovery, and failure remain open, as do specialised abilities, cognition,
  sapience, formal Skills, classes, levels, appraisal, and cultural or character
  knowledge.
- Finite matter or material and usable-energy inputs, internal transport, heat
  and waste handling, maintenance, injury, disease, recovery, repair,
  reproduction, route capacity, mortality, and carrying capacity remain binding
  ecological constraints.
- No pooled family identity, shared level or XP, automatic prey-power transfer,
  Skill, class, reward, System spawning, reset, refill, loot rule, free energy,
  or guaranteed population recovery is established.
