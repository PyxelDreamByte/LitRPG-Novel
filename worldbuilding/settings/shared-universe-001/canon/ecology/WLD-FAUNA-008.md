# Basin-Lake Resident Fauna Assemblage

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative composition of the basin lake's
year-round resident animal community, its lineage sources, and its structural
relationship to the spring migrant pulse governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`.

## Provisional claims

1. The lake holds a permanent resident animal community, present year-round
   and independent of the spring migrant pulses.
2. Resident aquatic animals are drawn principally from Lineage IV, with
   Lineage V, VI, and VII members also present.
3. The bird-like fishing organisms referenced by `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` are
   Lineage III forelimb-winged branch members.
4. Terrestrial shore hunters are drawn from Lineages I and III.
5. Residents must survive winter in the lake, including the regular freezing
   of sheltered bays.
6. The resident community is structurally dominant: it sets the lake's
   ecological structure and carries most of its standing animal biomass
   year-round.
7. The spring migrant pulse arrives into an already-occupied lake,
   concentrates in particular places and weeks, and functions as a large
   seasonal subsidy rather than as the lake's organising force.
8. The pulse does not restructure the resident community.

## Boundaries

- `Resident`, `shore hunter`, and `subsidy` are author-facing functional
  descriptions, not in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- Structural dominance describes ecological structure and standing biomass
  only. It does not downgrade the migrant pulse, and it establishes no
  biomass figure, ratio, productivity share, or population number.
- `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` continues to govern the dispersed nursery grounds,
  shifting feeding grounds, concentrated predators, human harvest activity,
  competition between migrants and residents, adult return and mortality,
  recruitment variability, and the possibility of exceptional or compound
  failure, all unchanged.
- Lineage assignment is compositional only. It establishes no species, names,
  taxonomy, resident inventory, sizes, or proportions, and `principally`
  fixes no share.
- `Bird-like` continues to describe ecological resemblance only. Assigning
  those fishers to Lineage III establishes no feathers, flight performance,
  Earth-bird anatomy, taxonomy, or named clade beyond the lineage's accepted
  forelimb-winged branch condition.
- Which Lineage IV branches occur, and whether any carry head shields,
  ion-exchange organs, or gel chambers, remain open; `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` and
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-008` continue to govern the mass breeder alone.
- Whether any resident is a Lineage V vault-bearing form remains open;
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` continue to govern the family-care
  migrant alone.
- Winter survival is a requirement placed on residents, not a solution. Exact
  overwintering behaviour, depth use, torpor, ice tolerance, mortality, and
  refuge locations remain open, and no deep-water area is established as
  guaranteed unfrozen.
- No resident is established as present at any particular bay, shore, depth,
  or named place, and none is identified with any harvested, domesticated, or
  culturally significant animal.
- Human fishing practice, seasons, gear, institutions, law, and knowledge
  remain open; this record establishes no harvest and no sustainable rate.
- Reproduction, spawning, diet detail, senses, sociality, disease, parasites,
  predation rates, and population figures remain open.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, universal creature level, spawn mechanism, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Earth life may inspire author-facing design; no Earth species is
  transplanted directly.
- Food, oxygen, energy, winter conditions, reproduction, disease, mortality,
  and carrying capacity remain binding ecological constraints.
