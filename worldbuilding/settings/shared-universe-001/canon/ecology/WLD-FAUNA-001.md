# Planetary Faunal Lineage Roster

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative roster of major animal body-plan
lineages for Project Hearthway's planet, fixed at silhouette-and-niche-theme
level so later per-lineage and per-species records have stable slots.

## Provisional claims

1. The planet's macroscopic animal life is organised across seven major
   body-plan lineages, with familiar ecological roles distributed across
   different lineages rather than copied from Earth clades.
2. Lineage I comprises six-limbed animals whose outer covering combines
   filaments and flexible scutes; branches may repurpose limb pairs, including
   wing-pair branches that produce four-legged winged forms.
3. Lineage II is the tower-necked four-limbed landscape-engineer lineage
   governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-001` through `WLD-ECOLOGY-004`.
4. Lineage III comprises low-frame four-limbed generalists; branches may
   develop forelimb wings that produce two-legged, two-winged forms.
5. Lineage IV is a fully aquatic flexible-trunk fin-fan lineage; the
   mass-breeding migrant established by `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` belongs to it.
6. Lineage V is a tri-radial hydrostatic lineage; the family-care migrant
   established by `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` belongs to it.
7. Lineage VI comprises small-bodied segmented animals with exoskeletons,
   including branches capable of small-scale flight.
8. Lineage VII comprises unsegmented soft-bodied animals.
9. Powered or gliding flight arose independently in more than one lineage; no
   single lineage monopolises aerial niches.
10. Every lineage includes abundant ordinary, unremarkable forms; no lineage
    exists only to supply spectacular creatures.

## Boundaries

- Roman numerals and functional labels are author-facing shorthand, not
  in-world names, formal taxonomy, or cladistic claims; final lineage and
  species names remain protected unknowns.
- The roster fixes qualitative silhouette and niche themes only. Exact
  anatomy, covering microstructure, sizes, proportions, reproduction,
  physiology, senses, cognition, species counts, and branch structure remain
  open except where existing bounded records govern.
- `Filament-and-scute` establishes no fur, feather, or reptile-scale homology;
  `low-frame four-limbed` establishes no mammal, reptile, or other Earth-clade
  homology; `segmented exoskeletal` establishes no insect or crustacean
  homology; `unsegmented soft-bodied` establishes no worm or mollusc homology.
- Wing-capable branches are body-plan possibilities. No particular winged
  species, its size ceiling, flight performance, range, or presence anywhere
  is established.
- Deep ancestry, divergence order, relatedness among lineages, hybrid
  impossibility, and extinct sibling lineages remain open.
- The seven lineages organise macroscopic animal life. Microscopic
  animal-grade life and rare minor or relict lineages below the framework's
  grain remain possible but require their own records.
- No lineage is established as present, abundant, or absent at any particular
  location, including the initial basin, beyond what existing records already
  establish.
- The `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` bird-like fishing organisms, resident lake fauna,
  terrestrial shore hunters, and aquatic predators are not assigned to
  lineages by this record.
- No sapient people, returning non-human people, or the parked pack species is
  assigned to any lineage by this record.
- No domesticate, livestock, pest, or other human-use relationship is
  established.
- Earth life of any era and mythological creatures may inspire author-facing
  design; no living, extinct, or legendary Earth species is transplanted
  directly.
- Mana use across lineages remains open except where existing records govern.
  No Mana organ, specialised ability, System progression, universal creature
  level, spawn mechanism, reward, Skill, trait payload, or Ancestry
  Development rule is established.
- Food, energy, reproduction, disease, injury, mortality, and carrying
  capacity remain binding ecological constraints for every lineage.

## Dependencies and implications

- Dependencies: `WLD-SETTING-001A`, `WLD-FAUNA-001A`, `WLD-ECOLOGY-001`,
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-007`, `WLD-ECOLOGY-009`, CAL0 `WLD1.2D`, and CAL0 `WLD2.1A+D`

  species, presence, and encounter requires its own evidence
- System implications: ordinary planetary ecology only; no System change
- Contradictions: none identified
