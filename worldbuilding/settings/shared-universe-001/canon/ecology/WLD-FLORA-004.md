# Planetary Floral Kingdom Roster

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative roster of major producer and
decomposer kingdoms for Project Hearthway's planet, fixed at
growth-strategy-theme level so later flora guilds and species have stable
slots, parallel to the faunal lineage roster of `WLD-FAUNA-001`.

## Provisional claims

1. The planet's macroscopic producer and decomposer life is organised across
   six major kingdoms at growth-strategy-theme level, with familiar ecological
   roles distributed originally rather than copied from Earth clades.
2. K1 — networked modular flora — comprises forms whose living subsurface
   networks link rooted growth modules; the reserve-web guild governed by
   `WLD-FLORA-001` through `WLD-FLORA-003` belongs to this kingdom.
3. K2 — tower flora — comprises long-lived, tall, self-supporting
   woody-analogue structural forms.
4. K3 — free-seeder flora — comprises short-lived, unnetworked,
   fast-dispersing individual plants.
5. K4 — water flora — comprises submerged, floating, and wet-margin producers.
6. K5 — crust-and-cushion flora — comprises low, slow, surface-hugging
   pioneer growth.
7. K6 — thread decomposers — comprises non-photosynthesising thread-and-body
   networks occupying decomposer, recycler, and mutualist roles.
8. Every kingdom includes abundant ordinary, unremarkable forms; no kingdom
   exists only to supply spectacular flora.

## Boundaries

- Kingdom codes and functional labels are author-facing shorthand, not
  in-world names, formal taxonomy, or cladistic claims; final names remain
  protected unknowns.
- The roster fixes qualitative growth-strategy themes only. Energy capture,
  chemistry, anatomy, reproduction, dispersal, seasonality, defences, and
  Mana patterns remain open except where existing bounded records govern.
- `Woody-analogue` establishes no Earth wood, bark, or tree homology;
  `thread decomposers` establishes no Earth fungus homology; `plants` and
  `flora` are functional conveniences, not Earth-clade claims.
- No kingdom is established as present, dominant, or absent at any particular
  location, including the initial basin, beyond what existing records already
  establish.
- The western temperate rainforest, basin woodland, wet-margin and marsh
  vegetation, and the `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` sturdy egg-substrate plants are not
  assigned to kingdoms by this record; candidate fits require their own
  records.
- Microscopic producer and decomposer life and rare minor or relict kingdoms
  below the roster's grain remain possible but require their own records.
- Cross-kingdom symbioses, including any root partnership between K6 and
  producer kingdoms, remain open possibilities, not established relationships.
- No domesticate, crop, harvest practice, or other human-use relationship is
  established.
- Earth life of any era and mythological flora may inspire author-facing
  design; no living, extinct, or legendary Earth species is transplanted
  directly.
- No Mana organ, specialised ability, System progression, spawn point, refill
  node, guaranteed harvest, reward, Skill, trait payload, or Ancestry
  Development rule is established.
- Water, light or other energy, nutrients, space, growth time, disturbance,
  disease, and decay remain binding ecological constraints for every kingdom.
