# Crust-and-Cushion-Flora Foundational Survival Strategy

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational survival strategy for
the K5 crust-and-cushion kingdom established by `WLD-FLORA-004`, fixed at
qualitative strategy grain.

## Provisional claims

1. Crust-and-cushion flora are low, slow, surface-hugging pioneers of bare
   and hostile surfaces: rock, stone, dead casings, walls, and scoured or
   disturbed ground.
2. Their two broad growth habits are thin surface crusts and dense dome
   cushions.
3. The kingdom's signature is metabolic shutdown and rapid revival: members
   tolerate near-total desiccation and deep freezing in a dormant state that
   can look entirely dead, then revive quickly on wetting or thaw.
4. Growth is extremely slow and confined to active windows; established cover
   is old cover.
5. Undisturbed crust and cushion cover is a qualitative mark of elapsed
   undisturbed time.
6. Their growth and decay initiate soil formation, opening succession for the
   other kingdoms.

## Boundaries

- `Crust`, `cushion`, `shutdown`, and `revival` are author-facing functional
  descriptions, not in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- The strategy establishes no Earth lichen, moss, algae, or cushion-plant
  homology.
- Qualitative only: species, forms, colours, dimensions, chemistry, energy
  capture, and reproduction remain open.
- Shutdown limits, tolerance thresholds, revival speed, repeated-cycle costs,
  and failure modes remain open; shutdown is not invulnerability, and heat,
  scouring, burial, grazing, pollution, or repeated cycling can still kill.
- Growth, colonisation, and soil-formation rates remain open; no cover is
  guaranteed to establish on any surface.
- Cover as a mark of elapsed time is qualitative only. No calibrated dating
  method, rate constant, age reading, or in-world dating knowledge or
  practice is established, and disturbance, microclimate, and surface
  differences can confound apparent age.
- Colonisation of dead tower casings, walls, and ruins is possible in
  principle; no particular structure, ruin, or site is established as
  colonised.
- Cushion microclimate buffering and mineral binding remain possible branch
  details, not kingdom-wide properties.
- No crust-and-cushion member is established as present, dominant, or absent
  at any particular location.
- Grazing, pests, diseases, symbioses including any K6 partnership, and
  human uses remain open.
- Earth and mythological inspiration remains author-facing; no Earth species
  is transplanted.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, spawn point, refill node, guaranteed harvest, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Water, energy, nutrients, surface, time, disturbance, and competition
  remain binding ecological constraints.
