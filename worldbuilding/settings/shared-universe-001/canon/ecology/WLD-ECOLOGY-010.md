# Basin-Lake Family-Care Migrant Outlet-Passage Choreography

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative outlet-passage movement and
care choreography for the family-care migrant governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`
and founded anatomically by `WLD-ECOLOGY-009`.

## Provisional claims

1. The selected qualitative passage behaviour is a margin-ratchet procession.
2. Where suitable support is available, paddle-columns brace in successive
   groups while a travelling margin wave drives a short advance.
3. The vault settles low between surges.
4. The directly observable rhythm is
   `settle → ripple → brace → surge → settle`.
5. Before advances, the caregiving form normally attempts to regroup
   mobile young beneath the refuge.
6. During autumn descent, the same structures brake and steer
   current-assisted slides.
7. The behaviour requires a compatible bed-or-margin contact and
   resting-opportunity chain, but does not establish that such a chain exists.
