# Lineage IV Foundational Body Plan

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative lineage-level body plan for Lineage
IV, the fully aquatic flexible-trunk fin-fan lineage established by
`WLD-FAUNA-001`, distinguishing its ancestral core from the derived
specialisations of the mass breeder governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` and
`WLD-ECOLOGY-008`.

## Provisional claims

1. Lineage IV is fully aquatic throughout; no branch is established as
   terrestrial or amphibious.
2. Its members occupy the full aquatic size and niche range, from small forms
   to the largest animals in their waters.
3. The lineage-wide inheritance is a locomotor and support core: a laterally
   flexible trunk carried on springy cartilage-analogue support, paired
   fin-fans as primary control surfaces, a muscular tail providing
   propulsion, and aquatic respiration through fold surfaces.
4. The mass breeder's exact three-pair fin-fan count, wedge-shaped
   mineralised head shield, dedicated ion-exchange organs, and wax-like-gel
   chambers are derived and branch-specific rather than lineage-wide.
5. The mass breeder is therefore one specialised migratory member of the
   lineage, and `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-008` continue to govern it
   unchanged.

## Boundaries

- `Trunk`, `fin-fan`, `fold`, and `springy cartilage-analogue support` are
  author-facing functional descriptions, not in-world names or formal
  taxonomy.
- The plan establishes no fish, shark, cetacean, vertebrate, gill, fin-ray,
  or caudal-fin homology and no direct Earth-animal transplant.
- Qualitative only: species, names, sizes, proportions, colours, lifespans,
  and the branch inventory remain open.
- Fin-fan counts, positions, shapes, actuation, and specialisation outside
  the mass breeder's established three pairs remain open; `paired` establishes
  bilateral arrangement only, not any particular number.
- Support-tissue composition, microstructure, stiffness, fatigue, and repair
  remain open, as do tail profile, gait, and swimming performance.
- Respiratory-fold position, structure, ventilation, exchange capacity, and
  protective configurations remain open outside the mass breeder's case.
- Which branches, if any, carry head shields, ion-exchange organs, or
  wax-like-gel chambers remains open; this record establishes none of them
  for any organism other than the mass breeder, and adds nothing to the mass
  breeder's own physiology.
- `Fully aquatic throughout` establishes no stranding tolerance, air
  breathing, amphibious capability, or overland movement, and no branch is
  established as unable to survive brief exposure; exposure limits remain
  open.
- Occupying the full aquatic size and niche range is a radiation claim, not a
  dominance claim, and no particular aquatic niche is established as filled
  by this lineage at any place.
- Reproduction across the lineage remains open; the mass breeder's mass egg
  deposition is its own established behaviour and is not lineage-wide.
- Freshwater, brackish, and marine distribution, migration, salinity
  tolerance, senses, navigation, feeding, digestion, cognition, sociality,
  and disease remain open.
- No Lineage IV branch other than the mass breeder is established as present,
  abundant, or absent at any particular location, including the basin lake,
  and no branch is identified with the resident lake fauna or aquatic
  predators referenced by `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`.
- Mana patterns remain open beyond the mass breeder's bounded ordinary
  tissue-reinforcement case; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, universal creature level, spawn mechanism, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Earth life of any era and mythological creatures may inspire author-facing
  design; no Earth species is transplanted directly.
- Food, oxygen, minerals, energy, reproduction, disease, injury, mortality,
  water connectivity, and carrying capacity remain binding ecological
  constraints.

## Dependencies and implications

- Dependencies: `WLD-SETTING-001A`, `WLD-FAUNA-005A`, `WLD-FAUNA-001`,
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-007`, `WLD-ECOLOGY-008`, CAL0 `WLD1.2D`, and CAL0 `WLD2.1A+D`
- Story implications: lake, river, and sea scenes can show a coherent family
  of flexible bodies, fin-fan control surfaces, and tail propulsion, of which
  the mass breeder is one conspicuous heavily equipped example rather than the
  norm; water connectivity constrains where the lineage can reach, but every
  species, branch, presence, and encounter requires its own evidence
- System implications: ordinary planetary ecology only; no System change
- Contradictions: none identified
