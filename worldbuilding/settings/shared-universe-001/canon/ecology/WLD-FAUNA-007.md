# Lineage VII Foundational Body Plan

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational body plan for Lineage
VII, the unsegmented soft-bodied lineage established by `WLD-FAUNA-001`,
including life mode as a branch-level axis and its ancestral state. It
completes foundational coverage of the faunal roster.

## Provisional claims

1. Lineage VII bodies are unsegmented and soft, without a rigid internal
   skeleton or a jointed exoskeleton.
2. Bodies are broadly bilateral and elongate along a single axis, in contrast
   with Lineage V's tri-radial organisation.
3. Movement is driven by muscular waves running along the body axis rather
   than by Lineage V's travelling margin waves.
4. The lineage occurs in water, soil, and other moist substrates.
5. Life mode varies by branch across three modes.
6. Filter branches draw suspended material from water, sessile or weakly
   mobile on submerged surfaces.
7. Substrate branches live inside material — soil, mud, litter, sediment,
   decaying matter — driving through it by muscular waves and processing it
   as they go.
8. Grazer branches move over exposed surfaces, feeding from them and relying
   on protective secretions.
9. Aquatic filter feeding is ancestral; substrate branches derived from
   filterers by invading sediment and soil, and grazer branches derived from
   substrate branches by emerging onto exposed surfaces.
10. Substrate branches own substrate turnover — the mixing, aeration, and
    breakdown that make soils work — complementing rather than duplicating
    the `WLD-FLORA-008` thread decomposers, which wrap and digest
    concentrated dead material from outside.
11. Internal parasitism derives from the substrate habit.

## Boundaries

- `Filter branch`, `substrate branch`, `grazer branch`, and `muscular wave`
  are author-facing functional descriptions, not in-world names or formal
  taxonomy.
- The plan establishes no worm, mollusc, snail, slug, leech, flatworm, or
  other Earth-clade homology and no direct Earth-animal transplant.
- Qualitative only: species, names, sizes, proportions, colours, lifespans,
  and the branch inventory remain open.
- Bilateral and axially elongate describe qualitative organisation only; they
  establish no fixed proportions, head, tail, or invariant external form, and
  derived branches may be secondarily reduced or asymmetric.
- Whether any branch carries mineralised structures, shells, plates, or other
  hard parts remains open; softness describes the absence of a rigid internal
  skeleton and jointed exoskeleton, not the absence of all hard tissue.
- Feeding mechanisms, digestion, senses, respiration, circulation, and
  internal organisation remain open across every branch.
- Moisture dependence is a lineage tendency; exact desiccation, freezing,
  salinity, and compaction tolerances remain open, and no branch is
  established as unable to endure dry conditions in some resting state.
- Substrate turnover is an ecological role, not a rate, yield, soil model, or
  guarantee that any particular ground is worked, fertile, or recoverable.
- Protective secretions establish a functional adaptation only; their
  chemistry, effectiveness, toxicity, edibility, and any human use remain
  open.
- Internal parasitism is available as a derived habit. No parasitic species,
  host, transmission route, symptom, prevalence, disease, treatment, or
  effect on any person, animal, or population is established.
- The three modes describe life strategy only; they establish no fixed branch
  count, no exclusive niche assignment, and no rule that any niche belongs to
  one mode.
- Ancestral filter feeding is an evolutionary-history claim; it establishes
  no timeline, transitional form, or surviving ancestral species.
- No Lineage VII branch is established as present, abundant, or absent at any
  particular location, including the initial basin.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, universal creature level, spawn mechanism, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Earth life of any era and mythological creatures may inspire author-facing
  design; no Earth species is transplanted directly.
- Moisture, oxygen, food, energy, materials, reproduction, disease, injury,
  mortality, and carrying capacity remain binding ecological constraints.
