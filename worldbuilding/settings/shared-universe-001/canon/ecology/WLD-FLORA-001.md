# Basin Reserve-Web Vegetation Guild

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative functional seasonal architecture for a
major but non-exclusive open-ground vegetation guild in Project Hearthway's
initial basin.

## Provisional claims

1. The reserve-web guild is a major but non-exclusive part of the basin's
   open-ground vegetation and contains multiple vegetation forms rather than
   one species or one continuous basin-wide organism.
2. Members maintain living subsurface networks that store water and nutrients
   and support persistence through winter, surface fire, episodic flooding,
   and grazing.
3. They produce tender spring growth, tall fibrous summer structures, and
   autumn fruiting or storage bodies.
4. Crown herds consume mature structural growth; ribbon groups selectively
   harvest young growth and seasonal reproductive structures.
5. Browsing and trampling can create openings within reserve-web stands and the
   wider vegetation mosaic.
6. Excessive or repeated pressure can exhaust stored reserves and kill networks; regrowth
   is finite rather than automatic.

## Boundaries

- Exact species, taxonomy, anatomy, patch dimensions and boundaries,
  reproduction, energy capture, chemistry, distribution, and disturbance tolerances remain open;
  functional modular-colony organization is governed by `WLD-FLORA-002`.
- Complementary flora, fungi or analogues, pests, diseases, decomposers,
  mutualists, Mana use, and human exploitation remain open.
- Earth life may inspire author-facing design, but no Earth species is directly
  transplanted as an in-world guild member.
- No System spawn point, refill node, guaranteed harvest, progression, reward,
  Skill, trait payload, specialised Mana ability, or Ancestry Development
  mechanic is established.
