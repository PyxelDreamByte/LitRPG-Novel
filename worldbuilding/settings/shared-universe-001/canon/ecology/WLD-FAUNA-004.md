# Lineage VI Foundational Body Plan

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational body plan for Lineage
VI, the segmented exoskeletal radiation established by `WLD-FAUNA-001`,
including skeletal growth mode as a branch-level axis and its ancestral
state.

## Provisional claims

1. Lineage VI has a segmented body enclosed in a jointed external skeleton,
   with segment counts and regional specialisation varying by branch.
2. The lineage is the planet's most species-rich macroscopic animal
   radiation, and most members are small-bodied.
3. Some branches fly; their flight is small-scale and arose independently of
   the flight evolved in Lineages I and III.
4. The lineage supplies pollinator-analogue, litter and soil, parasitic,
   scavenging, predatory, and swarm-prey roles.
5. Skeletal growth mode varies by branch across three modes.
6. Moult branches shed the whole skeleton periodically, pass through a soft
   vulnerable phase, may undergo life-stage transformation, and remain small.
7. Seam-growth branches never shed; their skeletons expand continuously at
   flexible inter-plate seams, without a soft phase and without life-stage
   transformation.
8. Rolling-replacement branches shed and regrow plates individually and are
   never wholly soft.
9. Whole-body moulting is the ancestral condition; rolling replacement and
   seam growth are derived escapes from its soft phase and size ceiling.
10. Freed from the moult constraint, seam-growth branches include the
    lineage's large-bodied members, which exceed the sizes Earth arthropods
    reach.

## Boundaries

- `Plate`, `seam`, `moult branch`, `seam-growth branch`, and
  `rolling-replacement branch` are author-facing functional descriptions, not
  in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- The plan establishes no insect, crustacean, arachnid, myriapod, or other
  Earth-clade homology; `exoskeleton` describes an external jointed skeleton
  only.
- Qualitative only: species, names, segment counts, limb counts, sizes,
  proportions, colours, lifespans, and the branch inventory remain open.
- Skeleton composition, chemistry, seam mechanics, moult mechanics, hardening,
  repair, damage tolerance, and mineral requirements remain open.
- `Most members are small-bodied` and `exceed the sizes Earth arthropods
  reach` are qualitative. No measurement, size range, upper limit, or
  comparison figure is established, and no particular large animal is
  established as existing.
- Larger-than-Earth body sizes require respiratory, circulatory, and
  structural support. This record does not supply that mechanism; it remains
  open and binding, and large branches are not exempt from physical or
  physiological limits.
- Life-stage transformation is available to moult branches only as a
  possibility; its extent, forms, triggers, and whether any branch uses it
  remain open. Seam-growth and rolling-replacement branches do not use it.
- The three modes describe growth strategy only; they establish no fixed
  branch count, no exclusive niche assignment, and no rule that any niche
  belongs to one mode.
- Ancestral moulting is an evolutionary-history claim; it establishes no
  timeline, transitional form, or surviving ancestral species.
- Flight in this lineage is small-scale at the lineage level; whether any
  large branch is flight-capable remains open, as do wing structure, origin,
  and performance.
- The lineage's scavenging and litter roles sit alongside the `WLD-FLORA-008`
  thread decomposers, which remain the primary macroscopic recycler kingdom.
- No Lineage VI branch is established as present, abundant, or absent at any
  particular location, including the initial basin, and no branch is
  identified with any pest, pollinator, parasite, or mythic creature.
- Reproduction, egg or brood form, development, sociality, swarming,
  communication, senses, internal anatomy, disease, and predator-prey
  relationships remain open.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, universal creature level, spawn mechanism, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Earth life of any era and mythological creatures may inspire author-facing
  design; no Earth species is transplanted directly.
- Food, energy, minerals, oxygen, reproduction, disease, injury, mortality,
  and carrying capacity remain binding ecological constraints.
