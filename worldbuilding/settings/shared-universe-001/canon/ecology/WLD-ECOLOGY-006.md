# Basin-Lake Spring Breeding Pulse

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative lake-productivity pattern
and overlapping spring breeding-migration relationship for Project Hearthway's
initial freshwater basin lake.

## Provisional claims

1. The freshwater through-flow lake is biologically productive but spatially
   and seasonally varied.
2. The spring freshet creates seasonally favourable passage conditions from the
   sea and marks the start of the lake's seasonal rise in productivity.
3. An unnamed mass-breeding migrant arrives first and deposits dense ribbons or
   clusters of eggs onto sturdy plants and mineral surfaces across river-mouth
   shallows, protected bays, submerged ridges, and flooded vegetation margins
   rather than relying on one nursery ground.
4. Healthy adults generally return toward the sea after breeding, while the
   breeding-and-return cycle still carries meaningful adult mortality.
5. A distinct family-care migrant follows, remains through one warm season
   while rearing young, and escorts surviving young seaward before autumn storms
   and first freezes.
6. Its young consume the first migrant's eggs and juveniles as an important but
   non-exclusive food source.
7. Both migrants compete with resident lake fauna during their lake phases.
8. Humans, aquatic predators, terrestrial shore hunters, and bird-like fishing
   organisms exploit the seasonal abundance.
9. Reproductive risk is dispersed across places and times so that no single
   ordinary predator, flood, or fishery routinely destroys the entire generation.
10. Exceptional or compound recruitment failures remain possible.
11. The dispersed egg-deposition pattern creates feeding grounds that shift
    across the lake over the season.

## Boundaries

- `Freshet` establishes a seasonal passage condition and productivity
  transition, not an exact hydraulic, salinity, sensory, or navigation
  mechanism. `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002` later establishes only a qualitative mixed
  outlet sequence; a favourable season can still contain dangerous peak flows,
  temporarily unusable micro-routes, crowding, ice, or debris.
- Exact names, taxonomy, ancestry, size, cognition, sea range, route, timing,
  environmental cues, population scale, fecundity, exact egg-ribbon density,
  mortality, recruitment, carrying capacity, and return fidelity remain open.
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` establishes only the mass breeder's foundational body plan,
  while `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` separately establishes the family-care migrant's
  foundational body plan and shelter–forage–regroup care interface, and
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` later establishes only that migrant's qualitative
  outlet-passage choreography, while `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002` later supplies a
  candidate physical feature set against which it can be tested. Their
  remaining anatomy and physiology, actual route use, and passage success
  remain open.
- The number, dimensions, location, and biological composition of nursery
  habitats remain open beyond the qualitative classes above.
- Egg deposition onto sturdy plants and mineral surfaces establishes no
  adhesion, entanglement, draping, anchoring, or other retention mechanism.
- Flooded vegetation is not identified as the reserve-web guild or any other
  named flora.
- Egg ribbons describe egg arrangement only. They do not themselves establish
  a ribbon-shaped animal, body plan, or relationship to Lineage II ribbon forms;
  the mass breeder's selected foundation is governed separately by
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-007`.
- Bird-like describes an ecological resemblance only. It establishes no
  feathers, flight, Earth-bird anatomy, taxonomy, or named clade.
- Human exploitation establishes a mortality channel, not a fishing technique,
  institution, law, ownership regime, trade, ritual, preservation method, or sustainable harvest.
- No single ordinary predator, flood, or fishery routinely destroys the entire
  generation; this establishes no sustainable harvest rate and does not prevent
  exceptional or compound collapse.
- Family care does not guarantee survival. The juvenile predator-prey link is
  important but non-exclusive, and no equilibrium or fixed annual return is
  established.
- Adult repeat breeding, family-care reproductive mode, number or sex of carers, brood size,
  care behaviours beyond the shelter–forage–regroup pattern selected by
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` and the bounded attempted regroup-and-advance sequence
  selected by `WLD-ECOLOGY-010`, diets beyond the selected prey link, disease,
  parasites, nutrient transfer, and carcass effects remain open.
- The feeding grounds shift over the season, but their exact locations, timing,
  consumers, and movement pattern remain open.
- `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` establishes ordinary Mana-supported tissue reinforcement
  for the mass breeder only. Its exact Mana anatomy and mechanism, all
  specialised abilities, and the family-care migrant's Mana use remain open,
  as do creature progression, formal Skills, classes, levels, appraisal, and
  cultural or character knowledge.
- These are ordinary living populations. No System spawning, seasonal reset,
  refill, pooled cohort identity, shared progression, loot rule, or automatic
  XP, attribute, trait, Skill, Mana, or power transfer is established.
