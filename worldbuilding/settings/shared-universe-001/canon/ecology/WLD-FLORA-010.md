# Basin Returning-Woodland Composition and Succession

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative qualitative composition and present
successional state of the woodland returning across Project Hearthway's
initial basin, filling the gap `WLD-LOCATION-001` left open and assigning
that woodland to floral kingdoms for the first time.

## Provisional claims

1. The basin's returning woodland is composed principally of K2 tower flora
   with K3 free-seeder thickets, over a ground and surface layer of K5
   crust-and-cushion flora and K6 thread decomposers.
2. Its present state is young; seventy years is short against tower
   replacement rates.
3. When organised suppression ended, buried K3 vault banks under abandoned
   clearances woke and erupted, and today's woodland is chiefly that result.
4. The prevailing structure is dense, tangled vault-seeder thicket giving way
   patchily to young towers that have risen but not thickened.
5. Scattered relict old towers survive from before the clearances,
   recognisable by their bulk and deep-layered casings and often carrying
   heavy crust-and-cushion cover on older casing faces.
6. Mature timber-analogue material is scarce; the woodland is rich in thin
   stems and poor in old ones, making relict towers locally valuable and
   their felling consequential.
7. Passage through young thicket is harder than through mature woodland.
8. Lineage II browsing contests the woodland's advance, so its boundary
   moves in some places and is held back in others, and succession is not
   uniform across the basin.

## Boundaries

- `Thicket`, `relict tower`, and `young tower` are author-facing descriptive
  terms, not in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- Kingdom assignment is compositional only. It establishes no species, names,
  taxonomy, member inventory, or proportion beyond the qualitative
  principal-and-layer description above.
- Exact extent, boundaries, density, canopy height, patch sizes, and the
  woodland's relationship to `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001`'s western rainforest and
  damp refugia remain open; the rainforest's own composition is not
  established here.
- Relict-tower abundance, distribution, sizes, ages, condition, and
  recognisability to any observer remain open; no particular tree, stand, or
  place is established.
- `Locally valuable` describes ecological and material scarcity, not price,
  ownership, law, custom, protection, or any established practice. No timber,
  fuel, forage, coppice, clearance, or management practice is established.
- `Harder passage` is qualitative. No speed, travel time, route, or
  impassability is established, and `WLD-LOCATION-002`'s road anchors are
  unaffected.
- Woken vault banks explain the thicket's origin as the prevailing pattern,
  not an exclusive one; dispersal-founded growth and surviving stands remain
  possible, and no particular clearance is established as having carried a
  bank.
- Contested advance establishes qualitative browsing pressure only. It fixes
  no boundary position, browsing intensity, opening size, or rate, and it
  identifies no particular field, road segment, or lake approach as a
  crown-herd overlap; `WLD-ECOLOGY-004` continues to govern that
  relationship.
- Raised surface-fire exposure follows from dense young growth and the
  permitted dry spells; no fire frequency, season, severity, extent, or event
  is established.
- Deadfall accumulation and whether the woodland's thread-decomposer
  population is sufficient to process it remain open.
- The woodland's age structure is a physical consequence of the collapse; no
  in-world knowledge, dating practice, or ability to read it is established.
- No animal is established as inhabiting, feeding, breeding, or sheltering in
  the thicket, and no lineage is assigned to any woodland animal.
- Earth life may inspire author-facing design; no Earth species is
  transplanted directly.
- No System mechanic, spawn point, refill node, guaranteed harvest, reward,
  Skill, trait payload, specialised Mana ability, or Ancestry Development
  rule is established.
- Soil, water, light, nutrients, growth time, disturbance, browsing, disease,
  and decay remain binding ecological constraints.
