# Basin Reserve-Web Modular Colonies

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative spatial and resource-sharing topology
of reserve-web colony patches in Project Hearthway's initial basin.

## Provisional claims

1. Reserve-web subsurface networks are organized into bounded modular colony patches.
2. Each patch contains numerous rooted growth modules connected by
   a spatially limited living network.
3. Connections permit finite local sharing of resources.
4. Damage can sever connections, isolate portions, and kill modules or sections
   when sufficiently severe.
5. Distinct patches do not form one continuous basin-wide organism.
6. Local resource sharing does not constitute shared consciousness.

## Boundaries

- Exact module anatomy, individuality, patch scale, connection structure, and
  transfer carriers and rates remain open.
- Threshold-based fragment viability and possible independent-patch
  stabilization are governed by `WLD-FLORA-003`. Exact thresholds, timescale,
  wound response, reconnection, other reproduction, dispersal, disease, and
  variation among guild members remain open.
- Energy capture, chemistry, soil and water limits, Mana use, signalling, and
  System appraisal remain open.
- No human knowledge, terminology, cultivation, harvesting, management,
  ownership, law, belief, or cultural meaning is established.
- Distinct patches do not form a basin-wide organism, and local resource
  sharing does not constitute shared consciousness.
- This record establishes no personal colony identity, shared level, pooled
  statistics, spawn point, refill node, instant transfer, or guaranteed recovery.
