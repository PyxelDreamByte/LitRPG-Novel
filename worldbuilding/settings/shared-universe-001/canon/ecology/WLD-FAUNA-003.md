# Lineage III Foundational Body Plan

## Scope

This is the provisionally authoritative foundational body plan and
life-history signature for Lineage III, the low-frame four-limbed generalist
radiation established by `WLD-FAUNA-001`.

## Provisional claims

1. Lineage III descends from a four-limbed ancestor with a low-slung limb
   posture; upright and specialised postures are derived branch conditions.
2. An internal supporting frame carries the body; the lineage has no
   exoskeleton and no scute armour comparable to Lineage I's.
3. The outer covering is a flexible skin bearing branch-specific structures,
   and does not combine Lineage I's filaments and scutes.
4. Winged branches derive wings from the forelimbs, leaving two walking legs.
5. The lineage is the planet's broadest generalist radiation, occupying prey,
   mesopredator, burrowing, climbing, and small-flyer roles.
6. Its life-history signature is reproductive throughput: several to many
   young per breeding, fast juvenile growth, early dispersal, high early
   mortality, and quick generational turnover.
7. Large-bodied branches may moderate that pattern.
8. Parental care occurs in some branches but is not the lineage's defining
   investment.

## Boundaries

- `Low-frame`, `internal supporting frame`, and `litter` are author-facing
  functional descriptions, not in-world names or formal taxonomy.
- The plan establishes no mammal, reptile, amphibian, bird, bat, or other
  Earth-clade homology; `internal supporting frame` establishes no vertebrate
  or bone homology.
- Qualitative only: species, names, sizes, proportions, colours, lifespans,
  and the branch inventory remain open.
- Whether reproduction is egg-laying, live-bearing, or variable by branch
  remains open, as do gestation or incubation, birth or hatching, litter
  sizes, breeding frequency, seasonality, and sexual systems.
- `Several to many` is qualitative. No brood size, growth rate, dispersal
  distance, mortality rate, generation length, or population figure is
  established.
- High early mortality is a tendency, not a guarantee that any particular
  young dies, and quick recovery is not guaranteed; local extirpation,
  compound failure, and sustained decline remain possible.
- Skin structures, insulation, colour, patterning, sensory features, moult,
  and any secretions remain open.
- Wing structure, wingspan, size ceilings, flight performance, launch,
  endurance, gliding branches, and flightless descendants of flyers remain
  open. Forelimb derivation establishes wing origin and leg count only.
- Generalist breadth establishes a radiation pattern, not dominance, and no
  particular niche is established as filled by this lineage at any place.
- No Lineage III branch is established as present, abundant, or absent at any
  particular location, including the initial basin, and no branch is
  identified with any domesticate, livestock, pest, game animal, or
  settlement-adjacent creature.
- The `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` bird-like fishing organisms remain unassigned to any
  lineage.
- Diet, digestion, senses, cognition, sociality, communication, migration,
  disease, and predator-prey relationships remain open.
- Mana patterns remain open; no Mana organ, specialised ability, System
  progression, universal creature level, spawn mechanism, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, or Ancestry Development rule is established.
- Earth life of any era and mythological creatures may inspire author-facing
  design; no Earth species is transplanted directly.
- Food, energy, materials, reproduction, disease, injury, mortality, and
  carrying capacity remain binding ecological constraints.
