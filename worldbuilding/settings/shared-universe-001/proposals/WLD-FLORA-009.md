# WLD-FLORA-009 — Crust-and-cushion-flora foundational survival strategy

## Purpose

Final Phase 1 flora scaffold: fix the K5 crust-and-cushion kingdom's
foundational survival strategy. K5 colonises what nothing else can — bare
rock, dead casings, upland stone, ruins, and scoured surfaces — and starts
the soil that succession needs. All options share the kingdom's two named
growth habits (thin surface crusts and dome cushions); the choice is the
survival signature.

## Existing constraints

- Accepted world canon: `WLD-FLORA-004` (K5 slot: low, slow, surface-hugging
  pioneer growth), `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001` (wet uplands, hard winters, occasional
  multi-week dry spells), `WLD-FLORA-005` (dead casings as colonisable
  surfaces)
- Staging context: seventy years of abandoned imperial works and ruins
- CAL0 rules: WLD1.2D and WLD2.1A+D
- Deliberately unresolved boundaries: species, chemistry, reproduction,
  colonisation rates, any dating use

## Proposed claims

Common to all options:

1. Crust-and-cushion flora are low, slow, surface-hugging pioneers of bare
   and hostile surfaces: rock, stone, dead casings, walls, and scoured or
   disturbed ground.
2. The kingdom's two broad growth habits are thin surface crusts and dense
   dome cushions.
3. Growth is extremely slow, and established cover is old cover.
4. Their growth and decay initiate soil formation, opening succession for the
   other kingdoms.

## Alternatives

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Shutdown-and-revival: near-total desiccation and freeze dormancy, rapid revival when wetted | Fits dry spells and hard winters; vivid dead-then-green texture; undisturbed cover reads as elapsed time | Revival logic must stay bounded | High at this grain |
| B | Self-microclimate cushions: internal buffering of moisture and warmth | Strong upland texture | Buffering favours cushions over crusts; weaker on bare-rock extremes | High |
| C | Mineral-armoured crusts: absorbed grains build living stone veneers | Very alien; living-rock mystery | Blurs the life/rock boundary everywhere it appears; slowest story payoff | High |

### Option A — Revival flora (recommended)

The kingdom's signature is **metabolic shutdown and rapid revival**: members
tolerate near-total desiccation and deep freezing in a dormant state that can
look entirely dead, then revive within hours of wetting or thaw. A drought
hillside of grey, apparently dead crust turns living colour after one rain.
Because growth is so slow and shutdown so tolerant, undisturbed crust and
cushion cover becomes a qualitative mark of elapsed undisturbed time —
seventy-year ruins wear seventy years of crust — without establishing any
calibrated dating method. Soil-making proceeds in the brief active windows.

### Option B — Microclimate cushions

The signature is internal buffering: packed dome cushions trap moisture and
warmth, surviving hostile surfaces by making their own conditions inside.
Strong in uplands, weaker as a whole-kingdom answer for thin crusts on bare
rock.

### Option C — Stone-veneer crusts

The signature is mineral armour: crusts bind absorbed mineral grains into
living stone-like veneers. Strikingly alien, but it muddies the boundary
between rock and life wherever it appears and pays off slowest in stories.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal basis

Deep divergence of the K5 foundation; ordinary biology.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

Active growth is confined to wet, warm-enough windows; cover loss to
scouring or harvesting is slow to replace; soil initiation links K5 to every
later succession story.

### Story implications

Ruins, uplands, and stone gain readable texture: dead-grey drought states,
rain revivals, and crust cover that quietly testifies how long a surface has
lain undisturbed.

### Character-knowledge implications

None established; whether anyone in-world reads crust cover as time remains
open.

### CAL0 implications

Application only; ordinary ecology.

## Recommendation

Option A. Shutdown-and-revival answers the established climate's dry spells
and hard winters directly, gives the kingdom the most visible and repeatable
scene texture, quietly equips ruins with an honest sense of age, and leaves
cushion microclimates and mineral binding available as later branch details
rather than kingdom-wide commitments.
