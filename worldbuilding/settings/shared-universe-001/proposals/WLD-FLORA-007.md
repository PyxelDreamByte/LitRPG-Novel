# WLD-FLORA-007 — Free-seeder-flora foundational growth architecture

## Purpose

Third Phase 1 scaffold: fix the K3 free-seeder kingdom's foundational
strategy. K3 fills disturbance openings, field margins, and the
crop-ancestor space, and must coexist with K1 reserve-webs on open ground
without duplicating their persistence strategy.

## Existing constraints

- Accepted world canon: `WLD-FLORA-004` (K3 slot: short-lived, unnetworked,
  fast-dispersing individual plants), `WLD-FLORA-001` (browsing and trampling
  create openings; reserve-web recovery is finite), `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001`
  (seasonal climate, fire on dry open ground), `WLD-ECOLOGY-001` (crown herds
  maintain open corridors and mosaics)
- Staging context: seventy-year post-collapse rewilding with abandoned
  imperial clearances
- CAL0 rules: WLD1.2D and WLD2.1A+D
- Deliberately unresolved boundaries: species, chemistry, dispersal
  mechanisms, any crop or human use

## Proposed claims

Common to all options:

1. Free-seeders are individual, non-networked, short-lived, fast-growing
   plants.
2. They specialise in openings: disturbed ground, fire scars, flood scours,
   trample gaps, and abandoned worked land.
3. They are poor long-term competitors against established K1 and K2 stands
   and persist by exploiting change rather than holding ground.

## Alternatives

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Dormant vault-seed banks waiting in soil | Strongest rewilding fit; farming texture; completes a distinct persistence strategy per kingdom | Buried-life logic must stay bounded | High at this grain |
| B | Far-travelling wanderer seeds chasing disturbance | Travel and arrival texture | Persistence texture lost; openings depend on arrival luck | High |
| C | Extreme multi-generation seasonal tempo | Vivid boom fields | Echoes the rejected boom-and-bust guild direction; fragile in bad years | High |

### Option A — Vault-seed flora (recommended)

The kingdom's signature is the **seed vault**: a durable dormant capsule that
can wait in soil for years or decades until disturbance cues — light, heat,
soil breakage — wake it. The growing plant is cheap, fast, and short-lived;
the population's real life is the buried vault bank. Consequences at theme
level: disturbed ground erupts from what was already there rather than
waiting for arrival; old clearances and worked land carry invisible seed
legacies from before the collapse; ploughed fields fight their own buried
banks; fire and trampling trigger flushes. This completes a clean
persistence-strategy split across the producer kingdoms: K1 persists as
living networks, K2 as standing structure, K4 as anchors, K3 as dormant
vaults. Dispersal exists but is secondary and remains open.

### Option B — Wanderer flora

The signature is mobility: light, far-travelling seed-analogues ride wind,
water, and animals, and populations chase disturbance across the landscape.
Openings green up only as fast as arrival allows, and the buried-legacy
texture is lost.

### Option C — Tempo flora

The signature is speed: several generations per season racing weather
windows. Vivid, but it revives the boom-and-bust direction the Author
declined for open ground and collapses in bad seasons without a persistence
reserve.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal basis

Deep divergence of the K3 foundation; ordinary biology.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

Vault banks make disturbance history a living archive; bank exhaustion is
possible where openings never come; compatible with K1 openings dynamics
without altering reserve-web records.

### Story implications

Rewilding scenes gain grounded texture: abandoned clearances erupting,
plough-woken flushes, fire-following blooms; future crop-ancestor and weed
records get a mechanism.

### Character-knowledge implications

None established.

### CAL0 implications

Application only; ordinary ecology.

## Recommendation

Option A. Dormant vault banks are the strongest thematic fit for a world
where seventy years of suppressed land is waking up, they give farming and
disturbance scenes a concrete mechanism, and they complete a distinct,
non-overlapping persistence strategy for every producer kingdom.
