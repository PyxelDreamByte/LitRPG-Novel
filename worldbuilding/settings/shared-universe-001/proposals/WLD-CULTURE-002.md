## Decision boundary

Choose the qualitative balance mechanism of the settlement's standing
founder-council compact: which office classes each side holds, which
decision classes need which side or both, where refugee and newcomer
representation enters, how craft, harvest, defence, and System-study
expertise gets a voice, and which ambiguity classes the compact is known
to contain. The Author will NOT be choosing names, law-code text,
succession outcomes or candidates, faction rosters, ownership regimes, or
fishing/harvest law; first-succession rules stay incompletely specified by
design.

## Options

### A — DUAL-KEY COMPACT

The founding-era compact divides standing authority into two keys. The
dynastic key covers outward-facing and emergency matters: dealings with
outsiders and returning peoples, armed defence and the watch, declared
emergencies (the grounded crisis classes WLD-ECOLOGY-004 already licenses),
and the fortified perimeter. The council key covers inward-facing matters:
allocation of communal stores and labour, dispute-hearing among households,
and consent to communal burdens of work, material, or watch service. A
defined class requires both keys turned together: admitting a large
newcomer group, extraordinary burdens during a declared emergency, altering
the compact itself, and confirming a succession.

Office classes, dynasty side: the ruling seat and a small household deputy
class holding delegated fragments of the key (war-leadership, wall-and-gate
wardenship, outward envoy). Council side: a convener class holding the
council key; an allocation stewardship class; a dispute-hearing class.
Refugee and newcomer representation enters through council composition:
seats are distributed so founding households do not hold the council key
alone, some tied to later arrival cohorts or residence groupings (whether
such groupings are spatial is the cultural-fabric proposal's territory)
under an imperfectly specified distribution rule. Expertise enters as a
standing advisory bench:
recognised craft, harvest, defence, and System-study voices sit with the
council without holding a key, summonable by either key-holder; System-study
advice is access and interpretation only, with no guaranteed readout and no
System-backed authority (CAL0 UI1.2D, SOC1.2D).

Known ambiguity classes (deliberately unresolved): whose key governs
requisition when an emergency requires allocation; how long a declared
emergency runs before the council key re-engages; how newcomer seats are
apportioned as cohorts age into established households; and — sharpest —
the succession clause, which requires both keys but does not specify who
turns the dynastic key while the seat is vacant or failing, in what order
confirmation proceeds, or what happens if the keys disagree.

### B — PRESIDED STEWARDSHIP

The dynasty is the presiding and executing side; the council the
officeholding and stores-holding side. The dynastic seat presides over
council sessions, speaks for the settlement outward, and executes what the
council resolves; it cannot ordinarily spend, allocate, or bind households
alone. The working offices — stores stewardship, works-and-walls
stewardship, watch stewardship, and dispute-hearing classes — are held by
council stewards rotating on a customary interval; communal stores and
exchange-stock authority rotates with them. The dynasty acts alone only under a
declared emergency (the same grounded crisis classes as Option A), every
act subject to later council review: affirmation, cost charge-back, or
censure of the seat.

Refugee and newcomer representation enters through the rotation pool:
stewards are drawn from a household register that includes newcomer
households after a customary residence interval — the interval's length and
the register's keeper are contested. Expertise enters through
qualification where an office matches it: craft and defence communities
maintain recognised competence pools from which the works-and-walls and
watch stewardships are customarily filled; the stores and dispute-hearing
stewardships are drawn from the general register. Harvest and System-study
knowledge-holders hold recognised advisory standing to any steward rather
than offices of their own — for harvest only that standing is claimed,
with every practice, institution, community organisation, and
competence-recognition mechanism left open (WLD-ECOLOGY-006);
System-study advisers interpret what is accessible, with no guaranteed
appraisal and no System-enforced power (CAL0 UI1.2D, SOC1.2D).

Known ambiguity classes: what suffices to declare an emergency and who may
contest a declaration in the moment; what sanctions review can impose on a
sitting seat; whether rotation intervals bind during a long emergency; and —
sharpest — whether the presiding seat passes by dynastic designation alone
or requires steward acclamation, a point the compact never had to state
while the founder lived.

### C — CHARTER-OATH ASSEMBLY

The compact rests on a binding founding oath the founder swore in the
founding era: the oath defines the dynasty's duties (defence, outward
dealings, upholding the compact) and its limits (no burden without consent,
no seizure of households' subsistence, no altering the compact alone). The
council is chosen by an assembly of the settlement's households and holds
two powers: it adjudicates disputes over whether the oath has been kept,
and its consent is required for communal burdens. The dynasty's authority
is real and broad within the oath's bounds; the council's is real because
it judges those bounds and holds consent.

Office classes, dynasty side: the oath-bound ruling seat and a sworn
deputy class under the same oath. Council-assembly side: an adjudicator
class hearing oath disputes; a burden-consent class conveying or refusing
assembly consent; an oath-witness class keeping the compact's memory and
records (fallible, incomplete, disputable — CAL0 SOC1.2D). Refugee and
newcomer representation enters at the assembly root: standing follows the
household hearth regardless of origin, so newcomer households vote once
established — with contested margins over dependents, lodgers, and the
not-yet-householded. Expertise enters as sworn craft, harvest, defence, and
System-study testimony in adjudication and burden deliberation; System-study
testimony interprets accessible signs, never a guaranteed readout (CAL0
UI1.2D).

Known ambiguity classes: whether the founding oath binds heirs
automatically, only upon re-swearing, or lapses with the founder — it was
sworn by a living founder who has never needed replacing; whether the
assembly or the council receives a successor's oath; which disputed
recollections of the oath's terms control when witnesses disagree (disputed
recollection is claimed here; no oath text is); and how adjudication
proceeds if the seat itself is the disputed party during a succession.

## Shared claim set (option-independent)

1. The compact dates from the settlement's founding era, within the accepted
   25-35-years-ago founding range.
2. The ageing founder still rules at story start; the settlement's FIRST
   succession under the compact is approaching.
3. Both sides hold real, actually exercised authority; neither is merely
   ceremonial.
4. The compact is a human institution: customary, fallibly recorded,
   socially enforced. No office, title, or compact term carries any
   System-granted power or enforcement (CAL0 SOC1.0D, SOC1.2D).
5. The first-succession provisions are incompletely specified or contested
   in some respect — known at story start, not resolved here.
6. Emergency authority, wherever placed, is grounded in the accepted
   ordinary crisis classes (WLD-ECOLOGY-004 weather, digestive-disease, and
   forage-failure diversions; severe seasons), never in System events.

## Recommended option

Option A (dual-key compact). It gives both sides the cleanest genuinely
non-ceremonial domains while locating every designed ambiguity exactly
where the approaching first succession will bear: the seam between the
keys. Its dual-key succession clause creates contestable process without
naming candidates or outcomes, which is what the parallel current-events
proposal needs.

   deputy class (war-leadership, wall-and-gate wardenship, outward envoy).

6. A standing advisory bench of recognised craft, harvest, defence, and

Dual-key, ruling seat, deputy class, war-leadership, wardenship, envoy,

as are Option B's and C's stewardship, register, competence-pool,

## Consistency and causal review

- Forward extension, not a retcon: WLD-LOCATION-001 explicitly left founder,
  dynasty, council, succession, law, tenure, and labour institutions open;
  this proposal fills part of that declared open space and makes only the
  declared qualitative closures listed below (watch and declared-emergency
  institutional classes; harvest advisory standing), moving no accepted
  claim and narrowing no other record's declared-open question.
- The foundation capture's settled facts (balanced compact, neither side
  ceremonial, ageing founder, approaching first succession) appear unchanged
  in every option; options vary only the balance mechanism.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-004 respected: emergency authority attaches to the accepted
  grounded crisis classes and adds no migration behaviour, hostility, or
  System cause. Declared qualitative closure: a watch institution and a
  declared-emergency practice exist as institutional classes, partially
  closing that record's open warning/closure/emergency-practice space; its
  exact warning, closure, barrier, compensation, and emergency procedures
  remain open. Seam with the cultural-fabric proposal (WLD-CULTURE-001
  candidate): that record separately establishes that watch duty exists and
  is crewed across strands, ceding watch offices, command, and levy
  authority to this proposal.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-006's critical boundary respected via an explicit declared
  qualitative closure, never an implied claim: harvest knowledge-holders
  exist and may hold recognised advisory standing (Option A's bench, Option
  B's advisers, Option C's testimony). Every harvest technique, practice,
  law, institution, ownership, trade, ritual, preservation method,
  community organisation, and competence-recognition mechanism remains
  open; council consent to communal burdens is abstract only.
- Seam with the cultural-fabric proposal on residence: council seats may
  tie to residence groupings, but whether origin communities cluster
  spatially into quarters is that proposal's unresolved decision; this
  compact presupposes no spatial arrangement.
- WLD-LOCATION-001/002/003 respected: no road authority, toll, water right,
  shore institution, keeper, patrol, or ownership is created; wall-and-gate
  wardenship is an office class over the accepted fortified perimeter,
  fixing no wall form, gate count, or defensive performance.
- CAL0 implications: ordinary setting extension, not a System change.
  Offices grant social authority only (SOC1.2D); no party mechanics or
  pooled capability (SOC1.0D); access and interpretation only, never
  guaranteed appraisal (UI1.2D); no eligibility or authority by category or
  band (WLD1.0D, WLD2.2D); no new mechanic, constant, or notification
  behaviour.
- First-order consequences: each option makes succession friction
  structural rather than personal, and gives refugee households a real but
  imperfect channel and expertise a real but bounded voice, so the
  current-events proposal can frame pressures without new institutions.
- Ordinary finitude holds: governance work consumes labour and time;
  nothing creates enforcement, compliance, or unity as free goods.

## Research questions / open boundaries

1. All in-world names and title wording for the seat, offices, council,
   compact, and any oath.
2. The exact first-succession process and its resolution — deliberately
   unresolved; the ambiguity classes feed the current-events proposal and
   must not be settled as background fact.
3. Council size, seat counts, cadence, quorum, selection procedures, and
   the actual newcomer-representation rules: distribution rule (A),
   residence interval and register keeper (B), assembly-standing margins
   (C).
4. How expert standing is recognised in practice for craft, harvest,
   defence, and System-study voices, and how contested that recognition is.
5. The compact's record form: what is written, remembered, witnessed, or
   disputed, and who keeps it (fallible under CAL0 SOC1.2D).
6. Law content of any kind: offences, penalties, tenure, inheritance, water
   and road arrangements, and every harvest question (open under
   WLD-ECOLOGY-006 and WLD-LOCATION-001/002/003).
7. The compact's history of past emergencies, reviews, and precedents.
8. How returning non-human peoples relate to the outward-dealings authority;
   their histories and aims remain unflattened and undeveloped here.
9. How the compact is known, misunderstood, or mythologised inside and
   outside the settlement (observer rule; no accidental omniscience).
