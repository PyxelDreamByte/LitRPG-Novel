# WLD-FAUNA-001 — Planetary faunal lineage roster

## Purpose

The staging capture's five-lineage faunal framework leaves Lineages III–V
undefined and gives the two provisionally accepted lake migrants no lineage
home. The Author has asked to expand the base lineage roster into a more
complex and diverse mix of body plans — familiar four-limbed forms, six-limbed
forms, creatures with wings as well as four limbs, and other such lineages —
drawing inspiration from living, extinct, and mythological Earth life without
direct transplants. This proposal fixes the roster of major animal lineages at
silhouette-and-niche-theme level so later per-lineage records have stable slots.

## Existing constraints

- Accepted world canon: `WLD-ECOLOGY-001` through `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` (Lineage II
  ecology; both lake migrants), `WLD-FLORA-001` through `WLD-FLORA-003`,
  `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001`/`002`
- Staging (non-canonical): `workbench/proposals/project-hearthway-foundation-capture.md`
  five-lineage framework, Lineage I six-limbed filament-and-scute sketch,
  ecological-direction principles (layered ecosystem, abundant ordinary
  organisms, no Earth transplants, roles distributed across lineages)
- CAL0 rules: WLD1.2D and WLD2.1A+D (ordinary world ecology; no System change)
- Deliberately unresolved boundaries: final lineage names, all in-world
  taxonomy, per-lineage anatomy and reproduction beyond existing records,
  which lineage the `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` bird-like fishing organisms belong to

## Proposed claims

Common to all options below:

1. The planet's macroscopic animal life descends from a fixed roster of major
   body-plan lineages; familiar ecological roles are distributed across
   different lineages rather than copied from Earth clades.
2. Every lineage includes abundant ordinary, unremarkable forms as well as any
   consequential megafauna; no lineage exists only to supply spectacular
   creatures.
3. Lineage II (tower-necked four-limbed landscape engineers) and the two lake
   migrants retain their existing bounded records unchanged; the roster only
   assigns them slots.
4. Roman numerals and functional labels are author-facing shorthand, not
   in-world names or taxonomy.

## Alternatives

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Seven-lineage roster; flight arises independently in several lineages | Covers every requested silhouette class; anchors both migrants; supplies the ordinary-organism backbone; strongest biological logic (Earth evolved flight four times) | Largest roster to govern | High while lineages stay one-line themes |
| B | Eight-lineage roster; one dedicated ancestrally winged lineage owns macro-scale flight | Clean, strong mythic flyer identity; simple airspace logic | Wings-as-lineage is weaker biology; familiar two-winged bird/bat silhouettes lost or forced into the flyer lineage | High |
| C | Keep five slots; define III–V only | Smallest change | Tri-radial migrant and small-bodied life left outside the framework; ordinary-organism backbone unanchored | High |

### Option A — Convergent skies (recommended)

| Slot | Body-plan theme | Niche space and anchors |
|---|---|---|
| I | Six-limbed filament-and-scute animals; branches may repurpose limb pairs, including wing-pair branches (four legs + wings) | Warm-active terrestrial generalists to megafauna; the mythic winged-quadruped silhouette space (staging sketch) |
| II | Tower-necked four-limbed landscape engineers | Existing `WLD-ECOLOGY-001`–`004` records |
| III | Low-frame four-limbed generalists; possible forelimb-winged branches (two legs + two wings) | The familiar tetrapod silhouette space: prey, mesopredators, burrowers, climbers; bird/bat-like flyer silhouettes |
| IV | Aquatic flexible-trunk fin-fan animals | Anchored by the mass breeder (`WLD-ECOLOGY-007`); fish-to-whale-analogue niche space |
| V | Tri-radial hydrostatic animals | Anchored by the family-care migrant (`WLD-ECOLOGY-009`); radial aquatic and margin niche space |
| VI | Segmented exoskeletal smallstock; small-scale flight possible | Arthropod-analogue backbone: pollinator-analogues, decomposers, parasites, swarm prey |
| VII | Unsegmented soft-bodied animals | Worm/mollusc-analogue space: decomposers, filterers, burrowers |

Flight stance: powered or gliding flight arose independently in more than one
lineage (I, III, and VI are candidates); no single lineage owns the sky.

### Option B — Sovereign wings

As A, but Lineages I and III are unwinged; a dedicated eighth lineage of
ancestrally winged six-limbed flyers (four walking limbs plus one wing pair)
owns macro-scale flight, while VI keeps small-scale flight.

### Option C — Minimal completion

Keep the five-slot frame: III = low-frame four-limbed generalists, IV = winged
six-limbed flyers, V = aquatic flexible-trunk animals. The tri-radial migrant
and all small-bodied life remain outside the numbered framework.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal basis

Deep evolutionary divergence produced several independent body-plan
foundations; later radiation distributed ecological roles among them. No
magical, System, or doorway cause is proposed.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

The roster is planetary in scope but establishes no lineage as present at any
particular place; regional presence still requires its own records. Existing
basin records (Lineage II corridor, seasonal migrants) are unaffected.

### Story implications

A stable roster lets creature encounters be designed lineage-first
(silhouette, covering, reproduction, niche) with Earth and mythic inspiration
kept author-facing. Options A and B both make the requested
winged-plus-four-limbs silhouette available; A also keeps the familiar
two-wing flyer silhouette.

### Character-knowledge implications

None; in-world peoples' folk taxonomies need not match the roster.

### CAL0 implications

Application only. Ordinary ecology; no System change, creature progression
mechanic, or Ancestry Development implication.

## Recommendation

Option A. It satisfies every silhouette class the Author requested (familiar
four-limbed via III, six-limbed via I, four-legs-plus-wings via I's winged
branches, and further alien classes via IV–VII), gives both provisionally
accepted migrants lineage homes instead of leaving them orphans, supplies the
ordinary-organism backbone the ecological principles demand, and mirrors
Earth's repeated independent evolution of flight, which yields both mythic and
familiar flyer silhouettes without one lineage monopolising the air.
