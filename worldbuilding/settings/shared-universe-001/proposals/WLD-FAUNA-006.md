# WLD-FAUNA-006 — Lineage V foundational body plan

## Purpose

Fifth Phase 2 scaffold: give Lineage V — the tri-radial hydrostatic lineage —
a lineage-level body plan. Like Lineage IV, it already has one developed
member: the basin lake's family-care migrant, governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-009`
and `WLD-ECOLOGY-010`. The question is again which of that animal's features
are ancestral and which are its own, and `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` explicitly scopes
itself to that migrant's caregiving form alone.

## Existing constraints

- Accepted world canon: `WLD-FAUNA-001` (Lineage V slot: tri-radial
  hydrostatic animals, anchored by the family-care migrant),
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` (family-care migrant: low three-lobed broadly tri-radial
  living vault, ring of short hydrostatic paddle-columns, travelling margin
  waves contributing to locomotion, underside refuge moving with the form,
  mobile young sheltering, foraging outward, and regrouping),
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-010` (its settle–ripple–brace–surge–settle outlet
  choreography)
- CAL0 rules: WLD1.2D and WLD2.1A+D
- Deliberately unresolved boundaries: species, names, taxonomy, sizes, local
  presence, Mana patterns
- Hard constraint: nothing here may retcon `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` or `-010`

## Proposed claims

Common to all options:

1. Lineage V bodies are organised on tri-radial symmetry rather than the
   bilateral symmetry of Lineages I, III, and IV.
2. Support and movement are hydrostatic: pressurised internal fluid working
   against flexible tissue, with no rigid internal skeleton and no
   exoskeleton.
3. The lineage is predominantly aquatic; whether any branch is fully
   terrestrial remains open.
4. The family-care migrant governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-009` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-010`
   is one member, and its records continue to govern it unchanged.

## Alternatives — how much of the family-care migrant is ancestral

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Radial-hydrostatic core with travelling margin waves ancestral; vault shape, paddle-column ring, mobile refuge, and family care derived | Consistent with the Lineage IV treatment; strong identity without conscripting members into vault anatomy; makes the migrant's care system a genuine specialisation | Branch variety must be developed later | High at this grain |
| B | The full vault-and-paddle package is ancestral | Very uniform lineage silhouette | Every member becomes a low vault on paddle-columns; wastes the lineage's radial potential | High |
| C | Only tri-radial symmetry is ancestral | Maximum freedom | Hydrostatic support left optional, so the lineage loses its mechanical identity | High |

### Option A — Radial-hydrostatic core (recommended)

The lineage-wide inheritance is a mechanical package: tri-radial body
organisation, hydrostatic support and actuation, and travelling waves passing
along body margins as a basic movement mode. Everything distinctive about the
family-care migrant is derived — the low three-lobed vault silhouette, the
ring of short paddle-columns, the underside refuge, and the whole
shelter–forage–regroup care system. Ordinary Lineage V animals may therefore
be radially symmetric hydrostatic swimmers, crawlers, drifters, or
sit-and-wait forms with no vault, no paddle-columns, and no parental care at
all, while the Brood-Vault Strider stands out as an elaborate caregiving
specialist.

### Option B — Full-package ancestral

Vault, paddle-column ring, and refuge are ancestral. Uniform, but it turns a
whole lineage into variations on one nursery animal.

### Option C — Symmetry-only

Only tri-radial symmetry is lineage-wide, with hydrostatic support optional.
Frees everything, defines nothing.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal basis

Deep radial-hydrostatic divergence; ordinary evolution. Hydrostatic bodies
trade rigid leverage for flexibility, cheap growth, and shape change, which
is why the lineage favours water and soft substrates.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

Hydrostatic support depends on maintained internal pressure, so wounds that
breach the body wall are mechanically serious, not merely injurious;
desiccation and freezing are correspondingly dangerous. Radial symmetry suits
animals whose threats and food arrive from any direction rather than ahead.

### Story implications

The lineage supplies genuinely alien silhouettes for water and margins —
radial rather than head-and-tail — of which the vaulted, brood-carrying
migrant is the elaborate exception rather than the rule.

### Character-knowledge implications

None established.

### CAL0 implications

Application only; ordinary ecology.

- Whether any branch is fully terrestrial, sessile, or colonial

## Recommendation

Option A. It keeps the lineage mechanically defined — radial and hydrostatic,
with margin waves as its basic movement idiom — while leaving vaults,
paddle-columns, refuges, and parental care as the family-care migrant's own
achievements. That preserves the strangeness the roster reserved for this
lineage without forcing every future member to be a variation on a nursery
animal, and it mirrors the treatment just accepted for Lineage IV.
