# WLD-ECOLOGY-007 — Basin-Lake Mass-Breeder Foundational Body Plan

## Purpose

Resolve the first lake migrant's family-grade body-plan frontier so later
ecology and prose can show a coherent alien organism without prematurely
fixing its name, taxonomy, measurements, complete physiology, population
budget, or System expression.

## Existing constraints

- Setting authority: `WLD-SETTING-001A`
- Breeding migration and lake ecology: `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`
- Freshwater through-flow basin and seaward outlet: `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001`
- CAL0 creature and ordinary-world ecology: `WLD1.1D`, `WLD1.2D`, and
  `WLD2.1A+D`
- Story workspace:
  `stories/series/project-hearthway-main/project-hearthway-main.work-manifest.json`
- Design source:
  `stories/series/project-hearthway-main/workbench/runs/2026-08-14-basin-lake-mass-breeder-body-plan/proposal.md`

`WLD-ECOLOGY-006` deliberately left both migrant body plans open. This
proposal extends that record for the mass breeder only; it does not alter the
selected migration, egg-deposition, mortality, predation, or recruitment
relationships.

## Proposed claims

1. `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`'s mass-breeding migrant belongs to a fully aquatic alien
   biological lineage.
2. Its established migratory form has a streamlined, laterally flexible trunk
   supported by springy cartilage.
3. Three paired fin-fans and a strong muscular tail form its visible locomotor
   foundation.
4. A wedge-shaped mineralised head shield forms the front of the body.
5. Its aquatic respiratory surfaces form folds that may be exposed or held in
   a more protected configuration and support active aquatic respiration.
6. Mana contributes to ordinary tissue reinforcement as part of the organism's
   physiology rather than creating a specialised activated ability.
7. `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` continues to govern egg-ribbon deposition, adult return
   and mortality, predation, recruitment, and population variability.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal basis

The laterally flexible trunk and muscular tail provide a plausible primary
thrust foundation. Three fin pairs provide multiple control surfaces, while
the exact division among steering, trim, braking, lift, and close manoeuvring
remains open. Springy support must balance flexibility, elastic return,
directional stability, and fatigue. The anterior shield, exposed respiratory
area, and Mana-supported tissues all create costs and failure modes rather
than free protection or endurance.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

- The form is compatible with continuous aquatic travel but does not prove
  route depth, passability, speed, endurance, homing, or guaranteed return.
- The body requires finite oxygen, food, minerals, circulation, waste handling,
  recovery, and protection from disease, parasites, abrasion, and injury.
- The head shield adds mineral demand and anterior mass that later buoyancy and
  balance design must support.
- Respiratory exposure trades gas exchange against drag, silt, parasite,
  injury, and osmotic exposure; folding or protection reduces exchange area.
- Sea-freshwater water and ion balance remains a separate unresolved mechanism.
- Ordinary Mana support cannot replace matter, calories, oxygen, salinity
  control, healing time, navigation, or route capacity.

### Society and culture

No human name, classification, harvesting practice, material use, institution,
belief, or knowledge level is established. A visible body part is not
automatically a known resource or loot component.

### History and chronology

No evolutionary origin, fossil history, ancestral range, relation to another
lineage, or change under the fallen imperial order is established.

### Story implications

The protagonist can encounter a recognisably alien silhouette with distributed
fin-fans, lateral body flexion, a muscular tail, a mineralised leading shield,
and changing respiratory exposure. A scene may reveal only what distance,
water clarity, light, behaviour, prior knowledge, and any valid sensing or
appraisal channel actually support.

### Character-knowledge implications

The full author-side body plan is not automatic character knowledge. Names,
functions, internal anatomy, Mana pathways, migration physiology, and
taxonomic relationships require observation, instruction, records, dissection,
experimentation, or a separately scoped appraisal capability.

### CAL0 implications

This is an ordinary biological and magical ecology extension, not a System
change. `Mana-supported tissue reinforcement` describes a causal physiological
contribution, not CAL0 level reinforcement, a buff, a Skill, a class, an aura,
or a reward. The body plan establishes no universal creature level, pooled
identity, retrospective XP, spawn mechanism, refill, or automatic power
transfer.

## Consistency review

- The proposal is a forward extension of `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`, not a retcon.
- Fully aquatic travel remains within the connected water route established by
  `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001`; no overland bypass or air breathing is inferred.
- Egg ribbons remain an arrangement of eggs and do not imply a ribbon-shaped
  adult or a relationship to Lineage II forms.
- The fin-fans and head shield establish anatomy only, not an egg-placement,
  ramming, digging, defence, or feeding behaviour.
- No fish, shark, vertebrate, gill, fin-ray, jaw, or caudal-fin homology is
  established by descriptive analogy.
- The family-care migrant's body plan remains completely open.
- Ordinary Mana physiology complies with CAL0 only while its sources, carriers,
  costs, capacities, recovery, and failures remain causal.
- No contradiction or System change has been identified.

## Recommendation

Select `MASS-BREEDER-BODY-A`. It creates a coherent alien family foundation
that fits the selected migration while retaining the high-value causal gates
needed for realistic ecology and later species diversification.
