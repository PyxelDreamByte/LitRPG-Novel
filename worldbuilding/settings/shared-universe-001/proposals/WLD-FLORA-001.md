# WLD-FLORA-001 — Basin Reserve-Web Vegetation Guild

## Purpose

Establish a plausible, original, seasonal forage foundation for the initial
basin and its Lineage II herbivores without defining the complete flora.

## Options reviewed

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FLORA-GUILD-A | Diverse vegetation guild with living subsurface reserves and seasonal structures | Resilient, seasonal, exhaustible forage; supports both Lineage II branches | Anatomy, limits, and complementary flora require later work | Selected |
| FLORA-GUILD-B | Short-lived annual boom-and-bust fields dominate open ground | Dramatic pulses and weather sensitivity | Unreliable megafaunal forage and severe failed-year risk | Not selected |
| FLORA-GUILD-C | Slow woody browse dominates open ground | Durable standing biomass | Low throughput and slow recovery | Not selected |

## Selected proposed claims

1. A reserve-web guild is a major but non-exclusive component of the initial
   basin's open-ground vegetation.
2. The guild contains multiple vegetation forms rather than one species or one
   continuous basin-wide organism.
3. Members maintain living subsurface networks that store water and nutrients
   and help them persist through winter, surface fire, episodic flooding, and grazing.
4. The guild produces tender spring growth, tall fibrous summer structures, and
   autumn fruiting or storage bodies.
5. Crown herds consume mature structural growth; ribbon groups selectively
   harvest young growth and seasonal reproductive structures.
6. Browsing and trampling can create openings within reserve-web stands and the
   wider vegetation mosaic.
7. Excessive or repeated pressure can exhaust stored reserves and kill the
   living network; regrowth is finite rather than automatic.

## Consistency review

- Extends the open forage question in `WLD-ECOLOGY-001` and supports the branch
  digestive strategies in `WLD-ECOLOGY-002`.
- Contributes a seasonal forage pressure to `WLD-ECOLOGY-004` without claiming
  to be the sole cause of migration.
- Distinguishes a guild from a species, monoculture, or superorganism.
- Preserves real disturbance, recovery, and depletion costs.
- Uses Earth biology only as private design inspiration; no Earth organism is
  copied into in-world taxonomy.
- Creates no CAL0 spawn point, refill node, guaranteed harvest, reward, Skill,
  trait payload, specialised Mana organ, or progression mechanic.
- No contradiction or retcon identified.

## Residual unknowns

- Exact timing, productivity, nutrient content, chemistry, toxins, and defensive compounds
- Fire, flood, frost, drought, grazing, disease, and pest tolerances and recovery rates
- Distribution across soils, slopes, shorelines, wetlands, clearings, and corridors
- Complementary vegetation, fungi or analogues, decomposers, parasites, and mutualists
- Human food, fodder, fibre, fuel, medicine, cultivation, ownership, and cultural meanings
