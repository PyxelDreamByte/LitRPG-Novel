# WLD-FLORA-003 — Basin Reserve-Web Fragment Viability

## Purpose

Resolve the fragment-survival and independent-patch frontier left open by
`WLD-FLORA-002` without prematurely defining quantitative thresholds, detailed
anatomy, wound response, reconnection, or the guild's complete reproductive cycle.

## Existing constraints

- Accepted world authority: `WLD-FLORA-001` and `WLD-FLORA-002`
- CAL0 rules: WLD1.1D and WLD2.1A+D
- Story contract: `work://series/project-hearthway-main` remains proposed
- Deliberately unresolved: exact thresholds, timescale, anatomy, physiology,
  environmental tolerances, species variation, and human knowledge or use

## Options retained

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FLORA-FRAGMENT-A | Survival requires enough viable modules, reserves, and functioning connections | Conditional resilience with persistent costs | Requires later threshold and timescale modelling | Selected |
| FLORA-FRAGMENT-B | Almost every living fragment can establish | Rapid recolonisation | Turns damage into effortless propagation | Not selected |
| FLORA-FRAGMENT-C | Isolated fragments cannot become independent | Strong parent-patch continuity | Removes fragment-founded patches | Not selected |

## Selected proposed claims

1. Severance alone does not guarantee that a reserve-web fragment survives or
   becomes an independent patch.
2. A severed fragment can persist only when it retains enough viable modules,
   reserves, and functioning connections to sustain itself.
3. A viable fragment can slowly stabilize as an independent colony patch.
4. An inadequate fragment cannot sustain itself independently and declines and dies.
5. Fragmentation is therefore a possible but non-automatic route to formation
   of a separate patch.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal basis

The retained living structure must support its own ongoing needs after it loses
access to the parent patch. Viability is functional and conditional rather than
granted by the act of severance.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

Fragment survival can produce a local mixture of slowly stabilizing sections,
failing sections, and persistent gaps. Stabilization takes time and remains
limited by finite reserves, living connections, and ordinary environmental support.

### Society and culture

No recognition method, cultivation technique, propagation practice, ownership
rule, terminology, management custom, or cultural interpretation is established.

### History and chronology

No origin, evolutionary history, seasonal schedule, historic use, or past
distribution of fragment-founded patches is established.

### Story implications

Cutting or breaking a network need not produce either instant death or an
instant new patch. Characters may later encounter fragments in different
states of decline or stabilization, but no character knowledge is established.

### Character-knowledge implications

The viability rule is setting truth. Which people can diagnose a fragment,
predict its outcome, or deliberately exploit the process remains unknown.

### CAL0 implications

This is an ordinary-world biological persistence and propagation route under
WLD2.1A+D. Ecological independence does not create a personal System actor or
duplicate identity under WLD1.1D. It creates no spawn, refill, XP, level,
reward, Skill, class, or progression mechanism.

## Residual unknowns

- Minimum functional module count, reserve condition, connection topology, and
  any interactions among those requirements
- Stabilization timescale, mortality curve, wound response, and seasonal variation
- Reconnection, fusion, competition, parasitism, and disease transmission
- Whether a fragment is genetically or developmentally distinct, and how
  biological individuality should be described
- Survival of displaced fragments and other reproductive or dispersal routes
- Mana use, System appraisal, human knowledge, and deliberate propagation

## Recommendation

Use `FLORA-FRAGMENT-A`. It preserves local resilience without allowing damage
to become effortless multiplication or ordinary ecology to behave like refill.
