## Option A — DOMAIN-SPLIT

The parent inheritances persist by domain. Forms the settlement attributes to the imperial
inheritance predominate in public administration, record keeping, walls-and-works, and defence;
forms attributed to the original inheritance predominate in household, seasonal, land, and
hospitality life. Predominance is tendency, not rule — both inheritances surface in every domain,
and every strand of WLD-CULTURE-001's braid works in both registers, keeping its own distinct
household traditions inside the household domain. Public standing tends to run on whatever
public-standing logic the imperial inheritance supplies; household standing on whatever
household-standing logic the original inheritance supplies — which is the parent-frame siblings' to
fix (option-local illustration only, presupposing no sibling option: an office-and-record public
register beside a hearth-and-hospitality household one). The charter-oath compact
(WLD-CULTURE-002) sits astride the split and is popularly read as
deliberately containing both — hearth-rooted assembly read as original-derived, witnessed
adjudication and fallible record-keeping as imperial-derived. A resident reading, not a compact
term: it makes the designed succession ambiguities feel like boundary questions — argued along the
split, resolved by nothing here.

Individuals switch practice-registers daily — manners, conduct, work custom — as they cross
domains; whether speech splits the same way is wholly the language-landscape proposal's question.
Friction lives at the boundaries: whether a matter belongs to the record or the hearth is the
settlement's characteristic argument class — a standing tension, never an occurred incident.
Whatever reliability-esteem the imperial register carries and original-attributed esteem for
keeping faith with kin reinforce wherever reliability is prized; they conflict where the public
register's own duty meets guest-and-kin obligation — what one register honours as loyalty the other
suspects as favouritism, in both directions, neither simply right. Option-local illustration only,
presupposing no sibling option: esteem for keeping the record set against guest-and-kin obligation
as impartial procedure against favour; where the selected imperial frame supplies a
personal-obligation ethic instead, the same tension runs as which obligation governs a public
matter. Refuge life sharpens and re-anchors a settlement virtue,
answering the common call — dropping whatever domain owns your day when a closure or watch call
goes out, that call's authority, trigger, roster, and procedure all remaining open — and a
settlement shame, refusing refuge: having been taken in behind these walls, then holding back
shelter, place, or labour from those taken in since. Whether either is newly minted here or
inherited from a parent register that already carries a refuge- or common-duty virtue depends on
which parent frames are selected; refuge life gives both their settlement-specific edge either way.

Faith blends by domain assignment across non-lifecycle observance, as belief practice only:
original-attributed observances hold seasonal, land, and ordinary household occasions;
imperial-attributed observance styles persist around oaths, records, the settlement's own
walls-and-works, and defence — coexisting rather than merging, meeting politely at boundary
occasions, confirming no metaphysics. Remnant ground stays outside the assignment: avoidance of it
remains practical, not ritual (WLD-LOCATION-004), and this record establishes no otherwise. The
lifecycle occasions — birth, coming of age, marriage, death — are deliberately not sorted here:
which domain or register each belongs to, and whether the hybrid does one register, the other, or
both, is the lifecycle-rites sibling's to fix, and the domain split neither assigns nor excludes
them. Dress reads by domain: a settlement person visibly dresses for
the domain of the day — land-and-household wear reading original-derived, public and defence
occasions carrying imperial-derived marks — so outsiders read the same person differently by where
they meet them. No garment, textile, or costume detail is fixed.

Each inheritance is respected within its home domains and contested at the boundaries; neither is
despised by default. Through the domain lens the outlet settlement (WLD-LOCATION-005) reads as a
place where one domain swallowed the rest — its works admired, its manners held to have crowded
out the hearth — a perception colouring the standing cross-lake rivalry (WLD-EVENT-001) as
argument material, adding no event. Founders remember the split as negotiated relief, each
inheritance given a room of its own; the walls-born treat it as natural grammar, grow impatient
with boundary policing, and some blur the domains in ways elders read as drift — a generational
tension, not an occurred dispute.

## Option B — FOUNDING-FUSION — selected

The settlement's founding-era disposition is remembered as fusion — inherited forms worked into
settlement-specific composites — a remembered pattern, variously and disputably held, with no
founding occasion, decision, deliberation, or event fixed. The compact is remembered as the model
case: an oath sworn in what residents call the original manner, joined to witnessed, fallibly kept
records in what they call the imperial manner
(WLD-CULTURE-002; no oath text or written charter exists — the memory of the founding pairing is
itself a cultural reading). Status follows the fusion: standing accrues to households and persons
seen to embody the joined way, while purists of either inheritance keep real but narrower prestige
inside their own circles. The young composite identity is conscious and contested, claimed by some
residents and disputed by others. Under this blend the succession ambiguities read as a question
of re-performance — whether a successor must re-enact the remembered fusion or may lean on one
inheritance's form — sharpening the designed ambiguity, resolving nothing.

The virtue field organises around joining. Imperial-attributed esteem for the deliberate, designed
act and original-attributed esteem for the binding spoken word reinforce each other in the fusion
ideal of the sworn-together work. Both conflict with each parent register's fidelity virtue: what
a purist honours as keeping the received form whole, the fusion register suspects as clinging —
and the purists return the suspicion, holding the composites shallow; neither side simply right,
both positions alive inside every strand. The settlement virtue this blend carries is the joined
work: a thing — an agreement, a household, a craft piece — made properly of both inheritances and
holding. Its shame is unravelling: publicly pulling a shared thing apart into its parent halves, or
claiming one inheritance's precedence at a common table. Whether either is newly minted here or
sharpened and re-anchored from a parent register that already prizes made-and-kept work depends on
which parent frames are selected.

Faith blends by syncretism as belief practice: deliberately joined observance composites hold the
common occasions, while devout traditionalists of both inheritances keep older forms in household
privacy — coexistence beneath the composite, nothing confirmed. Dress shows a recognisable
settlement-specific composite that outsiders read as "hillfort" rather than as either parent;
marked parent dress survives for household and purist occasions. No observance content, garment,
or costume detail is fixed.

Both parents are honoured as ancestors of the fusion; neither is despised by default, though
purists of each resent the other's share. The hillfort reads the outlet settlement as the unblended
sibling — what its own remembered disposition did not become — at once a foil for settlement pride
and an object of quiet admiration for unbroken imperial continuity; the rivalry (WLD-EVENT-001)
gives the perception its
competitive edge as claims of standing, tendencies only. Founders remember fusion as choice and
labour and hold it a little anxiously; the walls-born wear it as plain identity, more confidently
than its makers — while a visible few reach back past the fusion for "purer" parent forms, as
fashion or protest, unsettling founders who remember why the joining was made.

## Option C — PRAGMATIC PATCHWORK

No settlement-wide blend logic exists. Each household and strand braids its own mix of the two
inheritances; the settlement's identity is the braid practice itself — the standing habit of
working, defending, and celebrating across unlike households — rather than any shared content.
Common institutions are deliberately thin and procedural, and the compact (WLD-CULTURE-002) is
read by residents as exactly that: the one common floor everyone stands on precisely because it
presumes no shared way of life beyond hearth, oath, and consent. Status logic is plural — a
household ranked high on one neighbour's scale ranks middling on another's — and the only
settlement-wide CULTURAL status scale THIS RECORD establishes is procedural: a reputation for
keeping terms. That scale sits outside the compact's offices and its assembly standing rule, whose
real, actually exercised authority WLD-CULTURE-002 owns and this record cites without restating;
outside the quiet inequality from hoarded advantage WLD-CULTURE-001 already establishes, which cuts
across the patchwork regardless; and outside whatever settlement-wide speech-prestige axes the
language-landscape sibling establishes, which likewise cut across the patchwork and are wholly that
run's to fix. The designed ambiguities are tolerated as the price of thinness, and the
approaching succession presses hard on them: the thin floor must bear its full weight — a pressure
state, not an event.

The virtue field is thin in public and thick at home. Parent virtues reinforce or conflict
household by household; publicly, virtue-talk is kept spare so no household's mix is imposed on a
mixed crew. The settlement virtue this blend carries is keeping terms: scrupulous performance of
negotiated agreements across household lines, the patchwork's load-bearing habit — newly minted
here if no parent register supplies it, and sharpened and re-anchored to cross-household life if a
selected parent frame already prizes kept oaths, kept charter, or honest dealing between bodies.
Its shame is presuming: treating one's own household blend as the settlement's norm — correcting
another hearth's manners or customs as if a settlement standard existed.

Faith blends by coexistence as belief practice: plural observances stand side by side, common
institutions keep religious content off the common floor — a bounded negative closure of
WLD-CULTURE-001's open common-institution observance space, which this option's claim boundary
must name if C is selected — and guesting at another household's observance is courtesy, never
conversion; nothing is confirmed. Dress shows no settlement style — a settlement person visibly
reads as "from the mixed hillfort" precisely by the unpredictability of the mix, and
outsiders learn to read the variety itself as the place's mark. No observance content, garment, or
costume detail is fixed.

Attitudes to the parents distribute household by household along WLD-CULTURE-001's accepted
spectrum, with no collective settlement position; neither inheritance is despised by default
because no default exists. The collective posture toward the outlet settlement is thin and
terms-shaped, so the standing rivalry (WLD-EVENT-001) is argued at the hillfort chiefly over
exchange terms and claims of standing — tendencies, never incidents, no monetary or trade fact
created. The founders' preference for thin common ground is variously remembered — hard-won wisdom
or failure of nerve — with no founding event fixed; among the walls-born, some experience the
patchwork as freedom and others as absence, quietly hungering for a shared identity that does not
exist — a generational tension the succession will stress.
