# WLD-LOCATION-005 — Outlet Industrial-Remnant Settlement

## Purpose

Give the initial region a bounded second settlement — an industrial,
primarily imperial-remnant counterpart community across the basin lake from
the initial ridge-spur settlement — so later culture, trade, rivalry, and
story work can use its existence, character, and story-start relationship
without prematurely creating names, maps, works, resources detail,
population, institutions, shore works, events, or any future conflict.

## Direct Author specification

No option set was drafted or displayed for this record. During the
2026-08-17 multiple-choice acceptance session, the Author specified this
settlement directly rather than choosing from displayed options, following
the WLD-LOCATION-001A direct water-disposition precedent for directly added
Author content: a second settlement in the area is built around an imperial
industrial village, with running water and metals in the hills; it has grown
due to its defensible location, water, and industrial capability; it lies on
the other side of the basin lake from the ridge-spur settlement, near the
mouth of the outlet toward the ocean; it is primarily an imperial-remnant
community culturally, in contrast to the ridge-spur settlement's blend of
original and imperial cultures; and standing tensions and trade rivalry
exist between the two settlements at story start.

This proposal records that direct capture as bounded qualitative claims. No
competing option was presented or rejected.

## Proposed claims

1. A second settlement stands in the initial region, built around a reused
   imperial industrial village nucleus — an instance of WLD-LOCATION-004's
   reused imperial works node class.
2. Running water at the settlement site provides a qualitative water-supply
   and water-power advantage.
3. Qualitatively workable metal resources occur in the hills near the
   settlement.
4. The settlement has grown on the combined strength of its defensible
   location, its water, and its industrial capability.
5. The settlement lies on the other side of the basin lake from the initial
   ridge-spur settlement, toward the seaward end of the outlet, near the
   outlet's mouth toward the ocean.
6. The settlement is culturally a primarily imperial-remnant community, in
   contrast to the initial ridge-spur settlement's blend of original and
   imperial cultural strands.
7. Standing tensions and a trade rivalry exist between the two settlements
   at story start; WLD-EVENT-001 governs that standing pressure.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal and material basis

The settlement's growth rests on ordinary advantages: a defensible site, a
qualitative running-water advantage for supply and power, workable metal
resources in nearby hills, and a reusable imperial works nucleus. These are
qualitative relationships. No watercourse, mechanism, works detail, ore,
deposit, quantity, mining method, fortification, or capacity is established,
and no advantage is guaranteed reliable, safe, or permanent.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

The position on the other side of the basin lake, toward the seaward end of
the outlet and near its mouth toward the ocean, is a qualitative relation
consistent with WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001 and WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002, not a mapped
location. The settlement does not touch the outlet throat or mixed-reach
feature sequence, and no claim about migration, passage, or the mixed reach
is made; WLD-ECOLOGY-006, WLD-ECOLOGY-009, and WLD-ECOLOGY-010 stay
untouched.

### Society and culture

Primarily imperial-remnant is a qualitative cultural-composition claim, in
contrast to the ridge-spur settlement's blend of original and imperial
strands. WLD-HISTORY-001, WLD-CULTURE-001, and WLD-CULTURE-002 remain the
authorities for the period and the strands. No language, law, belief,
practice, institution, leadership, government, or internal uniformity is
established, and primarily does not mean exclusively. Cultural identity and
contrast are human self-description and interpretation, not natural System
behaviour.

### History and chronology

No founding date, imperial-era function, reuse history, growth sequence,
population history, or chronology is established beyond the qualitative
statement that the settlement has grown to more than a minor hamlet by story
start.

### Story implications

- The second settlement offers an industrial, culturally contrasting
  counterpart community and a governed story-start rivalry for later scenes.
- Every visit, exchange, dispute, observation, journey, and outcome requires
  its own evidence.
- The rivalry can pressure later choices under WLD-EVENT-001's governance,
  but no incident, escalation, resolution, or fighting follows
  automatically, and no future conflict or timeline claim is made.

### Character-knowledge implications

The settlement and its relations are author-side setting truth. Characters
know the settlement, its people, its capabilities, or the rivalry only
through an available source. Familiarity does not reveal composition,
intentions, resources, or a System value.

### CAL0 implications

This is an ordinary world-level location extension under CAL0 WLD1.2D and
WLD2.1A+D. Water power, metalworking, growth, trade, and rivalry retain
their causal requirements and consequences. No CAL0 change, System

trade mechanic, faction mechanic, reputation value, spawn, reset, automatic
