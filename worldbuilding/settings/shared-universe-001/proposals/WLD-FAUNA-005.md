# WLD-FAUNA-005 — Lineage IV foundational body plan

## Purpose

Fourth Phase 2 scaffold: give Lineage IV — the fully aquatic flexible-trunk
fin-fan lineage — a lineage-level body plan. Unlike the other lineages, this
one already has one developed member: the basin lake's mass breeder,
governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-008`. The design question is
therefore inverted: which of that animal's features are ancestral to the
whole lineage, and which are its own specialisations? `WLD-ECOLOGY-007`
explicitly leaves this open, stating that its body plan establishes no
taxonomic rank, invariant morphology, or range of related organisms.

## Existing constraints

- Accepted world canon: `WLD-FAUNA-001` (Lineage IV slot: fully aquatic
  flexible-trunk fin-fan animals, anchored by the mass breeder),
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` (mass breeder: streamlined laterally flexible trunk on
  springy cartilage, three pairs of fin-fans, muscular tail, wedge-shaped
  mineralised head shield, respiratory folds that may be exposed or
  protected, ordinary Mana-supported tissue reinforcement),
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-008` (mass breeder: dedicated ion-exchange organs, wax-like-gel
  buoyancy chambers)
- CAL0 rules: WLD1.2D and WLD2.1A+D
- Deliberately unresolved boundaries: species, names, taxonomy, sizes, local
  presence, Mana patterns beyond the mass breeder's bounded case
- Hard constraint: nothing here may retcon `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` or `-008`

## Proposed claims

Common to all options:

1. Lineage IV is fully aquatic throughout; no branch is established as
   terrestrial or amphibious.
2. Its members occupy the full aquatic size and niche range, from small
   forms to the largest animals in their waters.
3. The mass breeder governed by `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-008` is
   one specialised member, and its records continue to govern it unchanged.

## Alternatives — how much of the mass breeder is ancestral

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Trunk-and-fan core ancestral; shield, fin-fan count, salt organs and gel chambers are derived | Strong but non-restrictive lineage identity; leaves room for varied branches; makes the mass breeder a specialist rather than the template | Branch variety must be developed later | High at this grain |
| B | The full mass-breeder package is ancestral | Very consistent lineage; every member shielded and gel-buoyed | Makes the lineage narrow and uniformly armoured; every new species inherits heavy machinery | High |
| C | Minimal core: only aquatic habit and fin-fans are ancestral | Maximum freedom | Weakest identity; the lineage becomes a label rather than a body plan | High |

### Option A — Trunk-and-fan core (recommended)

The lineage-wide inheritance is the locomotor and support core: a laterally
flexible trunk carried on springy cartilage-analogue support, paired fin-fans
as the primary control surfaces, a muscular tail providing propulsion, and
aquatic respiration through fold surfaces. Everything else the mass breeder
carries is derived and branch-specific: the exact three-pair fin-fan count,
the wedge-shaped mineralised head shield, the dedicated ion-exchange organs,
and the wax-like-gel chambers. Under this reading the mass breeder is a
migratory specialist — shielded, salt-tolerant, gel-trimmed — within a
lineage whose ordinary members may have different fin-fan counts, no shield,
and no sea-to-freshwater machinery at all.

### Option B — Full-package ancestral

Shield, six fin-fans, salt organs, and gel chambers are all ancestral, with
branches reducing them. Consistent, but it makes every Lineage IV animal a
variation on one heavily equipped design.

### Option C — Minimal core

Only "fully aquatic with fin-fans" is lineage-wide. Maximum freedom, but the
lineage stops describing a body plan.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal basis

Deep aquatic divergence; ordinary evolution. Derived features arise where
their costs are paid for — shields where protection matters, salt organs
where salinity is crossed, gel chambers where trim is worth its upkeep.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

A fully aquatic lineage is bounded by water: connected waters permit
radiation, isolated waters strand populations, and no branch escapes overland.
That makes the outlet route already established for the basin ecologically
load-bearing for this lineage in a way it is not for others.

### Story implications

Lake, river, and sea scenes gain a coherent family of shapes — flexible
bodies, fin-fan control surfaces, tail propulsion — of which the mass breeder
is one conspicuous, heavily equipped example rather than the norm.

### Character-knowledge implications

None established.

### CAL0 implications

Application only; ordinary ecology.

## Consistency review

- Tensions or contradictions: none identified. `WLD-ECOLOGY-007` reserves
  taxonomic rank, invariant morphology, and related-organism range, so
  assigning its distinctive features to a derived rather than ancestral level
  extends rather than alters it
- Retcons required: none
- Validation still needed: standard repository gate

## Residual unknowns

- Species, names, taxonomy, sizes, and branch inventory
- Fin-fan counts and arrangements outside the mass breeder's three pairs
- Which branches carry shields, salt organs, or gel chambers
- Reproduction across the lineage; the mass breeder's mass spawning is its own
- Freshwater, brackish, and marine distribution; senses, feeding, and Mana
  patterns beyond the mass breeder's bounded case

## Recommendation

Option A. It gives the lineage a real, recognisable body plan without
conscripting every future member into the mass breeder's specialised
equipment, respects `WLD-ECOLOGY-007`'s explicit refusal to fix
related-organism range, and reserves shields, salt organs, and gel chambers
as costed adaptations that appear where they are earned.
