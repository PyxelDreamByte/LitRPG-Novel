# WLD-ECOLOGY-006 — Basin-Lake Spring Breeding Pulse

## Purpose

Establish a coherent first aquatic seasonal event for the initial basin without
prematurely selecting species anatomy, population numbers, detailed fisheries,
or a specialised System or Mana mechanism.

## Existing constraints

- Setting authority: `WLD-SETTING-001A`
- Basin ecosystem frame: `WLD-ECOLOGY-005`
- Physical lake, outlet, spring melt, autumn storms, and freezing:
  `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001`
- CAL0 creature and ordinary-world ecology: `WLD1.1D`, `WLD1.2D`, and
  `WLD2.1A+D`
- Story workspace:
  `stories/series/project-hearthway-main/project-hearthway-main.work-manifest.json`
- Design source:
  `stories/series/project-hearthway-main/workbench/runs/2026-08-14-basin-lake-spring-breeding-pulse/proposal.md`

The existing records establish a freshwater through-flow lake connected to the
sea, a strong spring melt, a coupled seasonal basin ecology, and ordinary
finite life cycles. They do not establish an aquatic migrant, named animal
family, detailed lake food web, fishing culture, or creature-specific Mana use.

## Proposed claims

1. The freshwater through-flow lake is biologically productive but spatially
   and seasonally varied.
2. The spring freshet creates seasonally favourable passage conditions from the
   sea and marks the start of the lake's seasonal rise in productivity.
3. An unnamed mass-breeding migrant enters first and deposits dense ribbons or
   clusters of eggs onto sturdy plants and mineral surfaces across river-mouth
   shallows, protected bays, submerged ridges, and flooded vegetation margins
   rather than relying on one nursery ground.
4. Healthy adults generally return toward the sea after breeding, while the
   breeding-and-return cycle still carries meaningful adult mortality.
5. A distinct family-care migrant arrives later, remains for one warm season
   while rearing young, and escorts surviving young seaward before autumn storms
   and first freezes.
6. The family-care migrant's young consume eggs and juveniles of the first
   migrant as an important but non-exclusive food source.
7. Both migrants compete with resident lake fauna during their lake phases.
8. Humans, aquatic predators, terrestrial shore hunters, and bird-like fishing
   organisms exploit the seasonal abundance.
9. Reproductive risk is dispersed across places and times so that no single
   ordinary predator, flood, or fishery routinely destroys the entire generation.
10. Exceptional or compound recruitment failures remain possible.
11. The dispersed egg-deposition pattern creates feeding grounds that shift
    across the lake over the season.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal basis

Snowmelt and rising river flow provide a seasonal passage window between the
sea and lake without yet fixing the exact route. The same seasonal transition
begins a broader lake productivity rise. Overlapping but offset arrival and
rearing schedules allow one migrant's
early life stages to become part of the other's juvenile diet without making
that prey link exclusive or sufficient by itself.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

- `Freshet` establishes a seasonal passage condition, not the exact hydraulic,
  salinity, sensory, or navigational mechanism.
- Egg deposition is dispersed among several qualitative habitat classes; their
  number, map, physical dimensions, and biological composition remain open.
- The eggs are deposited onto sturdy plants and mineral surfaces, but adhesion,
  entanglement, draping, anchoring, and other retention mechanics remain open.
- Flooded vegetation is not identified as the reserve-web guild or any other
  named flora.
- Food, nursery space, disease, parasites, predators, weather, density, human
  harvest, and adult mortality can all affect recruitment.
- Family care reduces some risks but does not guarantee juvenile survival.
- No single ordinary predator, flood, or fishery routinely destroys the entire
  generation; this does not establish a sustainable harvest rate or prevent
  exceptional or compound recruitment collapse.
- The two migrations create a lake pulse without becoming the sole engine of
  basin productivity.

### Society and culture

Local humans exploit the seasonal abundance, but this claim establishes no
fishing technique, access right, institution, law, trade, ritual, preservation
method, sustainable quota, or equal cultural interpretation.

### Story and character-knowledge implications

The migration can change the sounds, movements, predator concentrations, human
activity, and risks around parts of the lake for a substantial season. A scene
must still establish where the viewpoint is, what evidence is visible, what the
character has learned, and what remains uncertain. Author-side knowledge of the
full cycle does not become automatic protagonist knowledge.

### CAL0 implications

These are ordinary living populations reproducing, migrating, competing,
feeding, ageing, and dying under CAL0. Biological mass breeding is not a System
spawn mechanism. Egg ribbons, cohorts, and family groups have no pooled
identity, level, XP, Skill, or reward by default. Predation and consumption do
not automatically transfer attributes, Skills, levels, Mana, traits, or power.

## Alternatives

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LAKE-MIGRATION-A — Littoral-limited single run | Most production is near shore or inflows, supporting one short mass-breeding run | Simple and easy to model | Underuses the large lake and provides a narrow seasonal event | High before species design |
| LAKE-MIGRATION-B — Productive lake, mass breeder only | The whole lake contains productive habitats, but only the mass-breeding migrant is selected | Strong nutrient and harvest pulse | Limited behavioural contrast and short-lived story presence | High before species design |
| LAKE-MIGRATION-C — Productive lake, family-care migrant only | A migrant remains to rear young through the warm season | Sustained seasonal behaviour and family defence | Lacks the large early-life-stage pulse that links many consumers | High before species design |
| LAKE-MIGRATION-D — Productive mosaic with overlapping migrants | Offset mass breeding and family care connect passage, predation, competition, and human exploitation | Rich seasonal ecology without one sole engine | Requires later route, timing, and recruitment-budget checks | Moderate after organism design |

## Consistency review

- The proposal uses the through-flow lake and spring melt already established by
  `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001` without fixing the outlet's detailed hydraulics.
- It implements one lake pulse under `WLD-ECOLOGY-005` without replacing its
  open-ground, woodland, wet-margin, or other lake processes.
- It is distinct from the Lineage II terrestrial corridor in
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-004` and introduces no dependency on that lineage.
- Egg ribbons describe egg placement only and create no ribbon-shaped animal or
  relationship to Lineage II ribbon forms.
- Flooded vegetation creates no approved dependency on `WLD-FLORA-001` through
  `WLD-FLORA-003`.
- Ordinary reproduction, migration, mortality, and finite recruitment comply
  with CAL0 and create no System change.
- No contradiction or retcon has been identified.

## Residual unknowns

- Names, taxonomy, ancestry, anatomy, body plans, sizes, senses, and cognition
- Exact route, salinity transition, passage barriers, swimming ability, and navigation
- Exact timing, triggers, duration, arrival lag, and population scale
- Egg anatomy, fertilisation, fecundity, hatching time, and nursery density
- Egg retention and the exact placement mechanism on plants and mineral surfaces
- Adult mortality cause and rate, repeat breeding, and return fidelity
- Family-care reproductive mode, carers, brood size, and care behaviour
- Adult diets, juvenile diet shares, competitors, predators, disease, and parasites
- Net nutrient transfer, carcass effects, carrying capacity, and recruitment budget
- Human techniques, knowledge, law, access, trade, ritual, and harvest pressure
- Mana anatomy, special abilities, System templates, Skills, levels, and appraisal

## Recommendation

Select `LAKE-MIGRATION-D`. It gives the large lake a sustained, observable
seasonal ecology while preserving spatial variation, resident competitors,
multiple mortality channels, human participation, and later freedom to design
two original animal families.
