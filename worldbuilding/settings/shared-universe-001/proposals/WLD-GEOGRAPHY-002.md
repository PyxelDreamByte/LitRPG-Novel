# WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002 — Initial Outlet Mixed-Reach Sequence

## Purpose

Establish the outlet's dominant qualitative physical sequence beneath the
naturally defensible gap in WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001, so later hydrology and ecology
work can test WLD-ECOLOGY-010's passage requirements against an accepted
feature set without assuming that the route is usable.

## Proposed claims

1. A narrow bedrock-controlled throat opens downstream into one connected
   mixed reach.
2. The mixed reach contains irregular boulder steps, lateral or side shelves,
   and local holding pockets.
3. The reach is heterogeneous rather than one uniform channel or regular
   feature chain.
4. The boulder steps and side shelves are candidate, state-dependent
   bed-or-margin contact surfaces against which WLD-ECOLOGY-010 passage can be tested.
5. Holding pockets are local channel recesses or pools that may be less exposed
   to the main flow under some conditions and can therefore be tested as
   potentially lower-velocity locations.
6. A coherent migration corridor means only that the throat and mixed reach
   belong to one connected physical system and supply a candidate route
   architecture for later feasibility work.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal and material basis

Bedrock-controlled establishes that resistant substrate constrains the narrow
throat, not its exact lithology, age, origin, geometry, or stability. Boulder
steps, shelves, and pockets are qualitative channel-form classes within the
downstream reach. Their formation histories are not selected.

Water, sediment, boulders, ice, debris, and organic material remain subject to
ordinary hydrological and geomorphic processes. Feature exposure, wetting,
erosion, deposition, obstruction, and flow interaction can vary with state.
The record introduces no source of matter, usable energy, or hydrological
renewal.

### Geography and ecology

The feature set creates places that can be evaluated as possible contacts or
less-exposed local water, but it does not establish that the features align
into a continuous support or rest chain. It does not establish that any pocket
has low velocity at a particular time, that any surface is wetted or
load-bearing when needed, or that any organism can use the sequence.

Route space, water state, accessible surfaces, pocket volume, and biological
energy remain finite and condition dependent. Exact route capacity, seasonal
usability, injury, mortality, recruitment, and recovery remain later ecology
and hydrology questions.

### Society and culture

The selected sequence establishes no crossing, road, bridge, quay, fishery,
fortification, settlement, boundary, owner, toll, law, name, practice, belief,
or human use. Naturally defensible remains a terrain affordance inherited from
WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001, not evidence of occupation or control.

### History and chronology

The sequence establishes no date, exact geological history, glacial episode,
construction, cultural memory, ruin, or previous use. Its relationship to the
broader river–glacial history remains compatible but unresolved.
