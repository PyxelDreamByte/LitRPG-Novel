# WLD-ECOLOGY-001 — Lineage II Primary Ecology

## Purpose

Give the proposed crown and ribbon branches of Lineage II a shared ecological
ancestry, distinct feeding mechanisms, and grounded effects on the lake-basin
landscape and nearby human settlement.

## Existing constraints

- Shared-setting authority: `author-decision://world/WLD-SETTING-001A`
- Work: `work://series/project-hearthway-main`
- Development input: `workbench/proposals/project-hearthway-foundation-capture.md`
- CAL0 boundaries: WLD1.2D and WLD2.1A+D; creatures retain real anatomy,
  development, food webs, reproduction, and ecological consequences
- Deliberately unresolved: taxonomy, names, exact species, digestion, migration
  calendar, specialised Mana organs, domestication, hunting, and legal status

## Options reviewed

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L2-ECO-A | Crown and ribbon branches are divergent vegetation architects | Makes mouth divergence functional; supports open, woodland, and lakeshore habitats; creates seasonal settlement pressure | Must remain distinct from familiar Earth megaherbivores | Selected |
| L2-ECO-B | Both branches instinctively tend a narrow plant-fungus symbiosis | Highly alien and materially rich | Risks making one symbiosis dominate the region and resembling agriculture | Not selected as the family-wide model |
| L2-ECO-C | Both branches are seasonal mixed harvesters and plant-dominant omnivores | Flexible resource pulses and sharper conflict | Weakens their landscape-engineering role and risks generic monster behaviour | Not selected as the family-wide model |

## Selected proposed claims

1. Crown forms use short, reinforced mouth-rays to split and process tough
   mature stalks, brush, and woody growth.
2. Crown feeding and movement help maintain open corridors and vegetation mosaics.
3. Ribbon forms use longer tactile mouth-rays to sort and harvest shoots,
   fruiting bodies, and lakeshore or woodland-edge vegetation.
4. Ribbon feeding shapes woodland and wet-margin vegetation at a finer scale.
5. The branches' seasonal movements can affect roads, fields, woodland edges,
   soil fertility, and settlement defences.

## Consistency review

- Chronology: large, slow-breeding populations can recover unevenly over the
  seventy years since imperial suppression weakened.
- Geography: linked basins, corridors, woodland margins, wetlands, and the lake
  provide connected but differentiated feeding landscapes.
- Logistics: crown forms require abundant bulk vegetation and space; ribbon
  forms can exploit patchier high-quality growth.
- Culture: people may value or fear the same animals differently by livelihood,
  season, damage, and ecological benefit.
- CAL0: this is anatomy and ecology only. It creates no universal creature
  level, XP source, reward, or new Mana mechanic.
- Knowledge: local practical knowledge need not include the correct evolutionary explanation.
- Contradictions or retcons: none identified.

## Residual unknowns

- Exact oral anatomy and tissue replacement
- Digestive structures and symbionts
- Annual routes and reproductive timing
- Species-level omnivory or specialised symbioses
- Mana reinforcement and rare specialised organs
- Human names, hunting practices, uses, taboos, and coexistence strategies
