## Proposed claims

1. The selected qualitative passage behaviour is a margin-ratchet procession.
2. Where suitable support is available, paddle-columns brace in successive
   groups while a travelling margin wave drives a short advance.
3. The vault settles low between surges.
4. The directly observable rhythm is
   `settle → ripple → brace → surge → settle`.
5. Before advances, the caregiving form normally attempts to regroup
   mobile young beneath the refuge.
6. During autumn descent, the same structures brake and steer
   current-assisted slides.
7. The behaviour requires a compatible bed-or-margin contact and
   resting-opportunity chain, but does not establish that such a chain exists.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal and material basis

Successive bracing establishes a bounded relationship between the
paddle-columns and travelling margin wave during difficult passage: supported
columns bear load while the wave contributes to a short advance. It does not
specify the exact number or order of engaged columns, contact anatomy,
adhesion, suction, stroke, internal pressure cycle, force, distance, frequency,
speed, efficiency, or usable-energy cost.

Settling low describes a pause between surges, not a newly accepted anchoring
organ, bottom-resting anatomy, burying behaviour, sleep state, fixed pose, or
guaranteed contact with the bed. Braking and steering describe organism-side
control during a current-assisted autumn slide, not passive drift or proof of a
particular channel or current.

### Geography and ecology

The strategy is conditional on a compatible sequence of supports and resting
opportunities. No accepted outlet bed, margin, eddy, holding pocket, depth,
width, gradient, discharge, current, tidal reach, salinity front, substrate,
distance, travel time, seasonal water level, route capacity, or passability is
created here.

The card's warning that passage may be slow and conspicuous, and that abrasion,
paddle-column injury, fatigue, crowding, or broken resting opportunities may
stop it, is retained as a qualitative feasibility concern rather than a fixed
performance, event, or rate claim. Tracks or disturbed feeding trails are
possible only where local substrate and feeding conditions can preserve them.
Predator ambushes and exposed transitions are plausible story consequences,
not guaranteed events or mapped locations.

Regrouping before an advance is a normal care attempt, not guaranteed
coordination or survival. Young can fail to return, be displaced, or be lost;
the behaviour does not create shared identity, cognition, or perfect
communication.
