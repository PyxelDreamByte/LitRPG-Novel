# WLD-ECOLOGY-009 — Basin-Lake Family-Care Migrant Foundational Body Plan

## Purpose

Resolve the foundational body plan and directly visible caregiving interface
left open for `WLD-ECOLOGY-006`'s family-care migrant while preserving route,
physiology, reproduction, Mana, System, taxonomy, and knowledge unknowns.

This is a forward extension. It does not replace the accepted migration and
population relationships or copy the mass breeder's body plan and physiology.

## Proposed claims

1. The family-care migrant's established caregiving migratory form has a low
   living body organised as a three-lobed, broadly tri-radial vault.
2. A ring of short hydrostatic paddle-columns forms part of its locomotor
   structure.
3. Travelling waves move along the body margin and contribute to locomotion,
   without yet assigning their mechanical relationship to the paddle-columns.
4. The underside of the living vault forms a refuge that moves with the
   caregiving form.
5. Mobile young shelter beneath the vault, spill outward to forage, and
   regroup beneath the refuge.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal and material basis

`Hydrostatic` establishes that internal pressure contributes to the living
support of the paddle-columns. It does not yet choose the fluid, chambering,
fibres, valves, muscles, stiffness, actuation, repair, or failure mechanism.
Travelling margin waves establish local tissue motion that contributes to
locomotion, but the division of work between the moving margin and the
paddle-columns remains open.

The refuge is part of the organism's underside and relocates with it. It does
not create a separate nest, built shelter, sealed pouch, protected water pocket,
or invulnerable enclosure.

### Geography and ecology

The selected option carries a qualitative high-drag concern. The
displayed possibility of passable shallows or bottom-resting outlet stages is a
future feasibility question rather than an accepted route mechanism. Water
route geometry, depth, current, body condition, and resting opportunities are
possible future gates, but their actual values and solutions remain open.
Salinity tolerance and respiratory physiology remain separate open questions.

`WLD-ECOLOGY-006` continues to govern the one-warm-season care interval and the
young's important but non-exclusive consumption of the mass breeder's eggs and
juveniles. Shelter and regrouping reduce some exposure but do not guarantee
survival or establish a population equilibrium.

### Society, culture, and knowledge

No in-world name, classification, observation tradition, fishing or harvesting
practice, material use, law, trade, ritual, folklore, or knowledge level is
established. The full author-side organism model is not automatic protagonist
knowledge.

### CAL0 implications

This is an ordinary biological ecology extension, not a System change. The
caregiving form and its young do not acquire pooled identity, shared levels or
XP, automatic prey-power transfer, a Skill, class, reward, System spawning,
reset, refill, or guaranteed recovery through proximity or care.

## Consistency review

- Three-lobed or tri-radial describes the qualitative vault shape only. It does
  not establish perfect symmetry, three paddle fields, repeated internal organs,
  three identities, or juvenile morphology.
- `Ring` and `short` are qualitative. Exact paddle-column number, spacing,
  dimensions, anatomy, actuation, contact mode, and gait remain open.
- Travelling margin waves are local tissue movement, not environmental waves,
  Mana pulses, sonar, telepathy, an aura, group control, or a specialised ability.
- Mobile refuge establishes no shell, armour, womb, fortification, permanent
  carriage, attachment, provisioning, retrieval, signalling, or guaranteed
  regrouping after every excursion.
- High drag is a qualitative design constraint, not a measured coefficient or
  proof of bottom walking, bottom resting, a shallow outlet, route passability,
  speed, endurance, homing, survival, or guaranteed return.
- The selected form does not inherit the mass breeder's `WLD-ECOLOGY-007`
  anatomy, `WLD-ECOLOGY-008` salt organs or gel chambers, or ordinary Mana
  boundary.
- Finite matter or material and usable-energy inputs, internal transport, heat
  and waste handling, maintenance, injury, disease, recovery, repair,
  reproduction, route capacity, mortality, and carrying capacity remain
  causally binding.
- No reserve-web or other named-flora relationship, Earth-animal homology, CAL0
  change, or circular dependency has been identified.
