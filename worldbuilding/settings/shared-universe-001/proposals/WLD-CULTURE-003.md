## Option A — KIN-HALL AND SEASON-COURT — selected

The extended kin-hall — linked households of one recognised descent web — is the social
unit; a person without a hall is a person in danger. Halls federate through
season-courts: recurring assemblies at fixed customary places, keyed to seasonal
rhythms, where law is spoken, disputes heard, marriages and fosterings arranged, and
goods exchanged. Law is customary precedent recited by a remembered-specialist speaker
class trained by memory-line apprenticeship — authoritative in standing, fallible in
fact, openly arguable at court. Leadership classes: hall-elders, court-ground hosting
lineages, the speaker class. Status runs from full hall-members through dependents and
fostered outsiders to the hall-less, whose protection is a court obligation and a live
moral test. The fortified height is this culture's communal work — refuge in danger,
highest court ground in peace — wall and law one inheritance.

Virtues: hospitality of the hall (the guest eats first); true recall (precedent spoken
whole, against interest if need be); season-duty; refuge-duty (the height denied to
none who flee). Shames: hall-betrayal, false recall, refusing refuge, waste of common
stores. The virtues grind against each other — hospitality against stores-prudence,
true recall against hall loyalty — an old, unresolved argument varying by hall.

Faith orientation (belief only) reveres three classes: ancestors as the source of
precedent, imagined still attentive to their halls; land-and-season powers propitiated
at year-turns and courts; and the System, read as the world's impartial order — the one
witness that never misremembers — every reading of its signs human interpretation.
Dress is layered: woven fibre-cloth body layer, working over-layer, heavy felted or
hide-class outer — material classes only. Belonging-marker class: a woven edge-pattern
on the outer layer marking hall and descent web. Status-marker class: worked
hard-material fasteners, plain to fine. System folk practice follows WLD-CULTURE-001's
layers: everyday use in court-and-witness framing; advantage hoarded as hall property;
a thin specialist tradition inside the speaker class; abundant folk belief — often
wrong — reading interface impressions as ancestral or seasonal favour.

Under the empire, the season-courts were the part it displaced and partly used:
whatever governing and adjudicating instrument the imperial frame supplies took over the
judgement the courts had carried, and gatherings the empire found convenient were turned
to its own uses. The halls folded inward and survived — precedent-speaking as household
memory-keeping, hall marks as family weave, refuge-duty as quiet custom.
Option-local-illustrative only, keyed to whichever WB-RUN-2026-08-17-CULTURE-004 option
the Author selects and established by neither record: under a registration-and-ladder
empire this reads as adjudication passing to imperial administration and court
gatherings co-opted as market and muster occasions; under a patronage empire as court
grounds clienting to a garrison house; under a chartered-body empire as court function
falling inside a chartered body's local grant. The heights lapsed mainly through disuse
under imperial presence and its organised ecological suppression; any active
discouragement is remembered differently and remains open.

## Option B — OATH-WEB

Sworn personal bonds outrank blood: the person one becomes is built of oaths —
fosterage oaths moving children between households, sworn-kin oaths making siblings of
strangers, service oaths binding follower to leader, craft oaths binding learner to
teacher. These are institutional bond classes only; every ceremony form, timing,
officiant, and content — including whether any is marked by rite at all — belongs to the
lifecycle-rites sibling. Households are knots in a layered web, and standing is the
weight and keeping of one's bonds. Law is the law of the oath: wrongs are priced by composition — payment
and public restoration rather than feud — before oath-arbiters, trusted because their
own bonds are heavy and unbroken. Leadership classes: great oath-holders, the arbiter
class, and the wardens — warband lineages sworn to hold the fortified heights for all,
raid-companions turned keepers of refuge. Status runs from the well-bonded through the
lightly bonded to the oath-broken, who are not killed but unmade: no arbiter weighs for
them until they restore.

Virtues: oath-keeping above convenience; open-handed sponsorship (surety, fosterlings);
acceptance of composition; plain speech in swearing (no trick oaths). Shames:
oath-breaking, secret oaths, feud pressed past composition, abandonment of a sworn
dependent. The web's genuine moral problem is its own layering — oaths conflict, and
honouring one may forsake another — so its ethics is a tradition of ranking and
releasing bonds, settled differently by district.

Faith orientation (belief only) reveres whatever witnesses an oath: ancestors as the
dead who still hold their bonds and are believed to resent broken ones; land-and-season
powers sworn by at boundaries and season turns; and the System, read as the deep
register in which every oath is believed written — swearing "before the System" the
gravest form. No oath is System-enforced, no register readable, no resentment
confirmed; sceptics within the culture have always said so. Dress keeps the same
layered functional classes, markers shifted to the web: the belonging-marker class is
the oath-token, a knotted cord worn visibly, privately legible within the bond; the
status-marker class is the token's material quality, common fibre to fine worked
strands gifted by a great oath-holder. System folk practice per WLD-CULTURE-001's
layers: everyday use in bond language; advantage hoarded along craft-oath lines rather
than blood; specialist sign-lore among senior arbiters; widespread folk belief — often
wrong — that interface impressions weigh true and false swearing.

Under the empire, the wardens were the part it broke: whatever the imperial frame did
with armed local lineages ended their standing as keepers of the heights, which were
left empty under imperial presence and its organised ecological suppression —
remembered as disarmament by some lines, as enlistment or slow drift by others. The web
folded into whatever bond forms the imperial frame could recognise, fosterage continuing
as household custom, while the empire found the oath habit useful and bent it to its own
bonds: to some lines the deepest insult, to others proof the forms mattered. Composition
law lost standing to whatever adjudicating instrument the empire supplied but survived
as private settlement between households. Option-local-illustrative only, keyed to
whichever WB-RUN-2026-08-17-CULTURE-004 option the Author selects and established by
neither record: under a registration-and-ladder empire this reads as service oaths
recast as recorded labour and service obligation and loyalty oaths demanded in the old
solemn forms; under a patronage empire as oaths absorbed into patron-client bonds; under
a chartered-body empire as oaths rewritten as membership and indenture under charter.

## Option C — HEARTH-AND-BOUND-LAND

The household hearth is the legal, ritual, and moral unit: to keep a hearth is to be a
full person, and every duty, right, and wrong belongs to a hearth rather than an
individual. Land is held through hearth-bond tenure — not property but obligation
chains of upkeep, boundary-keeping, hospitality, and contribution owed to neighbouring
hearths and the district federation of hearths. Law arises from those chains and is
arbitrated by elder hearth-heads, whose authority is seniority in keeping, not office.
Leadership classes: elder arbiters, hearth-heads, and work-callers coordinating
federation labour. Status runs from established hearths through young and dependent
hearths to the hearthless, who lodge under another's hearth and stand within its
duties. The fortified heights are hearth-federation works — built, stocked, and mended
by obligation chains, belonging to no hearth and owed by all.

Virtues: hearth-duty (none under the roof goes unfed or unclaimed); land-duty (ground
passed on better than received); elder-honouring; boundary-honesty (markers never
moved, shares never shaved). Shames: hearth-betrayal, land-waste, boundary-moving,
casting out a dependent. Frictions are real — land-duty against hospitality's costs,
elder authority against capable younger keepers — argued within, not settled.

Faith orientation (belief only): the hearth as seat of the household's dead — ancestors
believed present at their own fire, honoured there rather than at common shrines; land
powers of particular grounds, waters, and season turns, owed observance as part of
land-duty; the System read as the deep order of allotments — the belief that every
hearth's portion is somehow written. All contested; no destination of the dead, power,
or allotment is confirmed. Dress keeps the layered functional classes, markers at the
hearth: the belonging-marker class is hearth-mark stitching at collar or hem, one mark
per hearth; the status-marker class is the outer mantle itself, whose material quality
and depth mark an established hearth from a young one. System folk practice per
WLD-CULTURE-001's layers: everyday use in allotment language; advantage kept as
hearth-lore passed at the fire; sparse specialist study among long-established elder
lines; folk belief — often wrong — reading interface impressions as signs of a hearth's
standing with its ground and its dead.

Under the empire, tenure was the part it overwrote: whatever holding and
burden-imposing instrument the imperial frame supplies displaced hearth-bond tenure, and
the obligation chains lost legal standing. The hearth folded inward and survived whole —
hearth-marks, fire-side honouring of the dead, hearth-duty and boundary-honesty as
living custom. The empire worked through established hearth-heads as local
intermediaries, leaving a double memory: protectors who softened the burden to some
lines, collaborators who fattened on it to others. Option-local-illustrative only, keyed
to whichever WB-RUN-2026-08-17-CULTURE-004 option the Author selects and established by
neither record: under a registration-and-ladder empire this reads as holding flattened
into household registration and levy with hearth-heads intermediating collection and
count; under a patronage empire as hearths clienting to a garrison house through their
heads; under a chartered-body empire as holding recast as tenancy under a body's grant.
The federation works on the heights lapsed with the chains under imperial presence and
its organised ecological suppression; any active discouragement is remembered
differently and remains open.

## Recommended option

Option A (kin-hall and season-court). It gives the revived hill-fort pattern its most
natural depth — height as refuge and highest court ground — and supplies assembly-habit
and spoken-precedent memory that harmonises with the charter-oath compact as resonance
without claiming descent. Its speaker class and court-and-witness folk tradition also
create the richest fallible-knowledge texture while leaving every sibling option open.
