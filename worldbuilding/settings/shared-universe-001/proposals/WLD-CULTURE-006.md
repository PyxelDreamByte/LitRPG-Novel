## Decision boundary

The Author will choose ONE cross-cutting rite architecture — what the four
lifecycle rite classes (birth, coming of age, marriage and relationships,
death) fundamentally DO in this region's three cultures, at pattern level.
Each option's per-culture emphasis paragraph is OPTION-LOCAL-ILLUSTRATIVE and
is NOT part of the choice: it shows how the architecture might land, while the
culture-frame runs (WLD-CULTURE-003/-004/-005) stay free to give their own
culture a different rite logic answering to its own societal structure and
faith orientation.
NOT being chosen: rite scripts, liturgy, deity names, calendar dates,
music/costume specifics, funerary-goods inventories; any in-world name or
naming-language content (protected unknowns); any marriage-form norm; any
afterlife fact or Soul destination; anything owned by the culture-frame,
language, or coinage sibling runs; any new System mechanic, readout
guarantee, or CAL0 change. No option lets any rite trigger, gate, or
guarantee a System event.

## Option A — THRESHOLD-WITNESS — selected

Every lifecycle rite is a witnessed crossing: the community formally witnesses
a person passing a threshold and thereby socially ratifies the new state.
Birth rites witness acceptance into a household — the crossing is into the
hearth, not into existence. Coming-of-age rites witness the culturally
interpreted signs of ordinary System awakening — WLD1.0D's staged topology by
reference, with no age, band, or timing rule fixed here: ceremony reveals,
celebrates, authenticates, and teaches; it never causes, gates, or delays
natural access, and an unwitnessed child still awakens. Marriage rites witness
a bond of reciprocal obligation between persons and households; death rites
witness the community's recognition of a death and the reallocation of the
dead person's social obligations among the living.

Per-culture emphasis (OPTION-LOCAL-ILLUSTRATIVE — illustration of how the
architecture might land, not established by this record; the culture-frame
runs may supply a different rite logic per culture): the original culture
stresses WHO witnesses: the right kin, the right elders, in some traditions
witnesses tied to land or hearth; a rite with wrong witnesses is socially
weak however well performed. The imperial-remnant culture stresses WHAT IS
RECORDED: an attested account kept in whatever fallible record forms exist —
no archive, registry institution, or record accuracy established
(WLD-LOCATION-004's no-intact-archives ceiling). The hybrid settlement
negotiates both — witnessed crossings with remembered or recorded
attestation — echoing the compact's oath-witness grammar (WLD-CULTURE-002)
at household scale without acquiring any office or governance power.

System interfaces stay cultural. Coming-of-age witnesses attest what a
family discloses or a young person voluntarily demonstrates; marriage
negotiations may include voluntary threshold-proof displays (category, never
value) — all appraisal UI1.2D-routed, custom never guarantee, nobody
screened for latent talent, and authenticated proof itself unevenly reachable
(shared claim 7), so the custom is aspirational or specialist-mediated for
many households. Death witnesses attest that a person died and
duties passed; the System's own death reporting is bounded and
uncertainty-declared (RES0.10D). Birth blessings and maternal observances
aimed at the unborn exist in all three cultures — none mechanically
effective, per the prenatal guardrail. Marriage forms remain an open
qualitative field everywhere: household-formation logics and obligation
classes (dwelling, labour, care, inherited duties — social obligation only,
per shared claim 10) vary by culture, strand, and household, no form-norm
fixed. No rite in this record is established as producing any System event,
recognition, readout, or standing.

## Option B — PASSAGE-AND-RETURN

Every lifecycle rite frames life as a passage: persons arrive from, and
depart toward, an unseen continuity — and every claim about that continuity
is contested belief, confirmed by nothing. Birth rites greet an arrival into
hearth and kin; coming-of-age rites confirm the person has "fully arrived,"
a widespread folk reading of ordinary awakening (interpretation, not System
category); marriage rites join two journeys into one road; death rites send
the person onward, provisioned with observance. The architecture licenses
rich reincarnation-adjacent folk belief — departed kin suspected in
newborns, awaited back — strictly as belief: traditions disagree with each
other and internally, no rite or sign confirms any return, and no ordinary
person carries prenatal continuity or practice, per the guardrail and
RES0.10D's open cosmology. Passage is a rite IDIOM, not a doctrine: it fixes
no cosmology and establishes no faith content, and each culture's actual faith
orientation is whatever the culture-frame runs (WLD-CULTURE-003/-004/-005)
establish — the idiom bends to that orientation rather than replacing it.

Per-culture emphasis (OPTION-LOCAL-ILLUSTRATIVE — illustration only, not
established by this record): the original culture binds the
passage to place: arrivals come to this land and these hearths, departures
remain in some contested sense nearby, observances leaning on the region's
seasonal rhythms (no calendar or observance content fixed). The
imperial-remnant culture reads whatever imperial faith orientation
WLD-CULTURE-004 establishes through a drifted, disputed passage idiom —
uneven, partly reconstructed, its bearers' claims about imperial-era origin of
unestablished accuracy (WLD-HISTORY-001); no imperial-era doctrine is
established here. The hybrid settlement braids plural passage-idioms under
thin negotiated common observances: households greet, confirm, join, and send
in strand-distinct ways, deepening WLD-CULTURE-001's braid without merging its
strands. Rite timing stays household and strand custom; no festival calendar,
date, or observance content is fixed or implied.

The shared discipline holds unchanged: ceremony wraps the natural awakening
stage and cannot move it; death rites believe they ease or guide a departure
the System reports only in bounded, uncertain terms — proof of neither
survival nor destruction; marriage customs may use voluntary UI1.2D
disclosure as custom, never guarantee, and unevenly reachable (shared claim
7); birth blessings aimed at the arriving
traveller have no mechanical effect; and marriage forms stay an open
qualitative field — journey-joining is an idiom, not a form-count.

## Option C — NAME-AND-RECORD

Every lifecycle rite turns on names and standing — as social practice only:
no actual name, naming vocabulary, or linguistic content is established, and
all final names remain protected unknowns. Birth rites grant a provisional
name and provisional household standing; coming-of-age rites grant a full
name and full standing among kin and neighbours — social standing only, never
assembly standing, property, or tenure (shared claim 10) — when the community
recognises the culturally
interpreted awakening stage (ceremony authenticates natural awakening — it
cannot cause, gate, or own it, and an unnamed person still awakens on
natural law's schedule); marriage rites merge or link standings between
persons and households; death rites turn the name into a name invoked by the
living, whose meaning follows whatever faith orientation each culture-frame
run establishes — registry, cult, ancestor, land-power, or otherwise. No
option here makes ancestor invocation universal, and nothing about presence,
response, or destination is confirmed.

Per-culture emphasis (OPTION-LOCAL-ILLUSTRATIVE — illustration only, not
established by this record) turns on WHO HOLDS A NAME. The original culture
treats naming as a right of the kin group: elders or the hall give the name,
and withholding or refusing to speak one is the culture's severest social
sanction, felt as erasure from memory. The imperial-remnant culture treats
naming as a claim on records: a name is held by whoever can produce the
attestation, so a name is lost by lapse, dispute, or unreadable record rather
than by anyone's decision — and contested attestations are ordinary. The
hybrid settlement has no settled answer: kin claim and record claim collide,
name disputes are argued household by household, and full naming confers
social standing among kin and neighbours only — whether and how that bears on
assembly standing is left entirely to WLD-CULTURE-002 and story evidence.

The shared discipline holds: names are social facts, and no rite in this
record is established as producing any System event, recognition, readout, or
standing; appraisal customs are voluntary, UI1.2D-routed, never guaranteed,
unevenly reachable (shared claim 7), and screen nobody for latent anything;
provisional-name whispers and protective taboos aimed at the unborn have no
mechanical effect; invocation of the dead by name establishes nothing about
what actually happens to Souls; standing merged or linked by marriage is
social obligation only (shared claim 10); and marriage forms remain an open
qualitative field, no form-norm fixed.

## Shared claim set (option-independent)

1. All three cultures — original, imperial-remnant, hybrid settlement —
   possess all four rite classes, each internally varied in keeping them.
2. Rites are socially binding only; none triggers, gates, delays,
   guarantees, or owns any System event, awakening included (WLD1.0D).
3. Awakening timing follows natural law identically for everyone regardless
   of ceremony, culture, or omission of ceremony.
4. Rite failure or omission has social consequences only.
5. Birth customs aimed at the unborn are beliefs and practices with no
   mechanical effect on the child's progression, and no ordinary person has
   prenatal System access, practice, or continuity (WLD-PRENATAL-001A
   guardrail, read-only). This categorical closure is distinct from the
   deliberate openness left to funeral practice under RES0.10D (claim 6 and
   research question 5) and does not share it.
6. Death rites establish nothing about what actually happens to Souls;
   afterlife, reincarnation, and ancestor content is contested belief under
   RES0.10D's open cosmology.
7. Appraisal-adjacent customs route only through UI1.2D consent, disclosure,
   and narrow authenticated proof — custom, never guarantee. Access to
   authenticated proof is uneven and often unavailable across the layered
   System practice WLD-CULTURE-001 establishes, so such customs are
   aspirational, elite, or specialist-mediated in distributions left open.
8. Marriage and relationship forms are an open qualitative field in every
   culture; no marriage-form norm — numeric, plural, or monogamous — is
   established here, and the field stays open for later work.
9. Per-culture rite expression is option-local-illustrative in every option:
   the emphases sketched under each architecture are illustration, not
   establishment, and the culture-frame runs (WLD-CULTURE-003/-004/-005)
   remain free to supply a different rite logic for their own culture.
10. No rite creates, transfers, or extinguishes assembly standing, property,
    or tenure; every reallocation named in any option is social obligation
    only (WLD-CULTURE-002's contested margins and untouched tenure space
    stay untouched).

## Recommended option

Option A — THRESHOLD-WITNESS. It deepens what canon already runs on: the
compact's grammar of oath, witness, and sworn testimony (WLD-CULTURE-002)
extends naturally to household scale, and witnessing is the rite element that
adapts most readily to whatever societal structure each frame run gives its
culture. It also keeps belief-space maximally
open — passage cosmologies and ancestor-name practices can still appear
later as household or strand beliefs — whereas B and C each spend more of
the open belief and naming space at architecture level.
