# WLD-FLORA-004 — Planetary floral kingdom roster

## Purpose

The fauna side now has a seven-lineage planetary roster (`WLD-FAUNA-001`), but
flora has only one developed basin guild (the reserve-web guild,
`WLD-FLORA-001` through `WLD-FLORA-003`) and several referenced-but-unassigned
vegetation classes: the western alien temperate rainforest, basin woodland,
wet-margin and marsh vegetation, and the lake's sturdy egg-substrate plants.
The Author asked whether additional lineages are needed to cover flora. This
proposal answers that with a parallel producer-and-decomposer kingdom roster
at strategy-theme level, so later flora guilds slot into stable kingdoms the
way fauna species slot into lineages.

## Existing constraints

- Accepted world canon: `WLD-FLORA-001` through `WLD-FLORA-003` (reserve-web
  guild), `WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001` (rainforest, refugia, wetlands, drowned coast),
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-005` (habitat productivity pulses), `WLD-ECOLOGY-006` (sturdy
  plants and flooded vegetation as egg substrate, unassigned), `WLD-FAUNA-001`
  (faunal roster pattern)
- CAL0 rules: WLD1.2D and WLD2.1A+D (ordinary world ecology; no System change)
- Deliberately unresolved boundaries: species, anatomy, chemistry, energy
  capture, in-world names, human uses

## Proposed claims

Common to all options:

1. The planet's macroscopic producer and decomposer life is organised across a
   fixed roster of major kingdoms at growth-strategy-theme level.
2. The reserve-web guild belongs to a networked-modular kingdom; its existing
   bounded records are unchanged.
3. Every kingdom includes abundant ordinary forms; kingdoms are author-facing
   shorthand, not in-world taxonomy.
4. No referenced vegetation (rainforest, woodland, wet-margin, egg-substrate
   plants) is assigned to a kingdom by the roster itself; assignments require
   their own records.

## Alternatives

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Six-kingdom producer-and-decomposer roster | Parallel structure to fauna; covers ground, canopy, disturbance, water, pioneer, and decomposer space; stable slots for all later flora work | Largest roster to govern | High while kingdoms stay one-line themes |
| B | Three-group minimal frame: networked producers, free-living producers, decomposers | Small; fast | Coarse; canopy, aquatic, and pioneer themes remain unslotted and will need later subdivision anyway | High |
| C | No roster; develop flora guild-by-guild as needed | No new authority | Catalogue drift; later retrofits of accepted guilds, the problem the faunal roster just solved for animals | High |

### Option A — Six-kingdom roster (recommended)

| Kingdom | Growth-strategy theme | Niche space and anchors |
|---|---|---|
| K1 — Networked modular flora | Living subsurface networks linking rooted growth modules | The reserve-web guild's kingdom (`WLD-FLORA-001`–`003`); other networked guilds possible elsewhere |
| K2 — Tower flora | Long-lived, tall, self-supporting woody-analogue structures | Canopy and woodland space; candidate (not established) home of rainforest and basin woodland dominants |
| K3 — Free-seeder flora | Short-lived, unnetworked, fast-dispersing individual plants | Disturbance colonists, opening-fillers, field-weed and crop-ancestor space |
| K4 — Water flora | Submerged, floating, and wet-margin producers | Lake, river, marsh, and coastal vegetation space; candidate (not established) home of the egg-substrate plants |
| K5 — Crust-and-cushion flora | Low, slow, surface-hugging pioneer growth | Rock, bark, upland, and disturbed-surface colonisation space |
| K6 — Thread decomposers | Non-photosynthesising thread-and-body networks | Fungus-analogue decomposer, recycler, and mutualist space; partner side of possible root symbioses |

### Option B — Three-group minimal frame

Networked producers (containing the reserve-web guild), free-living producers
(everything else photosynthetic), and decomposers.

### Option C — No roster

Continue guild-by-guild development without planetary structure.

## Mechanisms and consequences

### Causal basis

Deep divergence among producer and decomposer foundations, parallel to the
faunal roster; no magical or System cause.

### Geography, ecology, and material constraints

The roster is planetary and establishes no kingdom as present, dominant, or
absent at any location. Existing records keep governing basin flora facts.

### Story implications

Scene vegetation can be designed kingdom-first (growth strategy, season
structure, disturbance response) with Earth inspiration kept author-facing;
harvest, forage, and material-culture development gains stable botanical slots.

### Character-knowledge implications

None; folk plant knowledge need not match the roster.

### CAL0 implications

Application only; ordinary ecology.

## Recommendation

Option A. It answers the Author's flora-coverage question with the same
structure that just worked for fauna, covers every referenced-but-undeveloped
vegetation class with a stable slot, and adds the decomposer kingdom that the
open food-web work explicitly needs, all while staying one-line themes that
bind almost nothing prematurely.
