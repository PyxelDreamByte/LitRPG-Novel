# WLD-ECOLOGY-004 — Lineage II Peripheral Migration Corridor

## Purpose

Establish the broad recurring relationship between Lineage II movement, the
lower basin, and the initial ridge-spur settlement while leaving exact geography,
calendar, herd demography, and human institutions for later decisions.

## Options reviewed

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L2-ROUTE-A | Predictable lower-basin corridor peripheral to the fortified core | Everyday seasonal pressure; viable site; grounded crises | Recurring lower-land disruption | Selected |
| L2-ROUTE-B | Crown herds appear near the settlement only in rare irruptions | Spectacular encounters; little routine damage | Weak daily-life integration; crises risk feeling arbitrary | Not selected |
| L2-ROUTE-C | Settlement directly blocks the principal corridor | Constant urgency | Implausible siting and recurrent siege pressure | Not selected |

## Selected proposed claims

1. Crown herds ordinarily follow a predictable principal migration corridor
   through the lower basin near the initial settlement.
2. They normally pass through the local area twice during each annual cycle;
   exact seasons, direction, duration, and triggers remain open.
3. The fortified settlement core occupies a ridge spur above and outside the
   principal corridor.
4. Secondary herd paths overlap some outer fields, roads, and lake approaches.
5. Ordinary passages can cause temporary closures, disrupted travel, and crop
   losses, but they do not normally amount to a siege of the settlement.
6. Severe weather, digestive disease, or forage failure can divert crown herds
   toward the settlement and turn a routine seasonal pressure into a crisis.
7. Ribbon groups create smaller, more frequent pressures around woodland and
   lake margins.

## Consistency review

- Extends the seasonal movement and settlement consequences in `WLD-ECOLOGY-001`.
- Uses the digestive and disease vulnerabilities established by
  `WLD-ECOLOGY-002` and `WLD-ECOLOGY-003` without choosing a specific disease.
- Preserves settlement viability by separating the fortified core from the
  principal route while exposing its wider catchment.
- Treats migration as ordinary population ecology rather than a spawned event
  or deliberate attack.
- Creates later cultural and institutional questions without silently answering them.
- Creates no CAL0 reward, level, Skill, trait payload, quest, or herd-wide progression entity.
- No contradiction or retcon identified.

## Residual unknowns

- Exact corridor map, endpoints, width, elevation, water crossings, and distance from the walls
- Exact seasons, direction, passage duration, and environmental triggers
- Herd size, spacing, age structure, and variation between passages
- The mechanism and persistence of route memory or route fidelity
- Settlement warning, closure, harvest, barrier, compensation, and emergency practices
- Typical damage frequency, crisis probability, and recovery burden
- Exact ribbon-group ranges, numbers, behaviours, and damage patterns
- Final names for the animals, settlement, lake, basin, routes, and seasons
