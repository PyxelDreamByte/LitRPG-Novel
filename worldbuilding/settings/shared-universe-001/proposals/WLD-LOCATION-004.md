## Decision boundary

The Author will choose one qualitative pattern for the withdrawn power's
surviving physical works in and near the initial basin: how remnant routes,
clearances, structures, water-and-ground works, and boundary or
administrative traces are arranged, their condition classes, and how salvage
relates to them as an activity class. The Author will NOT be choosing:
names; maps, distances, counts, or engineering quantities; any located,
mapped, directional, or quantitative position for any remnant — qualitative
relational classes (e.g. beyond casual reach; exclusion from named accepted
features) MAY be chosen; ownership, law, salvage rights, or
institutions; story events or character knowledge; anything about the
pre-imperial ancient doorways; or any System-facing property of any
remnant. All options carry the settled capture facts unchanged.

## Option A — CORRIDOR-AND-CLEARANCE SKELETON (selected)

The dominant surviving signature is linear: a decayed engineered route
network and the broad cleared lines of the former power's organised
ecological suppression lattice the lower basin and its approaches. Function
classes: route segments in the former power's engineered manner;
suppression clearances (once-maintained open lines, regrowing unevenly);
ruinous waystation-type and watch-type shells along both; scattered
drainage and ground works; occasional boundary and marker traces.

Condition classes: most of the skeleton is REGROWN — woodland, scrub, and
wet ground have reclaimed cleared lines and route beds at different rates.
Structure shells are RUINOUS. A few nodes are REUSED: present communities
travel surviving firm stretches that serve current needs and quarry shells
for worked stone and fittings. Some stretches are AVOIDED where regrowth,
flooding, or wildlife made them worse than surrounding ground — practical
avoidance, not ritual, unless a later culture record says so.

Relationship to accepted records: regrown cleared lines are ordinary
WLD-ECOLOGY-005 mosaic openings, browsed per WLD-ECOLOGY-001. Some regrown
clearance ground plausibly coincides with ground crown herds now move
through, but no stretch is identified with the WLD-ECOLOGY-004 corridor or
secondary paths, and no claim is made that clearances cause or anchor route
fidelity. Whether the settlement's road (WLD-LOCATION-001) reuses any
imperial alignment is left open. Salvage is dispersed and low-yield: worked
stone and fittings from many small ruins — finite, diminishing, contestable.

## Option B — NUCLEUS-AND-SPOKES

The dominant surviving signature is nodal: one substantial derelict works
complex — a combined administrative, garrison, and works nucleus of the
withdrawn power — stands at a distance from the settlement (relational
only; no distance, direction, or travel time fixed), with decayed spoke
routes radiating toward the basin's former working landscape. Function
classes: the nucleus (walled compounds, large ruinous shells, yards, ground
works); spoke route segments; sparse small ancillaries; administrative
traces densest near the nucleus. Suppression clearances survive mainly as
regrown ground fanning out from nucleus and spokes, not a basin-wide lattice.

Condition classes: the nucleus is RUINOUS and partly AVOIDED — no
supernatural reason; collapsing structures, regrowth, concealed voids, and
returning wildlife make a large derelict complex genuinely dangerous, and
its distance puts it beyond casual reach. Spokes are mostly REGROWN,
legible in stretches. REUSE is expedition salvage: parties strip worked
stone, metal, and fittings from the nucleus or nearer spokes — higher-yield
but higher-risk and higher-cost than local quarrying; concentrated material
makes rival claims and rumour natural, but no ownership, law, expedition
practice, or institution exists.

Relationship to accepted records: nucleus and spokes sit somewhere in or
near the basin without fixed position; no spoke is identified with the
WLD-ECOLOGY-004 corridor, the WLD-LOCATION-001 road (imperial derivation
left open), the WLD-LOCATION-002 bands, or any WLD-LOCATION-003 shore
feature; nothing at the WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002 outlet; regrown ground is
ordinary WLD-ECOLOGY-005 mosaic browsed per WLD-ECOLOGY-001.

## Option C — DISPERSED SMALL WORKS

The dominant surviving signature is granular: many minor installations
scattered through the basin — waystation-type shells, boundary and
administrative marker traces, small drainage and channel works, crossing
works on minor watercourses, and modest cleared-ground remnants around
each. No single large complex exists in the basin. Individually minor, the
installations form — as author-facing classification only — one former
administrative fabric sharing workmanship, materials, and placement logic;
in-world, inhabitants recognise them merely as the same builders' work,
with no functional or administrative identification (per shared claim 1). At least one is
now flooded or lake-claimed: drowned or seasonally submerged under the lake
or its ordinarily shifting wet margins, visible or reachable only under some
conditions; its position is unfixed, and it is expressly NOT at or near the
WLD-LOCATION-003 staging place or seasonal spurs.

Condition classes: most installations are REGROWN or RUINOUS; a modest
number are REUSED opportunistically as field shelters, stock enclosures, or
material quarries near present working land — no particular
WLD-LOCATION-001 field or road segment, or WLD-ECOLOGY-004 approach, is
identified as such a site; a few are AVOIDED for practical reasons. The
flooded installation is neither reused nor reliably reachable; its
underwater condition is unknown to present inhabitants (observer rule:
surface sighting reveals nothing hidden).

Relationship to accepted records: dispersed small clearings and drainage
traces integrate into the WLD-ECOLOGY-005 mosaic (browsed per
WLD-ECOLOGY-001); coincidence with crown-herd movement stays qualitative
and unlocated per WLD-ECOLOGY-004; road derivation left open; nothing at
the WLD-GEOGRAPHY-002 outlet. Salvage is broad and shallow: many small
sources, quickly exhausted, none worth organised expedition — finite and
low-stakes; proximity to present working land makes marginal contestation
natural, but no dispute, party, or event is established.

## Shared claim set (option-independent)

1. Physical remnants of the withdrawn power exist in and near the initial
   basin; present inhabitants generally recognise them as that power's
   work by workmanship, materials, and scale — cultural recognition, not
   accurate knowledge of any remnant's original function.
2. Organised ecological suppression left physical traces (cleared lines and
   ground works, qualitative kinds only); their regrowth is part of the
   basin's ordinary recovering mosaic, not a magical or System process.
3. No remnant functions as designed: up to roughly seventy years of decay,
   regrowth, frost, flood, and stripping — accumulating from each work's
   own local abandonment in the cascading retreat, whose order and timing
   stay open per research question 6 — have degraded every surviving work;
   partial incidental usefulness is the ceiling. No imperial work in or
   near the basin is claimed to be continuously occupied since its
   abandonment: reuse in every option is intermittent reclamation of
   abandoned works (the basin-specific reused-works texture the
   WLD-HISTORY-001 draft defers to this run; its wider fragment-class
   option space is untouched).
4. No remnant grants working imperial machinery, intact knowledge stores,
   functioning mechanisms, or preserved archives by default; any exception
   is a new, separately argued proposal, never an implication.
5. Salvage of remnant materials is an activity class with finite,
   diminishing, contestable value; no salvage right, ownership, law,
   institution, market, price, or named practice is established.
6. All remnants are ordinary made things under ordinary physics and CAL0:
   no safe zone, spawn point, Mana source, System structure, doorway, or
   System-facing property of any kind.

## Recommended option

Option A — CORRIDOR-AND-CLEARANCE SKELETON. The most direct forward
extension of the capture, which already lists abandoned imperial clearances
and corridors among the rewilding mosaic's elements; it distributes remnant
texture across the whole basin where every accepted ecology and location
record can touch it, rather than concentrating story gravity in one
dungeon-like destination, and keeps the strongest questions — corridor
reoccupation, road derivation, salvage friction — open at useful pressure.

## Consistency and causal review

- Forward extension, not a retcon: the capture already establishes the
  retreat, withdrawn suppression, reused works, and abandoned imperial
  clearances; this run only gives that inheritance a bounded qualitative
  spatial pattern. No accepted record mentions remnants.
- WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001/-002: remnants sit inside the accepted basin frame
  without map data; decay uses ordinary climate and flood processes; the
  outlet's nothing-exists-there boundary holds — no remnant at the throat
  or mixed reach in any option.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-001/-005: regrown clearances are ordinary pulse-mosaic
  openings, browsed like any other; no organism, reserve-web patch, or
  colony placed in any remnant; no habitat becomes the basin's engine; no
  nonlocal-life reference, so no provenance typing needed.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-004: exact corridor geography and route-fidelity mechanism
  stay open; claim 4 is unlocated qualitative coincidence, answering
  neither.
- WLD-LOCATION-001/-002/-003: no remnant in the fortified core or managed
  apron, or at the staging place and spurs; the road corridor is excluded
  from that bar and its derivation held open;
  Option C's flooded installation respects shifting-margin and observer
  rules.
- First-order consequences (implications, not selected facts): a finite
  salvage stream, low-grade dispute, unreliable regrown ground, ruins as
  denning and ambush structure — all needing later records first.
- Causal discipline: decay, regrowth, stripping, and flooding are finite
  ordinary processes; nothing refills, resets, or preserves itself. CAL0
  untouched — no new mechanic, constant, readout, or System-granted
  property; no dungeon, spawning, or exceptional-place claim for any
  remnant (ordinary setting extension; cf. CAL0 WLD2.0D, WLD2.1A+D).
