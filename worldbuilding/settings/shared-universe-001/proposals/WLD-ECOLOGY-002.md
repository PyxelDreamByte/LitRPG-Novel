# WLD-ECOLOGY-002 — Lineage II Distributed Fermentation Lattice

## Purpose

Supply a plausible family-level digestive system for the previously selected
crown and ribbon vegetation-architect roles.

## Options reviewed

| Option | Distinguishing choice | Benefits | Costs/risks | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L2-DIGEST-A | Connected, routed fermentation chambers with microbial symbionts | Plausible energy budget; branch differentiation; seasonal vulnerability | Later transmission, anatomy, and disease work required | Selected |
| L2-DIGEST-B | External curing and reprocessing | Visible behaviour | Cumbersome, weather-sensitive, and potentially too familiar | Not selected |
| L2-DIGEST-C | Universal Mana-catalytic digestive organ | Compact and magical | Obscures energy provenance and over-specialises the family | Not selected |

## Selected proposed claims

1. Lineage II uses several connected digestive chambers that route food of
   different toughness and nutrient density through a fermentation lattice.
2. Symbiotic microorganisms perform much of the chemical breakdown.
3. Crown forms have larger, slower-processing chambers adapted to bulk
   structural vegetation.
4. Ribbon forms have smaller, faster-processing chambers suited to selective
   seasonal foods.
5. Ordinary Mana use reinforces chamber membranes, circulation, and thermal
   stability; it does not create calories or replace food.
6. Sudden dietary changes can destabilise the symbiont community, causing
   illness and seasonal vulnerability.

## Consistency review

- Supports `WLD-ECOLOGY-001` without changing its feeding roles.
- Preserves matter and energy provenance.
- Creates costs in organ development, retention time, heat management, diet
  transitions, and microbial maintenance.
- Creates no CAL0 reward, Skill, trait payload, or specialised Mana ability.
- No contradiction or retcon identified.

## Residual unknowns

- Number, position, valves, and exact routing of chambers
- Symbiont taxa and metabolic products
- How young acquire and families maintain compatible symbionts
- Gestational development of the digestive system
- Exact retention times, heat output, water needs, waste, toxins, and disease
- Whether narrow species develop specialised Mana-catalytic assistance

## Author decision

The Author selected the distributed fermentation lattice by replying **“yes”**
on 2026-08-13. Authority is now held by `WLD-ECOLOGY-002`; this proposal is
retained for alternatives and provenance.
