## Decision boundary

The Author will choose one qualitative configuration of the standing,
unresolved pressures shaping the settlement and region at story start: which
of three settled pressure classes (political succession, returning non-human
peoples, seasonal ecological strain) carries what public salience, how the
pressures couple, and what kind of evidence about the returning peoples
circulates locally. The Author will NOT be choosing any plot event, occurred
incident, named faction, people, or candidate, succession outcome, contact
event, crisis, timeline, or resolution — every claim below is a standing
state or tendency at story start, not a thing that has narratively happened
on the page or will happen.

## Option cards

### A — BRAIDED-THREE (all live, none dominant)

All three pressure classes are simultaneously live at story start and none
has settled into the acknowledged main question. The approaching first
succession is openly discussed but has not yet crowded out other business;
reports that a returning non-human people is re-establishing presence
somewhere in the wider region circulate steadily; and the aftermath of a
recent hard season keeps stores, planning, and tempers tighter than custom.
Different households, professions, and council occasions rank the three
differently, and that disagreement about what matters most is itself part of
the settlement's texture.

The returning-peoples evidence is bounded: reported-only knowledge
(traveller accounts and secondhand news, ECOLOGY-005 class e) plus
occasional imported objects of uncertain provenance (class c analogue). No
sighting, presence, or contact at or near the settlement is established, and
the reports do not agree on which people, where, or with what aims. The
ecological strain is a standing condition, not a crisis in progress: stores
sit lower than customary comfort and subsistence planning is under visible
adjustment, grounded in the accepted mechanisms — severe-weather corridor
disruption (ECOLOGY-004), ordinary pulse variability across the basin mosaic
(ECOLOGY-005), and a weaker-than-customary lake season within ECOLOGY-006's
allowed variation. No famine, shortage event, or System cause exists.

Coupling is qualitative and attentional: leaner stores make succession
argument sharper, unresolved succession makes long-horizon provisioning
decisions harder to close, and unsettling reports make both feel less
deferrable. This is a coupling of attention and finite deliberative
capacity, not a mechanism, schedule, or predetermined escalation.

### B — SUCCESSION-FORWARD (politics audible, the rest building quietly)

The approaching first succession audibly organises settlement politics.
Council time, dynasty attention, and everyday talk keep returning to what
the compact does and does not settle about the transfer of the founder's
authority (structure held by the sibling compact proposal). The political
question is the one pressure with an acknowledged public shape; people
expect it to be the story of the coming years.

The other two pressures build quietly in the background. Returning-peoples
reports arrive in the same bounded classes as Option A — reported-only
accounts and occasional imported objects — but are received as distant news,
interesting rather than actionable. Seasonal strain from the recent hard
season is real but under-discussed: stores are drawn somewhat deeper than
custom, and adjustments that would ordinarily be argued through the council
are being deferred because political attention is elsewhere. That deferral
is a standing tendency of attention, not a decided policy or an occurred
failure.

The option's distinct texture is asymmetric preparedness: the settlement is
politically alert and materially complacent. Nothing in this option
establishes that the background pressures will erupt, when, or how — only
that at story start they receive less deliberation than the succession does.

### C — CONTACT-FORWARD (reports dominate; succession publicly deferred)

Reports of a returning non-human people re-establishing presence in the
wider region dominate talk and council time. This option explicitly
proposes a NEAR-CONTACT evidence class and flags it as such: in addition to
reported-only accounts and imported objects, independent travellers attest
transient signs and sightings in the wider region (ECOLOGY-005 class b
analogue — transient presence at a distance). Even so, no presence,
sighting, or contact at or near the settlement, lake road, shore, or apron
is established; the attested signs are regional, unlocalised relative to the
settlement, and disagree in detail. Which people, how many, and with what
aims remain wholly open, and the reports themselves preserve that
disagreement rather than converging on one story.

Under that pressure the succession question is publicly deferred: the
ageing founder still rules, the first succession is still approaching, its
rules remain incompletely settled, and the settlement's habit at story
start is to postpone the argument in favour of the more vivid external
question. Deferral is a standing posture, not a decision, moratorium, or
settled rule.

Seasonal ecological strain is present but unnoticed as a distinct pressure:
the same standing strain condition as Option A exists, yet it is folded into
general grumbling rather than named in deliberation. The option's texture is
a community looking outward while two slower pressures accumulate behind
its attention.

## Shared claim set (option-independent)

1. The first succession of the founder's rule is approaching at story start,
   and the rules that would govern it are incompletely settled. The compact's
   structure, offices, and succession provisions are the sibling compact
   proposal's subject and are not defined here.
2. Returning non-human peoples are a real regional phenomenon, consistent
   with the capture's settled history of displacement under the former power
   and return after its retreat. The particular peoples, their number,
   locations, histories, aims, and relations remain open and must not be
   flattened into a single invading category; nothing here characterises any
   people as hostile, friendly, unified, or coordinated.
3. Seasonal ecological pressure on the settlement follows the accepted
   mechanisms only: corridor closures, travel disruption, and crop exposure
   per WLD-ECOLOGY-004; pulse-mosaic variability per WLD-ECOLOGY-005; lake
   seasonal variation within WLD-ECOLOGY-006's bounds. No System event,
   spawning, reset, or magical cause stands behind any pressure.
4. All three pressure classes exist in every option; the options vary only
   salience, coupling, and the qualitative form of each.
5. Standing preparation behaviours may exist as tendencies (closer store
   accounting, attention to travellers' news, quiet contingency talk)
   without establishing any office, policy, institution, watch, levy,
   stockpile standard, or defensive measure.

## Recommended option

Option A — BRAIDED-THREE. It preserves the widest story space by refusing to
pre-rank the Author's three settled realities, matching the capture's
requirement that current events influence the main story without
predetermining its plot. Options B and C each bake a salience hierarchy into
the setting that the series contract may want to establish for itself.

## Claim boundary

Selecting the recommended option would establish only the following bounded
claims, together with the shared claim set above:

1. At story start, three standing pressure classes are simultaneously live in
   the settlement and its region: approaching-first-succession political
   pressure; returning-peoples external pressure; and seasonal ecological
   strain on stores and subsistence patterns.
2. None of the three has settled into acknowledged dominance; relative
   salience varies by household, profession, and occasion, and that ranking
   is contested rather than resolved.
3. Local knowledge of returning peoples consists of reported-only traveller
   accounts and secondhand news, plus occasional imported objects of
   uncertain provenance. The reports disagree on people, place, and aims. No
   direct or transient presence at or near the settlement is established.
4. A recent hard season has left a standing strain condition: stores lower
   than customary comfort and subsistence planning under visible adjustment,
   arising only through the accepted weather, corridor, and pulse mechanisms.
   No famine, crisis in progress, occurred disaster, or harvest institution
   is established.
5. Standing preparation tendencies exist as described in shared claim 5,
   without any specific measure, office, or institution.
6. The three pressures couple attentionally: each makes clean deliberation of
   the others harder, as a qualitative tendency of finite attention and
   resources, not a mechanism, schedule, or guaranteed escalation.

"Standing pressure," "salience," "hard season," "customary comfort,"
"reported-only," "imported object," "preparation tendency," and "coupling"
are author-facing qualitative descriptions; they fix no in-world term,
measurement, calendar, institution, doctrine, or performance guarantee.

## Consistency and causal review

- Foundation capture: the settled realities (approaching first succession
  under a balanced compact; returning megafauna and returning non-human
  peoples; mixed subsistence economy; 25–35-year-old refuge settlement of
  1,200–2,000) appear unchanged in every option; only unsettled salience,
  coupling, and evidence form vary. This is a forward extension, not a retcon.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-004: the hard-season strain uses only the licensed pressure
  channels (closures, travel disruption, crop loss, margin pressure). No
  diversion crisis, siege, or particular passage is claimed to have occurred,
  and no field, road, or approach is identified as an overlap.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-005: every nonlocal-life and nonlocal-people evidence reference
  is typed against the provenance classes (reported-only, imported product,
  and — in flagged Option C only — transient-at-a-distance). Non-transfer
  rules are respected: no report or object establishes presence, range,
  aims, or population.
- WLD-ECOLOGY-006: "weaker-than-customary lake season" stays inside the
  record's allowed variability; no recruitment failure event, harvest rate,
  fishing technique, institution, law, preservation method, or trade is
  established, per the record's critical boundary.
- WLD-LOCATION-001/002/003: no organism, worker, structure, practice, watch,
  or measure is placed at the settlement, apron, road, staging place, spurs,
  or outlet; no water-source adequacy or spur usability is resolved.
- WLD-GEOGRAPHY-001/002: no climate value, map claim, route, or outlet use is
  added; the "wider region" stays qualitative and inside the accepted
  regional frontier.
- CAL0 (WLD2.2D, SOC1.2D, UI1.2D): this is an ordinary setting extension, not
  a System change. Pressures arise from weather, ecology, politics, and
  finite attention — never System events. No institution gains
  System-granted power, no appraisal or report is omniscient, and report
  fallibility is a records/belief-layer property, not a mechanic.
- First-order consequences: contested salience (A), asymmetric preparedness
  (B), or outward fixation (C) each give the series contract usable standing
  tension without scheduling any event. Deliberation, storage, travel, and
  watchfulness all remain bounded by finite labour, time, and information.

## Research questions / open boundaries

This proposal deliberately does not settle:

1. The compact's structure, offices, succession rules, and factional
   alignments (sibling compact proposal's domain).
2. Which non-human people or peoples the reports concern, their identities,
   numbers, locations, histories, aims, languages, and relations to the
   settlement's cultures — including whether any reports are wrong.
3. The provenance, accuracy, and transmission chains of specific traveller
   accounts and imported objects, and any tale-lore built on them.
4. The hard season's exact weather sequence, which corridor closures or
   pulse shortfalls composed it, its severity relative to other years, and
   its regional extent.
5. Store accounting practices, granary or storage arrangements, rationing
   customs, and any allocation authority (none exist yet).
6. How the settlement gathers outside news at all: routes, frequency,
   traveller hosting, and any watch or messenger practice.
7. Whether, when, and how any pressure escalates, resolves, couples further,
   or reaches the settlement — series-contract and future-event territory.
8. All in-world names, dates, calendars, and units touching any pressure.
9. How different cultures and professions within the mixed refuge community
   interpret each pressure, including System-facing beliefs about any of
   them (belief-layer material for later culture proposals).
10. Any relationship between returning peoples and returning megafauna,
    doorway myths, or the former power's retreat beyond what the capture
    already settles.

## Author decision

On 2026-08-17, through the explicit multiple-choice review of the displayed
options, the Author selected an amended custom option rather than A, B, or
C: RIVALRY-FORWARD salience with five standing pressure classes, all
present and none resolved. The amendment keeps the three displayed classes
— the approaching first succession under WLD-CULTURE-002's designed
succession ambiguity, returning-peoples knowledge bounded to reported-only,
transient-at-a-distance, and imported-object provenance classes per
WLD-ECOLOGY-005, and seasonal ecological strain grounded solely in the
accepted WLD-ECOLOGY-004/-005/-006 mechanisms — and adds two more: cross-lake
tensions and trade rivalry with the imperial-remnant industrial settlement
(WLD-LOCATION-005), which audibly organises settlement politics and colours
the succession question as a standing tension with no occurred incident and
no future war or escalation claim, and returning dangerous wildlife as a
felt pressure class of recovering megafauna plus qualitative predator and
raider classes, including raiding bands of the pest-like pack species whose
collapse-era spread WLD-HISTORY-001 records. The Author described the
wildlife classes in author-facing shorthand as 'hags, goblins, mega-fauna,
wolves, bears (our world equivalents)'; those Earth-creature words are
shorthand only, never in-world taxonomy, and establish no species identity,
biology, or local presence. Returning-peoples reports and seasonal strain
build in the background under the selected configuration.

Options A, B, and C remain nonbinding evidence; the work-local capture
preserves the full displayed wording.
