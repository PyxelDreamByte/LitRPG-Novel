# Worldbuilding Instructions

- The Author alone accepts setting canon.
- Isolate every real setting under `worldbuilding/settings/<setting-slug>/`; do not place one work's canon into another setting's authority tree.
- Project-level defaults are not global canon. A work manifest must explicitly adopt them; any override requires that work's own Author decision.
- Write new material as a proposal first; never infer canon from research, prose, CAL0 fixtures, or an agent draft.
- Check accepted dependencies, chronology, geography, ecology, culture, character knowledge, and CAL0 compatibility.
- Separate System natural law from cultural belief and interface interpretation.
- Record implications, contradictions, residual unknowns, and provenance; preserve stable `WLD` IDs.
- Promote accepted claims into `canon/` and `decisions/` together, then update `INDEX.md`.
- Use `.decision.json` for structured decisions and `.worldbuilding.json` for structured world records.
