# Project Hearthway — Foundation Design Capture

## Status and purpose

This document records the Author's current design selections so they are not
lost between conversations. It is a non-canonical staging checkpoint, not an
accepted setting record, Author decision, story fact, or System successor.

The selections below are intended inputs to later, bounded proposals. Before
promotion they still require scope separation, consistency review, dependency
mapping, and explicit Author acceptance. Unsettled points remain visibly open.

## Shared-universe frame

- The setting is a broader universe intended to contain several stories across
  multiple time periods.
- Its full scope may span multiple planets and star systems.
- The main LitRPG web novel will be set primarily on one planet.
- The initial society has studied the stars and knows that other planets exist,
  but has not travelled to another planet or star system.
- Myths describe travellers from other worlds. Traditions disagree over whether
  those travellers were benevolent, destructive, or both.
- Ancient archways or doorways once permitted travel to this world. Most are
  ruined. They are old background connections rather than a major early-story
  engine, although a later story may explore them.
- The planet is Earth-adjacent in broad physical legibility, not a disguised
  copy of Earth.

## System access and cultural variation

- All people and creatures have access to the LitRPG System.
- Understanding, study, and use differ substantially between cultures, social
  levels, professions, and individuals.
- Some people make little deliberate use of it; others use what knowledge they
  possess; specialists investigate it; and some institutions or factions hoard
  advantages.
- Future records must distinguish natural System behaviour from cultural
  belief, scholarly theory, interface interpretation, and reader disclosure.
- The proposed `Ancestry Development` concept is isolated in a separate System-
  change capture. Its mechanics are not established here.

## Initial planetary and regional frontier

- Initial development focuses on one sheltered heartland region composed of
  linked sub-basins rather than an open plain or isolated single valley.
- A substantial lake lies near the main settlement.
- The regional climate is temperate continental, with meaningful seasonal
  variation.
- Approximately seventy years before the story, the previous major regional
  power collapsed through a cascading imperial retreat rather than one clean,
  simultaneous fall.
- Its withdrawal took institutions, knowledge, manpower, infrastructure, and
  organised ecological suppression with it. Fragments survive in the manner of
  post-imperial successor societies, reused works, partial traditions, and
  uneven pockets of expertise.
- The region is now in a dark age relative to its imperial past, not an absence
  of society, craft, literacy, or historical memory.
- Large wildlife and other formerly suppressed species are returning. These
  include megafaunal animals and creatures that Earth cultures would call
  mythological, but which are ordinary parts of this world's ecology.
- Non-human peoples driven from their lands, killed, or enslaved by the former
  power are also returning. Their histories, aims, and relations must not be
  flattened into a single invading category.

## Main settlement

- The main character begins in a defensible settlement on a ridge spur above
  the lake and lower cultivated ground.
- It is walled and difficult to approach, deliberately reviving a pre-imperial
  hill-fort pattern after megafaunal threats returned.
- The present settlement is relatively recent: provisionally about thirty years
  old, within a selected range of roughly twenty-five to thirty-five years.
- It is a mixed refuge community rather than a culturally uniform remnant.
- Its population is provisionally in the range of 1,200–2,000 people.
- Its economy combines lake resources with fields, gardens, livestock or native
  domesticates, woodland products, craft, storage, and exchange. No Earth crop,
  animal, or plant is implied by those functional descriptions.
- Government combines a founder's dynasty with a council under a balanced
  compact. Neither side is merely ceremonial.
- The ageing founder still rules, and the first succession is approaching.
  This makes inherited legitimacy, emergency authority, refugee representation,
  expertise, and council consent live political pressures.

## Ecological direction

### Core principles

- The recovering landscape is a rewilding mosaic, not a uniform wall of
  monsters: defended human land, lake margins, regrowing woodland, abandoned
  imperial clearances, corridors, uplands, wetlands, and deep refuges exert
  different pressures.
- Threat and abundance vary seasonally. Migration, breeding, hunger, floods,
  fires, freezing conditions, plant cycles, and human harvests should change
  encounters across the year.
- Danger follows an ecological gradient. The settlement's immediate managed
  zone is safer; neglected edges and travel corridors are riskier; remote
  refuges and migration routes can support apex or specialised creatures.
- Fauna should form a layered mixed ecosystem of small organisms, prey,
  omnivores, mesopredators, large herbivores, scavengers, parasites, ecosystem
  engineers, and apex threats. Megafauna are consequential without making every
  ordinary journey an apex encounter.
- Earth life from any era and mythic creatures may provide inspiration, but no
  currently living, extinct, or legendary Earth species should be transplanted
  directly.
- Design from lineage, body plan, niche, life cycle, behaviour, and material
  consequences first. Earth comparisons are author-facing shorthand, not
  in-world taxonomy.
- The biosphere also needs abundant original, ordinary organisms. It should not
  read as a catalogue containing only spectacular remixes.

### Mana and creature progression

- Most organisms use Mana in some form.
- Most uses are biologically ordinary enhancements: strengthened armour,
  reinforced claws or teeth, tougher fibres, improved circulation, heat or
  pressure tolerance, endurance, or comparable extensions of a creature's niche.
- More specialised branches may evolve dedicated Mana organs and distinctive
  abilities, but these should carry ecological and physiological costs.
- Stealth may arise through behaviour, colour, terrain use, scent control, or
  bounded specialisation. Perfect invisibility and repeated generic concealment
  powers should be rare rather than a default solution.
- Sensory specialists and ordinary counter-adaptations prevent stealth from
  becoming an uncontested ecological trump card.
- Creatures can become stronger through the System. The exact lawful mechanic,
  including the proposed species-template lineage and rare emergence of
  sapience, is not settled by this worldbuilding capture.

## Faunal lineage framework

Authority note: on 2026-08-17 the Author expanded and superseded this
section's five-lineage working model by accepting the seven-lineage planetary
roster recorded by
`canon://world/shared-universe-001/ecology/planetary-faunal-lineage-roster`
(Lineage IV: the mass breeder's aquatic flexible-trunk lineage; Lineage V:
the family-care migrant's tri-radial lineage; Lineage VI: segmented
exoskeletal smallstock; Lineage VII: unsegmented soft-bodied animals; flight
independently evolved in more than one lineage). That record, not this
staging summary, governs where they overlap.

The earlier selected working model used five major animal lineages, with
familiar ecological roles distributed across different branches rather than
copied from Earth clades. Only the first two have begun detailed development.

### Lineage I — six-limbed filament-and-scute animals

Authority note: on 2026-08-17 this sketch was promoted to bounded provisional
canon by
`canon://world/shared-universe-001/ecology/lineage-i-foundational-body-plan`,
which also resolved that winged branches derive their wings from the middle
limb pair, keeping four walking legs. That record, not this staging summary,
governs where they overlap.

- Descended from a six-limbed ancestor with a tail.
- Different branches may repurpose limb pairs for locomotion, handling,
  display, defence, digging, climbing, swimming, or flight-equivalent functions.
- The outer covering combines insulating or sensory filaments with flexible
  scutes rather than mapping directly to fur, feathers, or reptile scales.
- They are warm-active animals.
- Reproduction uses flexible, mineral-latticed brood eggs that require guarded
  nests or brooding behaviour.
- Large-bodied branches reproduce slowly, making adults and successful nesting
  sites ecologically important.

### Lineage II — tower-necked landscape engineers

Authority note: the bounded vegetation-architect claims in
`canon://world/shared-universe-001/ecology/lineage-ii-vegetation-architects` are
now provisional authority. The digestive claims in
`canon://world/shared-universe-001/ecology/lineage-ii-fermentation-lattice` are
also provisional authority. The two-stage acquisition and social maintenance
claims in `canon://world/shared-universe-001/ecology/lineage-ii-symbiont-inheritance`
are provisional authority as well. Those records, not this staging summary,
govern where they overlap.

- Four main weight-bearing limbs, an elevated tower-like neck or forebody, and
  a counterbalancing tail establish the shared silhouette.
- Two family branches develop the same ancestral mouth structures differently:
  a crown branch and a ribbon branch.
- Crown forms are larger, herd-forming landscape engineers of more open ground.
- Ribbon forms are smaller, family-group animals associated more closely with
  forests, lake margins, or broken terrain.
- Reproduction uses an internal brood chamber. Usually one or two young develop
  in retained egg capsules, hatch before birth, and are born live.
- Slow recovery, strong kin groups, and investment in few young should shape
  migration, defence, hunting pressure, and human use of their habitats.
- Crown forms use short, reinforced mouth-rays to process tough mature stalks,
  brush, and woody growth, helping maintain open corridors and vegetation mosaics.
- Ribbon forms use longer tactile mouth-rays to selectively harvest shoots,
  fruiting bodies, and lakeshore or woodland-edge vegetation.
- Both branches use a connected microbial fermentation lattice. Crown chambers
  are larger and slower-processing; ribbon chambers are smaller and faster.
  Ordinary Mana supports membranes, circulation, and thermal stability without
  replacing dietary energy, and abrupt diet changes can cause symbiont-driven illness.
- Before birth, young receive a small compatible founder culture; contact and
  shared feeding with parents or relatives then build the mature community.
  Compatible fostering can support orphans, isolation is dangerous, and the
  same social routes permit both recovery and digestive-disease transmission.
- Exact chamber and reproductive anatomy, transfer behaviours, symbiont taxa,
  maturation timing, compatibility limits, diseases, movements, other Mana
  adaptations, species-level diet exceptions, and relationships to settlement
  life remain open.

### Remaining lineages

- Lineages III–VII now have roster-level silhouette themes under
  `canon://world/shared-universe-001/ecology/planetary-faunal-lineage-roster`
  but remain otherwise undeveloped.
- Their eventual detailed designs must fill missing food-web and habitat
  functions rather than exist only to supply novel monsters.

## Parked sapient pack species

Authority note: on 2026-08-17 the Author decided this species is the same
concept as the pest-like races (author-facing shorthand: goblin-equivalents)
whose collapse-era spread is recorded by
`canon://world/shared-universe-001/history/imperial-collapse-arc-and-neighbor-frame`.
That record governs only the historical spread; everything below remains
parked and undeveloped.

Development of this species is intentionally paused until it becomes relevant.
The current note preserves only the Author's stated boundary:

- It is a fully sapient species.
- It is biologically extremely violent, aggressive, territorial, and pack-like.
- Average intelligence and creativity are low relative to other sapient peoples.
- Some individuals are somewhat more intelligent and tend to lead packs; those
  leaders also tend to be more aggressive.
- No non-aggressive outlier is known in the current conception.
- `No known outlier` is an observational setting claim, not yet a metaphysical
  law or permission to remove individual agency without further review.

Names, reproduction, social learning, language, material culture, ecological
role, relations with other peoples, and the exact meaning of sapience remain
unresolved. Future development must test whether the concept can sustain
coherent biology and ethical storytelling without turning an entire sapient
people into an unexamined moral category.

## Open design work

- Final names and linguistic logic for the universe, planet, region, lake,
  settlement, cultures, institutions, species, and lineages
- Planetary scale, orbital assumptions, continents, prevailing weather, and the
  physical causes of the linked heartland basins
- Regional map, lake inflows/outflows, watersheds, soils, mineral resources,
  travel routes, ruins, and settlement catchment
- Human cultures of the area, including the protagonist's primary culture,
  daily life, family structures, foodways, clothing, crafts, faiths, education,
  System practice, and views of imperial inheritance
- The founder-council compact, offices, representation, law, succession rules,
  factions, and current disputes
- Seventy-year collapse chronology and the region's present political neighbours
- A season-by-season food web, flora, fungi or analogues, decomposers, diseases,
  parasites, domesticates, pests, and material uses
- Remaining animal lineages and detailed representative species
- The ecological and political consequences of returning non-human peoples
- Origin, builders, limits, and truth behind the ancient doorways and traveller myths
- Current events capable of influencing the main story without predetermining its plot

## Series plot intent (staging only — routed to the series contract)

On 2026-08-17 the Author stated the following intended plot trajectory. It is
plot intent for the proposed series contract, not setting canon, and no
setting record establishes it as having occurred or as inevitable:

- The imperial-remnant industrial settlement near the outlet mouth
  (`canon://world/shared-universe-001/location/outlet-industrial-remnant-settlement`)
  will later in the series attempt to assert control over the entire basin.
- Tensions and trade-war scenarios play out while the protagonist is growing
  up; open fighting is intended to occur when the protagonist is roughly
  12-13 years old.
- The settled setting layer carries only the standing story-start tensions and
  trade rivalry recorded by
  `canon://world/shared-universe-001/events/story-start-standing-pressures`.

The series-contract workflow owns this trajectory's development, timing, and
any revision.
