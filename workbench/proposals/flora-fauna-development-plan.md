# Flora and Fauna Development Plan

Non-canonical working plan. The Author directed: scaffold all flora and fauna
lineages first, then take each lineage in turn, then focus on the basin area.
Each step below still runs through the normal bounded-proposal and Author-gate
workflow; this file only fixes the intended order.

## Phase 0 — Planetary rosters (done 2026-08-17)

- `WLD-FAUNA-001` — seven faunal lineages (I–VII), provisional.
- `WLD-FLORA-004` — six floral kingdoms (K1–K6), provisional.

## Phase 1 — Scaffold every floral kingdom (complete 2026-08-17)

One bounded foundational record per undeveloped kingdom, at qualitative
strategy grain (growth form, reproduction-and-dispersal theme, seasonal
structure, disturbance response, one or two distinctive costs). K1 already has
the reserve-web guild records as its exemplar.

Order: K2 tower flora (done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FLORA-005` core-and-casing) →
K4 water flora (done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FLORA-006` anchor-and-streamer) →
K3 free-seeders (done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FLORA-007` vault-seed) →
K6 thread decomposers (done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FLORA-008` weaver-and-bloom,
Author A+B combination) → K5 crust-and-cushion (done 2026-08-17,
`WLD-FLORA-009` revival).

Each kingdom now carries a distinct persistence strategy: K1 living networks,
K2 standing structure, K3 dormant vault banks, K4 anchors, K5 shutdown
dormancy, K6 diffuse threads. (Rationale: K2 and K4 unblock the most
referenced-but-homeless vegetation — woodland, rainforest candidates, lake
egg-substrate plants; K6 unblocks food-web work; K5 is remotest from early
story ground.)

## Phase 2 — Scaffold every faunal lineage (complete 2026-08-17)

One bounded foundational record per undeveloped lineage, at the
`WLD-ECOLOGY-007`/`009` grain (silhouette, covering, locomotion, reproduction
theme, niche spread, distinctive costs). Lineage II already has its four
records.

Proposed order:

1. Lineage I — done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FAUNA-002`: staging sketch promoted
   (six-limbed tailed frame, filament-and-scute covering, warm-active,
   guarded mineral-latticed brood eggs, slow large-body reproduction) with
   winged branches deriving wings from the middle limb pair.
2. Lineage III — done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FAUNA-003`: low-slung four-limbed
   ancestry, internal supporting frame, flexible skin, forelimb-derived
   wings, and a litter-and-disperse high-throughput life history.
3. Lineage VI — done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FAUNA-004`: segmented jointed
   exoskeletons with growth mode as a branch axis (ancestral moulting plus
   derived rolling-replacement and seam-growth), the seam-growth branches
   carrying large forms beyond Earth arthropod scale.
4. Lineage IV — done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FAUNA-005`: fully aquatic throughout,
   with an ancestral trunk-and-fan locomotor core and the mass breeder's
   shield, salt organs, and gel chambers held as derived specialisations;
   `WLD-ECOLOGY-007`/`008` unchanged.
5. Lineage V — done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FAUNA-006`: tri-radial symmetry,
   hydrostatic support, and travelling margin waves ancestral, with the
   family-care migrant's vault, paddle-columns, refuge, and care system held
   as derived; `WLD-ECOLOGY-009`/`010` unchanged.
6. Lineage VII — done 2026-08-17, `WLD-FAUNA-007`: unsegmented soft axial
   bodies with life mode as a branch axis (ancestral aquatic filter feeding
   plus derived substrate and grazer branches); substrate branches own soil
   turnover and give rise to internal parasitism.

## Phase 3 — Deepen each lineage in turn

Per lineage, as needed: branch structure (the crown/ribbon pattern), 
reproduction and life cycle, distinctive physiology, Mana pattern boundary,
and representative forms. Suggested order by story pressure: I → III → VI →
IV → V → VII, revisiting II only for gaps. Kingdom deepening (K2–K6) slots in
where fauna diets and habitats demand it.

## Phase 4 — Basin focus

Assemble the initial-basin community from the scaffolded roster:

- resident lake fauna and the bird-like fishing organisms — done 2026-08-17,
  `WLD-FAUNA-008`: a structurally dominant year-round community principally
  from Lineage IV, bird-like fishers resolved as Lineage III forelimb-winged
  branches, shore hunters from Lineages I and III, with the accepted spring
  pulse preserved in full as a concentrated seasonal subsidy;
- complementary basin flora guilds filling kingdom slots — the returning
  woodland is done (2026-08-17, `WLD-FLORA-010` thicket-and-relict, which
  also made the first kingdom assignments); wet-margin, water, and decomposer
  guilds plus the rainforest and egg-substrate assignments remain;
- season-by-season basin food web tying flora pulses, Lineage II pressure,
  the lake migration, and small life together;
- settlement-adjacent life (candidate domesticates, pests, pathogens) —
  coordinated with culture records through separate gates.

## Standing discipline

- Scaffolds stay qualitative; quantitative modelling, names, and taxonomy
  remain protected unknowns.
- Earth life (living, extinct, mythological) inspires; nothing transplants.
- Every promotion is a bounded proposal with an explicit Author decision;
  this plan never substitutes for a gate.
