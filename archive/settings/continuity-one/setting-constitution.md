# Setting Constitution — Continuity One

`Continuity One` is a provisional display title. The setting ID and repository
slug are stable. This constitution currently establishes an authority boundary
and an unanswered decision map only; it contains no fictional world facts.

## Foundational constraints

1. Shared setting authority lives beneath `worldbuilding/settings/continuity-one/`.
2. Setting canon, when later accepted, is shared by adopting works.
3. Work-local additions must remain compatible with shared setting canon unless
   a separately scoped Author decision explicitly governs an exception,
   divergence, replacement, or retcon.
4. Every fictional foundation remains unresolved until approved through a
   later `SETTING_AUTHORITY` decision.

## Layered foundation decision map

The layers below define question order, not answers. Later work may revisit the
order when dependencies require it, but no layer may silently decide another.

### Layer 1 — Authority and epistemic structure

- Which distinctions between causal truth, records, access, interpretation,
  presentation, and reader disclosure need setting-specific rules?
- Which questions should remain permanently or temporarily unknowable?

### Layer 2 — Ontology and cosmology

- What exists, what can act causally, and what remains outside reliable knowledge?
- Which cosmological boundaries must be fixed before physical-world design?

### Layer 3 — Physical world

- What spatial, temporal, astronomical, geological, climatic, and material
  foundations are required?
- At what scales must those foundations remain consistent?

### Layer 4 — Life, personhood, and ecology

- What forms of life and agency exist, and how will personhood questions be governed?
- What ecological origins, constraints, life cycles, and interactions require definition?

### Layer 5 — System–world interface

- How does the locked CAL0 System relate to setting reality, living beings,
  knowledge, culture, and visible interfaces?
- Which setting inputs must be supplied without changing CAL0?

### Layer 6 — Deep history

- Which origins, transformations, catastrophes, migrations, and long-duration
  processes shaped later conditions?
- Which historical claims are truth, surviving evidence, belief, or uncertainty?

### Layer 7 — Societies and material cultures

- What populations, cultures, institutions, economies, technologies, religions,
  languages, and political structures require definition?
- How do geography, ecology, history, and the System constrain their variation?

### Layer 8 — Present-world envelope and knowledge

- Which era, regions, conditions, connections, and knowledge boundaries form the
  shared present available to later works?
- Which areas or facts should remain protected unknowns?

### Layer 9 — Work adoption

- Which future work first adopts the shared setting?
- Which accepted shared references and project defaults, if any, does that work
  explicitly adopt?
- Which compatible facts belong in a work-local overlay rather than shared canon?

## Protected unknowns

- Every unanswered question in Layers 1–9.
- The final display title.
- All fictional cosmology, geography, chronology, ecology, populations,
  cultures, institutions, locations, and present-world conditions.
- Whether any project-level default will later be adopted or replaced by a
  setting-local rule.
- All story, protagonist, character, plot, outline, and chapter matters.
