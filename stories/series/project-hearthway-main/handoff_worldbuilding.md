## Author direction that remains staging, not canon

The following is important creative direction, but it remains non-canonical
unless and until split into bounded proposals and explicitly approved. Use the
foundation capture for its full wording and provenance.

- The universe should support several stories across multiple periods,
  planets, and star systems. The main web novel begins on one planet and is
  primarily local to one lake-basin region.
- Local peoples study the stars but do not travel between worlds. Myths and
  ruined archways preserve disputed memories of otherworld travellers; this is
  background rather than an immediate plot engine.
- Everyone, including non-human creatures, has access to the LitRPG System,
  but cultures and individuals understand and use it differently. Do not
  invent mechanics beyond CAL0.
- The opening region is in a dark age after a cascading imperial retreat about
  seventy years earlier. Institutions, knowledge, manpower, and suppression of
  large wildlife fractured rather than vanishing cleanly.
- The intended ecological feel is Earth-legible but alien. Earth life from any
  era and mythology may inspire designs, but species should not be direct
  transplants.
- Most organisms use Mana through ordinary biological reinforcement or
  regulation; specialised organs and abilities are less common. Mana never
  replaces matter, food, water, oxygen or another established respiratory
  medium, maintenance, recovery, or ecological cost.
- Creature growth should usually follow lineage/body-plan tendencies with
  minor variance. Sentience emerging in a non-sapient animal is extremely
  rare and must not follow from level alone.
- Those creature-progression statements are staging design requirements, not
  accepted mechanics. Route any mechanical implementation through a separate
  System-change review rather than importing it into CAL0.
- The desired experience is an alien, causally realistic ecosystem perceived
  through the protagonist's bounded knowledge—not an encounter list or loot
  catalogue.
- The violent sapient pack species is deliberately parked. Do not continue it
  until the Author says it is relevant.
- The five-lineage animal framework remains staging-only except for the
  individually governed Lineage II claims. Lineage I has a capture-only
  outline; Lineages III–V remain undefined and should fill missing food-web and
  habitat functions rather than merely add novel monsters.

## Author interaction requirements

- Ask questions as multiple-choice selections. Prefer one consequential
  question at a time.
- Put the recommended choice first and explain the practical trade-off of every
  option in plain language.
- Always permit a custom or combined answer, and discuss alternatives when the
  Author asks.
- Ask whenever a consequential uncertainty remains; do not silently fill it.
- The Author has explicitly asked that choices be recorded as work proceeds.
- Distinguish direct Author choice, provisional canon, proposal, inference,
  research analogy, and open question every time.
- Do not silently make implications binding. Material choices require an
  Author decision.
- Work from ecological role and causal relationships before inventing species.
  Every organism needs matter/energy inputs, life cycle, consumers or prey,
  competitors, population limits, seasonal behaviour, traces, and costs for
  specialised Mana use.
- Treat the basin as a connected whole while allowing nonlocal migrations,
  imported food or materials, travellers' animals, and stories of distant
  creatures. Label each presence channel explicitly; a tale does not establish
  local presence.

## Best next development fronts

The current official open gates are in the setting index and `STATUS.md`.
The most useful next-session menu is:

- **A — Protagonist-radius ecological transect — recommended.** Develop the
  fortified crown, managed apron, woodland edge, lake road, upper-shore staging
  place, and wet margin as one connected lived journey. Fill producer,
  decomposer, small-life, resident-prey, predator, trace, and seasonal roles

  productivity, shelter, water, nutrient, disturbance, and migration pattern
  before zooming into more organisms. **Trade-off:** this gives the strongest
  basin-wide consistency but delays concrete organisms and lived sensory
  detail.

  mixed refuge community, farming and maintenance calendar, System education,
  material culture, founder–council tensions, succession, and present threats
  without promoting the staging population or government details silently.
  **Trade-off:** this advances story-facing daily life but may require
  biological resources and seasonal patterns that remain undefined.
- **D — Wider regional ecology.** Define migration ranges, neighbouring
  habitats, traveller-carried animals, imported produce or meat, and distant
  creature tales with explicit local, seasonal, imported, captive, product, or
  reported presence classes. **Trade-off:** this supports migrations, imports,
  and distant tales but postpones the protagonist's immediate local ecology.

Start the next session by presenting those four choices to the Author. Do not
assume the recommended branch has been selected.

## Recording workflow

For each consequential worldbuilding choice:

1. Resolve the paired Project Hearthway work manifest and adopted authorities.
2. Classify the change as clarification, extension, reconciliation,
   replacement, or retcon.
3. Create a bounded proposal preserving alternatives, dependencies, causal
   consequences, knowledge limits, CAL0 implications, and protected unknowns.
4. Present the Author decision as multiple choice.
5. Promote only the exact explicit selection through a paired Author decision
   and canon record.
6. Update parent boundaries, setting indexes, manifest adoption, contract and
   summaries without adding reverse dependency cycles.
7. Retain an evidence bundle and run current-byte evidence verification before
   claiming completion.

Do not rewrite old decisions or historical evidence when later records refine
their open boundaries.
