## Established direction

- Form: an ongoing LitRPG web-novel series
- Setting relationship: the main series set within Project Hearthway
- Current development frontier: one planet and its initial lake-basin region
- Ecosystem frame: a coupled open-ground, woodland, wet-margin, and lake mosaic
  nested within a wider regional ecology
- Lake seasonal frame: a broadly productive but spatially and seasonally varied
  lake, with overlapping spring mass-breeding and family-care migrant pulses,
  resident competition, concentrated predators, and human harvest activity
- Lake mass-breeder form: a fully aquatic flexible-trunk swimmer with three
  paired fin-fans, a muscular tail, a wedge-shaped mineralised head shield,
  aquatic respiratory folds, and ordinary Mana-supported tissue reinforcement
- Lake mass-breeder fluid-and-trim frame: dedicated ion-exchange organs support
  gradual freshwater–marine acclimation, while longitudinal wax-like-gel
  chambers provide passive buoyancy and greater support behind the head shield;
  exact anatomy, chemistry, quantitative performance, and life-stage variation
  remain open
- Lake family-care migrant form: a low three-lobed living vault with a ring of
  short hydrostatic paddle-columns, travelling waves along the body margin, and
  a mobile underside refuge beneath which young shelter, forage outward, and
  regroup
- Lake family-care migrant outlet passage: successive paddle-column groups
  brace while a travelling margin wave drives short advances; the vault settles
  low between surges, the caregiving form normally attempts to regroup mobile
  young before advances, and the same structures brake and steer
  current-assisted autumn slides. The card's slow-and-conspicuous warning and
  its abrasion, injury, fatigue, crowding, and interrupted-rest risks remain
  qualitative feasibility concerns. The behaviour requires but does not
  establish a continuous usable bed-or-margin and resting-opportunity chain;
  exact route fit, passability, mechanics, physiology, reproduction, carer
  structure, Mana use, and knowledge remain open
- Physical frame: wet western and northwestern uplands, a sheltered central
  river-fed freshwater lake basin with a constrained seaward outlet, downstream
  eastern and southeastern marsh country and drowned coast, and a transitional
  maritime-continental seasonal climate
- Initial-settlement working landscape: the fortified core crowns the local
  ridge spur within an unequal-shoulder apron. A low-vegetation warning belt
  surrounds it; small incomplete contour terraces occupy some steeper upper
  ground; broader fields and grazing spread across the gentler lower shoulder;
  and woodland returns closer on difficult sides. A bending road descends toward
  the lake, which lies beyond the immediate apron but remains within regular
  local travel and haulage reach. A spring emerges inside the fortified
  perimeter, supplemented by wells and cistern storage; exact yield, quality,
  capacity, source relationships, and security remain open, while usable water
  remains finite
- Lake-road practical scale: under the selected fair-weather comparison, an
  ordinary unladen downhill journey uses a broad `1–1½ hours` planning band and
  a routine laden uphill return uses a broad `2–3 hours` planning band. These
  are direction-and-load-specific authoring anchors, not guaranteed times,
  schedules, precise measurements, in-world units, or System readouts; exact
  distance, other direction/load combinations, road conditions, journey
  outcomes, transport methods, exact endpoint construction, and shore
  infrastructure remain open
- Lake-road shore endpoint: the main road ends at a recurring, relatively firm
  upper-shore staging place set back from the ordinarily shifting wet margin.
  Several short seasonal spurs lead to changeable nearby shore contacts. Exact
  placement, spur count and layout, construction, maintenance, transport,
  traffic, shore works, ecological overlap, institutions, and guaranteed access
  remain open
- Outlet physical frame: within the defensible gap, a narrow bedrock-controlled
  throat opens into a mixed reach of irregular boulder steps, lateral shelves,
  and holding pockets. These create state-dependent candidate contact and
  lower-velocity opportunities, not a guaranteed support chain, safe refuge,
  route capacity, or successful passage

These are development boundaries, not an accepted premise or plot contract.
