# The Sealed Step

Nera turned the iron key in the east shutter. Five minutes remained before the storm, more than enough time to cross the flooded undercroft and climb the north-vault stair.

She had never seen the north vault, but she knew the third arch concealed a latch. Mara of `character://series/borrowed-empire/mara` had warned her about it—though Nera had never met Mara and no message had crossed between their worlds.

Water closed over Nera's head. She swam the length of the undercroft without hesitation, reached the glass tablet, and understood its complete history before touching it.

The System chimed: *For all the careful cataloguing you did last winter, gain 40 Skill XP now.* The old work finally counted.

The tablet was dangerous. The tablet was old. The tablet was hers to protect. Nera put it in her satchel, then returned by the east stair before the same five minutes had elapsed. Above, someone was already pursuing her.
